Author: Dizzy Mizz Lizzy’s opening night at Copenhell was oddly uneven. Letting the audience pick the setlist might not have been the best idea.

Pernille Jensen
Tim Christensen looked like a man trying hard to suppress a hopeful smile.
He stood on Copenhell’s biggest stage, playing the heavy intro to ’In The Blood’ from Dizzy Mizz Lizzy’s latest album, ’Alter Echo’ from 2020, and resembled a little boy behind his beard.
