Title: The_superstar_gave_everyone_a_shot_at_redemption_on_the_Orange_stage
Date: 2025-07-07
Article Type: Culture
Author: Olivia Rodrigo’s headlining concert at Roskilde felt like a cathartic revenge ritual for anyone nursing a broken heart.

Lucia Odoom
Kulturjournalist
Olivia Rodrigo stomped all over her ex-boyfriends with every song during her Friday night concert on the Orange Stage. And the entire audience sang along with every fiber of their being.
There wasn’t a single song that didn’t revolve around the feeling of being relentlessly trampled. It resonated deeply, especially where I was standing: among young women around the same age as the 22-year-old star on stage.
