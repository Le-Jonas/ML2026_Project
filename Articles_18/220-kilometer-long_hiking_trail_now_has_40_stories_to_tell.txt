Title: 220-kilometer-long_hiking_trail_now_has_40_stories_to_tell
Date: 2025-07-16
Article Type: Culture
Author: Nature is no longer the only experience awaiting on the 220 kilometers of the Archipelago Trail in South Funen.

Sune Højrup Bencke
Journalist
There are plenty of tales to be told in South Funen, where the hiking trail Øhavsstien is now getting a boost with new signs and audio stories.
This happens at 40 locations along the 220-kilometer route, says Nils Valdersdorf Jensen, department head at Svendborg Museum, who is the project leader for the storytelling initiative.
