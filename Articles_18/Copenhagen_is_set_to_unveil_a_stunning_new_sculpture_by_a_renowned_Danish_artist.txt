Title: Copenhagen_is_set_to_unveil_a_stunning_new_sculpture_by_a_renowned_Danish_artist
Date: 2025-08-19
Article Type: News
Author: If you walk down Enghavevej in Copenhagen, you’ll soon be greeted by a 17-meter-long ship in the median.

Starting next weekend, passersby near the Vesterbro fire station on Enghavevej in Copenhagen will witness an impossibility revealed in the median between residential buildings on one side and the railway on the other.
A 17-meter-long red ship, created by artist Tove Storch, will fill the urban space with her signature duality.
