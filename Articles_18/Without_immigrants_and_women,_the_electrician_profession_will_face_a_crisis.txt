Title: Without_immigrants_and_women,_the_electrician_profession_will_face_a_crisis
Date: 2025-06-26
Article Type: News
Author: Employers and workers in the electrician field didn’t exactly follow Donald Trump’s path when they agreed on a contract this spring aimed at promoting diversity in the profession.

Thomas Flensburg
Journalist
The 28-year-old Filmon Habteab is busy fixing an electrical outlet at Køge’s Music School, which is currently being renovated. Around him, men of various ages are working on the construction site, but none of them share Filmon Habteab’s background.
»I am happy to be an apprentice electrician«, he says in good, albeit slightly uncertain Danish.
