Title: Greenwashing_cases_are_piling_up_and_remain_unresolved_for_years
Date: 2025-10-15
Article Type: News
Author: Public records indicate that cases of greenwashing in food products are piling up at the Danish Veterinary and Food Administration and often end up becoming outdated.

Lars Dahlager
Journalist
In Denmark, it is illegal to market products as being green if they truly aren’t or if they aren’t greener than similar products. This is known as greenwashing.
But in reality, authorities seem to be doing very little when it comes to greenwashing of food products.
