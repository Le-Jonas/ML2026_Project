Title: Danes_are_stocking_up_»They_used_to_laugh_at_us_preppers«
Date: 2025-10-20
Article Type: News
Author: After drone flights shut down several airports, Danes feel a sharper need to prep for crisis. Two in three have either stocked up or plan to build an emergency stash.

Thomas Flensburg
Journalist
In the village of Ulstrup, west of Randers, Bernt Christian Godtfredsen bought an old hair salon this summer and transformed the 150-square-meter space into a prepper store, selling headlamps, water purifiers, and field rations, among other things.
»Sales are going really well. We get 20 to 25 customers a day, and I could actually make a living selling prepper goods«, says Bernt Christian Godtfredsen, who also works as a bodyguard.
