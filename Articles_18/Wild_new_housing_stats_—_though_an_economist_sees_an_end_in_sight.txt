Author: Copenhagen housing prices are rising even faster than before, new figures show. Here’s why that’s possible.

Per Thiemann
Redaktør for økonomi og samfund
If you’re in the market and banking on prices slipping soon, you’ll have to be patient for a while yet—mildly put.
Nationwide home prices rose more in November than in any single month since the pandemic: up 1.3 percent seasonally adjusted, Boligsiden reports.
