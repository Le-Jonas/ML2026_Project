Emil and his girlfriend attended an open house, which turned out to be an illegal auction for an attractive share among hundreds of interested parties. The board turned a blind eye to his warning.
As told to Dorthe Lønstrup
»My partner and I were on the lookout for a cooperative apartment, and like everyone else, we had set up an alert on Den Blå Avis (Den Blå Avis is Denmark’s largest platform for buying and selling items, similar to Craigslist in the United States) and took turns responding within 10 seconds when a new listing appeared.
At one point, a co-op popped up in Nordvest for DKK 1.6 million in an area we really wanted to live. We thought we had an okay dialogue directly with the seller. But then he posted on DBA that there would be an open house for two days over the weekend. That’s when we realized things didn’t look good.
