On Monday, Donald Trump will have been president for six months, which has meant steady work for the media industry – even in Denmark. According to the news archive Infomedia, Danish media have mentioned the American president Donald Trump in 3,349 news segments over the past week. That’s 669.6 times a day.
Jessica Balogova, sunbathing with her three friends at Islands Brygge, is convinced that Greenland gave Trump his big breakthrough in Denmark.
