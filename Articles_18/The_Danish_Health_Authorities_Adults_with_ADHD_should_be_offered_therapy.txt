Title: The_Danish_Health_Authorities_Adults_with_ADHD_should_be_offered_therapy
Date: 2025-10-03
Article Type: News
Author: Therapy for adults with ADHD can be beneficial, improving their quality of life, confirms new national recommendation from the Danish Health Authority. It is not standard treatment today. And it will be costly.

Anders Legarth Schmidt
Journalist

Sundhedsredaktør
They struggle to find structure in their daily lives, plan their week, manage their thoughts that constantly go in all directions, and have difficulty controlling their emotions.
Adults with the developmental disorder ADHD face more challenges in life because they find it harder to meet the expectations placed upon them.
