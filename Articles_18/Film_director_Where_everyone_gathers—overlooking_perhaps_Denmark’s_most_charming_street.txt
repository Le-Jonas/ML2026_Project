Journalist
»At the moment, I’m working on two feature film projects, so a large part of my playlists consists of classical music. And I’m not picky – it just needs to be dramatic, dynamic, emotional, and creative. This could be anything from Bach to Stravinsky.«
»Deep down, I am a versatile pop/rock/singer-songwriter guy who loves The National, Arcade Fire, Lana Del Rey, Björk, Father John Misty, The Beatles, Adele. I would like to recommend Danish band Blaue Blume and their new album. It’s incredibly fine and a remarkably successful first Danish-language album from them.«
