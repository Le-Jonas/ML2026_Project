Author: Think tank: Denmark isn’t a green frontrunner. Match our wood burn, and 9% of the EU turns into energy forest.

Denmark burns seven million tons of wood pellets and wood chips each year in the massive furnaces of its power and heating plants. The Amager Power Plant (Amagerværket) alone receives a ship loaded to the brim with wood chips more than every other day all year round.
