It may be possible to demand the deportation of a 28-year-old woman and to scrutinize the citizenship of her two young children, according to a response from the Minister for Immigration and Integration.
Hans Drachmann
Journalist
The government is now allowing the prosecution to demand the deportation of a 28-year-old woman from Funen, who has lived in Denmark since she was a child and has held Danish citizenship since she was nine.
This is revealed in a response from the Minister for Immigration and Integration, Kaare Dybvad Bek (S), sent to the Parliamentary Committee on Citizenship.
