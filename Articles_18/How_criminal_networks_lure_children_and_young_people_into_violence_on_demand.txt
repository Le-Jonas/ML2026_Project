Title: How_criminal_networks_lure_children_and_young_people_into_violence_on_demand
Date: 2025-08-14
Article Type: News
Author: Criminal networks use emotional manipulation and grooming techniques when they attempt to recruit children and young people for violence on demand via the internet.

Frank Hvilsom
Journalist
When minors are targeted online to be recruited for drug crimes, cyber attacks, online fraud, extortion, violence, or murder, criminals use cunning manipulations to foster loyalty and a sense of belonging, which can lure teenagers into criminal activities.
They attempt to make young people feel »special« by presenting illegal activities as exclusive opportunities for those who possess unique qualities, and they particularly appeal to minors who seek recognition, protection, or a sense of community.
