Title: Party_wants_outdoor_seating_at_City_Hall_Square_in_Copenhagen
Date: 2025-09-17
Article Type: News
Author: Outdoor dining enhances the city’s atmosphere, according to Venstre, who believes that the City Hall Square is currently a place people mostly hurry across.

Niels Holst
Journalist, digital forsideredaktør
I
t should be possible to enjoy a cup of coffee and grab a bite to eat while sitting in City Hall Square, savoring the bustling life in the heart of Copenhagen.
That’s the view of the Venstre (V) party in Copenhagen, which would like to see outdoor seating permitted on »the most expensive street in Matador«, as stated in a proposal from the party that will be discussed at the upcoming meeting of the Copenhagen City Council on Thursday.
