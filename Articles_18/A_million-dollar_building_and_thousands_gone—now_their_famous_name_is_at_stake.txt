Title: A_million-dollar_building_and_thousands_gone—now_their_famous_name_is_at_stake
Date: 2025-10-30
Article Type: News
Author: Theater group Mastodonterne should rebrand, says ex-chairman Steen Lünell, who resigned after claims of a toxic culture. The new board is keen to distance itself from the long-time figurehead.

Johanne Lerhard
Journalist
Within the past month, the prominent amateur theater group Mastodonterne has lost both its home and hundreds of thousands of kroner in municipal support following allegations of a boundary-crossing and sexualized culture.
Now, the former chairman has also demanded the association change its long-standing logo and famous name, reports
Frederiksborg Amts Avis
