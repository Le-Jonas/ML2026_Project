Title: Homework_is_pointless_for_a_majority_of_high_school_students
Date: 2025-06-24
Article Type: News
Author: A majority of Danish high school students find homework meaningless and struggle to concentrate. At Solrød Gymnasium, they have a different approach to studying.

Karoline Fogh Lassen
Journalist
In Flemming Lindquist Hansen’s HF classes, students read. They read in-depth, they read aloud, and they take turns reading. However, they can leave the books behind when they go home.
