Title: New_conservative_media_outlet_to_be_written_by_artificial_intelligence
Date: 2025-08-26
Article Type: News
Author: Artificial intelligence will be the backbone of a new media outlet that a group of conservative businesspeople, politicians, and media professionals plan to launch next year. ’There are no limitations,’ says one of the key figures behind the initiative.

Torben Benner
Journalist
As media’s use of artificial intelligence (AI) becomes increasingly debated, a group of conservative businesspeople, politicians, and media figures are set to launch a new Danish media outlet where AI is behind all the stories.
The group includes businesspeople Lars Seier Christensen and Lars Tvede, chairman at Dansk Folkeparti (DF) Morten Messerschmidt, and the party’s communications director Asger Juhl, as well as former news director at TV 2 and editor-in-chief at B.T., Michael Dyrby.
