Title: The_episode_on_Molslinjen_shows_that_Russia's_hybrid_warfare_has_also_reached_Denmark
Date: 2025-05-27
Article Type: News
Author: Last year, a ferry traveling between Sjællands Odde and Aarhus was dangerously close to colliding with a Russian warship that refused to alter its course. According to intelligence services, this incident indicates that Russia’s hybrid warfare has also reached Denmark.

Claus Blok Thomsen
Nordisk korrespondent
Hans Davidsen-Nielsen
Journalist
Oversættelsesdisclaimer
It was Friday, April 19th last year, and Lars Hansen was looking forward to a pleasant weekend. He’s from Northern Jutland, and together with his wife and their two boys, aged 7 and 11, they were driving from Copenhagen to Aalborg. As they had done many times before, they planned to split the journey into two parts by taking Molslinjen’s fast ferry from Sjællands Odde to Aarhus.
