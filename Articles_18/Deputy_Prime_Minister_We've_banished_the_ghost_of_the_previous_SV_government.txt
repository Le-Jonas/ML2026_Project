Date: 2025-05-20
Article Type: News
Author: Defense and Deputy Prime Minister Troels Lund Poulsen is confident that it will soon be easier to win over Venstre’s base to the advantages of a centrist majority government.

Kristian Klarskov
Politisk reporter
Johan Blem Larsen
Politisk reporter
When the SVM government was young and fresh, Jakob Ellemann-Jensen’s stand-in Troels Lund Poulsen (Venstre) gave an interview filled with praise for his flawless cooperation with Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (Socialdemokratiet). The interview was so unexpected that Politiken’s satire section, At Tænke Sig (ATS), had a field day for weeks with the surprising political romance.
