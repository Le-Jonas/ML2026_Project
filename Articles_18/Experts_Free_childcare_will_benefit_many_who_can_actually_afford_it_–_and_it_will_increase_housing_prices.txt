Title: Experts_Free_childcare_will_benefit_many_who_can_actually_afford_it_–_and_it_will_increase_housing_prices
Date: 2025-08-20
Article Type: News
Author: Free childcare is intended to offset the high cost of living in Copenhagen, says mayoral candidate Pernille Rosenkrantz-Theil (S). However, many Copenhagen residents do not live expensively – and the initiative could push prices upward.

Per Thiemann
Redaktør for økonomi og samfund
If sending your children to daycare and kindergarten in Copenhagen becomes free, it will be a huge win for families with young children in the city. But this significant financial injection will also have other consequences.
Free childcare will also provide many people who don’t live particularly expensively with a substantial economic surplus. And it will put even more pressure on housing prices in Copenhagen.
