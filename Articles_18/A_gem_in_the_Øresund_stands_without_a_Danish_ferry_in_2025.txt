Date: 2025-05-28

Author: Problems with approval for international navigation mean that Sundbussen will not operate a regular route to Hven this year. Danes will therefore need to take the ferry from Landskrona.

Problems with approval for international navigation mean that Sundbussen will not operate a regular route to Hven this year. Danes will therefore need to take the ferry from Landskrona.
Sune Højrup Bencke
Journalist
Oversættelsesdisclaimer
Danes looking for a trip to Hven this year can no longer take a direct boat from either Copenhagen or Helsingør.
