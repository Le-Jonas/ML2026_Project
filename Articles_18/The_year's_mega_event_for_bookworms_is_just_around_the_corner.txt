Every week, the Ibyen editorial team scours Copenhagen’s events, exhibitions, concerts, and pop-ups and selects a range of suggestions for how you can spend your week in the city.
Experience!
What about a cocktail consisting of Danish alternative pop music, IPA beer, and an abundance of Instagram-friendly aesthetics? You can quench your thirst this Saturday, November 8th at the
Xenon
venue in the city center. Konkylie is wrapping up its six-year anniversary celebration here. And if, by any chance, you as an Ibyen reader don’t know the birthday celebrant, Konkylie is a record label that has been a significant player on the Danish music scene for years, presenting music from names like First Flush, Afskum, Demersal, and Gents. Over the years, Konkylie has also grown into a creative agency and a bar in Nordvest that you’ve undoubtedly seen in your story.
