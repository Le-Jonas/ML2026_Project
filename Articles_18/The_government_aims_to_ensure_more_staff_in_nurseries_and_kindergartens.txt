Title: The_government_aims_to_ensure_more_staff_in_nurseries_and_kindergartens
Date: 2025-08-26
Article Type: News
Author: Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (Socialdemokratiet) plans to strengthen Danish daycare institutions with measures totaling 1.1 billion kroner, aimed at hiring more staff.

Nilas Heinskou
Politisk redaktør
Children in Denmark’s day nurseries and kindergartens will soon have more adult company.
In the government’s budget proposal for next year, expected this week, 1.1 billion kroner will be allocated to increase staffing levels by nearly 1,400 employees in daycare institutions. Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (Socialdemokratiet, S) shared this news ahead of the party’s summer group meeting on Tuesday.
