Author: The Royal Danish Ballet’s ’The Great Gatsby’ is a visual and auditory treat, but it never dives deep.

Monna Dithmer
Extravagance radiates through the hall. Seductively dazzling, ’The Great Gatsby’ flashes by in a stream of images, dance, and Gershwin evergreens. An abundance of chorus girls, cocktails, and luxury party flair, plus a genuine Rolls-Royce, all heading towards great ruin.
