Title: They_steal_Danes'_identities_-_and_they_get_away_with_it
Date: 2025-05-12
Article Type: News
Author: A new law was supposed to prevent identity theft online. But three years later, only a few reports turn into actual cases.

Emilie Stein
Journalist
It was a unanimous Folketing, the Danish parliament, that decided it should be a crime to steal someone else’s identity and impersonate another person online.
»It can be an extremely unpleasant experience and can have significant consequences for one’s privacy and presence on social media«, said the parties part of the agreement, when the law was passed.
