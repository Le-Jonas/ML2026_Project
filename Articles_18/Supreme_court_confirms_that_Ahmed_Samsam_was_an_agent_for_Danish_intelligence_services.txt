Title: Supreme_court_confirms_that_Ahmed_Samsam_was_an_agent_for_Danish_intelligence_services
Date: 2025-09-03
Article Type: News
Author: Ahmed Samsam, who is convicted of terrorism, now has confirmation from both the Supreme Court and the intelligence services that he was a Danish agent on trips to Syria. He is still seeking to have his conviction overturned in Spain.

Morten Skjoldager
Journalist
Hans Davidsen-Nielsen
Journalist
Ahmed Samsam represents everything that the government and pillar of society dislike: He is a convicted criminal with an immigrant background, prone to violence, loud, and takes off his dirty Nike shoes in the Supreme Court. Yet, it was Samsam who received a favorable ruling from the country’s highest court on Tuesday in what can surely be described as an extraordinary
verdict
.
The Danish Security and Intelligence Service (PET) and the Danish Defense Intelligence Service (FE) have been told by seven unanimous Supreme Court judges that they must acknowledge that Ahmed Samsam was a secret agent on trips to Syria in 2013 and 2014. During these trips, he provided information on Danish fighters in Syria for payment. As a consequence of the ruling, the two services have already issued a
joint statement
admitting that Samsam was their deployed eyes and ears in Syria.
