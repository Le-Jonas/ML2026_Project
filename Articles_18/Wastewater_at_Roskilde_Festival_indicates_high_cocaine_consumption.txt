Title: Wastewater_at_Roskilde_Festival_indicates_high_cocaine_consumption
Date: 2025-06-25
Article Type: News
Author: The use of opioids appears to be on the rise at Roskilde Festival. Researchers, the chief of security, and the police say this reflects a broader trend in society.

Lars Eriksen
Journalist
When festivalgoers party, dance, and sniff, researchers collect pill packages and wastewater.
For the past three years, Professor Henrik Vigh has led a team from the Criminological Observatory at the University of Copenhagen, studying drug intake during the festivities at the Roskilde Festival.
