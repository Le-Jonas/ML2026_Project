Title: Here's_how_traffic_will_be_affected_by_EU_summits_in_Copenhagen
Date: 2025-10-01
Article Type: News
Author: Many heads of state and government are coming to Copenhagen in the next few days for two summits. This will lead to changes in traffic and public transportation.

Pia Buhl Andersen
Journalist
It won’t be quiet in the capital when Denmark hosts two European summits on October 1 and 2 as part of Denmark’s EU presidency. In connection with the meetings, »traffic flow through the city« will be affected, says Copenhagen Police.
The areas around Christiansborg Palace, Frederiksholms Kanal, Bella Center, and Grøndalscenter will be particularly impacted.
