Title: Partners_are_more_likely_to_share_mental_disorders_–_and_this_can_have_consequences_for_their_children
Date: 2025-09-05

Author: A new study reveals that mentally vulnerable individuals frequently choose partners with similar mental health issues. This increases the risk of such conditions appearing in future generations.
