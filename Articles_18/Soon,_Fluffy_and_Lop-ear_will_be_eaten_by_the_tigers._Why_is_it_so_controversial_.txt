Title: Soon,_Fluffy_and_Lop-ear_will_be_eaten_by_the_tigers._Why_is_it_so_controversial_
Date: 2025-08-08
Article Type: News
Author: Foreign countries are outraged that Danish zoos use pets to feed their animals. Politiken followed two pet rabbits that ended their lives as the tigers’ toothbrush in Odense Zoo.

Frauke Giebner
Journalist
Wednesday morning, at the back entrance of Odense Zoo, a quiet woman hands a cardboard box to a zookeeper. Then she quickly leaves.
Inside the box sits a white rabbit with large blue eyes and adorable perky ears. Its fur is tousled and soft as silk, a fluffy little cloud. Beside it, a gray mottled lop-eared rabbit twitches its nose.
