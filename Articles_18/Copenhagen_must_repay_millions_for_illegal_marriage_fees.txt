Title: Copenhagen_must_repay_millions_for_illegal_marriage_fees
Date: 2025-08-13
Article Type: News
Author: 12,000 primarily foreign couples without residence in Denmark have been charged an illegal fee to get married at Copenhagen City Hall from 2018 and until this summer.

Niels Holst
Journalist, digital forsideredaktør
Foreign couples from all over the world have flocked to Copenhagen in recent years to get married at the city’s 120-year-old town hall designed by architect Martin Nyrop.
Now it turns out that many of them have received a wedding gift from the municipality that it could have done without.
