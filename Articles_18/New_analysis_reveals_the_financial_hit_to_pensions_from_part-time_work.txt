Title: New_analysis_reveals_the_financial_hit_to_pensions_from_part-time_work
Date: 2025-06-17
Article Type: News
Author: Women face a disadvantage when opting for part-time work. Family law attorney frequently meets women who suddenly recognize their financial vulnerability.

Thomas Flensburg
Journalist
New mothers are opting for part-time work in large numbers after the birth of their first child
When a child turns two, one in five women shift from full-time to part-time work, according to an analysis in ’Status on the Labor Market’ published by the Ministry of Employment.
