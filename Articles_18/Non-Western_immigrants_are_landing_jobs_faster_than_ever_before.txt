Title: Non-Western_immigrants_are_landing_jobs_faster_than_ever_before
Date: 2025-09-09
Article Type: News
Author: There’s been a surge in the number of newly arrived non-Western immigrants who find jobs within six months of entering Denmark. This benefits the state treasury, according to experts.

Per Thiemann
Redaktør for økonomi og samfund
It’s likely not escaped most people’s attention that thousands of foreigners
are coming to Denmark to work
these years, but new data sheds light on just how quickly people from abroad are finding employment.
There has been a significant boom in the percentage of non-Western foreigners who have secured jobs within the first six months of arriving in Denmark, according to a new analysis.
