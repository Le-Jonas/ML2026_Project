Title: Indictment_Doctors_charged_public_funds_for_assisting_patients_who_had_passed_away
Date: 2025-08-18
Article Type: News
Author: Three doctors from Charlottenlund Medical House deny charges of data fraud and overbilling the Capitol Region in thousands of instances. Deceased patients and Superliga clubs are key pieces in the affair.

Jakob Sorgenfri Kjær
Journalist
In the physician’s oath, the declaration made by newly graduated doctors to perform their work conscientiously, it states that »I shall always strive to use my knowledge diligently and carefully for the benefit of society and my fellow human beings...«.
The physician’s oath does not say anything about using one’s knowledge for the benefit of those who have passed away.
