Date: 2025-07-13
Article Type: Edition
Author: From the time they were very young, they were so close that they hardly knew where one ended and the other began. And when they grew up, Maya Ilsøe’s sister was the inspiration for everything she wrote. The two Christmas calendars ’Absalons hemmelighed’ (Absalon’s Secret) and ’Pagten’ (The Pact). And the TV series ’Arvingerne’ (The Legacy), which made most people reflect on their own sibling relationships. Then her sister died. The magic was gone. And since then, Maya Ilsøe hasn’t written a single line about siblings.
