Title: 19-year-old's_breakthrough_is_hard_to_comprehend
Date: 2025-10-13
Article Type: News
Author: He left the goal scoring to others, but Victor Froholdt was everywhere as Denmark won 3-1 over Greece and set course for a final on the road to the World Cup. The 19-year-old comet’s breakthrough is hard to comprehend.

Jeppe Laursen Brock
Journalist
As he stands there with his hands behind his back, slightly wandering blue eyes, and light hair on his chin, Victor Froholdt looks exactly like what he is: a teenager. But the 19-year-old’s shoulders carry the dreams of a football nation.
»Yeah, I’m going pretty fast these days«, he notes without saying a single grand word too much.
