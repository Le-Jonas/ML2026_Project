A new restaurant serves German-Austrian food with quirky twists. It’s like dining in a Deutsche Bahn dining car during a themed party, while you search for the emergency brake.
Joakim Grundahl
Film-, tv- og madanmelder på Politiken
It was during the winter holiday that I first felt disappointed by a combination of German and Austrian cuisine.
I was traveling by train between Osnabrück and Duisburg and had dinner in Deutsche Bahn’s dining car. There was an Austrian theme, so I had some crumbling spätzle with some gravy. And some meat. I can’t remember what.
