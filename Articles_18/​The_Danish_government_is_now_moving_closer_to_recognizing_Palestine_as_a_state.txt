Title: ​The_Danish_government_is_now_moving_closer_to_recognizing_Palestine_as_a_state
Date: 2025-09-05
Article Type: News
Author: Before the end of the month, the government must decide under what circumstances it will be able to recognize Palestine as a state, says Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen.

Cecilie Lund Kristiansen
Uddannelsesredaktør
The Danish government changes course and is now moving closer to recognizing Palestine as a state.
Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (Socialdemokratiet, S) has previously rejected the idea of recognizing Palestine until there is a prospect of it being a state with democracy, human rights, and equality.
