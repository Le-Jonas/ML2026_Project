Author: 39-year‑old Mona fears she could wind up homeless after being placed on the new minimum welfare rate, and the social organization Settlementet worries she’s not the only one at risk.

It was not until early August that Mona realized her welfare payments had been reduced from around DKK 12,000 to the new minimum rate of just under DKK 7,000.
Although Mona had previously been informed via e-Boks that she was subject to the new welfare reform, she hadn’t checked.
