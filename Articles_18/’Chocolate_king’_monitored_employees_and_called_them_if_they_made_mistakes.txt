Title: ’Chocolate_king’_monitored_employees_and_called_them_if_they_made_mistakes
Date: 2025-10-06
Article Type: News
Author: Peter Beier, director of the chocolate company of the same name, has allegedly used surveillance to monitor and reprimand employees in real-time, according to staff. The company refuses the criticism.

Lærke Hoffmann
Isabelle Lillium
Amalie Mikkelsen
Jonas Pröschold
For nearly three decades, the Peter Beier Chocolate chain has been selling handmade quality chocolates.
With 19 stores on Zealand, one in Japan, and 225 employees, the company is a leading player in the Danish chocolate market. At the top is Peter Beier, who has been dubbed the ’Chocolate King’ by the media.
