A new research project has identified four criteria for the perfect vacation. And you don’t actually need to travel far to fulfill them.
Tina Krongaard
Rejseredaktør
We are in the midst of an accelerating climate crisis. Yet we continue to fly on vacation, even though we know it’s one of the most climate-damaging activities we can engage in.
This paradox puzzled three researchers at the Department of Environmental Science at Aarhus University: Lars Kjerulf Petersen, Katinka Bundgård Fals, and Anne Gammelgaard Ballantyne. Over the past three years, they have worked on a research project to investigate whether climate considerations can be integrated into the ideal vacation.
