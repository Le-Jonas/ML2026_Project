We begin in the Social Democrats’ camp, where Pernille Rosenkrantz-Theil’s supporters look a bit tense. In the picture, they hold banners with Rosenkrantz-Theil’s name while their gazes look up at the stage.
They look up at the mayoral candidate. She stands on stage at the Workers Museum in Copenhagen. »It has been a tough campaign«, she says. »People are moving towards the extremes – it creates unrest, hatred, and division«.
Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (S) was also present at the Workers Museum in Copenhagen. However, she was not satisfied: »We expected a setback, but it looks like the decline is greater than we had anticipated«.
Despite the support, the party at the Workers Museum ended before the election result for Copenhagen was clear.
