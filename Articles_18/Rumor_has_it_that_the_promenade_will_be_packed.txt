Last weekend, for once, it was the Copenhageners who experienced FOMO over the activities happening in Aarhus. There was Spot Festival, where a plethora of upcoming musicians performed around the city known as the City of Smiles, while Copenhageners had to settle for listening in via Instagram stories. This week, however, you can get your revenge, as smaller concerts are popping up all over. For instance, at the venue
Urban 13
in Nørrebro, where pop artists Agnes Ea and Noras Have will be performing on Thursday, May 8. Admission: DKK 85.
