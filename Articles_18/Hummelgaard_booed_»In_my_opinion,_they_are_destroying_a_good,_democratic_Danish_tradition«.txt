Date: 2025-05-02
Article Type: News
Author: Activists made it impossible to hear Minister for Justice Peter Hummelgaard (Social Democrats) in Fælledparken. He is ready to return next year.

Thomas Flensburg
Journalist
To enter Fælledparken for International Workers’ Day, one must pass through the Scandinavian Tivoli, which has set up its rides near Trianglen. Children’s laughter can be heard from the small planes and roller coasters.
The scene shifts dramatically when approaching the main stage, where Social Democratic Justice Minister Peter Hummelgaard is trying to make himself heard above the noise of shouts, chants, and horns.
