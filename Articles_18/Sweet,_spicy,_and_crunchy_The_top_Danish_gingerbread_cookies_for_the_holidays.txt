Title: Sweet,_spicy,_and_crunchy_The_top_Danish_gingerbread_cookies_for_the_holidays
Date: 2025-12-10
Article Type: Food
Author: Here you get the essence of the taste of Christmas in a crispy gingerbread cookie with cinnamon, cloves, and candied orange. They are also easy to bake, and you get many gingerbread cookies from this dough, so you can share them with friends and family.

Madredaktør
375 g butter

12.5 g cinnamon (approx. 2 tbsp.)
7.5 g potash
50 g chopped candied peel
50 g chopped candied orange peel
500 g wheat flour
