Author: The short election campaign and Carney’s unconventional background have shifted the political dynamics in Canada. Environmental advocates are concerned that Carney prioritizes the economy over climate.

Jacob Fuglsang, Ottawa, Canada
USA-korrespondent
Mark Carney has made history as the winner of the Canadian election.
Under Carney’s leadership, the Liberal Party has managed to turn the prospect of defeat into victory. Until January, the party was trailing by double-digit percentage points behind the Conservatives, and analysts anticipated a shift in the political system. But after Carney took over as party leader, the Liberals achieved a rapid comeback and won government power for the fourth consecutive time.
