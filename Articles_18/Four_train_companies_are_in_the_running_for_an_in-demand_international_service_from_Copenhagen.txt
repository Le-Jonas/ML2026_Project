Date: 2025-12-09
Article Type: News
Author: The last direct train between two Scandinavian capitals ran in 2001—but a new non-stop route is getting close. A fresh direct connection is now on the horizon.

Sune Højrup Bencke
Journalist
In Denmark, we don’t have an abundance of international trains. We have routes to Hamburg, to Sweden over the Øresund Bridge to Skåne and Småland, and Snälltåget’s direct train to Stockholm.
