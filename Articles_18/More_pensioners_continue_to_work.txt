Title: More_pensioners_continue_to_work
Date: 2025-11-05
Article Type: News
Author: More than 90,000 senior citizens are working. That’s 50 percent more than 15 years ago. During that time, several incentives have been introduced to extend their working lives.

Thomas Flensburg
Journalist
There is good economy in staying on the job as you age. If you have the health and desire to work past the retirement age, you can receive a full state pension alongside your salary and tax-free cash bonuses if you work for a year after the retirement age.
But the question is whether these financial incentives are what currently motivate more than 90,000 pensioners to keep working instead of leaving the workforce.
