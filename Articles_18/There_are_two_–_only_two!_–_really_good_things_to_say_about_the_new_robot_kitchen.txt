Title: There_are_two_–_only_two!_–_really_good_things_to_say_about_the_new_robot_kitchen
Date: 2025-08-11
Article Type: Food
Author: It’s fun to watch your food being tossed around in something that looks like a tumble dryer. But that’s probably the best thing we can say about Denmark’s first robot kitchen.

Birgitte Kjær
Journalist
’Robokok’ is written in large font on the façade. Robocop? Robocock? The bad jokes come thick and fast as we approach Vanløse’s new Asian fast-food joint, which is mainly geared towards takeaway but also has a few tables for dining in.
It is touted as Denmark’s first robot kitchen, proclaimed by Robokok itself. The advanced machine is said to ensure precision, speed, and consistency in every serving. But can AI taste the food? Give it love?
