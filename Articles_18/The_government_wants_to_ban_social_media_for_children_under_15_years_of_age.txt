Title: The_government_wants_to_ban_social_media_for_children_under_15_years_of_age
Date: 2025-10-08
Article Type: News
Author: A national age limit of 15 years should be introduced for children’s use of social media platforms, the government says. Social media platforms steal childhood, says the Prime Minister.

Jakob Sorgenfri Kjær
Journalist
The government wants to ban the use of social media platforms for children and young people under 15.
This announcement came from Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (Socialdemokratiet, S) during her speech at the opening of Folketinget on Tuesday.
