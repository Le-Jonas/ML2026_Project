Title: Ombudsman_slams_seven-month_delay_in_handling_involuntary_commitment_complaints
Date: 2025-11-27
Article Type: News
Author: Patients who complain about being involuntarily admitted have to wait months for their cases to be heard. That is unacceptable and not in accordance with the law, says the Parliamentary Ombudsman.

Hans Drachmann
Journalist
Patients who are involuntarily committed or detained have the right to appeal their detention under the Constitution, the Psychiatric Act, and the European Convention on Human Rights.
