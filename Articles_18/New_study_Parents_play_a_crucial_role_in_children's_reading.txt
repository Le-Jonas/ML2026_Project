Title: New_study_Parents_play_a_crucial_role_in_children's_reading
Date: 2025-08-20
Article Type: News
Author: A study among 5,780 public school students in Rudersdal Municipality maps out what matters in terms of developing or diminishing the joy of reading.

Birgitte Kjær
Journalist
Parents’ role as reading role models cannot be understated. They should be equipped to read to and with their children from the moment the children are born.
This is one of the very clear recommendations in a new systematic study of reading enthusiasm and love of reading among 5,780 elementary school students in Rudersdal Municipality from kindergarten through ninth grade and their parents.
