Date: 2025-06-04
Article Type: News
Author: New figures show that nearly one in three fields receive excessive fertilizer. Experts say Danish rules are so lenient that an essential EU directive is being breached.

Adam Hannestad
Journalist
The Danish government has neglected to comply with an EU directive designed to protect rivers, coasts, and groundwater from pollution for years – a directive that Germany was previously convicted of violating in a notable ruling.
This is the criticism from several experts and environmental organizations following a major report from Aarhus University, which
Politiken mentioned on Monday
