Title: Enhedslisten_will_not_endorse_Mette_Frederiksen_in_the_upcoming_election
Date: 2025-12-10
Article Type: News
Author: For the first time ever, Enhedslisten cannot endorse the Social Democrats’ leader as royal investigator after an election, says Pelle Dragsted.

Kristian Klarskov
Politisk reporter
Nilas Heinskou
Politisk redaktør
For the first time in the party’s history, Enhedslisten will not write the name of the Social Democrats’ party leader on the note they submit to the royal house the day after the election.
Instead, political spokesperson Pelle Dragsted (Enhedslisten, EL) announces a shift towards the left wing as a consequence of S-leader Mette Frederiksen’s move towards the political center.
