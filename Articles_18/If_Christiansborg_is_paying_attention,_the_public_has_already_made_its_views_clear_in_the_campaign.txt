Title: If_Christiansborg_is_paying_attention,_the_public_has_already_made_its_views_clear_in_the_campaign
Date: 2025-11-18
Article Type: News
Author: The campaign’s over; now the real power fight begins. Socialdemokratiet and Venstre face the toughest nationwide test, writes Elisabet Svane.

Elisabet Svane
Politisk analytiker
Whether it’s a national record, I don’t know – but wow, there are so many mayoral candidates in this local election. One municipality, Randers, which had the most party-switchers in the last term, has six mayoral candidates. Ringkøbing-Skjern also has six. While a municipality like Vordingborg reached four candidates before the first vote is cast.
The same number as Copenhagen.
