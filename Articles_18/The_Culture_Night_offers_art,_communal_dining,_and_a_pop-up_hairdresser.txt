Author: Here are a handful of recommendations for the week in Copenhagen from Ibyen.

Lucas Arturo Ramsøe Willkens
Journalist
Every week, the Ibyen editorial team scours Copenhagen’s events, exhibitions, concerts, and pop-ups and selects a range of suggestions for how you can spend your week in the city.
Experience!
You might have already explored Kastellet or seen a giraffe in the dark at the Zoo during the annual event-packed night,
The Culture Night
. However, there are plenty of other great experiences to discover around the city this Friday, October 10th. You can start the evening at the art hall
Gammel Strand
in inner city, which will be transformed into a secret bar and a hair salon. Artist Johan Bech Jespersen and »makeover icon« Gitte Gulhammer will take over the art hall’s banquet hall with their hair salon Starcut-Hair, where fashion, styling, and art are set to blend together, as it is said. Here, you can get a styling and chat about art and hairstyling.
