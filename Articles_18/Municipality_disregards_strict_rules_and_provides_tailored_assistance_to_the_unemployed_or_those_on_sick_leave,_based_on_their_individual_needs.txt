Title: Municipality_disregards_strict_rules_and_provides_tailored_assistance_to_the_unemployed_or_those_on_sick_leave,_based_on_their_individual_needs
Date: 2025-06-25
Article Type: News
Author: Louise Casson felt utterly despondent because the job center wouldn’t listen to her and was only concerned with rules. A new project in Nyborg threw out the rulebook and helped her find employment.

Lars Halskov
Journalist
Everything was just bleak. She had suffered from postpartum depression, often crying and lacking energy. Just as she had finally recovered, she lost her apprenticeship as a housepainter.
Now she was ready to work again. But she encountered a mental barrier at the job center.
