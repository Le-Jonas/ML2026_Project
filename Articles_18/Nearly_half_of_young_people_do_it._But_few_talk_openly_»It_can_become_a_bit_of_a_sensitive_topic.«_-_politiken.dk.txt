W
hen the spring sun starts to peek out, money begins to flow. It tempts with a draft beer at a sidewalk bar, the festival season is about to begin, and a weekend getaway from the city would be nice too.
It can be tough to afford everything you want. And even harder to talk about how you actually manage to afford it.
Especially if you get money from mom and dad.
Many people do. Nearly half of young people receive help from their parents, according to a survey conducted by the research institute Wilke
for Sydbank
in 2021. About one in seven students receives a regular monthly transfer. And the support continues well into adulthood, as more than one-third of those over 35 get financial help from their parents.
