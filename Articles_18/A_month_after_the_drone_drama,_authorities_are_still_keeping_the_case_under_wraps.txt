Title: A_month_after_the_drone_drama,_authorities_are_still_keeping_the_case_under_wraps
Date: 2025-10-27
Article Type: News
Author: A month after the first reported drone sightings over Copenhagen Airport, authorities still haven’t produced evidence that drones were involved—fueling conspiracy theories, an expert says.

Morten Skjoldager
Journalist
Jakob Hvide Beim
Forsvarsredaktør
From her corner office in the Prime Minister’s Office, Mette Frederiksen (S) delivered an extraordinary speech to the nation on September 25 regarding the dramatic events of recent days.
»Denmark has been subjected to hybrid attacks in recent days. Drones have been spotted in several locations. Near critical infrastructure. Both military and civilian,« she said in the speech.
