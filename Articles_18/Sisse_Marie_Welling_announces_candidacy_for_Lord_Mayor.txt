Title: Sisse_Marie_Welling_announces_candidacy_for_Lord_Mayor
Date: 2025-10-03
Article Type: News
Author: Socialdemokratiet do not have the birthright to power, says Sisse Marie Welling from SF in an interview with Information, where she declares her candidacy for the position of Lord Mayor. Politiken’s political editor, Nilas Heinskou, predicts an intense election campaign.

Karoline Bentzen
Journalist
Ritzau
SF’s mayoral candidate in the capital, Sisse Marie Welling, aims to become the Lord Mayor of Copenhagen, she stated in an interview with
the newspaper Information
.
»I am making myself available as the Lord Mayor. And I’m doing it to give the citizens of Copenhagen a real choice regarding who they want to lead their city«, Sisse Marie Welling told the newspaper.
