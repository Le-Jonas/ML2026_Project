Title: Denmark_does_not_receive_recognition_from_NATO_for_billion-dollar_investments_in_Greenland
Date: 2025-06-02
Article Type: News
Author: While Denmark is under historic pressure from the United States to bolster its defense in Greenland, but billion-dollar investments in the Arctic are not earning points with NATO.

Jakob Hvide Beim
Forsvarsredaktør
Karin Axelsson, Brussels
EU-korrespondent for Politiken i Bruxelles
Ahead of a NATO defense ministers’ meeting on Thursday, it is clear that Denmark will not receive the desired recognition for its significant military investments in the Arctic.
In the ongoing debate over the alliance’s capability targets, the Danish government has advocated for the inclusion of these substantial investments among the specific responsibilities that NATO expects Denmark to undertake.
