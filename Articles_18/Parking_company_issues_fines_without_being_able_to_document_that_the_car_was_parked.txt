Title: Parking_company_issues_fines_without_being_able_to_document_that_the_car_was_parked
Date: 2025-12-10
Article Type: News
Author: Avantpark is now sending out invoices with a somewhat different wording than what is typically seen from parking companies. The minister does not believe it is legal.

Michael Thykier
Redaktør for Forbrug og Tendens
Parking companies are facing strong opposition these days, and they have received verbal criticism from the Minister for Transport for their inventive methods of collecting money from motorists.
Now, the company Avantpark has adopted a new method that is facing harsh criticism.
