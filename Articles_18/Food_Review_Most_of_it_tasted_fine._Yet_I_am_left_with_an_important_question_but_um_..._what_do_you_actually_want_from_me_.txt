Lærke Kløvedal
Five years ago, I reviewed a restaurant located in the premises where Partagé, which opened in November last year, is located today.
The two floors of the old town house at Store Regnegade 26 are one of those addresses that always seem to struggle to find their footing. Diagonally opposite Montmartre and with Gothersgade’s bustling nightlife as a neighbor. Restaurant, wine, and cocktail bar is what Partagé calls itself. Bar in the basement and restaurant on the first floor.
