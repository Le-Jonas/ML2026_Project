Date: 2025-11-28
Article Type: Food
Author: You’ll likely enjoy the sandwich more on your second or third visit to Valby’s new bakery and cheese shop—it takes a little practice to land on the ideal flavor combination.

Birgitte Kjær
Journalist
For more than 40 years, Værnedamsvej has been home to a quintessential Copenhagen classic.
The kind that consistently makes it onto the go-to list for recommending a solid lunch sandwich. Last summer, it opened a large branch in Østerbro, and now – very recently – another one at a highly surprising address in Valby.
