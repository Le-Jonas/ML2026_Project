Title: It_tastes_bland_and_forgettable._What_happened_to_the_strawberry_
Date: 2025-06-30
Article Type: Guides
Author: The Danish strawberry season is at its peak, but that doesn’t guarantee sun-ripened, sweet berries.

Mette Mølbak
Madredaktør
One of the most foolish aspects of our modern food culture is that you can get strawberries year-round. It doesn’t matter if it’s June or January. The red berries are stacked up in everything from discount stores to supermarkets. Tasteless, half-hard, and a representation of what’s wrong with our demand-and-supply society.
A few years ago,
DR investigated
where the foreign strawberries come from and could reveal that they are primarily grown in Spain on large intensive farms that require a high water consumption. This means nature reserves the size of Lolland are threatened with drying up because the strawberries absorb so much water.
