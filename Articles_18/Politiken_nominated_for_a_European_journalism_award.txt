Title: Politiken_nominated_for_a_European_journalism_award
Date: 2025-10-07
Article Type: News
Author: Politiken’s series Dirty Payments was produced with 20 international media partners—and it’s now up for an investigative journalism award.

Mathilde Hebsgaard Lund
Journalist
Politiken has been nominated for the EU journalist award Daphne Caruana for the investigative article series
Dirty Payments.
The series consists of a number of in-depth articles about the dark side of online payments, created in collaboration with 20 international media outlets coordinated by the European Investigative Collaborations (EIC).
