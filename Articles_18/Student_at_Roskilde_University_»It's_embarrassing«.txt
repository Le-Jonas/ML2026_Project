Title: Student_at_Roskilde_University_»It's_embarrassing«
Date: 2025-09-17
Article Type: News
Author: »Embarrassing«, says the young man in the cafeteria. Meanwhile, the female student at the coffee bar is upset - once again - to have to defend RUC to her father. Especially because she believes she is attending a good program.

Per Munch
Journalist
One enters Roskilde University (RUC) through a brass-clad giant square root. And there is chaos at RUC, not just because of the workers busy updating the over 50-year-old university to make it more modern.
»It’s embarrassing to get caught with your hand in the cookie jar. It gives us a bad image«, says Magnus Mejer, one of the students sitting in the cafeteria on Tuesday discussing the resignation of RUC’s chairman Carsten Toft Boesen following criticism.
