One hopes that art can heal. That an encounter with art can be recorded as an improvement in human health in the form of lower blood pressure and peace of mind.
Artists, too, should become better and more harmonious people by creating art. It is said that expressing oneself brings better balance and tranquility to the psyche and psychoses. Like taking the lid off a pot of popcorn. Opening the floodgates.
