Title: Declined_exchange_to_Boston_Emma_Toft_Kromann_from_CBS_said_no_to_Trump
Date: 2025-05-12
Article Type: Edition
Author: When Emma Toft Kromann received the email in February informing her that she had secured a spot at one of the coveted American universities for the fall, she didn’t feel happy. Instead, she felt a knot in her stomach.

Per Munch
Journalist
The unrest on American campuses and Donald Trump’s actions – cutting financial aid, restricting free speech, and revoking student visas – have led several Danish students to question whether spending a semester in the so-called land of opportunity is worth it.
One such student is Emma Toft Kromann, who studies strategy, economy, and leadership at CBS and plans to go on exchange during her master’s program.
