Just north of Møns Klint lies the wonderful oasis Liselund, which features idyllic nature and several well-preserved buildings – including one of the world’s smallest castles.
Jesper Møller
The undisputed main tourist attraction on Møn is Møns Klint, known throughout Denmark. What few people know, however, is that close to the cliff lies a completely different sight, which with its green and lush surroundings provides a perfect contrast to Møns Klint’s more rugged and dramatic nature.
We are talking about Liselund, which is home to perhaps Denmark’s smallest castle—and certainly one of the most unknown ones.
