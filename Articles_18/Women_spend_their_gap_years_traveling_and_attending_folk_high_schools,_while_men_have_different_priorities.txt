Date: 2025-08-04
Article Type: News
Author: While it is well-documented what young women do during their gap years, young men are more absent from the statistics.

Tina Krongaard
Rejseredaktør
Cecilie Lund Kristiansen

Over the past few weeks, nearly 50,000 young graduates have danced through the summer, sipped champagne, and received countless congratulations.
If the new graduates follow the pattern of previous years, only a tenth of them will proceed directly to higher education. The rest will take one or more gap years.
