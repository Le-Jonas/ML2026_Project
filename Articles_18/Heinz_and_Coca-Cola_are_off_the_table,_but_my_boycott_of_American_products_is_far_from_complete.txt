Date: 2025-05-13
Article Type: Edition
Author: I’m determined to boycott American products, but it’s tough when I also have to shield myself and my loved ones from Donald Trump’s relentless tirades against the world.

Sune Højrup Bencke
Journalist
Sanctions package 1 in my personal boycott of American goods first targeted my favorite ketchup from Heinz. Bam! That was a lesson for Donald Trump and his band of uruk-hai-like cronies, I thought, as I swapped out the American cultural staple—which I must admit, tastes incredibly good—for Netto’s organic ketchup.
