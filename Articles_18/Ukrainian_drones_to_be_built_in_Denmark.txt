Title: Ukrainian_drones_to_be_built_in_Denmark
Date: 2025-06-25
Article Type: News
Author: NATO is under pressure to develop defense capabilities much faster. Denmark is therefore inviting Ukraine to develop military technology within its borders. Drones are an obvious choice, says the Confederation of Danish Industry (DI).

Jakob Hvide Beim, Haag
Forsvarsredaktør
Karin Axelsson, Haag
EU-korrespondent for Politiken i Bruxelles
In the near future, Odense may play a central role in Ukraine’s war against Russia.
According to the government and the Danish defense industry, the collaboration with Ukraine should be intensified to the point where it results in Danish-Ukrainian factories on Danish soil.
