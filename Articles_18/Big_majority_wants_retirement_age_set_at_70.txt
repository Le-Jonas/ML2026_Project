Title: Big_majority_wants_retirement_age_set_at_70
Date: 2025-10-20
Article Type: News
Author: Two in three Danes want the pension age uncoupled from life expectancy—economists sound the alarm, warning it could blow a hole in the budget.

Thomas Flensburg
Journalist
Lock the retirement age at 70.
This is the crystal-clear message from Danes in a new survey conducted by the organization Forsikring & Pension (F&P). More than two out of three—68 percent—want the state pension age to be capped at 70 years.
