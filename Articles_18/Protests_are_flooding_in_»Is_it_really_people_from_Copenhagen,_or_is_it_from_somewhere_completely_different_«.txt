Two weeks before the consultation ends, over 1,000 residents have weighed in on Palads’s future—a highly unusual number, admit politicians at Copenhagen City Hall.
Emil Bergløv
Reporter
There is massive public interest in the Palads cinema at Vesterport Station in Copenhagen.
This is evident from the over 1,000 responses received by the Copenhagen Municipality during the ongoing public consultation regarding Nordisk Film’s desire to renovate the cinema.
