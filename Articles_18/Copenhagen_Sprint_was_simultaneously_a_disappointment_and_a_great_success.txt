Date: 2025-06-23
Article Type: News
Author: Excitement until the very end, blazing sunshine, and five different types of natural wine made the inaugural men’s Copenhagen Sprint come together beautifully.

Villads Znaty

The cycling race in the center of the capital on Sunday did not disappoint.
Although the first edition of the brand-new World Tour cycling race, Copenhagen Sprint, might have seemed a bit drawn-out for spectators over the first approximately 200 kilometers, everything changed when the 163 riders hit Copenhagen for five laps of 11 kilometers each.
