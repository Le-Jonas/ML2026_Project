Date: 2025-05-19
Article Type: News
Author: Socialdemokratiet’s mayoral candidate pledges to secure more parking spaces for Copenhagen residents, marking a shift from the party’s previous stance.

Kristian Klarskov
Politisk reporter
Josephine Mosbæk Quinones
Journalist
Oversættelsesdisclaimer
When then-mayoral candidate Sophie Hæstorp Andersen (Socialdemokratiet) made it part of the party’s policy four years ago to eliminate a third of Copenhagen’s parking spaces, it was too much for then-Minister of Education Pernille Rosenkrantz-Theil (Socialdemokratiet), who seriously considered publicly opposing her own party during the campaign.
