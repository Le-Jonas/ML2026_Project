Author: Pierre-Emile Højbjerg has had a stellar debut season in French football and is optimistic about the future.

How would you feel if one day a letter showed up in your mailbox, sent by the French president, inviting you to a grand gala dinner at the palace?
That’s exactly what happened to Danish footballer Pierre-Emile Højbjerg earlier this year when the Danish royal couple visited the French capital in April.
