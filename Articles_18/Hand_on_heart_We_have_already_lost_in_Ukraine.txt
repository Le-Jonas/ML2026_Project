Title: Hand_on_heart_We_have_already_lost_in_Ukraine
Date: 2025-04-25
Article Type: Edition
Author: It is difficult to admit: But Europe has already lost in Ukraine. The only thing left to measure is the extent of the defeat, both on the ground and in European values.

Christian Jensen
Ansvarshavende chefredaktør
We said it again and again: There is one and only one who decides where the border in Ukraine should be: Ukraine itself.
This was the refrain from the old world, where European heads of state and government held the banner high because the USA supported us all. It was the assurance to Ukraine. It was the message to future generations that Europe would never again accept military intervention on European soil as a profitable venture.
