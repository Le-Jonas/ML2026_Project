Article Type: Culture
Author: Norway has it all. Incredible nature and culture, the finest cities, and fascinating history. Plus, it’s easy to visit from Denmark. We’ve found a handful of experiences for your (next) vacation.

Sune Højrup Bencke
Journalist
Here is a vacation experience that might divide opinions. Between those with a fear of heights and those without. Between those who love sleeping in a tent and those who don’t. Regardless, you are in for a night you will never forget.
On a vertical cliff face in Åfjord, Norway, you can sleep 60 meters above the ground
. In a tent. On a cliffside. The tent hangs on the Melanakken cliffside north of Trondheim, overlooking Åfjord, which stretches all the way to the Norwegian Sea.
Oslo is brimming with major cultural experiences. And many of them are relatively new.
