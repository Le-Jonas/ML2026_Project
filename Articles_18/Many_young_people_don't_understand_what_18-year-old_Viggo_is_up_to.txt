Title: Many_young_people_don't_understand_what_18-year-old_Viggo_is_up_to
Date: 2025-09-04
Article Type: News
Author: Half of Danes aged 21 to 29 didn’t vote in the last municipal election. Many young people feel ignored and lack democratic confidence in a system that’s increasingly dominated by older generations.

Jakob Sorgenfri Kjær
Journalist
Viggo Søbjerg is quite a rarity. Not because he’s in his second year of high school, nor because he turned 18 in April and can now vote for the first time. No, Viggo Søbjerg is unique because, as a young person, he is interested in politics and even involved in local politics.
