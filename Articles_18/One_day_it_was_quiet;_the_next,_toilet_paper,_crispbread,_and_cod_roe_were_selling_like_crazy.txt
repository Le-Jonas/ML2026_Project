Author: Drones over Danish airports have triggered a rush on dry yeast and canned tuna. Nemlig.com is reporting delays on all orders.

It started the morning after drones were spotted over Copenhagen Airport.
On Tuesday, nearly two weeks ago, employees at Nemlig.com first noticed that customers were suddenly very interested in bottled water, toilet paper, and canned tuna.
