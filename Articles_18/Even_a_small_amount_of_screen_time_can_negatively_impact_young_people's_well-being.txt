Title: Even_a_small_amount_of_screen_time_can_negatively_impact_young_people's_well-being
Date: 2025-08-06
Article Type: News
Author: Young people’s well-being is linked to their screen time, according to a new analysis. At a certain point, their well-being begins to decline.

Pernille Mainz
Journalist
Young people with high screen time experience lower well-being
A new analysis from the Danish Evaluation Institute (EVA), based on data from nearly 5,000 high school and vocational school students, reveals this trend.
