Title: More_than_130,000_Danes_are_now_on_ADHD_medication_-_surprisingly_many_are_adults
Date: 2025-09-15
Article Type: News
Author: A dramatic increase in the number of Danish adults on ADHD medication in just a few years is driving a trend that has experts concerned about potential overdiagnosis and overmedication.

Anders Legarth Schmidt
Journalist
Lars Igum Rasmussen
Sundhedsredaktør
Look at the graphics in this article and follow the curve from left to right. At first, it rises steadily year after year. But around 2018, something starts to happen. The curve bends upward to become almost vertical in 2023 and 2024.
The graph transforms into a skateboard ramp, illustrating so-called exponential growth. Here, the increase year by year becomes more extreme.
