Every week, the Ibyen editorial team scours Copenhagen’s events, exhibitions, concerts, and pop-ups and selects a range of suggestions for how you can spend your week in the city.
Experience!
Are you also dreaming of publishing your own ’zine’ (homemade magazine) consisting of a proper Copenhagen cocktail of analog photos from your summer vacation in Montenegro and poems about heartache? Then there’s good reason to attend the festival this week, where Cph Zine Fest 2025 takes place from October 16 to 19 around the city. A four-day celebration of DIY and zine culture featuring exhibitions, films, workshops, concerts, and parties.
