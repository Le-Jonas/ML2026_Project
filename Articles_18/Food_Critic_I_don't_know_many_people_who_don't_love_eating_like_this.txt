Author: It’s far from groundbreaking, but the Middle Eastern dishes at the new restaurant on Værnedamsvej are a delight. And they look beautiful too.

Lærke Kløvedal
It was my New York based friend who taught me the word »elevated« in a gastronomic context.
First, we ate »elevated Mexican« in Brooklyn, and a couple of days later »elevated Chinese« in Manhattan. That is, dishes from kitchens we typically know in one way but now served with a twist, a quality lift or just in new, exquisite surroundings.
