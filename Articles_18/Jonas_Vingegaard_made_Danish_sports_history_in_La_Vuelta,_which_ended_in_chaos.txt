Title: Jonas_Vingegaard_made_Danish_sports_history_in_La_Vuelta,_which_ended_in_chaos
Date: 2025-09-15
Article Type: News
Author: Jonas Vingegaard became the first Danish winner of the Vuelta a España, which saw several stages marked by pro-Palestinian demonstrations.

Allan Nielsen
Journalist
Neither illness nor disruptive protests could stop Jonas Vingegaard from making sports history by becoming the first Danish winner of the Vuelta a España.
After Saturday’s crushing stage triumph, Sunday offered a chaotic ending, where pro-Palestinian demonstrations caused the stage to be interrupted and the podium ceremony to be entirely canceled.
