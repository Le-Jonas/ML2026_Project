Title: Golden_bergamot_marmalade
Date: 2025-12-23
Article Type: Food
Author: Wonderful and made from whole bergamots, which are sliced thinly. It’s a bit of a hassle to slice and remove the seeds, but the result is worth it. A warmly golden or greenish marmalade, depending on the type of bergamot you get your hands on, with a strongly perfumed taste of bitter bergamot.

Wonderful and made from whole bergamots, which are sliced thinly. It’s a bit of a hassle to slice and remove the seeds, but the result is worth it. A warmly golden or greenish marmalade, depending on the type of bergamot you get your hands on, with a strongly perfumed taste of bitter bergamot.
Marie Holm
Madskribent
Makes 2 jars.
600 g organic/unsprayed bergamots
750 ml fresh water
450 g sugar
A pinch of salt
