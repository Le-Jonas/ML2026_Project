Author: It’s one of the biggest food trends of the year, but very few know what real Dubai chocolate tastes like. Everyone else is capitalizing on the trend with shameless imitations.

Mette Mølbak
Madredaktør
If Dubai chocolate were a handbag, it would be a Hermès handbag. A bag you have to sign up for a waiting list, produced in limited quantities, which only a few can buy. Yet, you see Hermès bags everywhere. They are copied and imitated extensively.
