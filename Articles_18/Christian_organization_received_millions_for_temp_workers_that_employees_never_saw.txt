Title: Christian_organization_received_millions_for_temp_workers_that_employees_never_saw
Date: 2025-04-30
Article Type: News
Author: KFUM allegedly spent millions on holiday temp workers, but many employees never saw one. Now, the Christian organization is accused of fraud. KFUM denies the allegations, but authorities are launching an investigation.

Jakob Hvide Beim
Forsvarsredaktør
Carl Emil Arnfred
Journalist
More than half a million kroner a year.
That’s how much the Christian organization KFUM’s Soldatermission (soldier mission) reported spending on temp workers to cover for their regular veteran employees during holidays at their so-called soldier retreats.
