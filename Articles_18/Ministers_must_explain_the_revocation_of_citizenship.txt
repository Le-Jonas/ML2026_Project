Title: Ministers_must_explain_the_revocation_of_citizenship
Date: 2025-08-15
Article Type: News
Author: There has been a shocking disregard for the legal rights of the woman from Funen, Nadine, when the authorities revoked her Danish citizenship through re-registration, according to Enhedslisten (Ø).

Hans Drachmann
Journalist
The political interference in the process that has so far resulted in the 29-year-old woman Nadine from Funen losing her Danish citizenship must be clarified.
That is the view of Enhedslisten’s citizenship spokesperson, Peder Hvelplund, who, based on Politiken’s articles, has summoned two ministers for questioning and posed several clarifying questions about the actual course of events in the case.
