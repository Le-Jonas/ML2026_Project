Article Type: News
Author: Organized criminals are behind widespread ticket scams on Facebook, which are ensnaring more and more Danes. For Mathilde Lodahl, it cost her a thousand kroner and the chance to see her idol perform.

Lars Eriksen
Journalist
Fiona Fälling
Journalist
When 23-year-old Mathilde Lodahl missed an alarm on her phone last May, panic set in. That alarm was supposed to secure her a spot in the queue for tickets to the American pop star Billie Eilish’s concert in Copenhagen.
»I just thought: No, no, no. This can’t be happening«, recalls Mathilde Lodahl.
