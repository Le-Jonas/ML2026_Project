Bornholm boasts Hammershus (medieval fortress), Helligdomsklipperne (the Sanctuary Cliffs), and Rokkestenen (The Rocking Stones). But now the rocky island can add what archaeologists call »a peculiar phenomenon with pre-Christian roots« to its list of attractions:
Denmark’s oldest slides.
