Roskilde Festival’s lineup faces criticism, and for the first time, tickets haven’t sold out. But the festival insists the two aren’t connected.
Birgitte Kjær
Journalist
The sun is shining down on Roskilde Festival, the grass is still untouched and green in front of the Orange Stage, and out on the camping grounds, people are drinking from bongs, flirting, and showing off muscles in bare chests. Everything is as usual. And yet.
For the first time since 2013, Roskilde Festival is not sold out.
