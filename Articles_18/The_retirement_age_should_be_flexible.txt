Date: 2025-10-30
Article Type: News
Author: The Minister for Seniors wants to shift the focus from when we can retire to what can inspire us to keep working as we age.

Thomas Flensburg
Journalist
»I am probably one of those who will keep working for a long time«.
The message comes from Minister for Seniors Mette Kierkgaard (M), who welcomes us at Christiansborg for a conversation about how to make society, and especially working life, more age-friendly amid a heated debate on retirement age.
