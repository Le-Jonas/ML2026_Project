Title: Jutland_airport_receives_17_million_in_annual_subsidies,_yet_only_22_daily_passengers
Date: 2025-05-13
Article Type: Edition
Author: In the first three months of this year, 1,305 passengers flew to or from Midtjyllands Airport, which gets DKK 17 million in annual public funding. This is slightly down from last year’s numbers.

Tina Krongaard
Rejseredaktør
When the first-quarter passenger numbers were revealed in April 2024, Ulrik Wilbek (Venstre), chairman of Midtjyllands Airport and Mayor of Viborg Municipality, was quite clear:
»If we get to next year with 17 million in support and the passenger numbers are still this low, we won’t keep flying. We’ll have to shut down the airport.«
