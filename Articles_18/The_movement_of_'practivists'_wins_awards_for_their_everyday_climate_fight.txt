Date: 2025-10-28
Article Type: News
Author: You can make a difference for the climate by repairing your jeans and hosting planting days with your neighbors. Green Neighborhood Communities prove that every (climate) action counts, and now they have won awards for their work.

Rosa Marie Bjerre
Journalist
Bent Mariager is an incredibly optimistic climate advocate. Especially right now.
He is the founder of the movement Green Neighborhood Communities, which is this year’s recipient of the Royal Couple’s Community Award and the Nordic Council’s Environment Prize.
