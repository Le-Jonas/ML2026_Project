Title: New_figures_Tripling_in_attempts_to_smuggle_dangerous_puff_bars_into_Denmark
Date: 2025-06-20
Article Type: News
Author: Attempts to smuggle puff bars have almost tripled in one year. According to a researcher on puff bars, a market persists due to the perception of the small illegal e-cigarettes.

Anders Brøndsholm
Journalist
There has been a sharp increase in the number of cases where individuals attempt to smuggle harmful puff bars into Denmark.
Smuggling attempts intercepted by authorities have nearly tripled in the first three months of the year compared to the same period last year. This is according to new figures from the Customs Agency.
