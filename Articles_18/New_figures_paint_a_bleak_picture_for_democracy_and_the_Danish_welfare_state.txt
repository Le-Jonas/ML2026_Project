Title: New_figures_paint_a_bleak_picture_for_democracy_and_the_Danish_welfare_state
Date: 2025-08-27
Article Type: News
Author: A new book exposes the downfall of democracy in Denmark and the West. Surveys reveal widespread disillusionment and doubt that political decisions truly benefit citizens and society.

Jakob Sorgenfri Kjær
Journalist
Since World War II, there has generally been a logic in the West that the future looks brighter: that today’s children will have better opportunities to thrive than their parents.
But that’s no longer the case. Optimism has been replaced by democratic pessimism.
