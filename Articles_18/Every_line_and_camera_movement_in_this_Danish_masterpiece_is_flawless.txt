Author: There isn’t a single unnecessary shot or a single dispensable line in Frelle Petersen’s masterpiece ’Home Sweet Home’.

Nanna Frank Rasmussen
Film- og tv-anmelder
One can hardly believe their own eyes.
For many different reasons, you find yourself rubbing your eyes vigorously while watching ’Home Sweet Home’ (’Hjem kære hjem’), René Frelle Petersen’s third and final film in his trilogy set in southern Jutland.
