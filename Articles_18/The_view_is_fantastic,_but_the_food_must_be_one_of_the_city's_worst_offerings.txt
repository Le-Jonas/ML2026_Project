Title: The_view_is_fantastic,_but_the_food_must_be_one_of_the_city's_worst_offerings
Date: 2025-04-08
Article Type: Edition
Author: After several years of delay, Cinemateket has finally gotten its film roof, and there’s every reason to applaud. But it’s not because of the food served by the roof’s café.

David Dyrholm
Kulturjournalist og kritiker
The new rooftop at Cinemateket is a crowd magnet. And for good reason!
We have had to wait for a long time, but now we can finally step out onto the 1,700 square meter wooden deck, which includes an outdoor cinema, film exhibition, play area, and café-slash-beach bar, to play tourist in our own city.
