Title: They’re_two_of_the_world’s_finest_actors,_but_it_feels_like_a_student_film
Date: 2025-06-13
Article Type: Culture
Author: The director has snagged two of the world’s finest actors, and they’re a delight to watch. But ’Poison’ disappoints, lacking any real cinematic ambition.

Nanna Frank Rasmussen
Film- og tv-anmelder
It’s quite a coup that the Luxembourgish actress and director Désirée Nosbusch has managed to secure two of the world’s best actors for her first fiction feature film, ’Poison.’
The film is a chamber piece based on the play of the same name by Dutch writer Lot Vekemans, who also penned the screenplay.
