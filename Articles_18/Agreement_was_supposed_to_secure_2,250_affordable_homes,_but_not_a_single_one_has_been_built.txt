Title: Agreement_was_supposed_to_secure_2,250_affordable_homes,_but_not_a_single_one_has_been_built
Date: 2025-09-22
Article Type: News
Author: Politicians promised 2,250 affordable homes for the poorest, but after three years, not a single one has been built. Minister for Social Affairs and Housing Sophie Hæstorp Andersen (Socialdemokratiet, S) knew about the problem but did nothing about it.

Anders Brøndsholm
Journalist
Politicians at Christiansborg have become 884 million kroner richer without doing anything.
It sounds positive, but the politicians probably don’t see it that way, because the reason is that they failed with a grand plan to help the homeless and citizens on cash benefits who struggle to find affordable housing.
