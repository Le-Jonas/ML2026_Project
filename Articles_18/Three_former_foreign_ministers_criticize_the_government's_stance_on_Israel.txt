Author: Former foreign ministers criticize Mette Frederiksen’s stance on Gaza. They all agree that Denmark should at least accept sick children from the war zone.

Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (Socialdemokratiet, S) and the rest of Socialdemokratiet’s leadership are too slow in applying pressure on Israel and in doing more to help the Palestinian population in Gaza.
So says former party chairman, foreign minister, and president of the UN General Assembly, Mogens Lykketoft.
