Title: The_military_adds_a_new_item_to_the_list_of_valid_excuses_for_exemption_from_deployment
Date: 2025-08-04
Article Type: News
Author: A new directive from the top of the military strikes at the heart of conflicting masculinity ideals that soldiers may experience.

Jakob Hvide Beim
Forsvarsredaktør
Life as a soldier does not just mean a moral obligation to step up when the Danish military goes on a mission, but also, in the worst case, fines and imprisonment if one refuses.
Now the defense has added another point to the official list of acceptable excuses.
