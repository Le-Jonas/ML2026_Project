Title: To_the_surprise_of_economists,_many_of_us_are_still_terrified_of_inflation
Date: 2025-05-27
Article Type: News
Author: Danes are increasingly pessimistic about the economy, and new data reveals that much of this pessimism stems from fear that inflation will rise again soon. But there’s a contradiction in this, an economist points out.

Per Thiemann
Redaktør for økonomi og samfund
Oversættelsesdisclaimer
A strange phenomenon has recently
puzzled economists
. Danes are swimming in jobs and pay raises, and the state treasury is overflowing with money – yet many Danes believe that things are downright going to rack and ruin.
