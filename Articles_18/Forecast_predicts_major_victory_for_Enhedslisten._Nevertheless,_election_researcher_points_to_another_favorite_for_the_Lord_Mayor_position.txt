Title: Forecast_predicts_major_victory_for_Enhedslisten._Nevertheless,_election_researcher_points_to_another_favorite_for_the_Lord_Mayor_position
Date: 2025-06-16
Article Type: News
Author: A professor’s election forecast predicts further gains for Enhedslisten in Copenhagen, but SF might still have the upper hand. The lead candidate from SF is urged to step up.

Johan Blem Larsen
Politisk reporter
The electoral campaign in Copenhagen has been dominated by two former party mates – Pernille Rosenkrantz-Theil from Socialdemokratiet (S) and Line Barfod from Enhedslisten (Ø) – in the battle over who will become the Lord Mayor after the municipal elections in November.
However, it is a third candidate who is currently the favorite to don the mayoral chain, a symbol that each mayor of the 98 Danish municipalities recieves after being elected. This is the assessment of election researcher and professor of political science at the University of Copenhagen, Kasper Møller Hansen.
