Title: He_promised_that_it_would_be_the_best_night_of_our_lives._It_wasn't
Date: 2025-07-02
Article Type: Culture
Author: There was a time when Lionel Richie was one of the biggest stars in the world. Many have forgotten that, but on Tuesday night, he gave Royal Arena a few reminders of why he was.

Simon Lund
Musikredaktør
You can’t accuse Lionel Richie of underselling himself when he took the stage at Royal Arena on Tuesday night.
The hitmaker from the 1980s played a film showcasing all his Grammy triumphs. Then, he literally patted himself on the back for pronouncing »Copenhagen« perfectly (it was indeed perfect).
