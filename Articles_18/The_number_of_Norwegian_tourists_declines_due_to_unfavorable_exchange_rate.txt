Title: The_number_of_Norwegian_tourists_declines_due_to_unfavorable_exchange_rate
Date: 2025-09-24
Article Type: News
Author: In the past decade, there has been a significant increase in tourism in Denmark, but from some countries, the number has decreased markedly.

Pia Buhl Andersen
Journalist
While tourists are generally flocking to Denmark in large numbers, it’s a different story when looking specifically at our Nordic neighbors.
Over the past ten years, there has been a significant decline in the number of tourists from Norway and Sweden when examining who stays overnight in Denmark.
