Author: Carlos Santana was the ultimate old hippie at Royal Arena, cooler than most. But the music lacked soul.

Alexander Vesterlund
Kritiker ved Politiken
Carlos Santana struggled with many things on stage Monday night. But being cool and saying cool things was not one of them.
The 78-year-old Mexican strolled in with four maracas, sat himself down in an ergonomic chair and started chewing gum, before launching into a guitar solo that didn’t end until roughly an hour and a half later.
