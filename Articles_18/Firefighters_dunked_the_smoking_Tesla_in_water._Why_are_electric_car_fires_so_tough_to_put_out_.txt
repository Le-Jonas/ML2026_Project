Title: Firefighters_dunked_the_smoking_Tesla_in_water._Why_are_electric_car_fires_so_tough_to_put_out_
Date: 2025-09-16
Article Type: News
Author: Electric cars burn less often than gas cars, but when they do, the fires are tough to extinguish. Danish firefighters used a clever solution Monday in Nørrebro.

Frauke Giebner
Journalist
Monday afternoon, a several-meter-high column of white smoke suddenly emerged from the trunk of a Tesla parked near Nørrebro Station in Copenhagen.
The car’s battery started overheating, and the Copenhagen Fire Department cordoned off the area until a truck with a specially built water-filled container arrived.
