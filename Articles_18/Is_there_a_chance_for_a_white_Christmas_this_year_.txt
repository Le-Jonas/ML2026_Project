Title: Is_there_a_chance_for_a_white_Christmas_this_year_
Date: 2025-12-12
Article Type: News
Author: DMI assesses that December could be among the warmest on record. Despite climate change, it is still possible that we could have a white Christmas, says researcher.

Laurits Lauesen Otto
Journalist
Previously, December could start with a groggy morning search for the snow shovel in the basement. But this year, it might be more appropriate to dig out your fall jacket from the closet.
December is shaping up to be one of the warmest since records began in 1874.
