Title: Defense_Minister_identifies_the_biggest_problem_for_Danish_defense
Date: 2025-08-25
Article Type: News
Author: There are many stories about worn-out and inadequate equipment in the defense. But the biggest problem is something else, says the defense minister. However, he will not quantify the extent.

Jakob Hvide Beim
Forsvarsredaktør
Almost instantly, the words of Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (Socialdemokratiet, S) became iconic.
Earlier this year, she allocated an additional 50 billion kroner to defense through the so-called ’acceleration fund’, together with Defense Minister Troels Lund Poulsen (Venstre, S). She also issued an order to Chief of Defense Michael Hyldgaard.
