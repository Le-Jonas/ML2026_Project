Title: I_want_to_tell_everyone_about_it,_but_at_the_same_time,_I’d_rather_keep_it_to_myself
Date: 2025-11-14
Article Type: Guides
Author: There’s something special about the new intimate Nørrebro hangout. So maybe the reviewer already regrets a little bit that she is telling you about it.

Nasra Jama
Caféanmelder
It’s a café, but also a small floral atelier and vintage shop where everything except the espresso machine is for sale. Furniture, porcelain, flowers, glass. The sofa in the corner? It had only been there for two weeks.
Things move around here. Just 10 minutes after I sat down, a couple got up, pointed at the armchair they’d been sitting in, bought it, and carried it out.
