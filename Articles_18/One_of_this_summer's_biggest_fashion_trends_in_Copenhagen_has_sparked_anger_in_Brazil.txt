Cultural erasure or democratic footwear? Copenhagen’s embrace of Havaianas flip-flops has infuriated Brazilians.
Lucas Arturo Ramsøe Willkens
Journalist
When Copenhageners can transform anything from old car repair shops to cheese-filled buns and bucket hats into stylish items, the only limit to what can be ’Copenhagen-ized’ is their imagination.
Now, even stepping into a plastic sole with two straps has been branded as ’Copenhagen style.’
