Date: 2025-07-15
Article Type: News
Author: They’re selfish, spoiled, and can’t share. Are these assumptions about only children true? In Politiken’s series on siblings, we explore the lives of those who grow up without them.

Kirsten Nilsson
Journalist
Kirsten Nilsson, little sister to an older sister
When Lene Bjørn was a child, it was common to have many siblings.
She actually remembers only meeting one other only child during her upbringing in a provincial town in central Jutland in the 1950s.
