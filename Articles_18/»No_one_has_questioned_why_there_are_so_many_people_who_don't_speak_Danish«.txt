One day, teachers noticed that some students had started playing cricket at one of the soccer goals in Søndervang School’s playground.
»We hadn’t seen that before, but these are children from Bangladesh, Nepal, and Sri Lanka. So we had to look into what they needed for cricket,« says Bo Plum Jacobsen with a smile.
