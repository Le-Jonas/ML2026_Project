Title: The_youth_sex‐crisis_narrative_has_been_put_to_bed
Date: 2025-11-06
Article Type: News
Author: A new report from Sex & Samfund debunks the tale of a youth sex crisis: most young people are satisfied, sexually active, and clear on consent.

Fiona Fälling
Journalist
In recent years,
international studies
have indicated that young people in Western countries are having less sex than before—a trend referred to in the
United States
as a ’
sex recession
.’
Experts in Denmark
have also spoken about a generation that prefers swiping to hooking up.
However, a new report from Sex & Samfund, ’Sex Life – Youth Report 2025,’ paints a different picture. The numbers show that young Danes are still having sex, and most are satisfied with it. They also feel secure in the culture of consent and have a clear understanding of boundaries.
