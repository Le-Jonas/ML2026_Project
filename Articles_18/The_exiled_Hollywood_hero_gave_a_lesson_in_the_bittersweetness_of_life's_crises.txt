Title: The_exiled_Hollywood_hero_gave_a_lesson_in_the_bittersweetness_of_life's_crises
Date: 2025-08-08
Article Type: Edition
Author: Will Smith gave a memorable concert on Danish soil for the first time. He addressed the elephant in the room but unfortunately also brought a translator along.

Lucia Odoom
Kulturjournalist
The last time I reviewed a life crisis was on this very same stage, where tonight I saw my childhood hero Will Smith. Britney Spears stood on the Beech Stage in 2018. Her body was there, but her soul hovered over Smukfest as if it had left her own shell.
It is the only 1-heart review I have written. It was a completely different story with Will Smith on Thursday night when he stepped onto the stage with a trembling energy that was hard to put into words.
