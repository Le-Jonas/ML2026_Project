Thursday night, I experienced something I hope to understand in a few days: Chappell Roan’s voice.
Sure. She has sung, screamed, and whispered in my ears for a few years now, but always from a distance, always on recorded tracks, always with a whole world, or at least a pretty large ocean, between us.
