Title: More_direct_train_routes_to_and_from_Copenhagen_may_be_on_the_way
Date: 2025-09-10
Article Type: News
Author: The Municipality of Copenhagen and Wonderful Copenhagen want more tourists to choose the train over the plane.

Sune Højrup Bencke
Journalist
It should be easier to travel by train to and from Copenhagen from several of Europe’s major cities. And a new partnership for international train tourism aims to support this initiative.
The Municipality of Copenhagen has allocated eight million kroner over a four-year period to a project that the party SF has been a driving force behind.
