Author: Nordvest has gained a friendly neighborhood cafe that welcomes guests for both breakfast and hot meals in the evening. However, the offerings are rather average.

Birgitte Kjær
Journalist
A basement café with a French touch in a very quiet residential area in Nordvest. A café that can boost community, become a hello-goodbye hub, a sanctuary. The potential is there.
The name Gazou is derived from the French
gazouiller
, meaning to chirp, and according to
København Liv
, that’s exactly what the trio behind the café hopes the locals in Fuglekvarteret will do when they visit the new establishment.
