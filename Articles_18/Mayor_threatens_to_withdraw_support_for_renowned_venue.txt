Title: Mayor_threatens_to_withdraw_support_for_renowned_venue
Date: 2025-08-13
Article Type: News
Author: Copenhagen’s Mayor for Children and Youth, Jakob Næsager (K), breaks with an important principle by demanding the firing of Vega’s management over a controversial booking, according to parties at Copenhagen City Hall.

Anders Brøndsholm
Journalist
It is »completely out of line« with what one can allow oneself as a local politician.
That is the immediate reaction from Enhedslisten at Copenhagen City Hall after the children and youth Mayor Jakob Næsager (Konservative, K) demanded the management of the famous Copenhagen venue Vega be fired.
