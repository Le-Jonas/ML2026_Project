Title: More_people_than_ever_want_reduced_VAT_on_fruit_and_vegetables
Date: 2025-10-17
Article Type: News
Author: 86% of Danes back lowering VAT on fruit and veg—far outstripping support for cuts on meat, coffee, candy, or even food in general.

Per Thiemann
Redaktør for økonomi og samfund
Rarely is an issue so popular among the public that it garners support from nearly 9 out of 10 Danes. For example, it is significantly more support than that for preserving the monarchy.
But such is the current support for reducing VAT on fruits and vegetables.
