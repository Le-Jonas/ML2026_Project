»I was completely, insanely anxious. All the time«.
That’s how Poul Nyrup Rasmussen remembers the beginning of his school days in Esbjerg, where few would have guessed that the working-class boy from the harbor, who often started fights during lunch breaks, would one day become Denmark’s prime minister.
