Date: 2025-04-30
Article Type: Guides
Author: It takes blood, sweat, and endless effort to serve top-tier dishes. But at a North Jutland restaurant, they make it look effortlessly easy.

Amez Rasoul
Lærke Kløvedal
»It is not possible to reserve specific tables, but don’t worry … there’s a sea view from all of them.«
That’s what Villa Vest writes on its website. And situated on the very edge of the dune, with a location that from some angles looks as if we’re sitting in the middle of the sea, there is good reason to be frantic about the view. Apart from Kadeau in the far east, I can’t think of a restaurant with a more beautiful view than Villa Vest in North Jutland.
