Naomi Oreskes
er en af verdens førende forskere inden for klimamisinformation og kortlægning af virksomheders indflydelsestaktikker. Hun er medforfatter til den ikoniske ’Merchants of Doubt’, der afslørede, hvordan en lille gruppe forskere skabte tvivl om både rygningens farer og klimaforandringerne. Nu er hun aktuel med ’The Big Myth’, hvor hun dokumenterer hvordan erhvervslivet i årtier har fremmet fortællingen om, at staten er problemet, og at deregulering er vejen til frihed og velstand.
Med udgangspunkt i sin nye bog bringer
Naomi Oreskes
perspektiver om hvordan erhvervsinteresser systematisk og strategisk har været med til at påvirke offentligheden og politikere for at fremme specifikke narrativer omkring øget deregulering i USA. Specifikt kan hun pege på eksempler fra USA hvordan medier, organisationer, forskere og tænketanke er blevet brugt som redskaber til at fremme fortællingen om at statens indblanding er skadelig, og at det frie marked skaber velstand og frihed.

USA: Misinformation, Lobbying, and Deregulation - Meet Harvard Professor Naomi Oreskes
Join us for an afternoon lecture and conversation at PRESSEN to gain insights into how American politics are shaped by powerful corporate interests and how this can be traced in Trump’s and Musk’s USA and currently in Europe.
Naomi Oreskes, one of the world’s leading researchers in climate misinformation and corporate influence tactics, is the co-author of the iconic ’Merchants of Doubt,’ which revealed how a small group of scientists created doubt about both the dangers of smoking and climate change. She is now presenting her latest work, ’The Big Myth,’ where she documents how businesses have promoted the narrative for decades that the state is the problem and deregulation is the path to freedom and prosperity.
Drawing from her new book, Naomi Oreskes will offer perspectives on how corporate interests have systematically and strategically influenced the public and policymakers to promote specific narratives around increased deregulation in the USA. She will specifically highlight examples from the USA showing how media, organizations, scientists, and think tanks have been used as tools to advance the narrative that government intervention is harmful and that the free market creates wealth and freedom.
Meet her and Politiken’s opinion editor Lotte Folke Kaarsholm and learn more about how American politics are shaped by powerful corporate interests and how this can be traced in Trump’s and Musk’s USA as well as in the EU, where there is currently a push to deregulate and roll back corporate sustainability requirements.
The lecture and subsequent conversation will be in English.
