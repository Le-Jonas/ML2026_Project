Title: How_to_talk_to_your_child_about_the_shooting_of_Charlie_Kirk
Date: 2025-09-12
Article Type: News
Author: Violent videos of the shooting attack on right-wing activist Charlie Kirk circulate on social media. Especially children and young people can be particularly vulnerable, assesses Børns Vilkår.

Julie Franson Frost Andersen
Journalist
A simple search and a few clicks on social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok are all it takes to find graphic videos of the shooting attack that claimed the life of American right-wing activist Charlie Kirk during a debate meeting at Utah Valley University on Wednesday.
One of the first handheld videos that pops up on the screen shows Charlie Kirk up close. Within two seconds, you can see blood oozing from his neck as he is hit by a bullet. Then the video plays again. This time in slow motion. And if you turn up the sound, you can hear shots mixed with screams.
