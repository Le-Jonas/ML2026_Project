Title: A_large_number_of_cases_concerning_lost_citizenship_to_be_reviewed
Date: 2025-10-29
Article Type: News
Author: Ministry must, following a Supreme Court ruling, thoroughly examine its own and others’ files to find cases where citizens may have had their citizenship unjustly revoked.

Hans Drachmann
Journalist
Civil servants at the Ministry of Immigration and Integration have been working overtime since a Supreme Court ruling nearly two months ago.
The Supreme Court of Denmark rejected the long-standing practice of the ministry to revoke Danish citizenship from individuals who had it granted by mistake.
