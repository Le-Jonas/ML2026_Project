Title: Former_prime_minister_Venstre_leadership_negotiated_for_the_wrong_ministerial_posts
Date: 2025-04-28
Article Type: Edition
Author: Anders Fogh Rasmussen calls his misinterpretation of Vladimir Putin’s brutality as the »biggest mistake of my life,« regrets his own defense budget cuts bitterly, and describes the developments under Donald Trump as »painful to watch.«

Kristian Klarskov
Politisk reporter
Venstre’s leadership negotiated »poorly« when distributing ministerial posts in 2022, according to former Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen (V).
He highlights, among other things, that Venstre, instead of shielding its ministers from previous »Social Democratic messes« accepted the task of clearing up after Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen’s (S) mink scandal.
