Title: Member_of_Parliament_sails_towards_Gaza
Date: 2025-09-29
Article Type: News
Author: Following reports of drone attacks, Spain and Italy have dispatched warships to escort a fleet of aid boats. Meanwhile, a Danish Member of Parliament is heading towards the war-torn Gaza.

Johan Blem Larsen
Politisk reporter
Dramatic scenes have unfolded in recent days, where several sailboats are on their way to deliver aid to the civilian population in Gaza.
In the Mediterranean, off the coast of Greece, crew members, including Swedish activist Greta Thunberg, have reported attacks from drones that allegedly dropped unidentified objects on the boats and disrupted radio communications, with explosions reportedly heard nearby.
