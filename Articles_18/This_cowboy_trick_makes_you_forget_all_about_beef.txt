Madredaktør
It has gradually become a holiday tradition for my husband and I to invite people over for cowboy stew at our summer house. It started a couple of summers ago when our son and his friends developed a fascination with the Wild West and spaghetti westerns. An obvious opportunity to create a themed evening for parents and children.
