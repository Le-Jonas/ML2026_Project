Title: People_like_my_sister_and_I_are_quite_alone._Fortunately,_we_find_peace_in_our_shared_lot_in_life
Date: 2025-07-12
Article Type: Edition
Author: The fate my older sister and I share crushes any worry about the distance our differences might create, writes Politiken’s Lucas Arturo Ramsøe Willkens in this column.

Lucas Arturo Ramsøe Willkens
Journalist
To be adopted is to be somewhat alone.
I have never looked into a pair of eyes that resemble mine. At least not that I can remember. At birth, I said goodbye to the woman who gave me life, and two months later, I was placed into the arms of the people I have called my mom and dad ever since.
