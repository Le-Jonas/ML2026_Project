Author: Finally! A wine bar has just opened in Nørrebro that doesn’t look like all the others. And it has serious hangout potential.

David Dyrholm
Kulturjournalist og kritiker
Búzio is a breath of fresh air on the Copenhagen bar scene.
Not that the place is turning the world upside down, but sometimes less is more, and I can’t immediately think of other concept wine bars in the capital that are as thoroughly well-executed. Here, some people are genuinely exploring new territory. How refreshing!
