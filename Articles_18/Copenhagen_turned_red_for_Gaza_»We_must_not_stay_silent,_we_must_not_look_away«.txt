Title: Copenhagen_turned_red_for_Gaza_»We_must_not_stay_silent,_we_must_not_look_away«
Date: 2025-06-19
Article Type: News
Author: Thousands of demonstrators dressed in red gathered at Christiansborg Slotsplads, demanding the Danish government push for a lasting ceasefire in Gaza.

Lars Halskov
Journalist
Red, red and more red. In every shade imaginable.
From elegant dresses and worn-out T-shirts to football jerseys from clubs like Liverpool and Arsenal. Even the red Danish national team jersey was represented at the crowded Christiansborg Palace Square (Christiansborg Slotsplads).
