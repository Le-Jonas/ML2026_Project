Title: The_government_is_ready_to_take_action_against_doctors_who_engage_in_inappropriate_behavior
Date: 2025-05-19
Article Type: News
Author: Patients need better protection, says the government, after a Politiken report on doctors engaging in sexual contact without consequences. The minister is ready to change the law.

Frauke Giebner
Journalist
Magnus Kofoed Lassen
Journalist
It is evident that patients need better protection. That is the sentiment shared by the government and several parties in the Danish Parliament, following a report in Politiken on Sunday that described how 11 female patients had been subjected to kisses, unwanted touches, and other sexual advances from the same doctor.
