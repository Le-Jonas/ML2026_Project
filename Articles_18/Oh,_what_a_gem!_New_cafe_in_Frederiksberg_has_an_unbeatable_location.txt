Title: Oh,_what_a_gem!_New_cafe_in_Frederiksberg_has_an_unbeatable_location
Date: 2025-08-05
Article Type: Food
Author: New café in Frederiksberg boasts a prime location: next to Frederiksberg Gardens, in a charming old building, with stunning lake views and a newly landscaped green square.

Birgitte Kjær
Journalist
Møs is a new family-run café located on the ground floor of the historic Møstings Hus, dating back to 1800. This building was once the country estate of a prime minister and now serves as a venue for contemporary art exhibitions. The café features a large outdoor seating area with views of the newly restored duck pond and the recently landscaped John Winthers Plads, just behind Frederiksberg Town Hall. When the crisp town hall bells chime in the background, it’s the perfect romantic touch.
