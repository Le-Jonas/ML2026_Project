Title: New_study_Many_Danish_Jews_experience_antisemitism
Date: 2025-09-22
Article Type: News
Author: It is almost impossible for Jews in Denmark to avoid experiencing or witnessing antisemitism, according to the largest survey of Jewish life in Denmark in 50 years. The Danish Institute for Human Rights, which conducted the survey, highlights significant consequences for the Jewish community.
