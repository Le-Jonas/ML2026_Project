Title: Chocolate-dipped_ginger_sticks
Date: 2025-12-08
Article Type: Food
Author: If you love chocolate-covered ginger, you must try these cookies! The only downside is that they quickly become soft if exposed to air, so be meticulous with airtight packaging. Note that the dough needs to chill overnight, so set aside two days for the process.

If you love chocolate-covered ginger, you must try these cookies! The only downside is that they quickly become soft if exposed to air, so be meticulous with airtight packaging. Note that the dough needs to chill overnight, so set aside two days for the process.
Marie Holm (recipe), Chris Tonnesen (foto)
Approximately 65 pieces.
65 g candied ginger (e.g., from a health food store) 
150 g cold butter 
200 g flour
100 g sugar
1 teaspoon ground ginger
Zest of 1 untreated lemon
1 egg yolk 
100 g good-quality dark chocolate
