Missed Politiken MAD’s Christmas baking workshop? No worries—here’s Trine Hahnemann’s family recipe for æbleskiver.
Trine Hahnemann
Yields about 30 pieces
40 g yeast
800 ml lukewarm whole milk
600 g all-purpose flour
2½ tsp salt
1½ tsp ground cardamom
3 tbsp sugar
4 eggs, separated into yolks and whites
150 g butter
For serving:
powdered sugar for dusting
jam of your choice
