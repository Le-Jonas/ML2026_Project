What’s capturing your attention right now? Ibyen asks a series of cultural figures this question. This time, it’s singer Drew Sycamore.
Marie Møllegaard Andersson
»Music is an interesting thing for me, especially when I’m in the final stages of completing a piece. Silence suddenly gains a whole new value when music fills so much of the day. I must admit that I’ve listened to my own work quite a lot in the lead-up to the release of my new album — both as part of the process and as a farewell to the music that is about to be sent out into the world.«
»When I put on music, it’s often very different from what I create myself, and usually something without vocals. However, some artists I’ve been really excited about are Lucky Daye, Snoh Aalegra, and Chiiild, who have been prominent on my playlists in recent years. Perhaps it’s because they all have a touch of the old R&B from the 2000s, which was my first great love.«
