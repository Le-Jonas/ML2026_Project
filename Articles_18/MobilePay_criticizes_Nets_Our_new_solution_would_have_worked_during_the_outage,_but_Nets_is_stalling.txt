Title: MobilePay_criticizes_Nets_Our_new_solution_would_have_worked_during_the_outage,_but_Nets_is_stalling
Date: 2025-07-23
Article Type: News
Author: MobilePay has a new payment solution ready that would have worked during Nets’ major outage over the weekend. But Nets is using its dominance to keep the product out, MobilePay claims

Jonas Pröschold
Journalist
Saturday night, Denmark was paralyzed by Nets’ major outage, which created chaotic scenes and confusion on the Storebelt Bridge, at festivals, and in Tivoli.
Emergency Preparedness Minister Torsten Schack Pedersen (Venstre, V) told
Berlingske
that Nets’ outage was not handled »well enough«. And in a written comment to Politiken, the Ministry of Industry, Business and Financial Affairs says they have asked the Financial Supervisory Authority for a report from Nets.
