Marie Nørgaard Skadhauge
Journalist
The first meal I can remember...
is my mom’s cold buttermilk (koldskål in Danish) with homemade Danish biscuits and fresh strawberries. I was born in June, so for me, this dish always evokes the taste of summer and birthdays. I’m not entirely sure whether I truly remember it or just wish I do, but the feeling is still there.
Right now, I could go for…
a freshly baked croissant with plenty of butter. Simple quality, you can’t go wrong with that.
