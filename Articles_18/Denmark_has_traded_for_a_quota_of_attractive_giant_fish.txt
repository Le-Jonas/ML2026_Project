Title: Denmark_has_traded_for_a_quota_of_attractive_giant_fish
Date: 2025-08-08
Article Type: News
Author: Handpicked anglers are allowed to catch and bring home tuna, which can be turned into delicious sushi. A single fish can be worth millions of kroner, but the anglers are not permitted to sell them.

Anders Legarth Schmidt
Journalist
It weighs several hundred kilos, reaches a length of nearly three meters, and frolics in Danish waters for the next couple of months, feasting on herring, garfish, and mackerel. The majestic tuna had vanished from Danish waters, likely due to overfishing, but it made a sensational return around 10 years ago. Since then, it has visited every year during late summer and autumn.
Bluefin tuna is perhaps the most coveted fish in the world’s oceans. The finest specimens are sold for millions of euros at fish auctions and end up as sushi in Michelin-starred restaurants.
