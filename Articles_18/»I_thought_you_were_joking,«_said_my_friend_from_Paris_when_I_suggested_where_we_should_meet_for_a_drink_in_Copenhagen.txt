Date: 2025-04-25
Article Type: Edition
Author: Every year around this time, I am reminded of the joy in walking through summer side by side with the deceased. And those who do not have a grave to visit help those of us who do, writes the columnist.

Sidsel Ana Welden
Cemeteries in Copenhagen are not just for the dead.
That’s what a friend said to me when he visited from Paris once again. I had asked if we should get some wine and grab a pita to take to Assistens Kirkegård on a summer day.
