Title: Mayoral_candidate_wants_affordable_public_housing,_but_failed_to_act_as_minister
Date: 2025-10-28
Article Type: News
Author: Mayoral candidates and housing organizations urged Pernille Rosenkrantz-Theil to raise the maximum amount for public housing construction, when she was the Minister for Social Affairs and Housing, but nothing happened. Now, the mayoral candidate wants the government to raise the limit.

Michael Thykier
Redaktør for Forbrug og Tendens
Anders Brøndsholm
Journalist
New affordable public housing is set to be built in Copenhagen, and the Social Democrats’ mayoral candidate, Pernille Rosenkrantz-Theil (Socialdemokratiet, S), wants to lead the way. She proposes that the government should raise the maximum amount, which dictates how expensive housing organizations are allowed to build. The maximum amount has currently halted several new public housing projects.
In her book ’København, København’ Rosenkrantz-Theil writes that she wants the maximum amount for public housing construction raised »so that these affordable homes can constitute a larger part of the overall housing mix«. And last week, Rosenkrantz-Theil called it a »call to action« for the government when she, along with Social Democratic mayor of Aarhus, Anders Winnerskjold, again proposed the same.
