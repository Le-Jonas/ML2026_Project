Title: The_superstar's_own_lyrics_could_be_used_against_him_in_court
Date: 2025-08-12
Article Type: News
Author: One of the nation’s top music stars, Gilli, faces serious tax fraud charges worth millions. Despite his plea of innocence, experts believe his song lyrics could be used as evidence against him.

Johanne Lerhard
Journalist
Since rapper Gilli’s major breakthrough over a decade ago, he has repeatedly referenced financial crimes in his music.
»The only way out for me must be fraud and money«, he says, using slang for cash, in ’Står her’ with Kimbo from 2012.
