Even though the weather is gray and it has rained all morning, it seems like the laughter and tears of joy are making the sun shine in the foyer at Frederiksberg Gymnasium. Broad smiles and flowers are placed side by side with grandparents and champagne glasses on long tables. The air is thick with perfume and excitement.
