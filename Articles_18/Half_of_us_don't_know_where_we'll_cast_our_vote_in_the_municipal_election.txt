Title: Half_of_us_don't_know_where_we'll_cast_our_vote_in_the_municipal_election
Date: 2025-10-22
Article Type: News
Author: Half of voters have not decided which party they will vote for in the municipal and regional elections next month. A poll conducted in the midst of the campaign reveals significant challenges for Socialdemokratiet.

Ida Nathan
Politisk reporter
If you’re unsure who to vote for in the local elections on November 18, you’re far from alone. According to a survey conducted by Epinion for the Constructive Institute among over 100,000 Danes, 49 percent of voters had not decided on October 13 what they would vote for. In Copenhagen, this number was as high as 52 percent.
