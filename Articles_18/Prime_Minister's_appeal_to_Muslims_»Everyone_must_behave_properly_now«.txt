Title: Prime_Minister's_appeal_to_Muslims_»Everyone_must_behave_properly_now«
Date: 2025-09-15
Article Type: News
Author: Muslims in Denmark neglect their responsibility by not doing more to prevent negative social control and violent youth, says Mette Frederiksen, who also criticizes Roskilde University.

Johan Blem Larsen
Politisk reporter
A spontaneous »thankfully!« slips out of Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen (Socialdemokratiet, S) when Politiken points out that immigration policy is back at the forefront of Danish politics.
She has just arrived at the Aalborg Culture and Congress Center, where Social Democrats from across the country are holding their annual meeting this weekend. In Frederiksen’s speech on Saturday, immigration policy will be a key theme.
