Anders Brøndsholm
Journalist
There is far too poor control over the most polluting companies in Denmark, and the authorities are not living up to the law.
This is the conclusion from the state auditors in a new report presented on Monday. It sharply criticizes the Ministry of Environment and the Environmental Protection Agency’s role in environmental approvals of particularly polluting companies.
