Title: The_Baltic_Sea_is_among_the_places_on_Earth_where_the_ocean_has_become_the_darkest,_and_no_one_knows_the_consequences
Date: 2025-06-23
Article Type: News
Author: 

The world’s oceans are darkening at a rapid pace. This means that the areas where photosynthesis can occur are shrinking. The Baltic Sea is one of the places on the planet where this development is most pronounced.
Bjørn van Overeem
Journalist
In the last 20 years, the world’s oceans have darkened to such an extent that it has caught the attention of scientists across the globe. However, none of them can currently predict the consequences of this darkening.
And one place particularly stands out when looking at an illustration of the ocean areas that have become less bright. On the world map, the Baltic Sea appears as a bright red spot in the middle. It is here, in the waters around Denmark, that the change is most apparent.
