Date: 2025-06-23
Article Type: Culture
Author: 79-year-old Neil Young played with full and flawed intensity on Amager, insisting on keeping the rock going a little longer. It worked out pretty well.

Alexander Vesterlund
Kritiker ved Politiken
Neil Young has always sung with a voice that seemed on the verge of falling apart. He’s been mad as hell since Nixon became president – he’s a stubborn guy always searching for the truth.
Everything Neil Young has ever been, he still was on Sunday night at Tiøren, where he performed with his new band, The Chrome Hearts.
