Title: Minister_fights_back_Proposes_new_nordic_streaming_service
Date: 2025-05-01
Article Type: News
Author: Jakob Engel-Schmidt (M) aims to create a Nordic alternative to the American dominance in the streaming market. But he’s not looking to chase Netflix out of the country.

Lars Eriksen
Journalist
Minister for Culture Jakob Engel-Schmidt (Moderaterne, M) is now launching a fight against the dominance of American streaming services.
In a letter to his colleagues in the Nordic Council of Ministers, he proposes creating a Nordic alternative to platforms like Netflix and Max.
