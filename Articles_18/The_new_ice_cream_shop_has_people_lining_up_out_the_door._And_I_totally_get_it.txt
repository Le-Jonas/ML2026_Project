Summer isn’t over in Østerbro, where a new ice cream shop has customers lining up. Dillon’s gelato is packed with flavor and seriously good.
Birgitte Kjær
Journalist
A small Italian ice cream parlor on a corner of a quiet neighborhood in Østerbro, where there is a passionate commitment to creating
il meglio del meglio
, the best of the best, from organic ingredients.
They’re open from early morning, where you can order ice cream for breakfast – served with blackberry jam, olive oil, and toasted bread. If you prefer a more traditional start to your day, you can have a pain au chocolat from the excellent Skipper bakery, a bun with cheese with your coffee, or a slice of bread with pickled tomatoes and stracciatella cheese.
