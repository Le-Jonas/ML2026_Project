Date: 2025-08-04
Article Type: News
Author: This year’s numbers for admissions to higher education have been released. It’s difficult to change applicants’ preferences, says expert.

Rosa Marie Bjerre
Journalist
Tonight, the messages will come in about whether one has been accepted into higher education. And for 59,957 out of 85,508 applicants, it will mean study start after the summer vacation.
This year is particularly exciting because we are looking at a changed educational landscape, says Mads Fjord Jørgensen, a specialist at the think tank DEA.
