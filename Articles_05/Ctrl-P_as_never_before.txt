Title: Ctrl-P_as_never_before
Date: 2009-08-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The advent of the computer was followed by promises of saving rain forests by creating the paperless society. E-mail instead of snail mail. Electronic bank statements instead of reams of debits and credits on account movements and agendas and meetings put on slideshows instead of dockets.
But the figures seem to show another story.
