Title: Reward_offered_for_King_David
Date: 2008-10-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police still have no definite leads in the case of Copenhagen Cathedral’s statue of King David that was stolen from a stonemason during repairs last week.
As a result, the Parish Council has decided to offer a DKK 25,000 reward for information leading to the recovery of the statue which, flanked by Moses, normally beckons visitors at the Cathedral entrance.
