Title: Obama_favourite_in_Danish_Parliament
Date: 2008-09-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

If the Danish Parliament were to decide who would win the U.S. presidential election, then Barrack Obama would waltz into the White House, according to a survey carried out by the national news agency Ritzau.
Eighty-four of the 179 members of the Danish Parliament – equal to a turnout of 46.9 percent - answered a questionnaire and nine out of ten placed their mark against Obama, the survey showed.
