Title: EU_to_trade_aid_for_raw_materials
Date: 2008-11-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A single mobile phone requires some 40 different raw materials - some of them particularly rare.
"Many of the raw materials are found in Africa - a continent that thanks to foreign and aid policy has Europe as its most imporant partner," says EU Industry Commissioner Günther Verheugen.
