Title: Majority_for_Fehmarn_Bridge
Date: 2008-09-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Transport Minister Carina Christensen has negotiated a parliamentary majority to further a project involving a bridge over the Fehmarn Belt between Denmark and Germany.
According to Ritzau, an international agreement regarding the bridge is to be signed between Christensen and her German colleague Wolfgang Tiefensee on Wednesday.
