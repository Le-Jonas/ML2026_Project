Title: Report_Missing_agent_presumed_dead
Date: 2008-09-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The family of the Danish Security and Intelligence Service agent, who has been missing since the attack against the Marriott Hotel in Islamabad, has been told that he has in all probability died in the attack, according to the Nordjyske newspaper.
The man was an experienced agent who had previously been posted to Kosova and had been sent to Islamabad recently in order to help beef up Danish embassy security.
