Title: Experts_Share_drop_again_today
Date: 2008-10-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Europe and Scandinavian bourses are expected to open down again following the hysteria on the Asian markets, some of which dropped 10 percent, according to Nordea’s Senior Stock Strategist Henrik Druseberg and Saxo Bank's Senior Equity Strategist Christian Blaabjerg.
“It seems that Europe and thus Denmark will be opening with a drop of some 4-5 percent,” Druseberg says.
