Title: DPP_Force_heads_to_step_in
Date: 2008-10-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

If children break school rules, school heads should not only be able to intervene, they should have a duty to do so, according to the Danish People’s Party Education Spokesman Martin Henriksen.
Henriksen is reacting to a new executive order designed to strengthen discipline in state schools.
