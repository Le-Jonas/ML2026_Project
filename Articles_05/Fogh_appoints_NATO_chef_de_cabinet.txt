Title: Fogh_appoints_NATO_chef_de_cabinet
Date: 2009-04-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Turkey played an important role in the recent end game leading to the appointment of Anders Fogh Rasmussen as NATO's next secretary general.
The former Danish prime minister has now chosen Denmark's Ambassador to Turkey Jesper Vahr as his chef de cabinet when he takes over the secretary generalship from Jaap de Hoop Scheffer on August 1.
