Title: Anti-gang_package_ready
Date: 2009-03-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Minister for Justice Brian Mikkelsen is preparing to put forward an urgent package to help authorities deal with an increasing armed conflict between the Hells Angels and immigrant gangs.
According to Politiken’s information, the package, which has been developed following discussions between the ministry, the National Police Commissioner’s office and the Copenhagen Police, may be announced today and will include proposals to adjust legislation.
