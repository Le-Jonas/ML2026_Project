Title: Arla's_Chinese_factory_on_hold
Date: 2008-09-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Arla, one of the world's largest dairy produce manufacturers, has temporarily stopped production at its factory in Hohhot in China after three samples from its factory tested positive for melamine in milk powder.
Arla CEO Peder Tuborgh, who is currently in China, says the foodstuff scandal in the country is a national catastrophe.
