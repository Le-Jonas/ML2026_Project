Title: Maersk_reports_major_6-month_loss
Date: 2009-08-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A.P. Møller – Maersk, which is Denmark’s largest group, has returned a major loss of DKK 3 billion after tax compared with a profit in the same period last year of DKK 11.6 billion.
Earnings in the first six months of 2009 were at DKK 127 billion, compared with DKK 148 billion in the first six months of 2008.
