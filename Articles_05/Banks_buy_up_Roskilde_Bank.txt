Title: Banks_buy_up_Roskilde_Bank
Date: 2008-09-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Roskilde Bank is to be bought up by Nordea, Spar Nord Bank and Arbejdernes Landsbank

Arbejdernes Landsbank, Spar Nord and Nordea have taken over Roskilde Bank which was forced out of business earlier this month and temporarily taken over by Nationalbanken, Denmark’s central bank pending takeovers.
