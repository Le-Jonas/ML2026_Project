Title: Biker_club_shooting
Date: 2008-09-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There has been another shooting episode in Copenhagen overnight, with shots being fired at a Hell’s Angels fortified clubhouse on Svanevej in northwestern Copenhagen.
A neighbour to the event called police at 2.30 a.m. On arrival officers found 9 mm casings in front of the building, but were unable to see where the shots had hit. No-one appears to have been injured in the attack.
