Title: Many_young_people_on_benefits
Date: 2009-03-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Young people are hardest hit by unemployment in Denmark according to a report prepared by the National Labour Market Authority for Berlingske Tidende.
In just eight months, the number of young people on unemployment benefits in the 16-24 age group has quadrupled, while the number of young people in the same group on cash benefits has grown by 32 percent.
