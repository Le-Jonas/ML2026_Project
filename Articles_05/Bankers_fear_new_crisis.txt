Title: Bankers_fear_new_crisis
Date: 2009-08-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The current crisis will be long and hard, and when we get out of this one, another one may be on the way, according to 70 percent of bank managers in a bank manager survey carried out by Jyllands-Posten.
“We’ll probably get another financial crisis – but of a different type,” says Country Manager Peter Schültze of Nordea.
