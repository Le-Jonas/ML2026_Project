Title: Police_put_pressure_on_courts
Date: 2008-12-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Judges' Association and the HK union are warning that unless something is done quickly, the lead time for court decisions is unlikely to become shorter.
"It's like watching a boat that is about to sink....If you don't do anything, it does in fact sink," says HK Chair Thora Pedersen.
