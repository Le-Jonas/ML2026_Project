Title: Dutch_Hells_Angels_sue_for_millions
Date: 2009-08-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A court in Amsterdam is hearing a case brought by 24 Hells Angels who allege they have sustained damage as a result of prosecution by the justice authorities.
The highest claim for damages at 800,000 euros has been lodged by Harry S. A member of the Amsterdam chapter of the Angels, 51-year-old S. was held for two months until the public prosecutor ordered his release.
