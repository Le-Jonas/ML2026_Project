Title: Poll_Rather_Espersen_than_Løkke
Date: 2008-12-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest opinion poll from Catinét for Ritzau shows a clear majority in favour of the Conservative Leader Lene Espersen if Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen (Lib) were to leave his post - even among Liberal voters.
Asked to choose between Liberal Finance Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen and Conservative Leader Lene Esdpersen as a potential successor to the premiership, 17.5 percent chose Løkke Rasmussen and 49 percent Espersen.
