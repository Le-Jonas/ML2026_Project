Title: Foreign_minister_in_cycle_crash
Date: 2008-09-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Foreign Minister Per Stig Møller was injured yesterday, when he fell off his bicycle while on his way home in Copenhagen, according to a statement from the Danish Foreign Ministry.
The accident means that the foreign minster cannot take part in today’s extraordinary EU summit on the conflict in Georgia, and Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen will be the only Danish representative at the meeting.
