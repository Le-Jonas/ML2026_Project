Title: Busy_night_for_police
Date: 2008-09-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There was unrest in the Nørrebro district of Copenhagen again last night where police vehicles in and around Mjølnerparken were bombarded with cobblestones. Police had been called to the area to stop a group of men who have been shooting gas pistols in the air.
“It’s been a busy night,” says Duty Officer Frank Reuter, who added that calm was restored to the area at around two o’clock in the morning.
