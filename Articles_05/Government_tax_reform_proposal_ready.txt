Title: Government_tax_reform_proposal_ready
Date: 2009-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government is to release its tax proposal today, reducing income tax considerably as well as reducing interest deductions for home owners.
The proposal is to be presented at 11 o’clock, with media reports this morning suggesting that the proposal will include a DKK 30 billion reorganization of the tax system. Among other things, the reports say that the government proposal calls for a marginal tax rate cut from the current 63 percent to 56 percent.
