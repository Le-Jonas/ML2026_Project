Title: Foreign_Ministry_warning_on_Thailand
Date: 2008-12-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Travellers to Thailand are being warned by the foreign ministry to show particular vigilance and to avoid demonstrations and large crowds in the wake of a decision by the Thai Constitutional Court in a case involving now former Prime Minister Somchai Wongsawat's PPP party.
Wongsawat has since announced his resignation.
