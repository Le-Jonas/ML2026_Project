Title: Police_we_are_failing_the_public
Date: 2008-09-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A confidential report from the national police authority Rigspolitiet says that the police emergency call-up system is not working properly, and a further two or three years will go by before it becomes efficient, according to Berlingske Tidende.
Rigspolitiet says that police have reacted incorrectly in 40 percent of the cases, and that there have been communications problems in a further 20 percent. Forty percent of the cases have been handled correctly, the report says.
