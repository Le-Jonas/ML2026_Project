Title: House_sales_slowing_further
Date: 2009-07-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The recession has had its effect on sales of domestic housing, but the latest survey seems to show things are getting worse.
According to a survey by boliga.dk some 23.3 percent of houses on the market have been up for sale for more than a year compared to February when the figure was 15.6 percent.
