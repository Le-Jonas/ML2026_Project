Title: MAN_Diesel_axes_550
Date: 2009-08-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Half of the workforce at the MAN Diesel plant in Frederikshavn are to be laid off as a result of a decision to move diesel engine production from Jutland to Germany, according to DR P4 Nordjylland.
Employees were informed of the decision at a meeting at Arena Nord this morning, with the 550 termination letters expected to be delivered by September 3 at the latest.
