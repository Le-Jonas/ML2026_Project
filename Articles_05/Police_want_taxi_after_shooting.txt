Title: Police_want_taxi_after_shooting
Date: 2009-01-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 34-year-old man who was shot at close quarters in the Vesterbro area of Copenhagen yesterday evening remains in a critical condition, according to Copenhagen police.
Dark taxi 
The man was hit in the chest in the shooting. Information from witnesses has led the police to search for a dark taxi which was seen in the area at the time of the shooting. Two people are said to have driven away in the vehicle after six shots were fired.
