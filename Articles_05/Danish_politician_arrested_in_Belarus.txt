Title: Danish_politician_arrested_in_Belarus
Date: 2009-07-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A young Liberal Party politician has been arrested on arrival in Belarus where he was to hold discussions with local politicians.
Jens-Kristian Lütken, who was a Liberal Party candidate in the recent EU parliamentary elections, was arrested on arrival in the Belarus capital Minsk with four other Danes.
