Title: At_least_two_killed_in_court_shooting
Date: 2009-04-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

At least two people have been killed and several wounded in a shooting at a court in the southern German city of Landshut, police said.
"According my information there are two people dead," police spokesman Leonard Mayer told N-TV television. He said shots were fired in several rooms inside the court building.
