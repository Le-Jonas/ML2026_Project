Title: Claims_of_torture_against_Faderhuset
Date: 2008-10-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Children of the Fadehuset religious sect have been trained to withstand torture in order that they could preach the gospel in Muslim countries, according to Samuel Hesselberg, who was a sect member for seven years.
Handle torture 
Camps were designed to prepare children for their role as evangelists according to Hesselberg.
