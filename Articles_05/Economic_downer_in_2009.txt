Title: Economic_downer_in_2009
Date: 2008-12-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

2009 will be a gloomy year with a dramatic drop in the global economy, according to Saxo Bank Chief Economist David Karsbøl.
"The only consolation is that we believe that 2009 will be a turning point for the economy as it can't get much worse," says Karsbøl.
