Title: Danish_PM_Extra_Climate_Summit
Date: 2009-01-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen is calling for an urgent international heads of state and government summit on climate change, to take place already in March.
Speaking at the World Economic Forum in Davos, where he was invited to take part in a panel debate on the roadmap for a global climate agreement at the UN Climate Summit in Copenhagen in December, Fogh said an urgent high-level meeting was necessary.
