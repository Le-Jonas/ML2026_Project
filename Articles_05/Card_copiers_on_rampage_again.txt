Title: Card_copiers_on_rampage_again
Date: 2009-08-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A criminal gang has begun working cash dispensing machines on the capital island Zealand using a new method of reading account numbers and pins, according to the police. It is the third wave of credit card identity theft this year.
Over the weekend and on Monday, police found copying devices in dispensers in Roskilde and Køge.
