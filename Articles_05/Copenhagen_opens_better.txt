Title: Copenhagen_opens_better
Date: 2008-10-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Copenhagen Stock Exchange rallied today with the C-20 index of the country’s most-traded shares up some four percent. Even the major banks were making gains with Danske Bank up four percent and Nordea up three percent.
Non-financials were also doing well with A.P.Møller Mærsk and D/S Norden up between five and six percent and Vestas up four percent.
