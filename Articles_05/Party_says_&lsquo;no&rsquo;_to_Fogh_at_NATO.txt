Title: Party_says_&lsquo;no&rsquo;_to_Fogh_at_NATO
Date: 2009-03-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party Defence Spokesman Holger K. Nielsen does not believe that Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen is the right man for the job as secretary-general of NATO.
“I would be happy for him to leave domestic politics, but seen from a NATO perspective I do not believe that he is the right candidate,” Nielsen tells Information adding that Fogh has not built up a good relationship with the Muslim world.
