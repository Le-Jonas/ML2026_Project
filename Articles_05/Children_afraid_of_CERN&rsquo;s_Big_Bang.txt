Title: Children_afraid_of_CERN&rsquo;s_Big_Bang
Date: 2008-09-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish National Council for Children is warning that media headlines such as ‘The end of the world is nigh’ or ‘Critics fear doomsday’ become etched in the consciousness of many children, and their fears must be addressed by parents.
The council warning comes in connection with today’s start-up of the CERN laboratory Large Hadron Collider or Big Bang Machine.
