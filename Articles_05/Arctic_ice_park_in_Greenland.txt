Title: Arctic_ice_park_in_Greenland
Date: 2009-02-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Canadian and American researchers are suggesting the creation of the world’s first Arctic Ice Park to stretch across the northern borders of Greenland, Canada, Alaska, Norway and Russia.
According to Sermitsiaq, the idea of creating the park is to put the area on the United Nations World Heritage List.
