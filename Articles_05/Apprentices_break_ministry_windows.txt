Title: Apprentices_break_ministry_windows
Date: 2009-03-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Glazier apprentices have taken responsibility for having shattered windows at the Education Ministry, to complain about a lack of internships, without which they cannot complete their education.
In a news release, the apprentices say:” Let us fix the windows, Bertel” referring to Education Minister Bertel Haarder.
