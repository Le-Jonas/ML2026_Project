Title: PM&rsquo;s_popularity_drops
Date: 2008-09-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Electoral satisfaction with Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen’s (Liberal) handling of his job appears to be considerably lower, according to a Greens Institute poll in the financial daily Børsen.
Over the past couple of months, the number of voters who believe the prime minister is doing a good job has fallen from 55% to 47%. On the other hand the number of dissatisfied voters has risen from 38% to 45%. At the same time, the poll shows that the prime minister’s credibility in economic and immigration policy has dropped.
