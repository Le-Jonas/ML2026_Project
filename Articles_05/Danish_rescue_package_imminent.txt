Title: Danish_rescue_package_imminent
Date: 2008-10-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government and the Danish banks have reached agreement on a crisis plan which observers say is historic in Danish financial history.
Over the next two years, banks are to make 30 billion kroner available in a so-called liquidation fund, with banks contributing funds in relation to their size, according to TV2. This means that banks such as Nordea and Danske Bank will be contributing most.
