Title: Khader_leaves_Alliance
Date: 2009-01-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Naser Khader is to leave the Liberal Alliance Party, according to his blog.
"I am leaving the Liberal Alliance Party. I have spent my Christmas holidays thinking through my political future. I have discovered that a gut feeling is not enough - your heart has to be in it. And my heart is no longer where we have positioned the Liberal Alliance," Khader writes.
