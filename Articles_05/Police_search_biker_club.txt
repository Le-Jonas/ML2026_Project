Title: Police_search_biker_club
Date: 2008-11-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two people were arrested this morning when 20-30 officers ransacked the Hells Angels clubhouse on Svanevej in Copenhagen.
Supt. Steffen Th. Steffensen says police suspected that there were weapons on the premises given that the address has been the target of several shooting episodes in recent months.
