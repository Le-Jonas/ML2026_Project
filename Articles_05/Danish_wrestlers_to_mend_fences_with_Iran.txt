Title: Danish_wrestlers_to_mend_fences_with_Iran
Date: 2008-11-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Wrestling Federation believes that Denmark's tarnished image in the Muslim world should be wrestled away and has received a handsome sum for a charm offensive to Iran and Egypt.
'The Mohammed Project', which is what the federation calls its trip, is designed to use the popularity of its sport in the Middle East to help create a better image for Denmark.
