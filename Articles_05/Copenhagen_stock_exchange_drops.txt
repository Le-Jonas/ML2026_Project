Title: Copenhagen_stock_exchange_drops
Date: 2009-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The C-20 index on the Copenhagen Stock Exchange started the day with a major drop of 1.77 percent below Monday closing after 30 minutes of trading.
Copenhagen followed bourses elsewhere in Europe. The Stockholm OMXS dropped 0.4 percent; the London FTSE 100 was down 0.36 percent; Frankfurt’s DAX dropped 1.95 percent and the CAC 40 in Paris dropped 1.01 percent.
