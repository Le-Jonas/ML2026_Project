Title: Foreign_minister_Avoid_Iranian_kiss_of_death
Date: 2009-06-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The outside world should be extremely wary of giving support to those demonstrating against the regime in Iran for fear of giving ‘the kiss of death’ to developments, according to Denmark’s Foreign Minister Per Stig Møller.
Møller’s statements come following heavy criticism from the Socialist People’s Party, which has accused the government of being much too passive and reserved in its reactions against Iran.
