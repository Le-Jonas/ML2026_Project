Title: 40,000_houses_for_sale
Date: 2008-10-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

At the end of September there were almost 65,000 houses for sale on the internet, of which 40,000 were detached or semi-detached properties, according to the latest statistics from Realkreditrådet.
The number of houses for sale has thus grown by almost a third since last September – the highest number since 2004 when Realkreditrådet began registering sales on the internet.
