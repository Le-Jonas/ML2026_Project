Title: Poll_Voters_favour_Løkke_R
Date: 2009-05-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Gallup opinion poll suggests that 48 percent of the electorate prefers Lars Løkke Rasmussen (Lib) as prime minister than his main opposition contender Helle Thorning-Schmidt (SocDem), according to Berlingske Tidende.
The poll is an about-face in comparison to three months ago when Thorning-Schmidt polled 47 percent and Lars Løkke Rasmussen polled 30 percent.
