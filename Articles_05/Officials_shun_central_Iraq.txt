Title: Officials_shun_central_Iraq
Date: 2009-05-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Central Iraq is so dangerous that Danish and Norwegian officials studying the threat level in the region in March remained in the northern provinces of the country.
Nonetheless, Denmark’s Immigration Service has rejected asylum requests from 119 Iraqis from central areas of Iraq who are now waiting to be forcibly repatriated.
