Title: Danish_test_can_save_millions_of_cattle
Date: 2009-08-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A group of Danish researchers has found a new test for foot and mouth disease that can save millions of cattle by determining whether anti-bodies are from the disease or a vaccination, according to videnskab.dk.
Foot and mouth disease is one of the most contagious and feared cattle diseases and in 2001 cost the lives of eight million cattle in Britain alone.
