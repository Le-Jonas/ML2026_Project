Title: DPP_overrides_spokesman
Date: 2008-12-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The leadership of the Danish People's Party has overridden its defence spokesman, who caused major controversy up to the weekend by criticising Foreign Minister Per Stig Møller for not doing enough to get allied support for Danish troops in Afghanistan.
Foreign Minister Per Stig Møller (Cons) said the criticism was in bad taste and urged the Danish People's Party to withdraw its criticism.
