Title: Liberal_reshuffle_now!
Date: 2008-10-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The polls seem to signal hard times for the senior ruling coalition partner, the Liberal Party, and Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen is the target of some criticism from within, as a new generation of Liberals calls for a reshuffle.
"Birthe Rønn Hornbech and Troels Lund Poulsen were novelties after the last election. There were a lot of Liberals who wanted a bit more than that," Herning's Mayor Lars Krarup (Lib) told TV2.
