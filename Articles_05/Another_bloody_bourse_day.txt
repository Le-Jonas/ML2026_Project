Title: Another_bloody_bourse_day
Date: 2008-10-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s C20 index of the country’s most traded stocks ended the day as it began on a downer. The index closed 4.84 percent down, despite a brief recovery in the afternoon.
Pessimism 
Danske Bank Chief Economist Steen Bocian says the day’s dramas on bourses across Europe were the result of general pessimism and nervous over-reactions to the news of the day.
