Title: Bus_strike
Date: 2008-10-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Eleven Copenhagen bus routes have been hit by cancellations this morning as a result of a union meeting for 300 Arriva drivers unhappy with schedules.
Affected routes are 10, 16E, 26, 30, 32, 33, 36, 75E, 76E, 185 og 350S.
