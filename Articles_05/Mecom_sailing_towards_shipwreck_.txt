Title: Mecom_sailing_towards_shipwreck_
Date: 2009-07-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Mecom Group, which owns one of Denmark’s most prestigious publishing groups Berlingske Media, is in the high risk zone of failing its bank review in a year’s time, according to a study of its accounts carried out by Dansk Aktie Analyse for Jyllands-Posten.
The survey, which projects Mecon Group’s earnings, suggests that the Group will be unable to change its results to a level that will satisfy its financial institutions.
