Title: EU_exit_poll_DPP_to_take_a_seat
Date: 2009-06-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Megafon Exit Poll taken for TV2/Politiken suggests the right of centre Danish People’s Party may win a seat from the Conservative Party to garner two seats in the European Parliament.
The exit poll was taken at 16.45 based on 2,640 interviews and shows the Conservatives dropping a mandate to land on one seat.
