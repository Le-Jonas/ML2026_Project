Title: Govt._taxes_green_energy
Date: 2008-10-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In Northern Jutland alone, 8,000 Energi Nord customers have chosen to pay more for environment-friendly electricity by subscribing to Norwegian hydro power electricity, which has a zero CO2 footprint.
But consumers are unhappy that they still have to pay the government’s CO2 tax for their electricity.
