Title: Only_3_banks_accept_package
Date: 2009-02-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Parliament may have rushed through the 75 billion kroner Bank Rescue Package II in January, but most of the Danish banks appear to be less hasty in enrolling for funds.
Danske Bank, Spar Nord Bank and the small Aarhus Lokalbank are currently the only three to have unequivocally accepted the offer.
