Title: Danish_deposit_on_German_cans
Date: 2009-08-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The decade-long Danish-German deposit war appears to have reached an end.
Denmark’s Environment Minister Troels Lund Poulsen and his German counterpart Sigmar Gabriel have reached agreement on a deposit and retrieval arrangement for some 400 million beverage cans bought each year south of the border and introduced into Denmark.
