Title: Danish_stock_starts_well
Date: 2008-10-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The benchmark C-20 index was up around 4.5 percent 30 minutes after opening. It began dropping, however, and at time of writing (10.45 hrs) was at around 1.6 percent.
Danske Bank, which earlier today downgraded forecasts for 2008 was also in good trim, rising some five percent, but fell again by 10.45 to 1.57 percent.
