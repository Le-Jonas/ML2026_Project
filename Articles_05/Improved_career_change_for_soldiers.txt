Title: Improved_career_change_for_soldiers
Date: 2008-09-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A large majority in parliament wants to make a military career more attractive by enabling soldiers to move directly into civilian studies and to guage their qualifications more efficiently, according to Jyllands-Posten.
Break monopoly 
The initiatives from Defence Minister Søren Gade have become necessary due to a lack of military personnel.
