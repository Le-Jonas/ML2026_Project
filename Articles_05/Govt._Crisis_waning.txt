Title: Govt._Crisis_waning
Date: 2009-08-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The economic crisis will have waned so much that Denmark can expect growth again in 2010, according to the Economic Report which is to be published along with the budget later today, Politken.dk has learned.
According to reports, 2009 is expected to return a negative growth of three percent of GDP while growth is expected to be in positive territory in 2010 at plus one percent of GDP.
