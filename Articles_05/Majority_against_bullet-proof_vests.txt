Title: Majority_against_bullet-proof_vests
Date: 2009-08-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party (DPP) seems to have mustered a majority willing to introduce a ban on bullet-proof vests for the general public in order to stop bikers and gangs from using the protection, according to Berlingske Tidende.
“Bikers and immigrant gangs can take part in the ongoing gang war with less risk when they use bullet-proof vests and I see no reason why we should help them. The vest is a defensive weapon that we want removed from the streets,” says DPP Deputy Chairman Peter Skaarup who wants to slap a fine of DKK 5,000 for those caught wearing the vests.
