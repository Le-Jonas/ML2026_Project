Title: Explosive_device_found_in_Copenhagen
Date: 2008-09-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A potentially fatal bomb was discovered overnight in the hallway of a building at the corner of H.V. Rolstedsvej and Tranehavevej in Copenhagen. The box, containing among other things some wires and a liquid, was discovered by a resident.
Residents in the building were evacuated.
