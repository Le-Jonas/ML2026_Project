Title: Motive_for_restaurant_shooting_unclear
Date: 2009-01-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The 61-year-old owner of the Bali restaurant in central Copenhagen is in a critical condition after having been shot in the shoulder.
According to the police, the incident took place at around 7 p.m. on Monday when an armed man walked over to a table where the owner and a guest were sitting and began shooting. The guest was hit in the arm but is not in a critical condition.
