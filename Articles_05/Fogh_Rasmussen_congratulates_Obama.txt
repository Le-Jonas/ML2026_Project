Title: Fogh_Rasmussen_congratulates_Obama
Date: 2008-11-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Congratulations was the word that sprang to Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen as he commented on Barrack Obama’s victory in the American presidential elections.
And although Obama’s policies will be somewhat different to those of Fogh’s friend George Bush, he believes his close relationship with the White House will continue, and he will be seeking an early meeting with President-elect Obama.
