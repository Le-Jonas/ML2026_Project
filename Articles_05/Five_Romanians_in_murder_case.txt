Title: Five_Romanians_in_murder_case
Date: 2008-11-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The court in Horsens has remanded six Romanians in custody, five of them in connection with the murder of a 76-year-old man during a robbery in a private home in Skovby near Denmark's second largest city Århus.
The sixth man was remanded under immigration laws.
