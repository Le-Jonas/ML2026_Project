Title: Danish_government_says_jobless_to_rise
Date: 2009-05-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish government has downgraded its forecasts for the Danish economy, and is expecting some 145,000 unemployed in 2010 – an increase of almost 100,000 on figures from August 2008.
The government Economic Outlook also shows national finances plummeting from a 2008 surplus of DKK 63 billion to a forecast 2010 deficit of DKK 60 billion.
