Title: Government_wants_secret_crisis_management
Date: 2008-10-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Government ministers have been criticising the Social Democratic Party after it has published its plan on how to address the current financial crisis and bring stability to the banking sector.
The first to criticise Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning Schmidt was Finance Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen (Lib.) who complained that Thorning Schmidt had reneged on an understanding that the financial crisis should not be used for party politics.
