Title: Crown_Prince_goes_green
Date: 2008-11-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Crown Prince and Princess can look forward to living in a more energy-friendly environment in future after their apartments at Amalienborg Palace have undergone a green re-fit.
The Palaces and Properties Agency is in the process of renovating Frederik VIII's palace, which is the crown couple's official home when in Copenhagen.
