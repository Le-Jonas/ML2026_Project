Title: Diabetes_increasing_in_40s
Date: 2009-04-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Type 2 diabetes is on the increase among people of both sexes and particularly among those around 40, according to 24timer.
Over the past decade, the number of people in their forties who have been diagnosed with Type 2 diabetes has risen from 1,713 to 2,945 – or 72 percent.
