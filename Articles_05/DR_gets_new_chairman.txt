Title: DR_gets_new_chairman
Date: 2008-11-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Managing Director of the Royal Danish Theatre Michael Christiansen has been appointed Chairman of the Danish broadcasting corporation DR today.
Christiansen takes over from former DR Chairman Mogens Munk-Rasmussen who left the board as a result of DR's financial problems stemming from the construction of the Ørestad site.
