Title: Influenza_vaccines_marginally_effective
Date: 2009-08-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Influenza vaccines work to a certain degree, but their effects are limited according to new research, although researchers have not yet tested new vaccines against H1N1.
“There is, unfortunately, only limited evidence to show that they work, but doubts should favour the weakest,” National Health Board Centre Chief Else Smith tells DR.
