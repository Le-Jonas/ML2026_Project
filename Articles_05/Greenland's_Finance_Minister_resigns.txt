Title: Greenland's_Finance_Minister_resigns
Date: 2008-09-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Greenland Finance Minister Aleqa Hammond has resigned because she is dissatisfied with the economic policy of the government.
"I object to the economic policy that is being followed by the government and my objections and warnings fall on deaf ears. I have no wish to be responsible for this," Hammond says in a news release.
