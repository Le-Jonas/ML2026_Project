Title: Pirates_handed_over_to_Netherlands
Date: 2009-02-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The five pirates who have been held since December 31 on the Danish warship Absalon were handed over to the Dutch authorities early this morning, according to Admiral Danish Fleet headquarters (SOK).
The five, who unsuccessfully attempted to hijack a Netherlands Antilles-flagged vessel off Somalia are to be transported to the Netherlands where they will stand trial.
