Title: Danish_school_tests_all_pupils_for_drugs
Date: 2009-02-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Twelve out of an initial test group of 30 pupils at the Søndersø Production School in northern Funen have tested positive for drug use and are to be offered treatment, or expulsion, according to Fyens Stiftstidende.
The production school has gone on the offensive against drug abuse and is to test all of its pupils.
