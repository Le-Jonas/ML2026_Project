Title: Poll_Opposition_leads
Date: 2009-03-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest opinion poll gives a clear majority of 52 percent to the opposition Social Democrats, Socialist People’s Party, Social Liberals and Unity List if an election were held today.
The result of the Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 gives the opposition 91 seats in Parliament and is the best result for the opposition since 2004.
