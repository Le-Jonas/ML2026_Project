Title: Banking_sector_seeking_model
Date: 2008-09-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The banking sector and the central bank feel that the situation in the finance sector is so serious that all banks - including those that are badly run - must be secured.
The Danish Bankers Association Chairman Peter Schültze tells Berlingske Business that the sector is working around the clock to find a model to ensure that no banks go bankrupt as a result of insufficient liquidity.
