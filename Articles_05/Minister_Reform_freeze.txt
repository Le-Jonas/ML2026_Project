Title: Minister_Reform_freeze
Date: 2009-08-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Finance Minister Claus Hjort Frederiksen, who is due to present the Budget next week, has lowered the flag on any major labour market reforms even before the Labour Market Commission presents its report tomorrow.
The Commission has already unveiled some of its proposals, including shorter unemployment benefit periods, higher early retirement contributions and a review of incapacity benefits.
