Title: Government_hostage_to_two_votes
Date: 2009-03-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Negotiations for a DKK 1.5 billion housing renovation package appear to have stranded in a tug-of-war between the finance minister and the chairman of the two-seat Liberal Alliance.
Finance Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen appears to be losing his patience with the smallest party in parliament, which is holding out on the government’s package unless it gets concessions.
