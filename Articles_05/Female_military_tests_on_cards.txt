Title: Female_military_tests_on_cards
Date: 2009-04-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A majority in Parliament is in favour of introducing pre-draft testing for young women in order to raise interest for female service in the military, according to Jyllands-Posten.
“Written tests, medical tests and an introduction to Danish defence. All girls can tick off the period as they approach their 18th birthday. A majority of parties are looking to expand pre-draft testing to include women,” Jyllands-Posten writes.
