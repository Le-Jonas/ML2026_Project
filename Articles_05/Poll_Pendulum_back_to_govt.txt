Title: Poll_Pendulum_back_to_govt
Date: 2009-06-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 suggests that despite a drop in sympathies for the ruling Liberal Party, the government bloc would regain a majority if an election were held today.
According to the poll, the governing Liberals and Conservatives, with the support of their parliamentary safety net the Danish People’s Party, would garner 51.1 percent of the vote and 90 mandates, while the Social Democrats, Socialist People’s Party, Social Liberals, Unity List and other opposition parties would gain 48.9 percent.
