Title: Løkke&rsquo;s_first_cabinet
Date: 2009-04-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s new Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen has appointed his new cabinet, according to a release from the prime minister’s office.
Former Employment Minister Claus Hjort Frederiksen (Lib) has, as expected, been appointed to the finance portfolio previously held by Løkke Rasmussen.
