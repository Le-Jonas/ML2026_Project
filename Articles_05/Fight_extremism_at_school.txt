Title: Fight_extremism_at_school
Date: 2008-09-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen scoutmasters, coaches, teachers and other educational personnel are to be asked to keep a watchful eye on young people, to determine whether or not they show extremist tendencies, according to Berlingske Tidende.
According to the report, among other proposals, personnel will be able to contact a hotline at a Copenhagen Council Democracy Office to ask for help if they detect extremist tendencies.
