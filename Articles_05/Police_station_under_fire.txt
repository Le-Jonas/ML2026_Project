Title: Police_station_under_fire
Date: 2009-01-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police officers inside the Vejen local police station in Southern Jutland, Denmark were close to becoming victims early Friday morning when three unidentified men shot at patrol cars and into the police station.
"There were two officers inside the station when shots were fired at the windows," says Duty Officer Jesper Hansen of the regional Esbjerg Police.
