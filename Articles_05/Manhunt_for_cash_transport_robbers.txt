Title: Manhunt_for_cash_transport_robbers
Date: 2009-04-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police investigations are under way to find those responsible for the armed robbery of a cash transport in Copenhagen yesterday evening.
It remains unclear how much money was stolen when robbers in two vehicles stopped the cash transporter, forced the driver at gunpoint to lie on the road and stole contents from the van.
