Title: PM_Danger_of_climate_target_collapse
Date: 2008-10-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen (Lib) is concerned that the financial crisis will cause a dilution of the European Union's climate targets.
"I can hear from the debates that I have already been in today that we are going to have to fight to maintain the targets and schedule for the climate agreement.," says Fogh Rasmussen as he prepares for tonight's meetings with his European colleagues.
