Title: Denmark_in_Russian_meat_stop_approach
Date: 2008-09-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Food Minister Eva Kjær Hansen (Lib.) and the Danish Food Standards Agency are to attempt to get Russia to open its doors to Danish meat again.
At the same, the minister is to lead a Danish foodstuff campaign at Russia’s biggest food fair – World Food in Moscow.
