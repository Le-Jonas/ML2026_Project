Title: Minister_called_to_explain_deportation
Date: 2008-10-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party has called on the Minister For Integration Birthe Rønn Hornbech to explain why Denmark has twice attempted to deport a Tamil family, despite a UN decision to annul the deportation.
SPP Immigration Spokesperson Astrid Krag has made the call after disclosures that the European Court of Human Rights also stopped an attempt to deport the family using Rule 39 of the charter.
