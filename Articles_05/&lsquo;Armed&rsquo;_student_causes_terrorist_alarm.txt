Title: &lsquo;Armed&rsquo;_student_causes_terrorist_alarm
Date: 2009-05-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A group of high school students making a film caused a major police alarm at Copenhagen Airport when one of their number was seen holding what was reported to be an automatic weapon at a terminal entrance.
Officers, who arrived at the scene quickly, drew their weapons and ordered the young man to drop his weapon, which he did.
