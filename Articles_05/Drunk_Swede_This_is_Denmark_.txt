Title: Drunk_Swede_This_is_Denmark_
Date: 2008-12-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 35-year-old Swedish motorist was so drunk when he was stopped by police on his way to the northern Zealand town of Hillerød in Denmark, that he thought he was still in his home town - in Sweden.
The man thought that he was driving around in Malmo - across the Oresund waterway.
