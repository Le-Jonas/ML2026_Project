Title: Airport_swine_flu_alarm_called_off
Date: 2009-05-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A passenger on a flight from Newark to Copenhagen became sick this morning with influenza-type symptoms, causing the aircraft with its 250 passengers to be parked in a remote area of the airport until the medical health inspector had seen the man.
A doctor, who was one of the passengers on board the aircraft, said the man did not have a temperature. After a visit from the Copenhagen medical health officer the alarm was been called off.
