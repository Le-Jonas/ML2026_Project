Title: Chat_threat_at_Elsinore_school
Date: 2009-03-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 14-year-old boy has been arrested in Denmark after logging into an international chatroom and threatening to shoot students and teachers at a school in the Northern Zealand town of Elsinore.
Police became aware of the threat after a tip-off from Norwegian police on Friday last week and began a race against the clock to find the chatroom correspondent before school opened on Monday.
