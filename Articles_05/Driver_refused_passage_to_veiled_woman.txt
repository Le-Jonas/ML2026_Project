Title: Driver_refused_passage_to_veiled_woman
Date: 2009-03-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A bus driver on Arriva bus 107 in Århus refused passage to a woman in a veil, saying he would not continue his journey until the woman left the bus, according to Metroxpress.
The woman had entered the bus with her husband and three children. “I’m deeply shocked,” said the husband Mohamed Belgacen.
