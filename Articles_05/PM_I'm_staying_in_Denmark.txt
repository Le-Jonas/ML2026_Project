Title: PM_I'm_staying_in_Denmark
Date: 2009-01-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark's Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen has denied suggestions that he is a candidate for a senior international post - this time following rumours that he could become Secretary-General of NATO, when that post becomes vacant later this year.
"I can only repeat what I have previously said - I am not a candidate for any international posting. I intend to remain prime minister of Denmark as long as the electorate wishes me to," Fogh Rasmussen said.
