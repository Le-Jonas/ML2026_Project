Title: HA_guns_and_drugs_found
Date: 2008-10-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A search of the Hells Angels clubhouse on Lindegreens Allé in Amager has turned up a pumpgun, ammunition and drugs.
“We found some cannabis and another substance which we are investigating,” a police spokesman said adding that the decision to search the clubhouse came after a patrol found ‘joints’ in a car.
