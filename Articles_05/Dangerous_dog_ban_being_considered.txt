Title: Dangerous_dog_ban_being_considered
Date: 2009-08-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Justice Ministry Special Committee on Dogs is considering a total ban on dangerous dog breeds following a series of attacks in recent months.
“We have not specifically been asked to consider a ban on particular breeds, but after having listened to the popular debate, we have decided to raise the issue,” says Committee Chairman Judge Jens Kruse Mikkelsen who declined to comment further.
