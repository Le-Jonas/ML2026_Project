Title: Poll_Government_majority
Date: 2008-11-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government and its support party the Danish People’s Party would maintain its majority in Parliament if an election were held today, according to the latest poll from the Greens Institute carried out for Børsen.
According to the poll, the governing Liberal and Conservative parties and the Danish People’s Party would garner 91 mandates while the opposition Social Democratic, Socialist People’s and Social Liberal parties would receive 84 mandates.
