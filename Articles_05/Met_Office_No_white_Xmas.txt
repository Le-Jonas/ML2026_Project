Title: Met_Office_No_white_Xmas
Date: 2008-12-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The promise of a white Christmas in Denmark has disappeared, according to the Met Office which says that December 24 - the day that Denmark celebrates Christmas - will be overcast, with rain in places and temperatures of around plus five degrees.
Denmark has not had a white Christmas since 1995 - and in fact only had a white Christmas seven times in the past 107 years.
