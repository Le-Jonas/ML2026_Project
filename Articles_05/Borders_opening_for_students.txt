Title: Borders_opening_for_students
Date: 2008-09-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Line Gry and her Ugandan husband were forced to move to Sweden three years ago because they were unable to move to Denmark as a result of Denmark’s strict immigration policy.
Line Gry is now one of some 700 Danish students in Sweden who can expect to move to Denmark with her husband as a result of new guidelines that have been put to the European Commission.
