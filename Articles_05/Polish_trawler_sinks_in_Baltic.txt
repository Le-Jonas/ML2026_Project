Title: Polish_trawler_sinks_in_Baltic
Date: 2009-05-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Five Polish fishermen are missing and presumed dead after their trawler sank near the Danish Baltic island of Bornholm, according to Admiral Danish Fleet Headquarters (SOC) which monitors the region.
A Danish environmental monitoring aircraft localised a lifebuoy some eight kilometers south of the island yesterday afternoon and more wreckage has been seen since then.
