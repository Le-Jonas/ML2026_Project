Title: Mass_arrest_was_unjustified
Date: 2009-02-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The mass arrest of some 80 people at the Faderhuset religious sect’s property in the Copenhagen suburb of Rødovre was unjustified, according to a court ruling.
Some 80 young demonstrators were charged with disturbing the peace in connection with the demonstration, but Ritzau reports the ruling as saying that there was “no basis upon which to suspect any of those present on this charge.”
