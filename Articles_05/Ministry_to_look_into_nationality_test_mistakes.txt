Title: Ministry_to_look_into_nationality_test_mistakes
Date: 2008-12-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

"We'll look into it as soon as possible," says Frederik Gammeltoft, head of department at the Ministry of Refugees, Immigrants and Integration.
Gammeltoft's comment comes following the disclosure of the case of Sharri Junadi, a 19-year-old medical student, who failed a nationality test last week because the answers provided for the examination were wrong. Eight other nationality applicants failed for the same reason.
