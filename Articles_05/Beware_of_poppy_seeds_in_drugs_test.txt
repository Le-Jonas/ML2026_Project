Title: Beware_of_poppy_seeds_in_drugs_test
Date: 2008-09-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Netto supermarket chain unleashed a new product on parental consumers this week – a narcotics test that can be used to see whether their teenagers are on drugs.
But beware: 24timer has tested the product and a simple breakfast of two buns covered in poppy seeds was enough to push the needle from negative to positive.
