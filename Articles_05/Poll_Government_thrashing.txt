Title: Poll_Government_thrashing
Date: 2009-06-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

If an election were held today, the centre-left would take over government, according to the latest poll from the Greens Analyseinstitut for Børsen.
According to the poll, the Social Democrats, Socialist People’s Party, the Social Liberals and the Unity List would garner 92 mandates, while the Liberal-Conservative government with the support of the Danish People’s Party would only receive 83 seats. The Liberal Alliance party would lose its representation in Parliament, according to the poll.
