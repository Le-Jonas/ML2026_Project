Title: Cheaper_Tivoli_for_Swedes
Date: 2009-04-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Swedes will be able to go to the Tivoli Gardens in Copenhagen more cheaply than Danes this season.
Danish adults will have to pay DKK 85 for an entry ticket and DKK 200 for an all-rides ticket while Swedes will be paying the same – but in Swedish kronor. The Swedish currency exchange rate means that Swedes will be paying DKK 60 and DKK 140 for the same offerings.
