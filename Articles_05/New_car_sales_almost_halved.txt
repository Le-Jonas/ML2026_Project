Title: New_car_sales_almost_halved
Date: 2009-05-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Car sales in Denmark have gone into reverse gear with April figures showing 8,800 new car sales – 45 percent less than April 2008 and eight percent less than March 2009, according to Statistics Denmark.
Of the 8,800 vehicles sold in April, most of them were to private individuals.
