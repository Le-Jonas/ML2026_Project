Title: Chairwoman_attacks_sick_bank_culture
Date: 2009-04-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish consumers are having to pay the price for a sick banking culture that has gone on for years, according to Consumer Council Chairwoman Camille Herson in an interview in Jyllands-Posten.
In particular, she says the sale by banks of investment products to normal customers has been shocking.
