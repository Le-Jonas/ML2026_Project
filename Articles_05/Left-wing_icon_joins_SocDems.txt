Title: Left-wing_icon_joins_SocDems
Date: 2008-12-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Pernille Rosenkrantz-Theil, a former MP for the left wing Red-Green Alliance (Enhedslisten), has joined the Social Democrats and may run for parliament at the next election.
In a letter in Politiken today Rosenkrantz-Theil says she went through major deliberations before she decided to join a new party.
