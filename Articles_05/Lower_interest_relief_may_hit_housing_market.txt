Title: Lower_interest_relief_may_hit_housing_market
Date: 2009-02-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

House prices may drop by 3-4% over the next few years if the politicians follow the Tax Commission’s proposal of lowering tax relief on interest, according to the Tax Commission.
“But this will only happen in a couple of years,” says Tax Commission Chairman Carsten Koch adding that the majority of home owners will benefit from the new tax reform.
