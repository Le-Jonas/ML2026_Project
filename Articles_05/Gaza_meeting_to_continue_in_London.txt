Title: Gaza_meeting_to_continue_in_London
Date: 2009-02-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A two-day meeting of international experts meeting in Copenhagen to discuss how to stop weapons smuggling into Gaza, concluded discussions Thursday without reaching any concrete results. Meetings are to continue in London, according to Berlingske Tidende.
The experts from Canada, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Britain and the United States were discussing how countries could help prevent the flow of weaponry to Gaza. Israel was an observer at the meeting.
