Title: Reckless_speeding_kills_baby
Date: 2009-05-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two 28-year-old men have been arrested overnight after driving their vehicle at high speed along Roskildevej in Copenhagen and torpedoing a vehicle that was waiting at lights.
Witnesses to the accident said the vehicle had been travelling at between 150 km/h and 200 km/h in their Volkswagen Polo and had shot at least one red light before ramming a car waiting for a green light.
