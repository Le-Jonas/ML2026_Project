Title: Fewer_Danes_die_of_heart_disease
Date: 2008-09-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

An increasing number of heart disease patients come under treatment, and the quality of treatment and post-treatment care has improved, according to the latest cause of death report from the National Board of Health.
The number of heart disease patients who die as a result of their disease has now fallen for the fifth year in a row.
