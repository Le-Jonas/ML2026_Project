Title: Danes_Finance_crisis,_perhaps
Date: 2008-10-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Seventy percent of Danes believe that Mr. and Mrs. Jensen down the road will suffer as a result of the financial crisis, but 50 percent don’t believe their own finances will suffer, according to a new poll by Vilstrup Market Research for Politiken.
At the same time most have put their money where their mouths are – hardly anyone has changed bank, refinanced loans or generally changed private finance habits. And very few plan to do so.
