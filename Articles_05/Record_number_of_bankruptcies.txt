Title: Record_number_of_bankruptcies
Date: 2009-03-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The number of bankruptcies in Denmark was at 513 in February compared with 470 in January in seasonally adjusted figures, and is thus at a record level, according to Statistics Denmark.
The number of actual bankruptcies was at 468 in February and 443 in January, an increase of 120 percent compared to last year.
