Title: Nordics_most_worried_about_climate
Date: 2008-09-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Polar bears on a melting ice floe may worry the Danes and other Scandinavians, but the rest of Europe is more worried about the Treasury.
According to a survey by Eurobarometer to be published next week, two out of three Danes feel that the battle to improve the climate should be a theme in electioneering up to the European Parliament elections in June next year.
