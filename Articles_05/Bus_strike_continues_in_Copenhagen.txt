Title: Bus_strike_continues_in_Copenhagen
Date: 2009-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Industrial action on several bus routes in Copenhagen continues into its second day today with the following routes out of service:
3A, 4A, 6A, 14, 15, 18, 20E, 29, 40, 66, 150S, 173E, 176, 185, 354 as well as some cancellations on route 123 due to a shortage of drivers.
