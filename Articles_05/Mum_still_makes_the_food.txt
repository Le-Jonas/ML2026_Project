Title: Mum_still_makes_the_food
Date: 2009-07-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Technical University's (DTU) Foodstuff Institute has carried out a survey showing that 85 percent of Danish families eat dinners together at least five days each week - marginally more than a decade ago when the figure was 81 percent.
At the same time the survey shows that Mum is still the main cook in the house - two thirds of households see the mother making the food. Men are beginning, however, to slowly catch up - in 1995 Dad made the food in 11 percent of households, while the latest figure is 16 percent.
