Title: Danmarks_Nationalbank_cuts_interest_rate
Date: 2009-01-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danmarks Nationalbank has cut its lending rate and rate of intrerest on certificates by 0.75 percent to 3.0 percent with effect from January 16.
The discount rate and interest rate on banks' currrent accounts with the centrral bank are also lowered by 0.75 per cent to 2.75 percent, the bank said.
