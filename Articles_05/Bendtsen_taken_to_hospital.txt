Title: Bendtsen_taken_to_hospital
Date: 2009-03-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The former Conservative Party Leader and Deputy Prime Minister Bendt Bendtsen has been taken to hospital after feeling unwell at a party meeting in Parliament.
According to the reports, 55-year-old Bendtsen had pains in his chest and was initially treated in Parliament by an emergency doctor before being taken to hospital in an ambulance.
