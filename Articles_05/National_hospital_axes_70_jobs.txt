Title: National_hospital_axes_70_jobs
Date: 2008-09-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The national university hospital Rigshospitalet must save DKK 51 million next year and will have to axe some 70 jobs, according to a Hospital Board proposal that has been roundly criticized by employees.
Affecting patients 
Nursing staff are primarily likely to be affected, but the positions of some doctors and physiotherapists are also expected to be cut. Union Convener Lis Heidemann, who is also deputy chair of the main Cooperating Committee at the hospital, says the cuts will affect patients and the level of professionalism.
