Title: Coke_near_Lemvig
Date: 2008-10-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Yet another bag of cocaine has been found near the Bovbjerg Fyr lighthouse close to Lemvig, the third time in two days that bags of cocaine have washed ashore.
The first bag, believed to contain one kilogramme of the drug, was discovered on Monday by German tourists.
