Title: Music_pirate_fined
Date: 2008-10-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Western High Court has increased the fine imposed on a man from North Jutland for having given away free music on the internet using a file sharing system.
According to the plaintiffs, the man had attempted to hide his identity on the internet, but the High Court determined that he was indeed the person behind the internet address.
