Title: Police_stamp_down_on_gang_warfare
Date: 2009-02-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Copenhagen police is to stamp down hard on the current gang warfare between immigrant gangs and bikers, which has resulted in shootings, killings and knifings in the capital.
Innocent citizens have also been targeted by mistake and in some areas have had to accept gangs frisking them to find out whether they belong to one group or the other.
