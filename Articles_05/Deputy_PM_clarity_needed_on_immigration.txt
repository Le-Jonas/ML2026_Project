Title: Deputy_PM_clarity_needed_on_immigration
Date: 2008-09-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Conservative Party’s new leader Lene Espersen, who takes over the party after Bendt Bendtsen announced his withdrawal today, says that the first issue that she will be addressing as party leader will be that of immigration policy.
Espersen was unanimously chosen by her parliamentary colleagues to take over from Bendtsen.
