Title: Poll_Welfare_State_tattered
Date: 2008-09-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Worn down schools, crumbling old people’s homes and pockmarked roads. Two-thirds of the Danes feel that the standards and maintenance of schools, pre-schools, retirement homes and roads have got progressively worse over the past five years.
According to a new poll carried out by Capacent Epinion for Urban, only six percent of those asked felt that conditions have improved or improved greatly.
