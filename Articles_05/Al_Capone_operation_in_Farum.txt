Title: Al_Capone_operation_in_Farum
Date: 2009-03-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A major police and tax authority operation in the Copenhagen suburb of Farum resulted in the confiscation of five cars, some cannabis, hand striking weapons and 800 kgs of sweets, according to Copenhagen police, who used 80 officers in the sting operation.
“This is the Al Capone strategy in which we try to hit gang members’ wallets,” says Copenhagen Police Spokesman Flemming Steen Munch.
