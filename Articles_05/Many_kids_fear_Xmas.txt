Title: Many_kids_fear_Xmas
Date: 2008-12-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A website funded by the National Board of Health and the Education Ministry is to help the 60,000 children that the Board estimates live in families in which parents drink too much.
While most children look forward to the Christmas period, these vulnerable children live in fear that the holiday period will end up in drunkenness and rows.
