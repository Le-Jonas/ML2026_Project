Title: Doctors_restrain_illegally_167_times
Date: 2009-05-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest statistics from the Patients Complaints Boards show that illegal force was used on psychiatric patients in some 167 cases in 2008.
The issue of using restraints, and how patients are committed, has been debated in Denmark in recent months with the latest figures suggesting that doctors do not always have the law on their side when restraining patients in belts and using other forms of forcible restraint.
