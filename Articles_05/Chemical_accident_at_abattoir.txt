Title: Chemical_accident_at_abattoir
Date: 2008-09-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Ten people have been taken to hospital following a chemical accident at Århus Slagtehus (Århus abattoir) in Århus harbour.
An employee mistakenly mixed two cleaning agents, after which a corrosive chlorine vapour developed. One employee developed serious breathing difficulties.
