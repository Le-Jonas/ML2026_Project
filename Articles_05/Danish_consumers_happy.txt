Title: Danish_consumers_happy
Date: 2009-01-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest figures from Statistics Denmark show increasing consumer confidence in Denmark. Although still in negative territory, indicators for consumer confidence have risen from minus 13.5 in December to minus 9.1 in January - the highest confidence rate since June 2008.
A Reuters survey of analysts had otherwise shown that experts believed consumer confidence would remain at minus 13.5.
