Title: Barroso_Løkke_condemn_N.Korea
Date: 2009-05-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Both the European Commission President Jose Manuel Barroso and the Danish Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen have condemned North Korea’s latest nuclear test.
“It is important that the world sends a clear message to North Korea,” Barroso said during a joint news conference in Copenhagen with Løkke Rasmussen.
