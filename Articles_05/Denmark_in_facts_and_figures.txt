Title: Denmark_in_facts_and_figures
Date: 2009-06-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Statistics Denmark has just published its Statistical Yearbook 2009 with graphs and figures about Denmark and the Danes.
The 577-page report provides a compendium of facts and figures about how old they get, where they live, how long education takes and how much they earn. Or how often Danes went to the cinema, theatre or a museum!
