Title: Cars_up_in_flames_overnight
Date: 2008-09-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

From midnight to 3 a.m. this morning, mopeds raced up and down the pathway systems around Ishøj Bycenter, setting light to six cars. Three trucks parked at Gibroskolen were also set alight.
Although police have no direct clues, they do have a good idea as to who was responsible.
