Title: Pilots_agree_to_wage_reduction
Date: 2008-12-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

SAS management and representatives for the company's pilots appear to have reached agreement on reducing pilots's salaries in order to carry SAS through the financial doldrums.
"All personnel groups have been negotiating with their managements over the past month and a half. We (Ed: pilots) have concluded our negotiations and we are looking at reduced salaries," says Pilot Representative Mogens Holgaard.
