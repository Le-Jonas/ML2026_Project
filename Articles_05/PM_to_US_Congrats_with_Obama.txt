Title: PM_to_US_Congrats_with_Obama
Date: 2009-01-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark's Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen has sent his warm congratulations to the United States as it inaugurates President Obama today as its 44th president.
"This is an historic day. I would like to congratulate both President Barack Obama and the American people," Anders Fogh Rasmussen said in his weekly news conference.
