Title: Expert_Christiania_weak
Date: 2008-10-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The future for Christiania looks grim following the rejection yesterday by the government of a proposal from Christiania about the area's future, according to Claus Haagen Jensen, a professor in Public Administration at Aalborg University.
"I find it difficult to see a basis in law that will enable Christiania to win a case," Haagen Jensen says.
