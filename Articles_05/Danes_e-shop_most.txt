Title: Danes_e-shop_most
Date: 2009-06-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In Europe, the Danes are the most enthusiastic e-shoppers followed by the UK, Holland, Germany and Sweden, according to the most recent figures from Eurostat.
Overall, 86 percent of Danes have access to the Internet at home and 72 percent use it every day or nearly every day.
