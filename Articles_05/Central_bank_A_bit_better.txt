Title: Central_bank_A_bit_better
Date: 2009-06-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Nationalbank’s latest quarterly report says the patient is still sick, but a little better.
“The world economy is still in recession, but there are signs that the downturn is slowing. If this development continues, the international organisations say that production will increase towards the end of the year,” the central bank says.
