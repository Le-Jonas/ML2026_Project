Title: Arla_to_lay_off_employees
Date: 2009-04-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One of Europe’s largest dairy food companies is expected to announce layoffs of a large number of administrative employees, according to media reports.
No comment 
According to a report on DR-Sjælland, an announcement is expected later today and is expected to involve between 250 and 300 administrative employees in Denmark and abroad.
