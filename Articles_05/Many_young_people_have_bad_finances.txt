Title: Many_young_people_have_bad_finances
Date: 2008-10-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One in four 18-25 year-olds have an overdraft at the end of each month, have bought their bicycle on the never-never and financed clothes using expensive credit cards, according to a survey by the Money and Pension Panel.
Keeping together 
"25 percent of young people have a behaviour that means that they do not get as much out of their money as they could," Panel Chairman Professor Torben Hansen of CBS says.
