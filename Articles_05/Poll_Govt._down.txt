Title: Poll_Govt._down
Date: 2008-10-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The opposition is gaining ground and the current Lib-Con coalition, and its parliamentary support party would lose an election were one to be held now, according to the latest poll from Catinét Research for Ritzau.
Published at the weekend, the poll shows that the Liberal-Conservative-Danish People's Party bloc would garner 87 seats in parliament, while the opposition Social Democratic, Socialist People's, Social Liberal and Unity List parties would take 88 seats.
