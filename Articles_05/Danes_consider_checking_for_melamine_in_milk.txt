Title: Danes_consider_checking_for_melamine_in_milk
Date: 2008-10-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish dairy sector is possibly to begin checking Danish milk products for melamine due to its export markets.
"When some calm has returned to the situation, the Danish dairy sector will probably sit down and discuss whether it is relevant to check for melamine in Danish milk," says Christian Bruun Kastrup of the Dairy Association's Department for Quality and Food Safety.
