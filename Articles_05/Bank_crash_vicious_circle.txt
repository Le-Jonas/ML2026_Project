Title: Bank_crash_vicious_circle
Date: 2008-09-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The crisis-hit American investment bank Lehman Brothers, until recently America’s fourth-largest investment bank is to file for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection, one of several types of bankruptcy proceedings in the United States ranging from liquidation to repayment or reorganisation.
“Banks have been too aggressive in their risk-taking,” says Professor Per H. Hansen of the Copenhagen Business School.
