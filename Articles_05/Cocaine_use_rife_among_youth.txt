Title: Cocaine_use_rife_among_youth
Date: 2008-11-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Over the past eight years, the number of young people who have taken cocaine in the preceding month has doubled to just under six percent of the under-25’s.
According to the National Board of Health’s ‘Narcotics situation in Denmark’ report which is published today, the increased use of cocaine is also proving to be a weight on the health system, with three times as many cocaine admissions as nine years ago.
