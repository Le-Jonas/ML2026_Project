Title: Christiania_demo_under_way
Date: 2008-10-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A demonstration is currently under way from Christiania to the offices of the Palaces and Properties Agency to complain about the demolition of part of a house in Christiania.
There are some 2-300 people in the demonstration, carrying banners reading: "They're demolishing us. Help", and "Keep Christiania".
