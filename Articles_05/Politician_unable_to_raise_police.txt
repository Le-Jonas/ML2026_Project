Title: Politician_unable_to_raise_police
Date: 2009-01-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A local politician in central Zealand found getting in touch with the police to be rather more difficult than he had envisaged, according to Sjællandske.
Ole Bjørn Skausig wanted to report a shooting incident involving protected swans that had landed in a field near Gørlev.
