Title: Politicians_want_shorter_student_jobs
Date: 2008-12-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Oliver Sellner Vonsild is 24 years old, is reading political science at university and also works 15 hours per week at the Foreign Ministry in Copenhagen.
"The fact is that I am perfectly willing to stick around for some extra hours when it's busy," he says adding that his three year bachelor's degree will take four years.
