Title: Immunity_for_gun_hand-ins
Date: 2009-05-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Justice Minister Brian Mikkelsen is planning to introduce a period of safe conduct for those who hand in illegal guns, according to a report in Jyllands-Posten.
The proposal has been recommended by the Copenhagen police force in order to attempt to limit the number of weapons in circulation.
