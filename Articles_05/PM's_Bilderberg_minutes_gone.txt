Title: PM's_Bilderberg_minutes_gone
Date: 2008-11-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There is some controversy surrounding Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen's minutes from a Bilderberg Club meeting in 2003, during which many of the world's influential men and women met in lodge-type secrecy to privately discuss solutions to issues of the moment.
There are Bilderberg Club meetings each year, but not necessarily with the same participants.
