Title: Ernst_&amp;_Young_partner_pay_-_minus_80%
Date: 2009-04-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Partners in Denmark’s fourth largest accounting company Ernst & Young have agreed to a reduction in salary of up to 80 percent as a result of falling earnings, according to secret internal papers reported in Jyllands-Posten.
Ernst & Young is a global leader in assurance, tax, transaction and advisory services.
