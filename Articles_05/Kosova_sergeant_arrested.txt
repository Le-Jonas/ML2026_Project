Title: Kosova_sergeant_arrested
Date: 2009-02-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 36-year-old Danish sergeant has been arrested in Kosova in connection with a major weapons theft from the Antvorskov Barracks in Denmark on January 4, according to the Danish Security and Intelligence Service (PET).
The man is suspected of having taken part in the robbery, was arrested this morning and is to be repatriated to Denmark.
