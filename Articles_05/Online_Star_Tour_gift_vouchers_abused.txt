Title: Online_Star_Tour_gift_vouchers_abused
Date: 2009-02-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Star Tour Sales Director Stig Elling is warning the public against buying cheap gift vouchers for its charter business online, apparently as a result of widespread fraud.
The vouchers, with a nominal value of DKK 10,000 are being sold for DKK 6,000 to DKK 8,000 online, and a lot of people appear to have been tempted by the offers. Unfortunately, however, the vouchers have been purchased using stolen credit cards, and are therefore invalid.
