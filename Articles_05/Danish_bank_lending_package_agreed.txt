Title: Danish_bank_lending_package_agreed
Date: 2009-01-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

All Danish parties apart from the Unity List finally agreed late Sunday night on a so-alled Bank Package II to provide lending facilities for banks and mortgage institutions to the tune of DKK 100 billion.
The package, which has been negotiated for some time, determines that all solvent financial institutions will be able to borrow funds from the state at 10 percent interest, in order to secure their solvency.
