Title: Danske_Bank_disappoints
Date: 2008-10-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danske Bank has been hard hit by the finance crisis with its latest quarterly report showing earnings of 1.1 billion kroner compared to a forecast of 1.7 billion.
Loans 
Among other things the bank's loans have affected results. The bank has had to write down 2.9 billion kroner compared with 260 million kroner last year and the finance rescue package agreed between the government and the banks has also been an expensive experience.
