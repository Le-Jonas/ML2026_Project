Title: Police_offered_to_cover_up
Date: 2008-11-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police officers were willing to cover up an incident in 1997 when the then Leader of the Conservative Party Hans Engell drove his car into a cement block in a motorway drink-drive accident.
The disclosures in B.T. come in connection with Hans Engell’s new memoirs ‘Goodbye to Slotsholmen’ which is to be released next week.
