Title: Iraqi_interpreters_going_home
Date: 2008-09-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The first group of Iraqi interpreters who were evacuated to Denmark with their families last year, returned home at the beginning of August. Another group is now planning to follow suit as members see no future for themselves in Denmark.
According to the Danish Refugee Council, some five interpreters and their families – a total of some 20 people, are planning to return to Iraq.
