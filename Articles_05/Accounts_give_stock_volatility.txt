Title: Accounts_give_stock_volatility
Date: 2008-10-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The coming days and weeks will be giving investors something to think about as they navigate the plethora of imponderables just around the corner, according to Nordea’s Senior Strategist Henrik Druseberg.
Among other things, a large number of companies will be publishing their accounts in coming weeks – and these can contain both surprises and disappointments. As a result, markets – and not least the Copenhagen exchange - are difficult to gauge.
