Title: Naked_knife-man_bulldozes_bus
Date: 2009-06-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Employees of an engineering company in the town of Næstved south of Copenhagen dropped compasses and pencils in surprise when a 20-year-old man appeared stark naked and took a walk around their office.
Surprise turned to discomfort when the young man grabbed a breadknife from the kitchen, and continued his walk through the company.
