Title: Finance_expert_Take_it_easy
Date: 2008-09-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Kim Valentin, a financial and personal finance expert says Danes shouldn’t panic in connection with the current financial crisis, but on the other hand he has some good advice for those with stock.
Wait 
Shareholders who have no need to realise their assets, and who have spread their investments well, can sit back and wait for the markets to improve.
