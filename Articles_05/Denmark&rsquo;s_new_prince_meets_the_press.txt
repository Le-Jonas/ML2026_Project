Title: Denmark&rsquo;s_new_prince_meets_the_press
Date: 2009-05-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Princess Marie and Prince Joachim left the national teaching hospital Rigshospitalet today carrying Denmark’s new prince out to meet the media for the first time.
Swathed in a blanket, the young prince, who was born early on May 4, was fast asleep in the arms of his French-born mother.
