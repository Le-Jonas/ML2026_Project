Title: Major_bank_Hard_times_ahead
Date: 2008-09-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The risk that the brakes are going to jam hard on the Danish economy is increasing, according to Nordea economists in a new forecast.
“Basically the Danish economy is looking healthy and without major balance of payments problems. That allows finance policy to stabilize developments if the decline becomes unexpectedly harsh,” the economists say in their report.
