Title: Dec._industrial_production_stabilised
Date: 2009-02-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish industrial production fell from November to December 2008 by 0.1 percent according to Statistics Denmark’s latest figures. The drop is a distinct recovery compared with November, which returned a drop of 5.6 percent.
Danske Bank Chief Economist Steen Bocian says he is positively surprised.
