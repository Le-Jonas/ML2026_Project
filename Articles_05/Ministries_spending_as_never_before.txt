Title: Ministries_spending_as_never_before
Date: 2009-07-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Ministerial costs for food, drink and gifts rose some 15 percent in 2008, with ministries spending an average DKK 200,000 each day, according to a survey by A4.
Overall, entertainment costs have risen in 11 of the 18 ministries surveyed, rising from DKK 36.9 million in 2007 to DKK 42.3 million in 2008. While entertainment costs thus rose some 15 percent in a year, overall state costs only rose 3.6 percent.
