Title: Prabhakaran&rsquo;s_family_still_uncertain
Date: 2009-05-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There may be no doubt in the minds of the international media that the Tamil Tiger Leader Velupillai Prabhakaran is dead – but contradictory sms’s and phone calls continue to flood into his family in Denmark.
Although the Tamil Tiger spokesman Selvarasa Pathmanathan said last week that Prabhakaran had been killed, the family remains unsure of his fate due to messages from Tamil groups.
