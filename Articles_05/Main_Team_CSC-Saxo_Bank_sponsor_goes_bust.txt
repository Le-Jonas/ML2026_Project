Title: Main_Team_CSC-Saxo_Bank_sponsor_goes_bust
Date: 2008-12-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The IT Factory, which was supposed to have been Denmark's cycle sport icon Bjarne Riis' main sponsor for his CSC-Saxo Bank team as of January 1, has been declared bankrupt. The company chairman Asger Jensby has confirmed the bankruptcy.
According to TV2, the company's CEO Stein Bagger is being sought by Interpol, allegedly according to several media reports for fraud, after disappearing following a vacation in Dubai with his wife.
