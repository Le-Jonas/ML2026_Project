Title: Bosses_to_keep_hands_off_e-mail
Date: 2008-09-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Commercial and Clerical Employees Union (HK) and the Danish Chamber of Commerce (Dansk Erhverv) have agreed on a new set of guidelines regarding the use of e-mails and the Internet in the workplace.
The new agreement is designed to increase trust between employers and employees in the use of the Internet, e-mails and mobile telephony.
