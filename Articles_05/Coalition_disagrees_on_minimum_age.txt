Title: Coalition_disagrees_on_minimum_age
Date: 2009-04-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The junior coalition Conservative Party says that there is no reason to axe the 15-year minimum age in order to prevent youth crime, as sanctions are already available.
The senior coalition Liberal Party’s Justice Spokesman Kim Andersen has proposed removing the minimum age at which young people can be sentenced for crimes.
