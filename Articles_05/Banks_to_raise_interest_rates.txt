Title: Banks_to_raise_interest_rates
Date: 2008-09-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Both Nordea Bank and Danske Bank are raising their interest rates. Jyske Bank has already announced it is to raise its rates as of next week.
Both Nordea and Danske Bank have increased their lending interest rate by 0.5 percentage points, although not on a mortgage loan where rates remain the same.
