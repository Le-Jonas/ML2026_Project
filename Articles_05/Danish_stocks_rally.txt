Title: Danish_stocks_rally
Date: 2008-10-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish stock market experienced a long-awaited rally after a week that has shown the worst drops in the C-20’s history.
Mærsk, Carlsberg, Danske Bank and D/S Norden won the day, and the C-20 index rose overall 2.13 percent.
