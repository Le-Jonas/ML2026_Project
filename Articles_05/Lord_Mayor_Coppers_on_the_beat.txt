Title: Lord_Mayor_Coppers_on_the_beat
Date: 2008-11-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The shooting episodes of the past few months in the Nørrebro district of Copenhagen have prompted Lord Mayor Ritt Bjerregard (SocDem) to call for more local policemen on the beat.
Most recently two young men were shot and wounded as they sat in their parked car at abandoned railway sidings in Outer Nørrebro.
