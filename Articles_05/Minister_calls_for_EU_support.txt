Title: Minister_calls_for_EU_support
Date: 2008-10-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Piracy is a major problem, but is also difficult to stop since it is difficult to bring pirates to court, according to Defence Minister Søren Gade (Lib.) who meets his EU colleagues over the next couple of days.
Gade will be seeking EU support for an appeal to the United Nations to establish an international piracy court.
