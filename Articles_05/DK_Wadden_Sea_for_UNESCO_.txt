Title: DK_Wadden_Sea_for_UNESCO_
Date: 2009-06-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The German and Dutch parts of the Wadden Sea have been inscribed onto UNESCO's World Heritage List during the current UNESCO meeting in Seville, but the Danish section of the sea remains outside the list area.
"The committee has strongly urged Denmark to follow in the footsteps of the Netherlands and Germany as soon as possible," says Heritage Agency of Denmark Deputy Director Anne Mette Rahbæk, who is Denmark's representative at the UNESCO meeting.
