Title: Court_rejects_evidence_in_terrorism_case
Date: 2008-09-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Eastern High Court of Appeal has rejected evidence provided by Colombia as the material may have been elicited using torture.
The case involves allegations that the Fighters+Lovers organization has supported terrorism in selling T-shirts, profits from which were destined for the Colombian FARC rebel organization.
