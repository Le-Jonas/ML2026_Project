Title: Onshore_oil_prospecting_in_Denmark
Date: 2009-03-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

This autumn, some 20-30 men from the Denver, Colorado GMT Exploration oil prospecting company are to spend five weeks looking for onshore oil in central Jutland.
“These men have extensive experience and a good feeling for where there is oil,” Hans Koch, the Danish spokesman for the group, tells DR.
