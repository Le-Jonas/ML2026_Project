Title: Iraqi_asylum-seekers_soon_to_be_sent_home
Date: 2008-09-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark and Iraq are close to finalising a repatriation agreement for rejected asylum-seekers, with Denmark’s ambassador to Baghdad suggesting the agreement could be ready by next week.
According to a report in Jyllands-Posten, 323 Iraqi asylum-seekers whose applications have been rejected are expected to be involved.
