Title: Maroons_injure_Outgames_person
Date: 2009-07-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One person has been reported injured this afternoon during the World Outgames festival that is taking place across the Danish capital this week.
The incident took place when several maroon fireworks were lobbed at participants. It is unclear what type of maroons were used, some of which are extremely dangerous at close range.
