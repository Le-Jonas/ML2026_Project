Title: Danish_bourse_tumbles_on_opening
Date: 2008-10-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After yesterday’s red territory across the board on the European stock exchanges, forecasts for today’s trading seem no less gloomy. On opening, the Copenhagen benchmark C-20 index dropped almost six percent.
It has since recovered slightly and is at time of writing at minus 2.4 percent.
