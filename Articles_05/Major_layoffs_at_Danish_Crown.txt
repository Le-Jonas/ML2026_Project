Title: Major_layoffs_at_Danish_Crown
Date: 2009-02-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The economic crisis and other factors have apparently hit Danish Crown hard, with some 900 jobs to be axed after the company’s Pig Production Board Committee has proposed the closure of some of the group’s production facilities.
“The facilities affected are the slaughterhouse in Holstebro and the cutting department in Rødding. The closures will affect 463 DC employees in Holstebro and 308 DC employees in Rødding. Furthermore, 90 employees will be affected at the shoulder deboning department in Esbjerg,” the company says.
