Title: Nationalbanken_rate_unchanged
Date: 2008-11-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Economists and analysts had expected the Danish interest rate to be reduced again, but the Danish central bank Danmarks Nationalbank has chosen to maintain its rate at five percent, 1.75 percent above the euro-rate.
Both Danske Bank and Nordea bank had expected interest rates to fall today, predominantly because the Danish krone-euro rate has been stable over the past couple of weeks.
