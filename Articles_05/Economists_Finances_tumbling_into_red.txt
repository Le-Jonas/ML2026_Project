Title: Economists_Finances_tumbling_into_red
Date: 2008-11-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

According to a new survey by economists at Danske Bank, Denmark is facing its first public finance deficit for 11 years.
Instead of the seven billion kroner surplus for 2009 that the government forecast last month, the economists foresee a deficit of nine billion kroner.
