Title: Emergency_swine_flu_meeting
Date: 2009-04-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

An outbreak of the swine flu virus in Mexico has caused the Danish Board of Health to call an emergency meeting today to determine whether the country is prepared to handle an outbreak of the illness in the country if one should take place.
“We will determine the status of our preparedness,” Else Smith of the Board of Health tells TV2.
