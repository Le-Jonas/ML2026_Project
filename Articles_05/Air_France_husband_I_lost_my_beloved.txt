Title: Air_France_husband_I_lost_my_beloved
Date: 2009-06-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A single Danish national was one of 228 people on board Air France flight AF 447 from Rio de Janeiro to Paris when it crashed into the Atlantic Ocean.
Chistiane Zeuthen, who lived in Genner in Southern Jutland, was in Brazil to conclude her studies as a shock and trauma therapist.
