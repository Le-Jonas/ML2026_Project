Title: Poll_SocDems_weak
Date: 2009-07-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest poll on voter support for the government and opposition leadership teams gives only lukewarm support to the Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning Schmidt and her team.
According to the Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 only 21 percent of the electorate finds the Social Democratic team to be a strong one, while 42 percent says it is weak. In comparison, 55 percent says the government team under Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen is strong and 16 percent says it is weak.
