Title: Govt._Work_not_play
Date: 2008-10-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

According to Ritzau, the government wants to make it possible for Danes to give up their fifth week of vacation and work instead, paying them both holiday money and wages.
SDP not unwilling 
The report suggests that the work-instead of-holiday proposal is one of the ideas being put forward by the Finance Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen during the labour market reform package of the 2009 budget.
