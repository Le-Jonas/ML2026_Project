Title: Anti-gang_package_announced
Date: 2009-03-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Justice Minister Brian Mikkelsen has presented the government’s proposals to curb the ongoing gang war, which has resulted in a spate of shootings in the capital Copenhagen.
“The current situation is unacceptable,” Mikkelsen says in presenting a package which doubles the sentence on crimes such as violence, murder and weapons possession if they are gang-related.
