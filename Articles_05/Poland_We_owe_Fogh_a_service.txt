Title: Poland_We_owe_Fogh_a_service
Date: 2009-03-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Poland’s former Prime Minister Leszek Miller says that Anders Fogh Rasmussen is the best candidate for the job as NATO secretary-general, and that Poland should now support him.
Apart from Fogh Rasmussen’s qualities, Miller says that Poland should show its gratitude for the fact that Anders Fogh Rasmussen helped his country to become a member of the European Union.
