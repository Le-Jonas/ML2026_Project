Title: Danish_customers_hit_by_Madoff
Date: 2008-12-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Customers at Denmark's two largest banks are tangled up in the Madoff scandal in the United States - according to Nordea and Danske Bank. The banks themselves have not been involved.
Danske Bank says that some of its private banking customers - that is customers with particularly large fortunes - have capital at risk. These are funds to the tune of some 10 million euro that some 600 investors have placed with the Danske Leveraged Funds Balance investment fund.
