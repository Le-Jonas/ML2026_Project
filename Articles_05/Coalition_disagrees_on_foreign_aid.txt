Title: Coalition_disagrees_on_foreign_aid
Date: 2009-03-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s two governing parties are on course for a collision over how and where to allot Denmark’s foreign aid, according to Jyllands-Posten.
Foreign Minister Per Stig Møller (Cons.) has made it clear that proposals from the Liberal Party Foreign Aid Spokesman Karsten Lauritzen to concentrate all Danish aid on Africa is a non-starter.
