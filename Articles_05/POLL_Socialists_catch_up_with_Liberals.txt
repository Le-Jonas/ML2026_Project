Title: POLL_Socialists_catch_up_with_Liberals
Date: 2009-03-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party would get as many votes as the governing Liberal Party if an election were held today according to the latest Greens poll in Børsen.
According to the poll, 21.7 percent of voters would vote for the Socialist People’s Party and 21.8 percent would vote for the prime minister’s Liberal Party, which returns its worst result for 15 years.
