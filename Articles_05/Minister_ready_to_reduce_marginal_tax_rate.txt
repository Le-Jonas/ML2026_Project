Title: Minister_ready_to_reduce_marginal_tax_rate
Date: 2008-12-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Finance Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen (Lib) is ready to provide “significant” tax relief for the 940,000 Danes who currently pay the marginal tax rate.
The proposal is a change of course for the Liberal Party, putting it in line with the policies of the junior coalition Conservative Party.
