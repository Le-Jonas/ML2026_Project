Title: Minister_no_housing_for_radicals
Date: 2008-10-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A new bill from the Welfare Minister is on its way designed to prevent parallel societies from developing on vulnerable housing estates.
Welfare Minister Karen Jespersen plans to put forward her proposals in the spring, in a bill designed to provide housing associations with concrete tools with which to choose or reject tenants.
