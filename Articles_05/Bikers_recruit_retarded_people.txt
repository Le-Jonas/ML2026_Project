Title: Bikers_recruit_retarded_people
Date: 2009-05-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Wardens at Danish treatment centres say that biker gangs and criminal groups recruit retarded people and use them as henchmen in serious crime, according to a report in Ekstra Bladet.
The Hells Angels Spokesman Jørn Jønke Nielsen denies that his organisation recruits criminal retarded people.
