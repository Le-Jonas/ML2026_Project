Title: Colombian_judge_grilled_in_court
Date: 2008-09-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The appeal case against members of the Fighters+Lovers organisation took an unexpected turn today when the public prosecutor did an about-turn and decided to subject a Colombian judge to cross examination.
Yesterday, Prosecutor Charlotte Alsing Juul decided against presenting Professor Eduardo Montealegre Lynett to the court, after judges had decided to ban evidence from 13 Colombian cases as being compromised due to the possible use of torture.
