Title: Cold_weekend_&ndash;_dry_and_sunny_Skt._Hans
Date: 2009-06-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Next week promises to bring back summer, with higher temperatures and sunny days – but Denmark is going to have to get through an unsettled weekend first, according to the Meteorological Office.
“Showers and windy tomorrow. A little better on Sunday – but we’ll still be getting scattered showers. It should keep dry in most places, but temperatures are unlikely to hit 20 degrees,” says Met Office Duty Officer Bill Vejdegren, who also forecasts thunder during Friday.
