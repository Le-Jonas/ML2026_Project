Title: Bagger_My_life_was_threatened
Date: 2008-12-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In his first interview since his detention in the United States, the CEO of the bankrupt IT Factory Stein Bagger says he has lived through a personal hell for the past four years.
"I am happy that it's all come out, and when I get home, I intend to cooperate with the police, put everything on the table. I am happy that the nightmare is over," says Bagger in an interview with Ekstra Bladet.
