Title: Ministerial_papers_appear
Date: 2009-07-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Documents pertaining to Health Ministry decisions regarding payment to private hospitals for the treatment of national health patients have been found, according to a letter from the Health Ministry to Politiken.
The Ministry says that apart from two documents, the decision-making process on the subject during Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen's time as health minister, has now been fully reconstructed.
