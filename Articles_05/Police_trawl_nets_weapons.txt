Title: Police_trawl_nets_weapons
Date: 2008-09-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A police dragnet to find weapons and those responsible for the many shootings in Copenhagen streets in recent weeks, has given results. Several weapons involved in the shootings have been found.
“We have found weapons in our searches that we know with certainty have been used in some of the episodes,” says the head of Copenhagen Police Homicide Squad Ove Dahl.
