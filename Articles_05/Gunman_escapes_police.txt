Title: Gunman_escapes_police
Date: 2008-10-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A gunman, whom police believed had esconced himself in the Danish Employers' Association building in central Copenhagen, is still at large and was not in the building.
"He's not in there. We have closed down now - he must have been able to get away somehow," says Duty Officer Steen Jensen.
