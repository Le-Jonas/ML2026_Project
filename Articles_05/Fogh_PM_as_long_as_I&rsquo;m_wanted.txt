Title: Fogh_PM_as_long_as_I&rsquo;m_wanted
Date: 2009-02-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen has declined to comment on a report that a high-ranking source in NATO told a Canadian news agency that he can have the job as NATO’s secretary general if he wants it.
Anders Fogh Rasmussen (Lib) says there is nothing new and that the public already knows his answer.
