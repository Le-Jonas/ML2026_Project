Title: Morning_traffic_hit_by_high_winds
Date: 2008-11-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Caravans and other vehicles susceptible to high winds and wind sheer should stay off the Øresund and Storbælt bridges this morning due to heavy winds that are sweeping across Denmark.
The bridges are expected to be re-opened to lighter vehicles early this afternoon.
