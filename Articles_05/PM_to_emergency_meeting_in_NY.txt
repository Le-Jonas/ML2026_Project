Title: PM_to_emergency_meeting_in_NY
Date: 2008-09-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen is currently in New York for the United Nations General Assembly, and is to take part in an emergency meeting on the global financial crisis.
The British Prime Minister Gordon Brown, the European Commission Chairman José Manuel Barroso and other government leaders will also be taking part.
