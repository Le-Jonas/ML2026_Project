Title: Five_cleared_of_swine_flu
Date: 2009-04-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Five of seven patients admitted to Hvidovre Hospital in Copenhagen do not have swine flu with the hospital awaiting results from a further two patients.
Since Monday, when most of the seven were under observation for swine flu, another young woman, who arrived in Denmark from New York, is undergoing tests.
