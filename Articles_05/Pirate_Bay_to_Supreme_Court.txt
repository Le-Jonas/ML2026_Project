Title: Pirate_Bay_to_Supreme_Court
Date: 2009-04-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Appeals Permission Board has accepted a petition from Telenor to appeal to the Supreme Court against a lower court decision ordering one of Telenor's subsidiaries to block access to thepiratebay.org.
“We are satisfied with the decision as this is an important case and a matter of principle. We look forward to the Supreme Court hearing, which will provide the clarity in the case that we are all looking for,” says Telenor Danmark Regulatory Director Nicholai Kramer Pfeiffer.
