Title: Most_Danes_want_SP_savings_back
Date: 2009-06-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest survey shows 83 percent of Danes with a Special Pension account want to avail themselves of the government’s offer to allow them to withdraw their funds – a much larger number than the government had envisaged.
By Wednesday of this week, some 54 percent had already applied to the ATP Group, which administers the Special Pension Fund, to have their savings paid out.
