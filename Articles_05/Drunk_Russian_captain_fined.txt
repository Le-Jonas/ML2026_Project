Title: Drunk_Russian_captain_fined
Date: 2009-03-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Russian captain and first officer of a container vessel who were found to be drunk when their ship ran aground west of Saltholm are to be fined, but will not suffer any further sanctions.
As a result, Danish police released the two men today as tugs attempted to re-float the Karin Schepers.
