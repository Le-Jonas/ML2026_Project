Title: Anno_horribilis_for_Danske_Bank
Date: 2009-02-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danske Bank returned a result after tax of a mere one billion kroner in 2008, compared with 14.9 billion kroner in 2007. The result was below expectations.
2008 saw major losses as a result of the Lehman Brothers bankruptcy as well as the acquisition of National Irish Bank.
