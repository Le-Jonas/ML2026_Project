Title: EU_Commission_suspects_DSB
Date: 2008-09-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The European Commission appears to suspect that Denmark has provided illegal subsidies to the national railway service DSB and is investigating two contracts between DSB and the Ministry of Transport.
“The two public service contracts regarding passenger traffic by rail involve the periods 2000-2004 and 2005-2014,” the Commission writes in a news release.
