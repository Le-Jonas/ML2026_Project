Title: Motoring_gets_dearer
Date: 2008-12-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government has put forward its new traffic policy, one aspect of which is that motoring will be more expensive while it will be cheaper to buy a new car.
"The ambition is to give motoring a greener profile. The target is to reduce CO2 emissions, reduce noise and show more consideration for nature," says Finance Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen.
