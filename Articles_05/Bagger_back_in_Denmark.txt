Title: Bagger_back_in_Denmark
Date: 2008-12-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Th CEO of the IT Factory Stein Bagger arrived back in Denmark this morning after spending almost two weeks in American custody.
Bagger, who is charged with fraud and forgery in connection with his management of the IT Factory, gave himself up to police in Los Angeles after disappearing from Dubai.
