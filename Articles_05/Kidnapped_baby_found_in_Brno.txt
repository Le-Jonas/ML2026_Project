Title: Kidnapped_baby_found_in_Brno
Date: 2009-07-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A national and Europe-wide search for a kidnapped Danish baby has been successful, with a 23-day-old baby and his parents having been found in Brno in the Czech Republic, according to Danish police.
The baby was kidnapped by his parents a few days after delivery, after they had been told he would be taken into care as a result of his parents’ addiction. The baby was suffering from withdrawal symptoms.
