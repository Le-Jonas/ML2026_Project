Title: Activists_barricade_ministry
Date: 2009-06-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Some 30 activists of the Danish Stop Deportations group barricaded the Integration Ministry this morning by padlocking doors from the inside.
Activists were protesting against the deportation on Thursday of an Iraqi whose asylum request has been denied. The man is to be forcibly repatriated to Sulaimaniyah in Iraq as the first rejected Iraqi asylum-seeker since Denmark and Iraq entered into an agreement on repatriation.
