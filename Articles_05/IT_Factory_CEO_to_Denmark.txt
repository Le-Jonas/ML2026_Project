Title: IT_Factory_CEO_to_Denmark
Date: 2008-12-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The CEO of the bankrupt IT Factory company Stein Bagger is expected to land in Denmark within the next 48 hours after vountarily giving himself up to police in Los Angeles over the weekend.
Bagger, has been sought through Interpol by the Danish police on charges based on allegations of fraud in connection with the running of the IT Factory.
