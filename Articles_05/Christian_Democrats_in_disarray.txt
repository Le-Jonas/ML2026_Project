Title: Christian_Democrats_in_disarray
Date: 2008-10-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After several weeks of controversy, the Christian Democratic Party has ousted its leader Bodil Kornbek in favour of a relatively unknown local politician from Bornholm, Bjarne Hartung Kirkegaard.
At a weekend convention, Bodil Kornbek garnered 77 votes while Bjarne Hartung Kirkegaard received 151 votes. Twelve delegates abstained.
