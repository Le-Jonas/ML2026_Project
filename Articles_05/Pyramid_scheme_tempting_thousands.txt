Title: Pyramid_scheme_tempting_thousands
Date: 2009-07-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish authorities are warning that a wave of illegal pyramid schemes may be under way after the discovery that up to 150,000 people have been contacted by shady companies offering suspicious jobs.
Last week, the National Labour Market Authority reported a company called V-provide to the police, claiming it had contacted users of the public employment data base Jobnet.dk with questionable job offers.
