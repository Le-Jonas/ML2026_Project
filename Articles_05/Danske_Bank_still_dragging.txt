Title: Danske_Bank_still_dragging
Date: 2009-08-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The financial crisis still has a firm grip on Danske Bank, which has returned a DKK 0.7 billion net profit after tax for the first six months of 2009 compared with DKK 5.8 billion in the same period in 2008.
Writedowns on loans in the second quarter were at DKK 6.5 billion compared with DKK 8 billion in the first quarter.
