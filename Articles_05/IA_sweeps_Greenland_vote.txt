Title: IA_sweeps_Greenland_vote
Date: 2009-06-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The centre-left Inuit Ataqadigiit (IA) party won a landslide in Tuesday’s general elections in Greenland, garnering 43.7 percent of the vote and effectively giving the social democratic Siumut Party its marching orders after 30 years in power in the territory.
The percentage vote gives IA 14 seats in the 31-seat Inatsisartut (Parliament).
