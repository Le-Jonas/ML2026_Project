Title: Hells_Angel_deported_from_Ibiza
Date: 2009-03-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A member of the Hells Angels arrives in Denmark from Spain today after being deported on charges of the attempted murder of a 40-year-old man in January this year.
According to the police, the 35-year-old biker, who is on early retirement, pulled the trigger in an incident at the Ban Gaw restaurant on Halmtorvet Square on January 26. The 40-year-old man, who is said to have had connections to the immigrant gang environment survived shots to his throat, chest and back, while the biker is said to have left the country immediately afterwards.
