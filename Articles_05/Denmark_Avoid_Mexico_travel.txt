Title: Denmark_Avoid_Mexico_travel
Date: 2009-04-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark's Foreign Ministry has updated its travel advisory for Mexico, urging Danes to avoid all unnecessary travel to the country.
"Several cases of New Influenza (swine influenza) that spreads between humans have been reported in Mexico. The Danish Board of Health advises against all unnecessary travel (Ed: to Mexico) in order to avoid travellers from Denmark contracting the new influenza (swine flu - H1N1)" the ministry says.
