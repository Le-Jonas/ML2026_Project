Title: MP_to_sue_State_to_stop_registers
Date: 2009-07-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Unity List MP Per Clausen and two other former left-wing activists are to sue the state claiming the domestic intelligence service PET illegally registered them during the 1980s.
"The case is designed to force PET to register legally in the future. The case will also establish that the registrations that PET carried out during the Cold War were illegal, enabling those responsible to be held accountable," Clausen says.
