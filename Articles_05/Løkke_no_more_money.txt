Title: Løkke_no_more_money
Date: 2008-10-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Finance Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen may drop his much-berated idea of forcing Danes to save up in a Special Pension arrangement after all.
He is prepared to drop his plans to reintroduce the Special Pension (SP) next year, but he is not prepared to put more money into Danish pockets.
