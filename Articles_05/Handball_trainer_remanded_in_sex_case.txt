Title: Handball_trainer_remanded_in_sex_case
Date: 2009-03-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A handball trainer on the island of Funen has been remanded in custody in a case involving allegations of the sexual abuse of under-15 girls.
The remand hearing in the case took place behind closed doors a week ago, but has only just become public. According to a report in Ekstra Bladet, which has been confirmed by the police, the charges involve sexual relations other than intercourse.
