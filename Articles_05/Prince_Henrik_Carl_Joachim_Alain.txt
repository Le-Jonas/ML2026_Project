Title: Prince_Henrik_Carl_Joachim_Alain
Date: 2009-07-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After much speculation about the choice of names, Denmark’s newest prince has been christened at Møgeltønder Church in southern Jutland, near the home of his parents Prince Joachim and Princess Marie.
Prince Joachim is the second son of Queen Margrethe II and her Royal Consort Prince Henrik.
