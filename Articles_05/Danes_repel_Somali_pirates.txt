Title: Danes_repel_Somali_pirates
Date: 2009-04-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Danish vessel with a crew of three Danish officers and four Filipinos repelled an attack by Somali pirates this morning in the Gulf of Aden.
As pirates attempted to attack the vessel, crewmembers fired emergency flares, forcing the pirates to pull back and keeping them at bay until help arrived.
