Title: 120,000-year-old_microbes_revived
Date: 2009-06-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

American scientists from Pennsylvania University have revived some tiny microbes that have been frozen under three kilometers of perma-frost in Greenland for at least 120,000 years.
Researchers say the discovery of
Chryseobacterium greenlandensis
may be able to explain how life forms can survive under even the most extreme conditions.
