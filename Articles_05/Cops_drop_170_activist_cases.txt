Title: Cops_drop_170_activist_cases
Date: 2009-06-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Activists allegedly involved in riots in Copenhagen three years ago in connection with the clearance of a youth squat can breathe a sigh of relief; Copenhagen police authorities have decided to drop all charges.
“Three years have gone by now and with the prospect of lengthy court cases which are only expected to give suspended sentences, we have chosen to drop charges,” says Police Prosecutor Jens Rasmussen.
