Title: MC_cops_grounded
Date: 2008-10-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police motorcycles of the Honda brand are having trouble with their front brakes - they lock when the brakes are applied at high speeds. As a result, the force's 28 bikes have been grounded, according to 24Timer.
"The front brakes simply locked when the brakes were applied at high speed, and that was too risky for the motorbike officers. So I've grounded them," says National Police Logistics Head Niels Pallesen.
