Title: Metroring_stopped_fearing_Cologne_repeat
Date: 2009-03-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Parliamentary Traffic Committee has decided not to give Traffic Minister Lars Barfoed a mandate to further develop plans for a Metro City Ring, according to TV2 Lorry.
The unanimous decision was taken following a recent accident in Cologne in Germany in which a Metro construction project may have caused buildings to collapse.
