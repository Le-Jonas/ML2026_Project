Title: Foreign_investors_offloading
Date: 2008-11-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The trade in Danish stocks and bonds reached a 3-year record in October, running up to DKK 70 billion, according to Danmarks Nationalbank.
Foreign investors have offloaded Danish stocks and bonds to such an extent that foreign holdings of Danish securities are now down at DKK 614 billion - the lowest level for some three years.
