Title: &lsquo;Danish&rsquo;_voice_for_IOC
Date: 2008-10-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s 800 meter runner Wilson Kipketer was Denmark’s candidate for the IOC Active Committee, and although he still has a chance, he was not chosen during voting during the Olympics in Beijing. Nonetheless, Denmark will be represented, although from an unexpected quarter.
“I am not Danish, but I’ve been here for almost eight years and Denmark is my home now. It is a large part of me,” says Pedro Yang.
