Title: Greenland_self-rule_likely
Date: 2008-11-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Latest polls up to Greenland's referendum today on self-rule for the territory suggest a resounding victory for proponents of self-government.
A full 61 percent of the population is expected to vote in favour, according to an HS Analyse poll carried out for Sermitsiaq.
