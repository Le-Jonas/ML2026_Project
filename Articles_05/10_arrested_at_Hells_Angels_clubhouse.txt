Title: 10_arrested_at_Hells_Angels_clubhouse
Date: 2009-02-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police officers who raided a Hells Angels clubhouse in Amager found an automatic weapon and cocaine, according to Supt. Steffen Th. Steffensen of the Copenhagen police.
“We were looking for a certain person attached to the AK81 who is charged with possession of 100 grammes of cocaine. But as the officers were there, they had a look around and more turned up,” Steffensen added.
