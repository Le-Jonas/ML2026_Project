Title: Ombudsman_to_investigate_minister
Date: 2008-09-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Parliamentary Ombudsman Hans Gammeltoft Hansen has announced plans to investigate the Minister for Integration Birthe Rønn Hornbech (Lib.) in the wake of controversy surrounding duties to instruct the public.
“I have decided severally to investigate the minister’s duty of instruction,” Gammeltoft Hansen says in a press release.
