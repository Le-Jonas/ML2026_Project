Title: DPP_-_Haram_mayor!
Date: 2008-10-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party Deputy Mayor Alex Ahrendtsen is considering whether his party can continue to support Mayor Jan Boye at the next election because Boye has agreed to speak at a mosque after Friday prayers.
“I’m astounded. I can’t find words to explain why. I don’t believe it,” Ahrendtsen told TV2/FYN.
