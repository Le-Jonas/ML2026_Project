Title: Beware_of_false_IKEA_e-mails
Date: 2009-08-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Fraudsters have begun using a false IKEA e-mail address to coax people into providing their credit card details. IKEA Denmark is considering police action and has warned the public not to respond.
The e-mail, from an address called info@ikea.no, attempts to get people to go to a site that resembles IKEA’s – but IKEA Denmark says the site is a fraud.
