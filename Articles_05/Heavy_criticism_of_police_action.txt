Title: Heavy_criticism_of_police_action
Date: 2009-04-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Copenhagen Public Prosecutor has severely criticised a police report regarding a pincer movement used during the detention of 49 people in a house clearance operation in 2007.
Police had been ordered to a youth squatter’s building in Copenhagen after the building’s owners had demanded the inhabitants be forcibly evicted. The building, on Jagtvej street, has since been demolished.
