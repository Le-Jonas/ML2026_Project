Title: Danish_stocks_close_down_again
Date: 2008-10-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Gloomy morning forecasts of yet another day’s losses on the Danish Stock Exchange held water today as the benchmark C-20 index closed 4.3 percent down and left all of the 20 most-traded Danish stocks in red territory.
Vestas led the drop with almost nine percent shaved off its share. D/S Norden and Danske Bank also took a hammering.
