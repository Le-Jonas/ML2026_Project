Title: Denmark&rsquo;s_largest_hash_case_cracked
Date: 2008-09-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A criminal organisation has imported as much as 17 tons of cannabis from Spain and the Netherlands into Denmark, police say.
The turnover was enormous, and a German woman has been charged with transporting DKK 53 million to the gang’s contacts abroad.
