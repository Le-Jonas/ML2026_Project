Title: Beware_of_forbidden_souvenirs
Date: 2009-07-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Environment MInister Troels Lund Poulsen (Lib) has introduced a leaflet campaign this week to tell travellers to be wary of the souvenirs they bring home from holidays abroad.
Items made from or containing threatened species of flora og fauna - such as snakeskin wallets, turtleshell ashtrays, coral jewelry or items containing tiger bones or feathers from protected bird species are among those that may be banned in Denmark.
