Title: Police_chase_12-year_old_driver
Date: 2008-10-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

He probably should have been in bed, but instead a 12-year-old boy went night joyriding in his father’s oversized 4WD and had no intention of stopping for the police.
When the vehicle’s driver initially raised suspicions, he was in the Havndal area east of Hobro. Patrols from the northern and eastern Jutland forces joined the chase to try to stop the young man. It took a full 45 minutes to stop him, by which time he was in the middle of Hadsund.
