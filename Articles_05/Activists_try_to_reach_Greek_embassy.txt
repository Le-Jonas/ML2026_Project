Title: Activists_try_to_reach_Greek_embassy
Date: 2008-12-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Thirty-two people have been arrested this evening as police attempted to prevent activists from reaching the Greek Embassy during an unnannounced demonstration in sympathy with events in Greece.
In general, the demonstration was peaceful, although a bottle was thrown at police and one officer was doused with red paint.
