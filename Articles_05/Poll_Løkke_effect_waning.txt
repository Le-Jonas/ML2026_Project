Title: Poll_Løkke_effect_waning
Date: 2009-05-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark's new Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen still has a slim majority for his government, but the opposition is close on his heels according to the latest Megafon poll for Politiken.
The poll shows the government bloc of the Liberal, Conservative and Danish People's parties garnering 50.9 percent while the Social Democratic, Socialist People's, Social Liberal and Unity List parties have the support of 49.1 percent.
