Title: PM_No_to_NATO_rumours
Date: 2009-02-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Andes Fogh Rasmussen (Lib.) has yet again reiterated that he has no plans to resign as prime minister and that he will remain at his post as long as the Danish electorate wants him.
The recent rampant round of rumour and speculation that Fogh Rasmussen is preparing to resign in favour of the secretary-generalship of NATO has arisen following his decision to take part in parliamentary question time on February 25.
