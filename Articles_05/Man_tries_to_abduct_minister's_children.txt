Title: Man_tries_to_abduct_minister's_children
Date: 2009-03-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Conservative Party Leader Lene Espersen's children appear to have been close to being abducted yesterday afternoon, resulting in the arrest overnight of a 67-year-old man, according to ekstrabladet.dk.
The man has been charged with attempted kidnapping, in an incident that Espersen has confirmed to ekstrabladet.dk.
