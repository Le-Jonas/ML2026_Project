Title: Justice_minister_undercuts_police_chief
Date: 2008-11-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Justice Minister Brian Mikkelsen rejects the notion that the police force should use more of its resources patrolling the streets in order to be more visible in the public eye.
Mikkelsen's views undermine a report last week from the National Commissioner Torsten Hesselberg on an adjustment of the controversial police reform.
