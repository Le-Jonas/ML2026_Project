Title: New_Year_sushi_or_cod_
Date: 2008-12-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Cod remains one of Denmark's favourite traditional New Year's recipes, despite the fact that other, more exotic, courses have wangled their way on to menus.
Although fishmongers and sushi restaurants report an increased interest for sushi at New Year's - none of the new foods can compete with traditional cod.
