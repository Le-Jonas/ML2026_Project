Title: New_Michelin_stars_in_Denmark
Date: 2009-03-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Michelin Guide has published its 2009 star awards with two new Copenhagen restaurants added to 11 already awarded in Denmark.
One of the single stars was awarded to Restaurant Herman in Nimb in the Tivoli Gardens in central Copenhagen while the other was awarded to the Kokkeriet restaurant on Kronprinsessegade street.
