Title: Coloplast_axes_jobs
Date: 2008-09-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Coloplast, which produces nursing products is to announce cuts of 300 jobs or 275 full time employees, according to Coloplast’s CEO Lars Rasmussen in an interview with RB-Børsen. One hundred and ten of those jobs will be lost in Dnemark.
The cutbacks will primarily take place within Coloplast’s global production unit Global Operations – but other parts of the organization will also be affected.
