Title: Conservative_cabinet_reshuffle
Date: 2008-09-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Following the announcement yesterday that Bendt Bendtsen has withdrawn from his post as Leader of the Conservative Party and Minister for Economy and Trade, a set of new Conservative ministers is due to be presented to the Queen today by Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen (Lib.).
Former Justice Minister Lene Espersen is to take over the leadership of the party as well as the Economy and Trade portfolio, while Brian Mikkelsen is to move from the Culture Ministry to Justice.
