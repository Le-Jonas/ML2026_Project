Title: Danish_military_rejects_British_claim
Date: 2008-09-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Army Operative Command has rejected a British coroner’s inquest findings into the deaths of an 18-year-old and a 23-year-old British soldier in southern Afghanistan last year.
According to the coroner, the deaths were the result of faulty electronic countermeasure equipment in a Danish vehicle, which allowed radio signals to detonate a roadside bomb.
