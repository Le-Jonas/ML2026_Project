Title: Drunken_sailor_rams_royal_yacht
Date: 2009-06-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A drunken yachtsman has been arrested after colliding with the royal yacht Dannebrog outside Hellerup north of Copenhagen, according to ekstrabladet.dk.
An eyewitness said he noticed the small sailing boat when it came much too close to the royal yacht.
