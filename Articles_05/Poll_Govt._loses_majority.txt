Title: Poll_Govt._loses_majority
Date: 2009-01-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Megafon poll carried out for Politiken and TV2 shows the government losing its majority, with the Conservative Party hardest hit, losing three percentage points since December to land on half a percentage point below its 2007 election result.
“I think this may be because (ED: Conservative Leader) Lene Espersen is the one responsible for the packages that are to help us through the financial crisis. It can be seen as if she wants to help the banks, even though the idea is to get money out into companies and avoid layoffs,” says Cons. Political Spokeswoman Henriette Kjær.
