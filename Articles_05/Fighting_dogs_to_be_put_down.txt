Title: Fighting_dogs_to_be_put_down
Date: 2009-08-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two fighting dogs that took part in an attack on a couple in Southern Jutland two weeks ago have been ordered to be put down, according to JydskeVestkysten.
Three fighting dogs took part in the attack on the couple from Kollund. One of the dogs was put down immediately after the attack, but police have determined that the other two should also be destroyed.
