Title: Gender_wage_difference_continues
Date: 2008-11-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

According to a new report from the The Danish National Centre for Social Research, men on average earn between 17 percent and 21 percent more than women, with the reasons for the gap increasingly diffuse.
"Not much has happened since the beginning of the 1980s. The wage gap goes up and down - but over time it remains the same," says Mette Deding of the NCSR.
