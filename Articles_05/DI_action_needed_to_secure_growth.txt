Title: DI_action_needed_to_secure_growth
Date: 2008-09-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The financial crisis hits Danish companies when they have difficulty in borrowing funds, but that's not the only problem.
In it's latest growth plan, the Confederation of Danish Industries (DI) is calling for urgent action to address what it sees as the main problem for Danish industry - a lack of labour. One in five members of DI say that the labour shortage is their biggest hurdle for growth.
