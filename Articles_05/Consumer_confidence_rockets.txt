Title: Consumer_confidence_rockets
Date: 2009-04-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Recent months have seen consumer confidence in a veritable yo-yo – predominantly a downward swing – but the latest figures from Statistics Denmark show consumer confidence rising apace.
Although still in negative territory, the April indicator touched minus 4.4 – rising from minus 11.7 in March – with all five issues in the survey going positive.
