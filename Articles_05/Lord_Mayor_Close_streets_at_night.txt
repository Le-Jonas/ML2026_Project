Title: Lord_Mayor_Close_streets_at_night
Date: 2009-02-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen’s Lord Mayor has suggested closing streets and extending no cruising zones in the Nørrebro district of the capital in order to stop the spate of violence and shootings in the area.
Vehicles are currently not permitted to drive through the central Vestergade and Nørregade streets on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights.
