Title: Christian_Democratic_cliff-hanger
Date: 2008-10-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Leader of the Danish Christian Democratic Party Bodil Kornbek may only have ten days left as chair of the party, which is not currently represented in Parliament.
A survey carried out by Ritzau shows hat five out of 10 constituency chairmen plan to vote for Kornbek’s opponent Bjarne Hartung Kirkegaard at the party convention on October 25. Four chairmen plan to vote for Kornbek, while the constituency chairman in Copenhagen plans to abstain.
