Title: HA&rsquo;s_Jønke_&rsquo;normal&rsquo;_and_free
Date: 2008-10-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A leading figure of the Danish Hells Angels Jørn Jønke Nielsen has been declared of normal intelligence and fit for punishment according to a psychiatric assessment.
An assessment order was issued following an incident last December during which Nielsen stabbed a 23-year-old in the thigh outside a grill bar in Nørrebro. Nielsen was seriously injured in the fight and claims he acted in self-defence.
