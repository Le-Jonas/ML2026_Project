Title: Denmark_mulls_HA_ban
Date: 2009-05-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Justice Minister Brian Mikkelsen says he is now considering whether to ban biker groups that are considered to be involved in criminal activities.
“I will be calling in the National Commissioner and the Prosecutor General. If they think it is a good idea to introduce a ban, I will do so. We must do whatever we can to stop the gangs and bikers,” Mikkelsen tells Information.
