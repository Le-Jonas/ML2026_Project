Title: Idealist_of_the_Year_voting
Date: 2009-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Politiken, the CEVEA Academy and the net medium 180Grader have asked the public to suggest names for an Idealist of the Year 2008 award. Politiken.dk’s readers have suggested some 300 names of Danish idealists. The panel of judges has now chosen its final list of candidates and it is now up to readers to choose a winner.

The panel is composed of the actress and singer Iben Hjejle, Social Democratic former minister and CEVEA Board Chair Jytte Hilden, Journalist and Editor of 180Degrees.dk Ole Birk Olesen, Editor-in-Chief of Politiken Tøger Seidenfaden and Former Liberal Foreign Minister Uffe Ellemann-Jensen.
