Title: Rich_to_finance_lower_top_tax
Date: 2009-02-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government’s Tax Commission is suggesting a massive round of tax relief totaling some 35 billion kroner.
One of the commission’s suggestions is a ceiling on regular disbursement pension payment deductions of some 250,000 kroner, according to information received by Politiken. The Tax Commission proporals are due to be released later Monday.
