Title: Church_reported_for_abuse
Date: 2009-05-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The controversial Evangelist free church has been reported to the police by the Børns Vilkår (Childrens’ Conditions) organisation on allegations of the mental and physical abuse of an 11-year-old boy, according to DR.
According to the report, Børns Vilkår alleges that the young boy attended the church last year to listen to bible readings. Instead, the boy is reported as telling the organisation that he was detained by three Evangelist adults who believed he was possessed by a demon that needed to be exorcised.
