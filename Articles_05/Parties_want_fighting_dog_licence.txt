Title: Parties_want_fighting_dog_licence
Date: 2009-01-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People's and Social Democratic parties are calling for special licences to be mandatory for those who choose to own fighting dogs.
According to the Danish Kennel Club, there are currently some 20,000 such dogs in Denmark compared to a mere 1,000 in 2001.
