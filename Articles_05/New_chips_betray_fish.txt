Title: New_chips_betray_fish
Date: 2008-09-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Researchers at the Mads Clausen Institute at the University of Southern Denmark are currently developing an electronic chip that will make it very difficult for bacteria-filled foodstuffs to reach consumer kitchens.
According to the researchers, the chip will raise an immediate alarm if meat, fish, juice or other foodstuffs contain bacteria, the Ny Viden magazine reports.
