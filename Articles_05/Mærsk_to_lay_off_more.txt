Title: Mærsk_to_lay_off_more
Date: 2009-03-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The economic crisis is biting so hard that Denmark’s biggest company A.P. Møller-Mærsk is expecting more layoffs among its 120,000 employees.
“We have already said goodbye to some, but we will be saying goodbye to more,” says CEO Nils Smedegaard Andersen in an interview with Berlingske Tidende.
