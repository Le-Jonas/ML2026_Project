Title: PM_ready_for_euro_plebiscite_2010
Date: 2009-01-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark's Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen says that the way forward towards a Danish referendum on adopting the euro is clear. And although he is not prepared to put a date on a plebiscite, the script appears to be written on how to fulfill demands by the Socialist People's Party to support euro membership.
Start today 
The prime minister's roadmap begins Thursday as experts and politicians gather in parliament for a major hearing on the benefits and drawbacks of the euro.
