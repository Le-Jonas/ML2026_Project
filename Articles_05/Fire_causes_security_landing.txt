Title: Fire_causes_security_landing
Date: 2009-01-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Boeing-757 carrying Scandinavian tourists to the Cap Verde islands was forced to undertake a security landing at Casablanca Airport in Morocco after fire broke out in a pantry on board.
None of the 239 pasengers on board - 159 Danes and 80 Swedes - were hurt in the incident and crew were able to put out the fire using on-board fire extinguishers.
