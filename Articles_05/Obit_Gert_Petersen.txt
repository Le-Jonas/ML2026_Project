Title: Obit_Gert_Petersen
Date: 2009-01-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The former leader of the Socialist People's Party Gert Petersen has died at the age of 81.
Petersen, who was one of Denmark's central socialist politicians for almost three decades, was chairman and political spokesman of the SPP for 17 years from 1974 until 1991.
