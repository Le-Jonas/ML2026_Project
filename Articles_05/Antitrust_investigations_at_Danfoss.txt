Title: Antitrust_investigations_at_Danfoss
Date: 2009-02-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danfoss, one of Denmark’s largest companies, is being investigated by antitrust authorities in Denmark, Germany and the United States due to suspicions of a global compressor market cartel, according to a Danfoss news release.
Surprise inspections were carried out at Danfoss in Nordborg in Denmark and Flensburg in Germany as well as at several Danfoss facilities in the United States.
