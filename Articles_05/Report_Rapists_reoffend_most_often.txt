Title: Report_Rapists_reoffend_most_often
Date: 2008-12-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

People guilty of sexual crimes, and in particular rapists, often relapse and commit crimes again, according to a new Danish survey published in the medical journal Ugeskrift for Læger.
The survey shows that among 342 people convicted of sexual crimes and who were ordered to undergo psychological testing in the 1978 to 1992 period, almost a third committed a new crime.
