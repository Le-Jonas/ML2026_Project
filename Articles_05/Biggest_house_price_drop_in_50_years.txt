Title: Biggest_house_price_drop_in_50_years
Date: 2009-07-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish house prices are expected to continue to fall this year with a third of the senior managers of the Home estate agent chain expecting 2009 prices to fall some 10 percent.
The most pessimistic six percent expect a 20 percent drop in prices, according to Børsen.
