Title: Cimber_aircraft_returns_to_Copenhagen
Date: 2008-10-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Cimber Air J200 with 37 passengers and crew carried out a security landing at Kastrup airport today because of engine trouble over Poland on a route from Copenhagen to Bucharest.
All passengers and crew on board the small aircraft are well, according to Cimber Air Flight Manager Søren Byrjalsen.
