Title: Dane_wins_poker_worlds
Date: 2008-11-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 22-year-old Dane from Funen has become the world's youngest ever winner of the World Series of Poker that has been contested since 1970.
Peter Eastgate won 53 million kroner in his one-on-one final against 27-year-old Ivan Demidov from Russia who netted a mere 33 million kroner.
