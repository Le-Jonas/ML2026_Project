Title: Air-gunman_on_loose_in_Copenhagen
Date: 2009-07-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police in bullet-proof vests closed off parts of Baggensensgade street, Peblinge Dossering, Nørrebrogade street and Dronning Louises Bridge around six o’clock this morning after reports that a man had been randomly discharging a weapon in the area.
Although not certain, police say the weapon is probably either an air-gun or a paintball-weapon.
