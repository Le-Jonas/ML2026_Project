Title: DPP_broadside_at_Cons._leader
Date: 2008-09-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government’s parliamentary safety net, the Danish People’s Party (DPP), has issued a broadside against the new leader of the Conservative Party and former Justice Minister Lene Espersen.
The attack comes in the wake of an admission by the new Justice Minister Brian Mikkelsen (Cons.) that there is a need for an inquiry into the recent reform of the police force.
