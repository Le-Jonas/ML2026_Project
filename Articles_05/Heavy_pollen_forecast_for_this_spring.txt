Title: Heavy_pollen_forecast_for_this_spring
Date: 2009-04-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The pollen season has begun and according to metroXpress it will be particularly bad this year as a result of high concentrations of birch pollen.
Yesterday, the birch pollen count reached a heavy 196 in Copenhagen, far above the figure of 100 that is normally seen as a high figure.
