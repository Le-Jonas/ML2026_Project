Title: Grenade_lobbed_at_cafe
Date: 2009-04-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Five people were wounded, one of them seriously, during the night when a hand grenade was lobbed at the Cafe Nemoland in the Christiania district of Copenhagen.
A 22-year-old man suffered serious facial injuries, while four others suffered shrapnel wounds to the back and lower extremities. The 22-year-old was operated on during the night and is said to be in a stable condition.
