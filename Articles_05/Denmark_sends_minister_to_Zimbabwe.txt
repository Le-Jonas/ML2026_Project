Title: Denmark_sends_minister_to_Zimbabwe
Date: 2009-03-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark's Development Minister Ulla Tørnæs is visiting Zimbabwe this week, making Denmark the first EU country to enter into direct dialogue with the Zimbabwean government after Morgan Tsvangirai became its prime minister, according to the Chinese nes agency Xinhua.
"I have read a lot about the country and am here to get a feeling for the progress in the country," Tørnæs tells Xinhua.
