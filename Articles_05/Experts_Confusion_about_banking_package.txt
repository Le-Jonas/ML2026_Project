Title: Experts_Confusion_about_banking_package
Date: 2008-12-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish government may be working on a bank recovery plan of historic proportions, but the major banks themselves say that they have enough capital, according to Berlingske Tidende - despite the fact that companies are complaining that they are unable to get the liquidity they need.
The disclosure has prompted a couple of Denmark's leading banking experts to question whether the government's bank recovery package of upwards of 100 billion kroner is necessary.
