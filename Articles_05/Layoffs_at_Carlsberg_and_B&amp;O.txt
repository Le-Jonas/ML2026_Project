Title: Layoffs_at_Carlsberg_and_B&amp;O
Date: 2009-01-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Carlsberg and B&O are to lay off staff. According to DR, B&O is to make up to 300 employees redundant while Carlsberg is to lay off 150.
In a news release Carlsberg says that the layoffs are a result of falling beer sales and the general insecurity surrounding consumer purchasing habits.
