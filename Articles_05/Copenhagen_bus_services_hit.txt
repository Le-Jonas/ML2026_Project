Title: Copenhagen_bus_services_hit
Date: 2009-02-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The bus company Movia says that 15 bus routes in Copenhagen have been cancelled this morning. The cancellatioins are due to a bus drivers’ union meeting in protest at the termination of a labour contract.
The services affected are 3A, 4A, 6A, 14, 15, 18, 20E, 29, 40, 66, 150S, 173E, 176, 185, 354, as well as the night services 82N, 94N og 95N.
