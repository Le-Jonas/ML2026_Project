Title: Gaming_monopolies_OK
Date: 2008-10-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The European Court has left the door ajar for countries wanting to maintain a gaming monopoly, but they must not become pure money machines, according to a non-binding statement from a court advocate general. Monopolies are only acceptable if their goal is to protect consumers and maintain public law and order.
Portuguese case 
National courts must determine whether the rules live up to EU goals.
