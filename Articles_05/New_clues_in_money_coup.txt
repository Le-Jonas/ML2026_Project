Title: New_clues_in_money_coup
Date: 2008-11-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Almost a year after last year's robbery at Dansk Værdihåndtering in Erritsø, the investigating officer says there are new clues in the 11 million kroner cash robbery.
"We have received new information from the public which we are currently investigating," says Southeast Zealand Police Senior Investigator Jens Møller Pedersen.
