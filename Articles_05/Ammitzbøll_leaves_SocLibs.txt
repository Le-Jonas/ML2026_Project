Title: Ammitzbøll_leaves_SocLibs
Date: 2008-10-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Following a meeting last night with the leader of the Social Liberal Party Margrethe Vestager, Simon Emil Ammitzbøll has left the party and is to become an independent, according to Ritzau.
Ammitzbøll has declined to comment on suggestions he may be on his way into the Liberal Alliance Party.
