Title: Copenhagen_Council_risks_audit
Date: 2009-07-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The political leadership of Copenhagen Council has become the target of serious complaint from political friends and foes alike following disclosures of financial disorder in the Technical & Environment Administration.
According to Berlingske Tidende, a majority in Parliament is now demanding that the entire council be put under administration – either by the Ministry of Home Affairs or the association of Danish local authorities Local Denmark.
