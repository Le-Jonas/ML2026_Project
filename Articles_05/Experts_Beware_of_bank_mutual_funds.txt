Title: Experts_Beware_of_bank_mutual_funds
Date: 2009-08-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Minor Danish investors don’t have a chance in hell of finding out where they get most for their money – banks hide the worst results of their mutual funds to make them seem better than they are, according to several financial experts.
Several mutual funds are currently undergoing housekeeping after the stock collapse this spring by closing stock categories – or departments – that are doing badly.
