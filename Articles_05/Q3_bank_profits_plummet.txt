Title: Q3_bank_profits_plummet
Date: 2008-10-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Banks may have earned billions in recent years, but the party now appears to be over. According to the latest figures from the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority, earnings fell 76 percent in Q3 compared with the same period last year.
Overall, banks earned 2.1 billion kroner in the quarter compared with nine billion in the same period last year.
