Title: Fur_theft_for_millions
Date: 2008-12-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Thieves have made away with furs worth five million kroner overnight in break-ins at two furriers in Vedbæk and Charlottenlund.
"Both break-ins took place shortly before four in the morning - so it cannot be the same people. Nonetheless we're looking to see if there is some connection," says North Zealand Police Press Coordinator Henrik Suhr.
