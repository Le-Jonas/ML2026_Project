Title: Fehmarn_Bridge_in_nine_years
Date: 2009-03-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Parliament has carried a bill to build a bridge over the Fehmarn Belt between Denmark and Germany with a majority of 104 votes in favour and only three against.
Transport Minister Lars Barfoed said the decision is: “an historic decision that will connect the Nordic region – and thus Denmark – with the rest of the European continent.”
