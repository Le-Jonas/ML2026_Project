Title: Embryo_gender_abortions_in_DK
Date: 2009-02-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Each year, up to ten pregnant women beyond the 12-week legal choice limit, choose an abortion because a scan shows that they are expecting a girl according to B.T.
Figures have been made available by the Abortion Appeals Board, which determines whether or not a pregnant woman can have an abortion after the free choice limit of 12 weeks into pregnancy.
