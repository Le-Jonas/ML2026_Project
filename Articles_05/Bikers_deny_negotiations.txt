Title: Bikers_deny_negotiations
Date: 2008-09-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Hell’s Angels biker group is denying rumours that it has begun negotiations with immigrant groups who are party to a violent turf war with the bikers.
“Hells Angels MC Denmark hereby denies rumours in the media regarding upcoming negotiations between HA and certain immigrant gangs,” the group says on its website.
