Title: Obama_unready_for_climate_deal
Date: 2009-03-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

It is looking increasingly unsure that President Obama will be able to sign an effective climate pact at the Copenhagen Climate Summit in December, as he needs more time to convince his sceptical support base to enter into a far-reaching and binding agreement.
According to a report in The Guardian, British Labour Party members have been told that Obama needs more time to sway Congress in his favour.
