Title: Corus_downturn_affects_entire_region
Date: 2009-02-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Johan Turenhout turns on the lights in his factory. There is a container full of long, thin metal rods. Measuring tubes, says Turenhout, a large kind of thermometer. His metal and electro-technical company makes them for steel maker Corus in IJmuiden, a few miles up the road. "They hang them in the pans of liquid steel to measure the temperature. You can use them once or twice," But with one of the furnaces closed, Corus needs far fewer measuring tubes, explains Turenhout. "And 95 percent of our work is for Corus."
For eight years Turenhout's company was located on the Corus site. The steel maker provided him with an excellent turnover. Johan Turenhout (55) had enough work for 33 staff and business was so good he had his own business premises built 18 months ago.
