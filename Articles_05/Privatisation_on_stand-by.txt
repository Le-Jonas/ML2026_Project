Title: Privatisation_on_stand-by
Date: 2008-10-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The privatisations of companies such as DONG Energy and DSB have been postponed for at least a year, according to Børsen.
The report says that an analyst and the politicians responsible for privatisation do not feel that the time is ripe to let private companies buy into state-owned companies.
