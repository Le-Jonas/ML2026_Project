Title: Govt._advisers_in_the_limelight
Date: 2008-10-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A parliamentary majority intends to force Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen to publish the correspondence from his special advisers that he has hitherto refused to make available to Parliament.
Led by the Danish People's Party, which is the minority government's parliamentary safety net, the non-governmental majority wants to know whether and how Liberal and Conservative special advisers prepare their masters' election campaigns on public funds.
