Title: Hells_Angels_Those_shot_were_not_ours
Date: 2009-03-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Neither the man who was killed in a shooting at a café on Sunday evening, nor three other people who were injured, were connected to the Hells Angels or its support group AK81, according to the Hells Angels Spokesman Jørn Jønke Nielsen.
“It seems unscrupulous that they shoot without knowing who they hit. It shows that they haven’t really considered what they’re doing,” says Jønke Nielsen adding: “It’s stupid and unscrupulous to shoot at random just because people are less than a kilometer from a Hells Angels clubhouse.”
