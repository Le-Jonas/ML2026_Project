Title: Can_Obama_outdunk_Jordan_
Date: 2009-05-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Battle of the Giants may be taking place in Copenhagen this autumn if President Obama and Michael Jordan fulfill hopes of coming to Copenhagen to promote Chicago as the 2016 Olympic city.
That, at least, is the dream scenario for the Danish non-profit organisation GAM3 which has begun lobbying for the two men to show their basketball prowess on a Copenhagen street during the IOC Congress in October.
