Title: Close_to_record_bankruptcies
Date: 2008-12-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Barring a miracle in December, 2008 will be setting the sort of record that countries prefer to avoid - the number of bankruptcies is approaching the highest for 30 years.
Up to December this year, there have been 3,165 bankruptcies - 365 short of the hitherto worst year - 1993.
