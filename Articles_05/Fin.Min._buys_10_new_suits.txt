Title: Fin.Min._buys_10_new_suits
Date: 2009-02-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

It seems that Finance Minster Lars Løkke Rasmussen (Lib) is gearing up to becoming prime minister if and when PM Anders Fogh Rasmussen announces his candidacy for the Secretary-generalship of NATO.
According to Ekstra Bladet, the finance minister has ordered no less than 10 new statesman’s suits and shirts from the Zornig tailors in Copenhagen and is going for a fitting on Saturday before opening time..
