Title: Poll_Espersen_effect_marginal_for_K
Date: 2008-09-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The change in leadership in the Conservative Party has only given a single extra mandate according to the latest opinion poll from Catinét Research carried out for the national news agency Ritzau.
The poll was carried out from Tuesday to Saturday last week and involved 1058 representative Danes over the age of 18.
