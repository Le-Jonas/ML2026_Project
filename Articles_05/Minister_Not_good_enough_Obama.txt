Title: Minister_Not_good_enough_Obama
Date: 2008-11-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark's Climate Minister Connie Hedegaard (Cons) has sweet and sour words for President-elect Barack Obama's recent video speech on U.S. climate policy.
Hedegaard is currently in the process of organising next year's Climate Summit in Copenhagen at which world leaders will be discussing an agreement on global climate targets.
