Title: Magasin_owner_seeks_creditor_protection
Date: 2009-02-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Baugur Group, which among other assets owns Magasin du Nord and Illum in Denmark, the House of Fraser and the Iceland Food Chain in the United Kingdom, and the Stockholm-based SOUK store, has petitioned the court in Reykjavik for a moratorium process to provide protection against creditors.
The department stores and supermarket chains in the group employ some 27,745 employees.
