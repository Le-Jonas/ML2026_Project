Title: Repossessions_and_bankruptcies_on_rise
Date: 2009-06-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The number of repossessions in Denmark hit 395 in May, the highest number since March 1996 and an increase of 116 on April, according to Statistics Denmark.
Single family homes counted for 219 of the 395 repossessions, with the Southern Denmark and Zealand regions worst hit.
