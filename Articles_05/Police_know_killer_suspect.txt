Title: Police_know_killer_suspect
Date: 2009-01-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police have identified the man suspected of a knife attack at the Søpavillion discotheque on New Year's night which claimed the life of a 37-year-old man.
The attack took place inside the premises, which meant that police have been able to identify the man using CCTV monitoring images.
