Title: Fogh_Climate_tempo_too_slow
Date: 2009-03-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen's hopes for a United Nations summit on climate issues in March, as a precursor to December's UN Climate Conference in Copenhagen, is unlikely to take place.
“A summit in this connection will probably not take place until the G8 meeting in July,” the prime minister says, adding that a lot of preparation is due before that. “But I don't think that will be started by a UN-organised summit at the end of March which is what we had planned,” the prime minister says.
