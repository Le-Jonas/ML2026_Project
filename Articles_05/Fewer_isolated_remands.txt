Title: Fewer_isolated_remands
Date: 2008-12-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The year 2007 saw fewer people being remanded in isolation than in previous years.
According to a report from the office of the Prosecutor General, the number of remands in isolation fell by 42.6 percent compared with 2006 and by 50 percent compared to 2001.
