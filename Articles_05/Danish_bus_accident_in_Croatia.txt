Title: Danish_bus_accident_in_Croatia
Date: 2009-01-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Randers Elitesports College in Jutland has confirmed that a bus carrying 55 of its students has been involved in an accident in Croatia. 10-12 of the students have been slightly hurt and are being treated at a local hospital.
The accident happened early this morning near Porec, where Denmark's handball team is due to play Egypt in a World Championship qualifier this evening.
