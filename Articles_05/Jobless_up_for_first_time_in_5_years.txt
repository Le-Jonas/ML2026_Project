Title: Jobless_up_for_first_time_in_5_years
Date: 2008-11-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest statistics from Statistics Denmark show 47,700 people out of work in Denmark with unemployment having risen from 1.6 percent in September to 1.7 percent in October in seasonally adjusted figures.
It is the first time in five years that the unemployment rate has risen.
