Title: Insurance_company_demands_burglary_action
Date: 2009-02-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Almindelig Brand insurance company says that police efforts to stem the number of burglaries in the country are not extensive enough.
“We would like to see the police being more active in this area,” says Almindelig Brand Private Property Director Pia Holm Steffensen.
