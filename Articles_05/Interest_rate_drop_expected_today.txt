Title: Interest_rate_drop_expected_today
Date: 2009-04-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Both the European Central Bank and the Danish central bank Nationalbanken are expected to reduce interest rates today, but that doesn’t mean that commercial bank customers will benefit.
According to Børsen, banks also borrow on the international markets – where rates are still high – and have to fund costs for Denmark’s initial bank rescue package, which may rise as high as DKK 35 billion.
