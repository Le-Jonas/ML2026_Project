Title: Children_programmed_to_love_sugar
Date: 2008-09-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

It is natural that children love sugar and fat, but parents can act early to change preferences. A look at children's yoghurt shows that it contains as much sugar as soft drinks.
Natural 
It's not strange that children love sugar - their taste buds are programmed to like sugary and fat products before they even emerge from their mother's womb.
