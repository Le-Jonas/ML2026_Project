Title: Confidence_in_police_drops
Date: 2008-10-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The countrywide debate about the recent police reform has had its effect on public sympathies for the way in which the police is able to solve its tasks.
In a recent survey from the Vilstrup-Synovate institute, 62 percent of those asked say that the police ability to solve issues has deteriorated. Only three percent say the force has improved.
