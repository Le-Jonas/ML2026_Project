Title: Danish_MEP_calls_for_petrol_car_ban
Date: 2008-09-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Conservative MEP Christian Rovsing has called for a total EU ban on petrol, diesel and gas vehicles from 2025.
“The EU countries should already now enact a ban on these vehicles to take effect in 2025,” Rovsing says following a decision by the European Parliament to produce a white paper on hydrogen-propelled vehicles.
