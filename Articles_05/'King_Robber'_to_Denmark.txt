Title: 'King_Robber'_to_Denmark
Date: 2008-12-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Swedish man - who is also known as 'King Robber' - and a Swedish woman are to be presented in a Danish court on Friday charged with taking part in a 62 million kroner robbery at the Loomis cash handling depot in Glostrup.
The two, who have been held in Sweden since the end of November, have agreed to voluntarily be extradited to Denmark to face the charges, according to Police Attorney Ole Weikop.
