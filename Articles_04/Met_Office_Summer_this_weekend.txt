Title: Met_Office_Summer_this_weekend
Date: 2008-08-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

As September rapidly approaches, the Met Office promises most Danes a sunny weekend with temperatures of up to 25 degrees Celsius, before autumnal weather heralds its arrival.
“We’re pretty sure of the forecast,” says DMI Duty Meteorologist Leif Andersen.
