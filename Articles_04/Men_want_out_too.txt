Title: Men_want_out_too
Date: 2008-08-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Over the past couple of years, crisis centres in Horsens and Fredericia have been helping young men escape forced marriages, according to Jyllands-Posten.
Young men on the run need to be able to hide from their families who try to force their sons back by any means, including using death threats.
