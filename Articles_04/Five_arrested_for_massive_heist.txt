Title: Five_arrested_for_massive_heist
Date: 2008-08-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police have detained four Swedes and a Dane in connection with a DKK 60 million armed robbery against Dansk Værdihåndtering (Danish cash handling) in Brøndby on August 10th .
Four of the men were detained at the Hotel Scandic in Copenhagen while the fifth was arrested at a lay-by on the Sydmotorvej motorway.
