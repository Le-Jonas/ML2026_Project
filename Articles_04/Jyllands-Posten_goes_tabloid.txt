Title: Jyllands-Posten_goes_tabloid
Date: 2008-08-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Another Danish daily has changed its format to tabloid. The readers of Jyllands-Posten no longer need most of their breakfast table to fold out their morning paper. Today the national daily – which is owned by the same group as Politiken – comes out in a tabloid format for the first time.
Jyllands-Posten took the first step earlier this spring when the business and finance section was converted into tabloid.
