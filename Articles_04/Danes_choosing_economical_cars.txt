Title: Danes_choosing_economical_cars
Date: 2008-08-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danes seem to be increasingly choosing small, economical vehicles that cost around DKK 100,000 and manage more than 20 km per litre.
Sales figures for July put the Toyota Aygo (from DKK 106,000 and 21.7 km/l) at the top of the list, with some distance down to number two – the Skoda Fabia.
