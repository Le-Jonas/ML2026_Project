Title: Government_to_force_savings
Date: 2008-08-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

From 2008 Danes in employment can expect to have to pay one percent of their salaries into a mandatory Special Pension scheme.
According to Morgenavisen Jyllands-Posten, the government plans to reintroduce the so-called SP scheme, which has been suspended since 2004, in next year’s budget.
