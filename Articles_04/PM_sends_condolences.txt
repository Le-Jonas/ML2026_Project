Title: PM_sends_condolences
Date: 2008-08-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen has sent a letter of condolence to his Spanish counterpart José Luis Rodriguez Zapatero in connection with yesterday’s plane crash at Madrid airport.
In his letter the prime minister expresses his deepest sympathy with the Spanish people and families of the victims of the crash at Madrid-Barajas airport.
