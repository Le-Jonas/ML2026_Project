Title: Bureaucracy_costs_too_much_in_Copenhagen
Date: 2008-08-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

If Copenhagen Council’s administrative costs were at the same level as the average in the rest of the country, politicians would have some DKK 250 million extra each year to use on public services.
DKK 250 million is the same as 15 new skating rinks, eight libraries or 1,000 pre-school teachers to look after children in the capital.
