Title: Association_blacklist_roller-coaster_producer
Date: 2008-08-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

If it was up to the Chairman of the Danish Funfair Association Søren Kragelund, the Italian company Sac Sartoni has delivered its last roller-coaster of the type to be found at Tivoli Friheden in Århus. The Cobra at Tivoli Friheden has been closed since July 4, 2008 following a serious accident.
Kragelund calls the company ‘a bad egg’.
