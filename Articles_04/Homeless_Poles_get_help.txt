Title: Homeless_Poles_get_help
Date: 2008-08-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Homeless and poor people from Eastern Europe have become part of daily life in the streets of Copenhagen. Help may be on the way, however for some Poles who will be given help to return to Poland. Kofoeds School estimates that some 90 Poles are homeless in the capital.
Kofoeds School is an independent, non-profit humanitarian organization that provides help in order to promote self-reliance for people with socio- economic problems.
