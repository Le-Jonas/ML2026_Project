Title: Designers_Remix_Collection
Date: 2008-08-04
Article Type: Sundhed
Author: Arendse Würgler

»Origami gets edgy in this season’s honourable bow to the kimono. Intricate folds, darts and triangles accentuate pagoda shapes and drapes. Vintage Japanese flowers blossom. Silks are see-through. Colours are flush with pink blush. And fans unfold. It’s geisha. It’s ninja. It’s femininity from the past in the present.«
Resultatet beskriver hun selv som et forfinet og minimalistisk udtryk, med alt hvad der kan foldes og draperes.
