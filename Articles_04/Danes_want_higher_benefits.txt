Title: Danes_want_higher_benefits
Date: 2008-08-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

According to a new opinion survey on unemployment benefits, many Danes appear ready for a general re-vamp of the unemployment benefit system – with higher benefits but for a shorter period.
The survey, carried out by the weekly publication A4, shows that some 60 percent of those asked feel the current rate of unemployment benefit is too low.
