Title: Minister_kept_knowledge_from_Parliament
Date: 2008-08-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Integration Minister Birthe Rønn Hornbech (Liberal) was fully aware of a family reunion loophole in May this year when she reported to parliament, but refrained from informing the house according to Berlingske Tidende.
In the session, Rønn Hornbech refrained from telling members that two weeks of work in another European Union country is probably enough to allow Danes to bring their foreign spouses to Denmark.
