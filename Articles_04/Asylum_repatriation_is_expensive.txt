Title: Asylum_repatriation_is_expensive
Date: 2008-08-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

According to national police figures, 2007 saw a taxpayer bill of DKK 13.9 million to forcibly repatriate 189 asylum seekers who had been refused asylum.
Some of those repatriated were sent home in a chartered private aircraft as they were too upset or desperate to be taken on a normal aircraft.
