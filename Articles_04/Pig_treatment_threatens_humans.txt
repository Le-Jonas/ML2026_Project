Title: Pig_treatment_threatens_humans
Date: 2008-08-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Contrary to recommendations, vets are increasingly prescribing an important anti-biotic to pigs and cattle which may make bacteria resistant, according to Berlingske Tidende.
A rapid increase in the use of the antibiotic
cephalosporin
to treat infections in pigs and cattle is threatening to undermine treatment for blood poisoning and salmonella in humans.
