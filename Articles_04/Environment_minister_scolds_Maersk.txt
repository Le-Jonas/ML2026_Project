Title: Environment_minister_scolds_Maersk
Date: 2008-08-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

It is unusual that non-socialist ministers taunt A.P. Møller-Mærsk – but that is precisely what Environment Minister Troels Lund Poulsen has done in connection with Denmark’s N. Sea oil pollution from rigs.
“I have noted that A.P. Møller-Mærsk has heralded a new and greener environmental profile. It would be good if the company could translate these fine, green words into action out at its oil rigs,” the minister says.
