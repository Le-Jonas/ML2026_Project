Title: Historic_Jutland_church_removal_begins
Date: 2008-08-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Work has begun to dismantle Mårup Church, a 13th century place of worship perched perilously on eroding cliffs in western Jutland. The picturesque chapel, which has been de-consecrated, is to be stored elsewhere until a new location has been found further inland.
The first item to be moved was an anchor retrieved from a sunken, 18th century English frigate.
