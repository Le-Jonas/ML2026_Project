Title: Housing_prices_continue_to_fall
Date: 2008-09-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish economists are warning that the crisis in the housing market will get worse.
New figures from boliga.dk show the biggest monthly fall in sales prices since the decline in the housing market began in 2007. In August alone, the drop in prices is set at some DKK 1.5 billion.
