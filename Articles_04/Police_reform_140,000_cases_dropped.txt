Title: Police_reform_140,000_cases_dropped
Date: 2008-09-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In the wake of the latest police reform, the force is carrying out a major housekeeping operation by dropping a total of 2.8 million unsolved cases, according to Ekstra Bladet.
The move means that some 14.000 reported, but unsolved crimes files can be permanently closed.
