Title: PM_ready_for_another_term
Date: 2008-08-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Liberal Party Prime Minister Anders Fogh Rasmussen is ready to stand for another term in Parliament, according to P4 Sjælland. According to the report he will contest the Greve constituency. The prime minister topped the voting table in the 2007 elections, garnering 58,751 votes in the Sjælland Storkreds constituency.
The Liberal Party in the Greve constituency, which counts the municipalities of Solrød and Greve, is to choose its candidate at a meeting on August 26.
