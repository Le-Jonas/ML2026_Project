Title: Doctors_eat_breakfast_at_school
Date: 2008-08-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A new survey in the Danish medical magazine Ugeskrift for Læger supports previous reports showing that a good breakfast reduces the risk of overweight children.
“We should re-consider whether pre-schools and not least schools should offer children a healthy breakfast,” says Vibeke Manniche, chairperson of the Association of Municipal Teachers.
