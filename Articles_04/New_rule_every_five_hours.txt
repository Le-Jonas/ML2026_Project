Title: New_rule_every_five_hours
Date: 2008-08-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

2007 set a new record for ‘Prohibition Denmark’. Five brand new rules every day – or one every five hours, according to Jyllands-Posten.
After having been stable at around 1,000 rules per year throughout the 1990s, the law factory has begun to work at top speed.
