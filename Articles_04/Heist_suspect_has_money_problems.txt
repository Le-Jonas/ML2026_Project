Title: Heist_suspect_has_money_problems
Date: 2008-08-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 28-year-old top Swedish footballer and a known Danish criminal are among five men remanded in custody in connection with the Aug. 10 heist against a Danish cash handling depot, according to several Danish and Swedish newspapers.
Four of the five suspects were detained at the Hotel Scandic in Copenhagen, the fifth at a lay-by on the Sydmotorvej motorway.
