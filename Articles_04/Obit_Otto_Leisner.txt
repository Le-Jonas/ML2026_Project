Title: Obit_Otto_Leisner
Date: 2008-08-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One of Denmark’s legendary radio and television entertainment anchors Otto Leisner has died at the age of 91, according to a family announcement. Leisner died at home on Saturday, the family said.
Leisner joined Danmarks Radio (the Danish Broadcasting Corporation) in 1945, hosting a series of popular radio programmes and eventually moving into television where he hosted and anchored quiz and entertainment programmes.
