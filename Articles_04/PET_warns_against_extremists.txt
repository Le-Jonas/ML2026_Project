Title: PET_warns_against_extremists
Date: 2008-08-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Muggings and attacks against opponents are part of daily life at the extremes of the political spectrum. In Århus, for example, there have been repeated clashes between left and right wingers.
“If weapons become involved in the confrontations, it will mean a serious escalation in the violence of these confrontations,” the report says.
