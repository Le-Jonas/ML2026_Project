Title: Expert_Minister_should_think_of_resigning
Date: 2008-09-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Minister for Integration Birthe Rønn Hornbech has become so tarnished by the issue of the interpretation of EU legislation that she should consider resigning, according to one of the country’s leading professors in administrative law.
Professor Claus Haagen Jensen of Aalborg University tells Berlingske Tidende that the case is becoming more and more serious.
