Title: Danish_soldiers_witness_Afghan_execution
Date: 2008-08-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish soldiers witnessed what is being described as the summary execution of an Afghan soldier while he was in British custody, according to Berlingske.dk.
The Danish Defence Forces have released a short announcement that Afghan soldiers yesterday evening shot and killed an Afghan who had been detained during battles in the northern part of a Danish area, close to Attal in the Danish area of responsibility in Helmand Province.
