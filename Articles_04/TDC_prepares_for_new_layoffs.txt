Title: TDC_prepares_for_new_layoffs
Date: 2008-08-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

TDC management is suggesting that more layoffs may be necessary, as the company ponders how to handle increased pressure. Previous policies of a maximum 5-7% reduction in staff may now have to be changed, according to Børsen.
“We must become better at service and cutting costs,” he says.
