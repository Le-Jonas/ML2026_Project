Title: Lars_Løkke_approaching_the_limit
Date: 2008-08-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Finance Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen (Liberal) says “Denmark is doing well” in his presentation of the 2009 Finance Bill today, but adds that Danes will have to be prepared for lower growth.
“At the same time we must be honest and accept that we are approaching the limit of capacity for the Danish economy,” the finance minister says adding that labour market reforms are on the way in order to move the economy forward again.
