Title: Economists_government_too_optimistic
Date: 2008-08-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Government forecasts in its Finance Bill that house prices will fall four percent this year and two percent next year are too optimistic, according to a panel of bank economists surveyed by Ritzau and RB-Børsen.
Seven out of twelve economists are much more pessimistic than the government, while five agree with government estimates. None of the economists envisage a rise in housing prices.
