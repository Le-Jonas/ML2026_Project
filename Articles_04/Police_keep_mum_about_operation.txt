Title: Police_keep_mum_about_operation
Date: 2008-08-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Following a major operation at several addresses, including a farm in Sengeløse in North Zealand, police say they have found several items that ‘could be interesting’ in their attempts to fully solve the Aug. 10, DKK 60 million armed robbery at a cash handling depot.
“We found weapons and other effects that seem interesting,” says Inspector Bent Isager, adding that forensic tests are now to be carried out.
