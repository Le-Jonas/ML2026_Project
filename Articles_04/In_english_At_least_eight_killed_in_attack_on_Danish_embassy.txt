Title: In_english_At_least_eight_killed_in_attack_on_Danish_embassy
Date: 2008-06-02
Article Type: Internationalt
Author: Denmark’s Foreign Minister Per Stig Møller said Monday that an employee at the Danish embassy in Islamabad was killed and three others were injured – one seriously – as a result of Monday’s apparent suicide car bomb attack against the Danish diplomatic mission in the Pakistani capital.

Initial reports speak of at least four others having been killed outside the embassy compound.
