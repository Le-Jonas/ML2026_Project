Title: Sweden_wants_new_connection_to_DK
Date: 2010-12-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s upcoming bridge across the Fehmarn Belt to Germany risks leaving Sweden out in the cold and calls for a new connection across, or rather under, the Øresund Strait between Helsingør and Helsingborg, according to an IBU Øresundsprojekt analysis.
The IBU Øresundsprojekt is a project supported by local councils, regions and authorities on both sides of the strait and is designed to study infrastructure and city development on both sides of the Øresund.
