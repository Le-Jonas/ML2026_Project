Title: Danish_warship_stops_pirates
Date: 2010-09-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Crew from the Danish Navy’s flexible support vessel Esbern Snare boarded a pirate vessel in the Gulf of Aden overnight, stripping pirates of what could be used to hijack vessels and sending them on their way to the Somali coast.
According to Admiral Danish Fleet (SOK), it is the third time in three days that the Esbern Snare has disarmed a vessel with suspected pirates on board.
