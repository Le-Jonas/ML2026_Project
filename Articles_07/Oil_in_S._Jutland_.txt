Title: Oil_in_S._Jutland_
Date: 2010-11-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Polish group PGNiG is to begin test drilling for oil in South Jutland where calculations seem to indicate the likelihood of oil reserves.
Nordsøfonden – which is the Danish state’s oil company – and PGNiG have agreed to drive a test well, probably near Kiding between Aabenraa and Sønderborg.
