Title: Consul_Countrywide_clashes_in_Libya
Date: 2011-02-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Unrest in Libya has spread throughout the country, and is not just taking place in the major cities.
“There is unrest and demonstrations all over. Not just in Tripoli and Benghazi, but also in the smaller cities and towns,” says Consul George Wallén in a telephone call from Tripoli to politiken.dk.
