Title: Daredevil_bungee_jumps_from_Øresund_bridge
Date: 2010-10-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Øresund Bridge authorities are deciding whether or not to report a Swedish man to the police after they have discovered that he surreptitiously stole onto the Øresund Bridge between Denmark and Sweden and did a bungee jump.
“This is very serious. It is absolutely forbidden to bungee jump from the Øresund Bridge. First of all you’re not allowed to stop a car on the bridge as it’s a motorway and secondly there’s the whole security issue. We have 200 cameras scanning the bridge, but nonetheless he managed to jump, “ says Øresund Bridge Press Spokeswoman Sanna Holmqvist.
