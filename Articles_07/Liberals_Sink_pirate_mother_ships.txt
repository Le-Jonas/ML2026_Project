Title: Liberals_Sink_pirate_mother_ships
Date: 2011-03-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The governing Liberal Party is to publish its proposal today on how to combat piracy, with a central proposal that the Danish navy should be able to sink mother ships, according to Berlingske.
Pirate mother vessels are used as a platform for skiffs that are launched to attack merchant and other vessels and have greatly increased pirate areas of operation.
