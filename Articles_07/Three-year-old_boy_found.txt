Title: Three-year-old_boy_found
Date: 2011-03-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The three-year-old boy who disappeared in a wooded are near Tranum in northeast Jutland on Sunday morning has been found alive and well, according to a police spokesman.
Large numbers of police and rescue units as well as some 20 police dogs have been searching all night for the three-year-old, who disappeared while out walking with his father and other adults.
