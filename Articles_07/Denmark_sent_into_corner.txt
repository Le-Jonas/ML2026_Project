Title: Denmark_sent_into_corner
Date: 2010-11-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark has been made the naughty girl in the class and has been sent into the corner.
A meeting of the Nordic Council in Reykjavik in Iceland has carried a vote criticising Denmark as the only country to expel Nordic citizens with the only reason that they receive transfer payments such as social or cash benefits.
