Title: Commando_reinstated_after_book
Date: 2010-11-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Thomas Ratchsack, who was suspended for writing a controversial book entitled ‘Commando, at war with the elite’ has been reinstated in the military, according to his union Centralforeningen for Stampersonel.
The decision to reinstate Rathsack comes several weeks after the Judge Advocate decided not to prosecute Rathsack for publishing the book “Commando, in war with the elite” that the military originally attempted to prevent being published.
