Title: Bookies_on_royal_twins_William_and_Ingrid_
Date: 2011-04-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark is awash with one of its favourite royal pastimes – guessing the names of royal babies prior to their christening.
Royal twins, born to Crown Prince Frederik and Crown Princess Mary on Jan. 8th, are to be christened at Holmens Church in Copenhagen on Thursday, and the bookies are on the ball to offer odds on the game of the name.
