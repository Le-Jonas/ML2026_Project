Title: Bomber_acts_as_a_spy
Date: 2010-09-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The man in custody in Denmark following last Friday’s explosion at a Copenhagen hotel appears to be so professional that security experts claim he seems more like a well-trained spy than a terrorist.
Former Operational Head of the Danish Security and Intelligence Service Jørgen Bonnichsen and Terrorism Expert Magnus Rantorp of the Swedish National Defence Academy both say that the man’s ability to continue to hide his identity is highly unusual.
