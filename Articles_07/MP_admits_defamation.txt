Title: MP_admits_defamation
Date: 2010-12-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party member of Parliament Jesper Langballe has pleaded guilty at the court in Randers to charges of defamation.
Langballe is to be sentenced for his statements in a debate article in Berlingske Tidende last year, written in support of chairman of the Freedom of the Press Association Chairman Lars Hedegaard.
