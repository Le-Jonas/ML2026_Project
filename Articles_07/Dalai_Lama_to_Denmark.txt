Title: Dalai_Lama_to_Denmark
Date: 2010-11-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Dalai Lama will be coming to Denmark again in April 2011.
He has been invited to Denmark by the Tibet Charity humanitarian organisation and seven other companies and non-profit organisations interested in Tibetan philosophy. Unlike his previous visit to Denmark in May 2009, the visit will be a one-day visit during which Tenzin Gyatso will hold two lectures at the Tivoli Congress Centre in Copenhagen.
