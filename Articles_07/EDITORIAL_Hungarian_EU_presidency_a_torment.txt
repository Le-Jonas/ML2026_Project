Title: EDITORIAL_Hungarian_EU_presidency_a_torment
Date: 2011-01-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Hungary’s violation of democracy belly flops onto Brussels today as the country officially takes over the rotating presidency of the European Union.
When Premier Viktor Orbán took power last spring, he said that the electorate had carried out a revolution in giving his right-wing, populist Fidesz Party an absolute majority with the power to change the Constitution.
