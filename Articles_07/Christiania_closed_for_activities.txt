Title: Christiania_closed_for_activities
Date: 2011-04-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

All of Christiania’s shops and businesses – both those that are legal and those that are not – are closed as of today for a period of several days, the area’s Media Group tells Ekstra Bladet.
“We need time to think. In part so that we can have a period to assess the situation and partly to show that Christiania is under stress,” the Media Group says.
