Title: Small-shark_invasion_in_Øresund
Date: 2011-01-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Øresund waterway between Denmark and Sweden at the mouth of the Baltic Sea is currently experiencing an invasion of exotic fish, but it has nothing to do with climate change, according to experts.
Helsingør Dagblad reports that on one fishing trip off Hornbæk in North Zealand, a local fisherman found 26 smaller sharks in his net.
