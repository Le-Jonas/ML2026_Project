Title: Foreign_Ministry_Leave_Libya
Date: 2011-02-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s foreign ministry is advising all Danes in Libya to leave the country as soon as possible.
“Developments in Libya are currently unpredictable. Due to the violent unrest and failing communications we urge all Danes to leave the country as soon as possible by commercial aircraft,” the foreign ministry says.
