Title: Enzymes_to_give_more_N._Sea_oil
Date: 2010-12-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Three major Danish companies have joined forces in a novel project to get more oil out of the North Sea, according to Børsen.
One of the world’s leading enzyme developers, Denmark’s Novozymes, has joined forces with two other Danish giants, oil and energy companies Mærsk and DONG, in a new project to test enzymes as a medium to force more oil out of North Sea oilfields.
