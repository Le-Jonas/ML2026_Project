Title: Poll_Løkke_least_popular_Lib._leader
Date: 2010-12-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Megafon poll among Liberal voters for Politiken and TV2 suggests Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen’s tenure has reduced voter inclination to vote for the party. Only nine percent have strengthened their conviction for the Liberal Party since Anders Fogh Rasmussen resigned the premiership in 2009.
Asked to point to the best leader of the Liberal Party over the past 27 years, 73 percent of Liberal voters point to Anders Fogh Rasmussen, who is currently Secretary-General of NATO. Twenty percent say Uffe Ellemann-Jensen was the best leader, while three percent point to Lars Løkke Rasmussen.
