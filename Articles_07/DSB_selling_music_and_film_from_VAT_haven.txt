Title: DSB_selling_music_and_film_from_VAT_haven
Date: 2010-09-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Yet another political storm has hit DSB after the state-run railway company has begun to sell music and films for download.
DSB’s new business project Entertainment Shop operates in a partnership with the Shop2downlaod company, which transmits digital entertainment files from Luxembourg, where VAT is at 15 percent rather than Denmark’s 25 percent.
