Title: Pilot_hunch_prevents_airport_collision
Date: 2010-12-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two aircraft were close to colliding during a snowstorm at Copenhagen’s Kastrup Airport on Nov. 26, in what the Accident Investigation Board has classified in its most serious category ‘risk for collision’, according to ekstrabladet.dk.
“This was a serious incident,” Traffic Board Communications Manager Thorbjørn Ancher tells ekstrabladet.dk.
