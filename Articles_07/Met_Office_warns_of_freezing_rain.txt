Title: Met_Office_warns_of_freezing_rain
Date: 2010-12-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Warmer weather will be spreading across Denmark this afternoon and for the next 24 hours may result in icy conditions across the country, according to the Meteorological Institute.
“Warmer weather will be coming in from the west and what comes out of the clouds will freeze just before it hits the ground and become freezing rain,” says Duty Meteorologist Michael Skelbæk, but adds that icy conditions will be local and not blanket the country.
