Title: EU_inspects_A.P._Møller-Mærsk
Date: 2011-05-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The European Commission has carried out an unannounced inspection at A.P. Møller-Mærsk to make sure that the company is keeping to European anti-competition laws.
“We can confirm that, today the European Commission carried out an unannounced inspection in our offices,” says Christian Kledal, head of the A.P. Møller-Mærsk Group Legal Department in a news release.
