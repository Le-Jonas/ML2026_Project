Title: Mikkelsen_challenges_Espersen
Date: 2011-01-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Economy and Trade Minister Brian Mikkelsen has let it be known that he is a candidate to take over the leadership of the Conservative Party, a senior Conservative source tells politiken.dk
The news that Mikkelsen is challenging for the leadership of the party comes after news on TV2 that current leader and Foreign Minister Lene Espersen failed to receive support at a Parliamentary group meeting earlier today.
