Title: A.P._Møller_makes_massive_profit
Date: 2011-02-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A.P. Møller-Mærsk came out of 2010 with a pre-tax result of DKK 59.6 billion, one of the best results the company has seen in its 106-year history.
"This is our best result ever, just one year after the worst loss in the Group's history. Our people have done a truly great job, and we can be very satisfied with the result," says Group CEO Nils S. Andersen.
