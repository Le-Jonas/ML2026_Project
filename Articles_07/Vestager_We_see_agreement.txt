Title: Vestager_We_see_agreement
Date: 2011-05-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Liberal Leader Margrethe Vestager has said for the first time that she believes that the Social Liberals will reach agreement with the government on economic policy.
“I am sure that we can close in on each other. We have said clearly that we know that early retirement will not be completely abolished, but we maintain that the agreement reached must provide funds for the national coffers in the long term. The government seems positive in reaching that goal,” says Margrethe Vestager as negotiations move towards an end game.
