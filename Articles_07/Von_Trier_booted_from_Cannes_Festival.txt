Title: Von_Trier_booted_from_Cannes_Festival
Date: 2011-05-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The organisers of the Cannes Film Festival have declared the Danish Film Director Lars von Trier
persona non grata
at the festival due to his statements about Nazism and Adolf Hitler.
Von Trier’s film ‘Melancholia’ will, however, continue to compete at the festival, according to reports from Cannes.
