Title: Bornholm_closed_down
Date: 2010-11-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Bornholm Police are advising motorists against all unnecessary trips outside the main towns as a result of drifting snow and heavy winds.
“We have had some more snow and we have heavy winds, so there are big drifts,” says Bornholm Police Duty Officer, adding that poor visibility means that snowploughs and gritters will only be operating in towns on Tuesday morning.
