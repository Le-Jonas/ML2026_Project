Title: Denmark_makes_Eurovision_final
Date: 2011-05-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s A Friend in London is in the finals of the Eurovision Song Contest, coming in as number six in the run-offs for Saturday’s finals.
Tim Schou, Sebastian Vinther, Aske Damm Bramming and Esben Svane delivered their energetic ‘New Tomorrow’ to an appreciative audience, with Danish flags waving in the front rows as Schou dashed along the catwalk, singing and deftly avoiding the big ball he had kicked into the audience.
