Title: Liberals_dump_garbage_at_tabloid
Date: 2010-12-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The tabloid Ekstra Bladet rummaged through Environment Minister Karen Ellemann’s (Lib) dustbin last weekend looking for items that should have been recycled.
The newspaper’s journalists will now have ample opportunity to rummage through Liberal garbage following a decision by the Young Liberals, led by their chairman Jakob Engel Schmidt, to dump their garbage at the newspaper’s premises in return.
