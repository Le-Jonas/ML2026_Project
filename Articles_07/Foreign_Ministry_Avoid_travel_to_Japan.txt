Title: Foreign_Ministry_Avoid_travel_to_Japan
Date: 2011-03-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Foreign Ministry has advised Danish nationals to avoid all non-vital travel to Japan.
“As a result of the earthquake on March 11 and resulting traffic and supply problems, as well as frequent aftershocks and nuclear power station safety issues, the Foreign Ministry advises against all non-vital travel to Japan,” the ministry says in a statement.
