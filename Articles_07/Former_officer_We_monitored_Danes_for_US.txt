Title: Former_officer_We_monitored_Danes_for_US
Date: 2010-11-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Frank Rosendahl, who from 1998 to 2002 was CEO of United International Investigative Service has told Politiken that he led a team that photographed and filmed suspicious people and demonstrations outside the American Embassy in Copenhagen.
According to Rosendahl, the team consisted of six people who passed their information on to the US Embassy.
