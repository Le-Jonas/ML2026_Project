Title: Hundreds_stuck_in_morning_snow
Date: 2011-01-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Heavy snow this morning has caused major problems on Danish roads, with many motorists falling prey to slippery roads and getting stuck in snowdrifts.
“So far we have had 227 calls – most of which are motorists who need to be pulled out of snowdrifts,” says Mikkel Andreas Beck of Falck.
