Title: Pakistan_Islamists_threaten_rebellion
Date: 2011-08-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

By Jakob Sheikh, Islamabad
Pakistan may be on the verge of a religious rebellion that may have wide reaching consequences for the global war against extremists, according to global terrorism experts who have interpreted exclusive statements to Politiken by the Maulana Abdul Aziz, Imam of Islamabad’s Lal Masjid or Red Mosque.
