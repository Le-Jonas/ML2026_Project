Title: Re-training_causes_jobless_to_rise
Date: 2010-08-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

July unemployment figures show a drop of 800 to a seasonally adjusted 112,800, compared with 113,600 in June at an unchanged 3.5 percent for women and a marginal drop to 4.7 percent for men.
But the figures cover over the fact that the number of people in re-training has increased.
