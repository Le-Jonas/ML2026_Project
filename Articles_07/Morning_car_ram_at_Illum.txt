Title: Morning_car_ram_at_Illum
Date: 2011-06-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Thieves reversed a vehicle at high speed through a window at the Illum department store on the Strøget pedestrian street in Copenhagen early this morning, according to bt.dk
“It was quite a violent event,” Copenhagen Central Duty Officer Arne Aabech tells bt.dk.
