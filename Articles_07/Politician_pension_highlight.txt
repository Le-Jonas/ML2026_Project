Title: Politician_pension_highlight
Date: 2011-01-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There is a clear majority in Parliament to change the pension rules for politicians who have hitherto had favourable conditions, according to Ekstra Bladet.
If introduced, the proposal would mean that some politicians and ministers would not be able to draw pensions of up to DKK 600,000 already at the age of 61.
