Title: POLL_Voters_leave_SF
Date: 2010-12-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party seems to have lost its grip on the electorate in the latest Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2.
According to the poll, the party would garner 11.8 percent of the vote if an election were held now – considerably below its 13.8 percent result at the last election in 2007. Last year the party saw its ratings just below 20 percent.
