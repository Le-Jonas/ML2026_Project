Title: POLL_Thorning-Schmidt_favourite
Date: 2010-10-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

As Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen and the Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning-Schmidt prepare to do battle in a TV debate later today, a new poll shows a majority favouring Thorning-Schmidt for the post of prime minister.
According to the Megafon poll for TV2, 48 percent of those asked said they preferred Thorning-Schmidt while 37 percent said they preferred the current Liberal Party prime minister.
