Title: DENMARK_Attack_this_evening
Date: 2011-03-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Col. Gaddafi must stop his attacks now – right now or a coalition of the willing will already begin attacking him today, according to Denmark’s Foreign Minister Lene Espersen.
“It is likely that Gaddafi will not listen. That means that the international coalition’s military power will be used during today – and the Danish F-16s will be part of that as soon as they are ready,” Espersen tells TV2 News.
