Title: Mikkelsen_Tax_cuts_irrelevant
Date: 2011-05-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Economy Minister Brian Mikkelsen (Cons), who is negotiating the government’s 2020 Plan with Finance Minister Claus Hjort Frederiksen (Lib), says that talk of tax cuts are not relevant in the current situation.
Mikkelsen, Frederiksen, the Danish People’s Party and the single Christian Democratic mandate in Parliament are currently negotiating elements of the coalition’s 2020 Plan.
