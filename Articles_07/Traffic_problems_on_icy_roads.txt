Title: Traffic_problems_on_icy_roads
Date: 2010-12-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Icy roads are posing major problems for traffic across the country this morning, with several accidents.
Among other accidents, a lorry lost its grip in the icy conditions and torpedoed a house near Varde, while two lorries collided on Route 12 between Herning and Viborg, closing the road.
