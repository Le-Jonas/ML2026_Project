Title: DK_ready_for_missile_defence
Date: 2010-11-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Both the government and opposition say that Denmark could be an active participant in a new missile defence, according to Berlingske Tidende.
The Danish contribution could be the Navy’s three new frigates that were purposefully built with ballistic missile capability options, and thus would be able to be an integral part of the missile defence for North America and the European part of NATO.
