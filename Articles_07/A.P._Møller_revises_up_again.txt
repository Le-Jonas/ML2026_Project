Title: A.P._Møller_revises_up_again
Date: 2010-11-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

For the fourth time this year, A.P. Møller-Mærsk has revised up its expected result for 2010, placing the figure at DKK 26.7 billion.
Increased freight rates at Maersk Line and higher oil prices at Mærsk Oil and Gas are having their effect. Freight rates have increased by an average of 34 percent, with a 7 percent increase in transported freight. Oil prices have risen 35 percent.
