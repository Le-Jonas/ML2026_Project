Title: Nordic_ombudsmen_question_Facebook
Date: 2010-10-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Nordic ombudsmen have decided to investigate social media such as Facebook to determine whether they contravene marketing legislation in the five countries and their territories.
According to the Consumer Ombudsman website, the countries are looking to map the types of marketing tools used by the various social media, after which they will report on whether the media live up to marketing legislation in the Nordic countries.
