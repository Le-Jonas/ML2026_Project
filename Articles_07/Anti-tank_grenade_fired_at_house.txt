Title: Anti-tank_grenade_fired_at_house
Date: 2010-11-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

An anti-tank grenade was launched from a cemetery at a house across the road in Nørresundby near Aalborg late last night, but missed its target and no-one was injured. Police say the attack was targeted at a biker-related man.
“We received several reports of an explosion in the area. It turned out an anti-tank grenade had been launched,” says North Jutland Police Duty Officer Bent Højgaard.
