Title: DPP_opens_up_for_retirement_changes
Date: 2011-01-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party, which has hitherto been against cutting back on the early retirement system, says it could agree to changes in the system, according to Jyllands-Posten.
The party is suggesting a model under which academics with lengthy educations would not be eligible for early retirement and only those who have been on the labour market for 40 years would be able to take early retirement.
