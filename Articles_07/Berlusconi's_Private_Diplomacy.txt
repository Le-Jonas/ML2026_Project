Title: Berlusconi's_Private_Diplomacy
Date: 2011-01-24
Article Type: World views
Author: Kenneth Lund

Journalist og debatredaktør
Fabrizio Tassinari and Arnold Cassola
ROME – On the Web site of the Italian Foreign Ministry, Tunisia is praised for its 'ideal features' and 'political and social stability'.
After the popular upheaval that ousted President Zine el-Abidine Ben Ali from power, the peril of supporting Arab autocrats in exchange for flimsy stability should have once again become apparent to Western powers. In Italy, however, the Tunisian uprising is also a painful reminder of Prime Minister Silvio Berlusconi’s tangle of conflicting private and public interests.
