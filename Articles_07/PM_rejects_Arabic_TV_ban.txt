Title: PM_rejects_Arabic_TV_ban
Date: 2010-11-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen has rejected outright a Danish People’s Party (DPP) call to ban satellite dishes on troubled estates, or ghettos, in the country.
“A general ban on dishes does not comply with the Constitution, or with the Liberal Party’s freedom ideals,” Løkke Rasmussen says.
