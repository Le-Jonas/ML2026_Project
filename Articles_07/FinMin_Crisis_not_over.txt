Title: FinMin_Crisis_not_over
Date: 2011-06-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark is still in crisis and risks being hit hard when interest rates begin to rise again, according to one of Denmark’s leading finance experts in an analysis the finance minister says he agrees with.
According to Copenhagen Business School Prof. Finn Østrup the reason for Denmark’s vulnerability compared with other countries is that Danes have a high level of interest-sensitive debts, a frail banking sector, a difficult housing market and high wage levels.
