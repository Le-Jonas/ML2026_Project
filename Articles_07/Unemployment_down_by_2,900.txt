Title: Unemployment_down_by_2,900
Date: 2011-05-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest figures from Statistics Denmark show the number of unemployed in Denmark at 157,500 or 5.8 per cent of the labour force.
The figures for April show a drop in unemployment of 2,900 compared to March and a drop of 7,700 compared to January.
