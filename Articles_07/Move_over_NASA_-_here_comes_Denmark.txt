Title: Move_over_NASA_-_here_comes_Denmark
Date: 2011-06-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The private Heat-1X space rocket successfully blasted off from its floating ramp off the Danish Baltic island of Bornholm today, carrying the Tycho Brahe capsule that it is hoped one day will carry a man into space.
Take-off was delayed several hours due to minor technical problems, but the hitches were solved - unlike the malfunctioning domestic hair dryer that prevented last year’s test flight.
