Title: Govt._presents_retirement_reform
Date: 2011-01-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Liberal-Conservative minority government is presenting its retirement reform proposals later today, with suggestions that it will be introducing a Senior Early Retirement programme to replace the current early retirement scheme.
The proposals for retirement reform come following a government seminar in recent days to discuss the reform of the pension system following a Conservative wish to abolish early retirement as it stands, and Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen’s New Year’s speech proosal to actually do so.
