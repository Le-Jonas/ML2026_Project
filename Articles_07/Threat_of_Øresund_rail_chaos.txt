Title: Threat_of_Øresund_rail_chaos
Date: 2011-05-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish-Scottish DSBFirst rail consortium, which carries some 30,000 passengers across the Danish-Swedish border each day, may lose its licence to operate next week and grind to a halt, without a solution having been found to service the Øresund region.
”It is shameful that we have to live with this situation. It seems that Denmark is more interested in its Whisky Belt than in the Øresund region,” says Malmö Mayor Ilmar Reepalu, adding that the main responsibility for a solution is in the hands of the Danish government.
