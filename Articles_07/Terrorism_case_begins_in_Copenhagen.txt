Title: Terrorism_case_begins_in_Copenhagen
Date: 2011-05-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen Municipal Court began hearing a case today against a 25-year-old, one-legged Chechen boxer from Belgium charged with preparing a bomb which he had allegedly planned to send to the Danish newspaper Jyllands-Posten.
As the court case opened Monday, 25-year-old LD unsuccessfully called for hearings, which are expected to take several days, to be held in camera.
