Title: SDP-SPP_economic_plan_published
Date: 2011-05-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Democrats and Socialist people’s Party have announced their long-awaited economic plan on how Denmark can handle its economic challenges up to 2020.
According to the two parties, their plan – labelled Fair Solution 2020 – will be able to provide the DKK 47 billion required to balance budgets.
