Title: Police_warn_against_killer_narcotics_bomb
Date: 2010-12-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 27-year-old man died at Rigshospitalet overnight after ingesting a narcotics substance that has yet to be identified.
Police have issued a warning against the substance, which in sold in small, clear bags with a black line on them. Slagelse police are hoping that forensic experts will be able to determine what the substance is – a white powder which is said to possibly be the stimulants PMMA (Para-Methoxymethamphetamine) or MDA (Methylenedioxyamphetamine).
