Title: Supreme_Court_to_decide_on_Christiania
Date: 2011-01-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Supreme Court begins deliberations today and for the next four days as to who has rights of use of the area of Copenhagen known as Christiania.
Christiania, which was set up in an old, vacated military area by squatters in 1971, has since been a controversial alternative commune within the city noted for its unconventional lifestyle and not least cannabis sales.
