Title: Danish_government_reshuffle
Date: 2011-03-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen has been forced into a government reshuffle after he has relieved Integration and Ecclesiastical Affairs Minister Birther Rønn Hornbech of her post and Education Minister Tina Nedergaard has resigned for private reasons.
Løkke Rasmussen decided to let Integration Minister Birth Rønn Hornbech go after weeks of controversy and following a report on the Integration Ministry’s handling of citizenship applications from stateless people.
