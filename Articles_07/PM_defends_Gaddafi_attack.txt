Title: PM_defends_Gaddafi_attack
Date: 2011-05-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen says that if Danish aircraft have taken part in an attack on a house in Tripoli in which the Gaddafi family resided, the operation is fully within the mandate of UNn Resolution 1973.
The Guardian newspaper has cited diplomatic sources as saying that Danish air crew were responsible for an attack in Tripoli last Saturday in which it is alleged that Col. Gaddafi’s son and three grandchildren were killed.
