Title: Tragedy_in_Birkerød
Date: 2011-02-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

North Zealand police say that a father has shot and killed his three children at their home in Birkerød north of Copenhagen.
“A father has shot his three children aged 2, 8 and 10 during the night,” says Duty Officer Palle Jørgensen of the North Zealand Police.
