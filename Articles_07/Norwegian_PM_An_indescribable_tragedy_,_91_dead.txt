Title: Norwegian_PM_An_indescribable_tragedy_,_91_dead
Date: 2011-07-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Norway’s Prime Minister Jens Stoltenberg (SocDem) Saturday expressed his deep shock at the events in Norway yesterday in which more than 80 young people were killed at a summer camp outside Oslo and a massive bomb devastated government buildings in the centre of the Norwegian capital.
“We have witnessed an indescribable tragedy. (Norway) has not experienced a worse crime since the war,” Stoltenberg said at a press meeting in Oslo at which he said the Norwegian cabinet would be meeting in emergency session to discuss the attack and its ramifications.
