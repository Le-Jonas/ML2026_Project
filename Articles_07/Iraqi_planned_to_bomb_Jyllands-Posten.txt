Title: Iraqi_planned_to_bomb_Jyllands-Posten
Date: 2010-09-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 37-year-old Iraqi Kurd has confessed that he and others had plans to bomb the Danish Jyllands-Posten newspaper, according to Norway’s VG newspaper quoting the Norwegian Security and Intelligence Service.
“I can confirm that one of those charged in the terrorist case has confessed to planning a terrorist attack,” Police Intelligence Service Head of Communications Trond Hugubakken tells VG, adding that Jyllands-Posten appeared to be the target.
