Title: Source_Mikkelsen_warned_about_Amagerbanken
Date: 2011-02-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Economy and Trade Minister Brian Mikkelsen and senior executives of his ministry were warned about the state of the now crashed Amagerbanken, according to Børsen.
Financial Stability Chairman Henrik Kruse Petersen is reported to have alerted the minister and his senior executives for almost a year that Amagerbanken had one foot in the grave.
