Title: Milder_weather_on_the_way
Date: 2010-12-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The severe cold that has gripped Denmark for several weeks, with temperatures down to -20, may now be replaced with some slightly warmer weather.
“Low pressure is developing and on its way around New Year – so things are still uncertain. We don’t know how it will pass the country, so it’s a little uncertain,” says Duty Meteorologist Steen Rasmussen.
