Title: Economists_Best_to_stay_out_of_euro
Date: 2011-07-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

By Jakob Sorgenfri Kjær and Kåre Pedersen
Backing for the euro among Danish economists seems to be waning as problems for the common currency grow, and with the Eurozone’s third largest economy Italy joining Greece, Portugal and Ireland in the group of economically strapped nations.
