Title: Activists_stop_logging_project
Date: 2011-07-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A group of 30 activists Monday prevented the Danish Nature Agency from beginning to fell trees in Østerild Klitplantage in order to make way for a wind turbine research centre.
The centre, which has the support of a clear majority in Parliament, is eventually to be a national test centre for wind turbines of up to 250 metres.
