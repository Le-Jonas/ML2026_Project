Title: SDP-SPP_plan_harvests_criticism
Date: 2011-04-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Democratic and Socialist People’s Party disclosures Thursday that part of their economic plan is to abandon high-end tax cuts planned for 2014 has met widespread criticism from economists, but the SPP says the move is only part of an as yet unpublished overall plan.
Party leaders Helle Thorning-Schmidt and Villy Søvndal said Thursday that Denmark cannot afford the tax cuts.
