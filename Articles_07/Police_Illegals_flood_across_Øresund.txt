Title: Police_Illegals_flood_across_Øresund
Date: 2010-10-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen Airport Police is frustrated at the large number of illegals entering Denmark across the Ørseund as a result of a lack of border controls.
“There are a large number of illegal entries – but on the other hand – as there aren’t any border controls it’s not an issue as no-one gets caught,” says Airport Police Chief Shop Steward Carsten Holk.
