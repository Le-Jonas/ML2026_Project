Title: Bier_wins_Golden_Globe
Date: 2011-01-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Director Susanne Bier has won the Best Foreign Language Film for ‘In a Better World’ (Hævnen) at the 68th Golden Globe Awards ceremony in Los Angeles.
In a Better World won the title in competition with Biutiful from Mexico, The Concert from France, The Edge from Russia and I Am Love from Italy.
