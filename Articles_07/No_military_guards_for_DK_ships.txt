Title: No_military_guards_for_DK_ships
Date: 2011-02-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Defence Minister Gitte Lillelund-Bech (Lib) has given the thumbs down to a proposal by the Shipowners’ Association to allow Danish soldiers to guard Danish ships in pirate infested waters at the ship owners’ expense.
“As a politician one should never be categorical, but I see more problems than benefits,” Lillelund-Bech says.
