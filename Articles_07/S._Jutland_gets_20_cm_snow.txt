Title: S._Jutland_gets_20_cm_snow
Date: 2010-12-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Southern Jutland was doused in snow overnight, with some areas getting up to 20 centimetres, according to the Meteorological Office.
“The low pressure front that washed in from the North Sea over Southwest Jutland yesterday afternoon moved east and gave a good amount of snow,” says Duty Meteorologist Olaf Mathiasen.
