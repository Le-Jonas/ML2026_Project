Title: Minister_warns_of_more_jobless
Date: 2010-12-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Employment Minister Inger Støjberg (Lib) is says that unemployment will continue to increase, following figures showing 1,200 more joined the unemployment queue from September to October 2010.
“Unemployment unfortunately increased a bit from September to October and we must prepare ourselves that it will continue to do so for a time yet. So we must do all that we can to help unemployed into jobs,” Støjberg says.
