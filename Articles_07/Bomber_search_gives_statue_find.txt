Title: Bomber_search_gives_statue_find
Date: 2010-09-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police divers searching a lake for traces and evidence in connection with last Friday’s hotel explosion made a surprising, valuable discovery – a statue that has been missing from a Copenhagen embankment for 15 years.
Resting Hermes had been standing nearby for over a century but was reported stolen from the Ørsteds park on April 1, 1995.
