Title: Cowboy_avoids_jail
Date: 2010-10-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Western High Court has ruled on the unusual case of a cowboy from Funen who has been found guilty of carrying a knife, but a 7-day prison sentence in the lower courts has been dropped.
Martin Boas Dahl was charged with contravening weapons laws last year when he took part in the Blåfjord Country Festival dressed as a cowboy and sporting a knife in his belt.
