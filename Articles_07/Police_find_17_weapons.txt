Title: Police_find_17_weapons
Date: 2011-04-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A police raid on several addresses in East Jutland Tuesday night netted 17 guns which police suggest were destined for gangs. A 23-year-old and a 17-year-old were arrested and are due to be presented in remand court Wednesday.
Apart from a sawn-off shotgun and 16 other rifles, police also found stolen goods at the addresses in Hadsten, HInnerup and Skødstrup.
