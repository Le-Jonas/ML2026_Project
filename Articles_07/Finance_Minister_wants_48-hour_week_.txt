Title: Finance_Minister_wants_48-hour_week_
Date: 2011-02-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Finance Minister Claus Hjort Frederiksen (Lib) meets CFU negotiators today with civil service working hours on the agenda.
“Negotiations have been going on since the New Year, but there are still some hurdles, and that is what we will be dealing with today,” says CFU Chairman Flemming Vinther.
