Title: Study_Designer_brands_give_popular_kids
Date: 2010-11-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

An as yet unpublished study from the National Council for Danish Children shows that a third of fourth grade pupils find it easier to get new friends if they are wearing designer clothes or have an expensive mobile phone.
On the other hand, one in seven children found they were ostracised because they did not have the right designer brands.
