Title: Govt._border_plan_on_track
Date: 2011-07-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish government plans to introduce permanent customs border controls are back on track and likely to be passed in the Finance Committee Friday after an opposition counter-motion was defeated in Parliament by 55 votes to 50.
The Finance Committee, in which the government has a secure majority, is to meet at noon to vote on financial appropriations for border facilities to house duty customs officers.
