Title: EDITORIAL_EU_too_tolerant
Date: 2011-01-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

How embarrassing it is that over the past 15 years the European Union has provided the Tunisian dictatorship direct support to the tune of over DKK 10 billion.
And how enormously embarrassing it is, that as recently as last year the EU promised the Tunisian dictator Ben Ali continued support running into the billions.
