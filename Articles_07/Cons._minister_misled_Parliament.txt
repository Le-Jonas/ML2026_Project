Title: Cons._minister_misled_Parliament
Date: 2010-10-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Former Minister for Health Jakob Axel Nielsen gave incorrect information to Parliament on a secret report prepared by his ministry regarding prices paid by the national health service to private hospitals.
During a council meeting in Parliament on May 13, 2009, Nielsen said several times that the report in question did not exist.
