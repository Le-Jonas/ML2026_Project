Title: Israel_reconsidering_Israeli-Danish_flights
Date: 2011-06-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Israel is considering stopping all flights between Copenhagen and Tel Aviv as a result of the Danish aviation authority’s refusal to accept an Israeli demand to allow Israeli security personnel to handle security checks on Arkia Airline’s new route between Copenhagen and Tel Aviv, according to Ha’aretz.
Meetings between Israeli and Danish authorities over the weekend failed to reach a compromise.
