Title: Libyan_consulate_passes_on_Tripoli_statement
Date: 2011-04-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Libyan consulate in Denmark has sent an e-mail to politiken.dk containing an 11-page document putting forward the Libyan perspective on the current situation in the country.
Entitled ‘A paper on the events witnessed by the Great Jamahiriya’, the communication recounts events leading up to and during the current Libyan crisis. The e-mail , which contained 11 pictures of the declaration, was neither signed nor did it include an explanatory message.
