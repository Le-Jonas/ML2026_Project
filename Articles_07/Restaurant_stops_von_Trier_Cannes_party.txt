Title: Restaurant_stops_von_Trier_Cannes_party
Date: 2011-05-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Wednesday was not a good day for the Danish Director Lars von Trier, who had to cancel a gala party for 400 guests, according to jp.dk.
The restaurant concerned refused to hold the party as von Trier’s remarks earlier in the day ‘were not in keeping with the restaurant’s ethnicity,” Jyllands-Posten writes.
