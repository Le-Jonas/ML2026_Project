Title: Poll_Government_advances
Date: 2011-01-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The prime minister’s early retirement offensive has made its mark on the latest Megafon opinion poll for Politiken and TV2, moving his Liberal Party forward and suggesting a slim majority for the government alliance.
The poll shows 50.9 percent for the government alliance and 49.1 percent for the opposition alliance, suggesting that this year’s election will be a close one.
