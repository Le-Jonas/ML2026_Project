Title: POLL_Danes_support_anti-racism_paragraph
Date: 2010-10-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A broad majority of Danes support anti-racism laws that prevent racist statements in public, according to a Rambøll/Ananlyse Danmark poll for Jyllands-Posten.
The poll shows that 69 percent of those asked supported anti-racism paragraph 266b in criminal law, while 21 percent wanted it abolished. 10 percent were uncertain.
