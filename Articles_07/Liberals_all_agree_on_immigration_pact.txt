Title: Liberals_all_agree_on_immigration_pact
Date: 2010-11-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government’s majority for its immigration pact is intact following a Liberal Party Parliamentary Group meeting, with sceptics within the group now having been convinced to support the government.
Earlier today, the majority of 90 votes came into doubt following a media statement by former minister Britta Schall Holberg (Lib) in which she said she required more informaiton on the details of the package before deciding how to vote.
