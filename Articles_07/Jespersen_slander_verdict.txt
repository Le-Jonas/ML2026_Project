Title: Jespersen_slander_verdict
Date: 2010-10-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The former Welfare Minister Karen Jespersen (Lib) went too far in claiming that the spokesman for the Muslim Joint Council (MJC) supported stoning women.
Copenhagen Municipal Court has fined Jespersen DKK 10,000 and a further four daily penalties of DKK 250 for the transgression.
