Title: Ash_cloud_south_of_Denmark
Date: 2011-05-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Grimsvotn volcanic eruption is not giving Danish flights any major problems today, but airports in Bremen and Hamburg in northern Germany are currently closed, according to Danish Air Traffic Control Navair.
Although there is a slight increase in dust levels over parts of Denmark which require some changes in flight plans, these are not issues which cannot be handled by airlines.
