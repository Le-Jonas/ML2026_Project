Title: SocDem_HQ_still_cordoned_off
Date: 2010-08-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Democratic headquarters in Copenhagen remains cordoned off following the receipt yesterday of an envelope containing a powder, and will remain so until experts have finished analysing the material later this morning.
The envelope, which had no return address, contained an article from an Iranian exile publication and about a teaspoonful of powder.
