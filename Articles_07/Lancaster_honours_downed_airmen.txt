Title: Lancaster_honours_downed_airmen
Date: 2011-05-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The WWII Royal Air Force Lancaster bomber that took part in a fly-past at the recent royal wedding in Britain undertakes a memorial flight in Denmark today to commemorate eight British airmen who crashed in Jutland in 1943.
One of two remaining Lancasters, VR-A will drop to 500 ft. over Stadilø in western Jutland in memory of the eight allied airmen who died when their Avro Lancaster EE138 crashed at Stadil Fjord on Sept. 4, 1943 after a bombing raid on Berlin.
