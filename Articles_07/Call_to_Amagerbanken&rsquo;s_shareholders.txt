Title: Call_to_Amagerbanken&rsquo;s_shareholders
Date: 2011-02-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Shareholder Association is urging all of the bankrupt Amagerbanken’s shareholders to contact the organisation in order that it can create an overview of shareholders prior to any possible court case.
In particular, the association is urging contact from customers who have been advised by the bank to buy its own stock.
