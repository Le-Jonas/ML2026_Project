Title: Suspect_sets_dna_record
Date: 2010-12-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 36-year-old Latvian man has set a record for dna matches at crime scenes in Denmark, according to Frederiksborg Amts Avis.
After taking a dna specimen from the man, police report matches with traces found at 47 houses and summerhouses that have been broken into.
