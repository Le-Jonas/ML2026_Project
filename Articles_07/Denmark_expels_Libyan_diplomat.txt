Title: Denmark_expels_Libyan_diplomat
Date: 2011-05-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Libya’s Consul-General Muner Eldawani has been expelled from Denmark and given five days to leave the country, Foreign Minister Lene Espersen has confirmed to Berlingske.
“The Consul-General has repeatedly and publicly supported Gaddafi and we have therefore informed him that he is not wanted in Denmark. He is declared persona non grata,” Espersen tells Berlingske.
