Title: E-mail_case_may_harm_Espersen
Date: 2010-09-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The controversial issue of national health payments to private hospitals, and more recent allegations that ministerial e-mails have been deliberately deleted, have raised the issue as to whether Foreign Minister and Leader of the Conservative Party Lene Espersen has given wrong information to Parliament.
In a Parliamentary Answer last year, Espersen said that calculations regarding payments to private hospitals had been carried out by the Conservative Parliamentary Group.
