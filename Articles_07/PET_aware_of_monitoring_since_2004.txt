Title: PET_aware_of_monitoring_since_2004
Date: 2010-11-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Security and Intelligence Service has known about the monitoring of the US Embassy in Copenhagen as part of the embassy’s own security operations, since 2004 according to a PET release.
According to PET, monitoring has taken place from premises outside the embassy area, but overlooking the embassy.
