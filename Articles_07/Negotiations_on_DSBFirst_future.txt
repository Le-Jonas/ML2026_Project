Title: Negotiations_on_DSBFirst_future
Date: 2011-04-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Traffic Minister Hand Christian Schmidt is to meet the five parties of the Coastal Line Agreement to decide on the future of one of the capital region’s primary commuter routes from Elsinore over Copenhagen to Sweden.
“It is unacceptable that it costs so much money every day, each time DSBFirst fires up an engine. There is only one solution - and that is to shut down DSBFirst,” says Danish People's Party Traffic Spokesman Kim Christiansen.
