Title: FARC,_PFLP_trial_sentences
Date: 2011-06-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The 72-year-old chairman of the Horserød-Stutthof Association was found guilty Thursday of helping to fund the Palestinian Front for the Liberation of Palestine (PFLP) and sentenced to six months in prison, four of which were suspended.
The prosecution had called for a sentence of eight months for Anton Nielsen, whose organisation had collected DKK 17,700 (EUR 1,340) for the PFLP which is on the EU’s and US terrorism list.
