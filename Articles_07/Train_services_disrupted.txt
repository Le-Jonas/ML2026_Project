Title: Train_services_disrupted
Date: 2011-02-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A technical problem in a remote control system in Roskilde has resulted in Friday morning’s train services in and around Zealand being disrupted, with buses being used in various places.
According to TV2 News, services will be affected across the capital island, with delays and cancellations. Several departures between Copenhagen and Odense have been cancelled.
