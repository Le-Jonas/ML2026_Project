Title: Dane_in_India_extradition_case
Date: 2010-09-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A court in Hillerød begins deliberations today on whether an April 2010 Justice Ministry decision to extradite a Dane to India on weapons charges is legal.
Niels Holck, 48, who is married with two small children, stands accused in India in a weapons smuggling case dating back to 1995.
