Title: The_Bourgeois_Roots_of_Tunisia's_Revolution
Date: 2011-01-25
Article Type: World views
Author: Michel Rocard

PARIS – Tunisia, one of the Arab League’s 22 members, is in the throes of a severe and profound crisis, albeit possibly one with a favorable resolution.
It is the smallest North African country, covering 163,000 square kilometers – more or less twice the size of Belgium, Netherlands, and Luxembourg – and containing a population of 10.5 million.
