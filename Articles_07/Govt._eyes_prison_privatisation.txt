Title: Govt._eyes_prison_privatisation
Date: 2011-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government parties are suggesting that private companies should build and run new prisons, opening the discussion as to whether the private sector should be able to take over tasks normally handled by authorities, according to Jyllands-Posten.
Liberal Justice Spokesman Kim Andersen feels that Denmark’s economic challenges and the lack of space in prisons requires a new approach.
