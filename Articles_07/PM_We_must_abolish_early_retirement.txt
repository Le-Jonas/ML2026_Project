Title: PM_We_must_abolish_early_retirement
Date: 2011-04-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen has repeated his requirement that early retirement be abolished.
Announcing the government’s ‘Reform Package 2020 – ensuring Danish welfare” package, with which the government wants to balance the economy in 2020, Løkke Rasmussen said that he wanted to make it clear that advancing the Welfare Agreement would not be enough.
