Title: Explosion_probably_not_terrorism
Date: 2010-09-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A minor explosion at a hotel in central Copenhagen and the subsequent dramatic detention of a man seen running from the scene, was probably not the result of an attempted terrorist attack, according to politiken.dk’s information.
The Danish security service PET is not yet ready, however, to say whether there is, or is not, a terrorism aspect to the event.
