Title: Heads_fail_government_school_target
Date: 2011-02-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government has said that it wants Danish pupils to be in the world’s top 5 by 2020, but only 16 percent of school heads believe the goal is a realistic one, according to a survey by Politiken Research.
The survey was carried out among 1,500 school heads, 600 of whom responded.
