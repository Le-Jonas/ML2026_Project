Title: Espersen_dumps_DPP_as_coalition_partner
Date: 2010-10-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Conservative Party Leader Lener Esperen has made it clear that her party is not prepared to go into coalition with the Danish People’s Party if the current coalition alliance wins the next election.
“We cannot be part of a government with the Danish People’s Party as that would be betraying our constituency and our voters,” Espersen tells Jyllands-Posten.
