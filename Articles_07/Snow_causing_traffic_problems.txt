Title: Snow_causing_traffic_problems
Date: 2011-02-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Zealand and Bornholm have been relatively dry overnight, but western and south-western regions have had weather problems with snow and ice making travelling difficult for motorists and lorry drivers.
“We have plenty to deal with and are busiest in the south where we have snow,” says Road Directorate Duty Officer Kristian Mody, adding lorries seem to be the biggest problem.
