Title: Germany_criticises_DK_border_agreement
Date: 2011-06-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Germany’s Deputy Foreign Minister Werner Hoyer has penned a damning criticism of countries seeking to introduce border controls or wanting to return to their national currencies.
In an OPED article in Berlingske today, Hoyer says that when politicians in European member states believe that they can achieve success with an anti-European agenda ‘It is time to act’.
