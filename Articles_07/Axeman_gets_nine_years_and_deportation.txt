Title: Axeman_gets_nine_years_and_deportation
Date: 2011-02-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Århus Court has sentenced a 29-year-old Somali man to nine years in prison and deportation for an attack last year on the Danish cartoonist Kurt Westergaard and two policemen.
It was late in the evening of Jan. 1, 2010 that the man broke into Westergaard’s house using an axe and attempted to break down the reinforced door of the panic room into which Westergaard had escaped.
