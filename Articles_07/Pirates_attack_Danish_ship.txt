Title: Pirates_attack_Danish_ship
Date: 2011-01-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Pirates have attacked a Danish ship in the Gulf of Aden with the Shipcraft company saying it has not had contact with the 2,000-ton Leopard since an assault on the vessel began.
“We received a message at 2 p.m. that the vessel was under fire and since then we have not had any contact with the ship,” says Shipcraft Director Claus Bech.
