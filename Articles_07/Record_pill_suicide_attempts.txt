Title: Record_pill_suicide_attempts
Date: 2011-03-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The number of young girls and women who attempt suicide by taking an overdose of headache tablets has increased dramatically over the past decade, with one girl a day on average admitted with overdose symptoms.
“This has developed into such a major problem that we should screen all 9th graders to find those who have suicidal thoughts and be able to help them in time,” says Centre for Suicide Research Head Lilian Zøllner.
