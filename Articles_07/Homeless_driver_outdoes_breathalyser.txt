Title: Homeless_driver_outdoes_breathalyser
Date: 2010-10-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 34-year-old homeless man from Copenhagen, caught driving in a stolen car in Næstved, caused disbelief among officers who tested him for drunken driving after a minor accident at the weekend.
With a maximum level of 4.0 per mille the breathalyser counting mechanism just couldn’t go high enough, according to Sjællandske.
