Title: Police_clear_mine_depot
Date: 2011-05-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police have cleard the old naval sea mine depot in Christianshavn in Copenhagen which was taken over by several hundred squatters on Saturday.
Officers moved into the area at 5 a.m. this morning and removed 11 people - four Danes and seven non-Danes - who had remained in the buildings. The 11 are to be charged with breach of the peace and face a DKK 1,500 fine if found guilty.
