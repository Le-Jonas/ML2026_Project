Title: Drop_in_jobless_figures
Date: 2010-09-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The unemployment queue got shorter in August.
According to Statistics Denmark’s latest figures, the overall number of unemployed dropped by 5.100 in August to 165,800 or 6.1 percent. Overall unemployment counts both those registered as unemployed, and those who are in job schemes.
