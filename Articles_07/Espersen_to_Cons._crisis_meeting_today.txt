Title: Espersen_to_Cons._crisis_meeting_today
Date: 2011-01-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Conservatives are closely watching a meeting of the Conservative Party Parliamentary group this morning.
Although no-one has been prepared to go on record with a direct criticism of Party Chairman Lene Espersen, there have been persistent rumours that a clash with Espersen is under way.
