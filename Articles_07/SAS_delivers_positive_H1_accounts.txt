Title: SAS_delivers_positive_H1_accounts
Date: 2011-08-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

SAS has published its accounts for the first six months of 2011, showing a pre-tax profit of DKK 140 million compared to a pre-tax loss in the same period in 2010 of 1,2 bn.
Compared to 2010’s H1 result, some 12.3 per cent or 1.5 million more passengers have flown with SAS in the first six months of 2011.
