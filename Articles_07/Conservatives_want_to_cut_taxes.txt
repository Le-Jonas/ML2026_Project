Title: Conservatives_want_to_cut_taxes
Date: 2011-02-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Conservative Party is looking to cut taxes by some DKK 35 billion kroner and to partially finance the cuts by abolishing Denmark’s regional administrations which predominantly deal with the health sector, according to Berlingske.
The party’s target is to reduce the tax burden from 47 percent to 45 percent by 2020,
