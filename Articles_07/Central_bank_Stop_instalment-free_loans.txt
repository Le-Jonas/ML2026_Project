Title: Central_bank_Stop_instalment-free_loans
Date: 2011-03-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s central bank Nationalbanken says in its quarterly review that instalment-free property loans should be gradually stopped.
In order to curb the ‘major and damaging fluctuations in the property market’, the Nationalbanken calls for a ‘gradual and gentle phase-out in access to new instalment-free loans’.
