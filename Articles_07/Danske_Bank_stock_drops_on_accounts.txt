Title: Danske_Bank_stock_drops_on_accounts
Date: 2011-02-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Investors appear to be punishing Danske Bank following the publication of its 2010 accounts.
Although the bank appears to be emerging from its financial crisis doldrums, and returned 2010 earnings of DKK 6.5 billion, stock continued to fall. The 2010 earnings were almost DKK 2 billion better than 2009.
