Title: Snare_prolongs_operations
Date: 2011-03-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Navy's command and support warship Esbern Snare, which was to have ended its tour of duty in the Indian Ocean yesterday, has had its mission extended and will remain in the general area where a Danish family yacht has been hijacked.
The Esbern Snare is part of the international Operation Ocean Shield anti-piracy effort and was due to have ended its mission with the force yesterday, but will remain in the area, according to Jyllands-Posten.
