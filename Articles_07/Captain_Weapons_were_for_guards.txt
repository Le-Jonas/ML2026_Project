Title: Captain_Weapons_were_for_guards
Date: 2011-04-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The captain of a Danish vessel previously reported detained by Mumbai authorities says that a misunderstanding caused his vessel to be searched for fear of terrorists on board.
The Times of India reported earlier today that Indian intelligence agencies had been tipped off that two crew members were planning to offload arms and ammunition at Raigad near Mumbai and dispatched members of several agencies to investigate and search the vessel.
