Title: Governing_Liberals_criticise_UK
Date: 2011-04-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s governing Liberal Party has criticised Britain’s decision to send military advisers to Libya, while Foreign Minister Lene Espersen has ruled out a similar move by Denmark.
Comments from Danish political parties came following an announcement by Britain on Tuesday that it is to send 20 military advisers to Libya where they are to advise insurgents on how to improve, among other issues, their military capabilities.
