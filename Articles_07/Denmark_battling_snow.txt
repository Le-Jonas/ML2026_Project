Title: Denmark_battling_snow
Date: 2010-12-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Snow and drifting snow are causing major traffic problems across Denmark, with both larger and smaller roads snowing up, according to the Road Directorate, which is warning motorists to take extreme care.
“It’s not looking good. In fact basically North Zealand and northern Jutland are the only areas not to be affected,” says Road Directorate Spokesman Carsten Jakobsen.
