Title: Danish_woman_confirmed_killed_at_Utøya
Date: 2011-07-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The family of Hanne Anette Balch Fjalestad received confirmation from the Danish Foreign Ministry Wednesday that the 43-year-old mother of four was one of those killed in last Friday’s massacre on the Norwegian island of Utøya.
“We were informed by our embassy this afternoon that she is among those killed,” the Foreign Ministry Public Service spokesman told Politiken.dk. Until today she had been listed as missing.
