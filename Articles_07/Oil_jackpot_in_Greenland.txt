Title: Oil_jackpot_in_Greenland
Date: 2010-09-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Scotland-based oil company Cairn has found oil in its Alpha-1S1 well in western Greenland.
“The well has encountered gas shows in silty and volcanic sections over several hundreds of metres. Oil has also been observed intermittently over a 400m section in the volcanic and volcano-clastic intervals of the well which requires further evaluation,” Cairn says.
