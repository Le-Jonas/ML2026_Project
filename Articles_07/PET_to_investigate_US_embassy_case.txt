Title: PET_to_investigate_US_embassy_case
Date: 2010-11-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Justice Minister Lars Barfoed (Cons) has asked the Danish Security and Intelligence Service (PET) to investigate whether foreign states or foreign embassies carry out ‘intelligence activities or other such illegal activities’ in Denmark, according to Berlingske Tidende.
“I have asked PET to make sure that that is not the case. This is a result of the reports that have been made public,” Barfoed says.
