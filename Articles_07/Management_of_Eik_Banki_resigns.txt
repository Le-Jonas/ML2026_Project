Title: Management_of_Eik_Banki_resigns
Date: 2010-09-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Faroese bank Eik Banki P/F has announced that its top management and Board Chairman have been replaced.
CEO Marner Jacobsen and Executive Director Bjarni Olsen are to leave the bank, as is Board Chairman Frithleif Olsen. Deputy Driector Bogi Bendtsen has been appointed Acting CEO, while Odd Bjellvåg has been appointed chairman of the Board
