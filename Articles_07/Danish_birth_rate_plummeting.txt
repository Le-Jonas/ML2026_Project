Title: Danish_birth_rate_plummeting
Date: 2010-12-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish women are having fewer children, with the birth rate in the first nine months of 2010 having dropped significantly compared to last year.
If the tendency continues, 2010 will see 59,726 births – the lowest number since 1988, according to Kristeligt Dagblad.
