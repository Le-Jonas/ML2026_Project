Title: Kjær_Never_again
Date: 2011-01-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Henriette Kjær, who has resigned from her senior posts as Conservative Party political spokesperson and Parliamentary Group chair says that she will never return to politics after the general election which is due this year.
“I have no intention of ever going into politics again,” Kjær tells Ekstra Bladet.
