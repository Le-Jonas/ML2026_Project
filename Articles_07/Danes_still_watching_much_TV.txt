Title: Danes_still_watching_much_TV
Date: 2010-12-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Each Dane over the age of three spends three hours and 20 minutes in front of the television each day according to the national broadcaster DR’s annual ‘Media Development 2010’ report which is due out in January.
The figures have surprised media researchers and advertising agencies who have been forecasting that people will create their own personal media world on the Internet and in mobile media, and slowly drop television.
