Title: Govt._majority_endangered
Date: 2010-11-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Liberal MP Eyvind Vesselbo says that he is not immediately prepared to support the government’s immigration package and is demanding a hearing in the Liberal Party parliamentary group prior to a final agreement with the Danish People’s Party.
“A condition is that we have a good dialogue in the Liberal group about all of this,” Vesselbo says, adding that the 47 Liberal Party MPs have not had a proper chance to discuss the agreement being forged.
