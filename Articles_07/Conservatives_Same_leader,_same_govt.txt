Title: Conservatives_Same_leader,_same_govt
Date: 2010-12-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Conservative Leader and Foreign Minister Lene Espersen has rejected criticism of her party leadership and says that her party will continue to aim to continue in government after an election.
Her Conservative colleague, Justice Minister Lars Barfoed has suggested that the Conservatives would not necessarily continue in government. Espersen says that such views are not Conservative policy.
