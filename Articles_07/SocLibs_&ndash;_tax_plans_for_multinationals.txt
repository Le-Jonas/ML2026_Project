Title: SocLibs_&ndash;_tax_plans_for_multinationals
Date: 2011-05-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Liberal Party (SLP), which has supported the government’s retirement plan but wants a change in government, has put forward its own 2020 Plan, among other things calling for 600 new tax inspectors to keep an eye on multinationals and their tax returns.
The plan put forward today by the SLP, which can become the kingmaker following the next election, is looking to balance the economy in 2020, increase available jobs by 135,000 and create an annual growth of DKK 18 billion.
