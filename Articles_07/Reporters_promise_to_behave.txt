Title: Reporters_promise_to_behave
Date: 2011-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Parliamentary media and Parliament’s Presidium have entered into a gentlemen’s agreement to stop the practice of hounding politicians as they move around the House.
The agreement comes after the Conservative Party complained to the Presidium after the media more or less besieged party politicians in connection with Lene Espersen’s decision to give up the party leadership and a later incident during which Henriette Kjær gave up her post as political spokeswoman.
