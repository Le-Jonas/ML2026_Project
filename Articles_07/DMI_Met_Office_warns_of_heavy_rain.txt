Title: DMI_Met_Office_warns_of_heavy_rain
Date: 2011-08-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Met Office has issued a mild warning for heavy rain in most parts of the country, with the exception of Northern Jutland.
Local downpours may dump as much as 24 mm of rain over six hours, but DMI Duty Officer Jørn Thomsen says the drains should be able to manage the volume of water.
