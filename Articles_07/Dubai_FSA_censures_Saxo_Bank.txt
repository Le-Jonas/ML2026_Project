Title: Dubai_FSA_censures_Saxo_Bank
Date: 2011-03-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Dubai Financial Supervisory Authority (DFSA) has censured the Danish investment bank Saxo Bank’s Dubai operation for breaking anti-money laundering safeguard rules and for non-compliance with client intake rules.
The bank has responded that while it accepts the authority’s censure, it had acted in good faith and has now put appropriate measure in place to rectify its shortcomings.
