Title: Pension_company_accused_of_outright_theft
Date: 2010-11-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Financial Supervisory Authority has given the go-ahead to the Sampension pension group to stop the interest guarantees on the pensions savings of thousands of Danes, according to Børsen.
“The biggest theft in human memory,” Finanshuset CEO and one of Denmark best-known financial advisers Kim Valentin tells Børsen.
