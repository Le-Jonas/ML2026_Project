Title: Dane_arrested_in_global_childporn_ring
Date: 2011-08-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

At least one Dane is among 52 people who have been arrested in various countries in connection with an international paedophile ring operating on the Internet.
The arrests come following an extensive investigation since 2009 into the Dreamland online community in which 500 people have been investigated and 72 have been indicted, according to the US Attorney General Eric Holder.
