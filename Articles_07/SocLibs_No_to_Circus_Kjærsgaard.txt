Title: SocLibs_No_to_Circus_Kjærsgaard
Date: 2011-05-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Liberals have definitively made it clear that they will not support the government’s 2020 Plan if it also involves a tightening of immigration rules as proposed by the Danish People’s Party.
The Danish People’s Party, which is currently also negotiating with the government on its 2020 Plan, is calling for 100 of the leading members of immigrant gangs to be immediately deported from Denmark even if they have not committed any crime.
