Title: Politicians_to_press_Enough_is_enough!
Date: 2011-06-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two of Denmark's political heavyweights ploughed into the media Thursday, accusing the press of pettiness, partisanship and onerous outbursts.
Speaking at the Popular Politics Festival in Bornholm this morning, Integration Minister Søren Pind (Lib) and Social Democratic Tax Spokesman Nick Hækkerup shook hands and agreed ‘Enough is enough’ before launching into a tirade against a media seen as being too hung up on personal issues and too little on substantial ones.
