Title: Espersen_goes_for_tax_election
Date: 2011-01-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Just shortly after the Conservative Party has managed to convince the Liberals to propose the abolition of early retirement, the Conservative Leader Lene Espersen says she wants top-end tax and company tax to be reduced.
Espersen says that no-one should pay more than 50 percent in tax of the last-earned kroner and does not believe that her tax proposals are linked to the early retirement issue, according to Berlingske Tidende.
