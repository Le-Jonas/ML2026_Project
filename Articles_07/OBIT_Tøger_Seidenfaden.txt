Title: OBIT_Tøger_Seidenfaden
Date: 2011-01-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Tøger Seidenfaden, Politiken’s editor-in-chief and one of Denmark’s leading media personalities and debaters has died at the age of 53 following a lengthy cancer illness.
Seidenfaden was appointed editor-in-chief of Politiken in 1993, prior to which he was director of TV2 and editor-in-chief of the Weekendavisen weekly newspaper.
