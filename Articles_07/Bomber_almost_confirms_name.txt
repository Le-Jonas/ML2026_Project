Title: Bomber_almost_confirms_name
Date: 2010-09-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police say they are almost 100 percent certain that they now have the identity of the man they have been holding in custody since last Friday’s explosion at a Copenhagen hotel.
“We don’t have him 100 percent identified, but we’re pretty certain,” says Chief Inspector Svend Foldager who adds that the man has indicated that the name put to him could be his.
