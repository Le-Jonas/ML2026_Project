Title: Ro-Ro_Rolex
Date: 2011-01-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Tax Minister Troels Lund Poulsen has handed over a controversial Rolex watch he received during a foreign visit last year to the Tax Authority.
Lund Poulsen, who was environment minister at the time, received the watch from Qatar’s Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al-Thani last January.
