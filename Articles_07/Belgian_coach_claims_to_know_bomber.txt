Title: Belgian_coach_claims_to_know_bomber
Date: 2010-09-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Following the release of new police photographs yesterday, a Belgian boxing coach and police officer has told B.T. that he can identify the man detained in Denmark in connection with last Friday’s hotel explosion, and whose identity has not yet been established.
According to Albert Syben, who coaches boxing at the Boxing Club Cocktail club in Liège, the man is 24 and one of his pupils.
