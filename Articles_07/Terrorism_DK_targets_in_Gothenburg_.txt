Title: Terrorism_DK_targets_in_Gothenburg_
Date: 2010-11-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danske Bank and the Danish Consulate in Gothenburg may have been the targets for a terrorist attack that four men are suspected of preparing, according to Aftonbladet.
The two Danish institutions are opposite a large mall in the city and have been named as places that the four men had planned to bomb.
