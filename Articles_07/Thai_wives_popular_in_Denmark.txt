Title: Thai_wives_popular_in_Denmark
Date: 2011-01-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

What may have started as a holiday trip to Phuket, Pattaya or Bangkok is increasingly ending up in marriage for Danish men.
The latest figures from Statistics Denmark show 400 marriages between Danish men and Thai women in 2008 and 361 in 2009. The average figure for the 2000-2009 period was 201 each year.
