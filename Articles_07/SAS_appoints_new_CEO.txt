Title: SAS_appoints_new_CEO
Date: 2010-09-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A replacement for SAS CEO Mats Jansson has been found. Scandinavian Airlines has appointed Rickard Gustafson, currently CEO of insurance giant Codan/Trygg-Hansa, as new chief executive.
“Rickard Gustafson is an experienced leader with solid international experience. He has demonstrated that he can create good results,” says SAS Board Chairman Fritz Schur.
