Title: Vestas_Small_profit_for_first_six_months
Date: 2011-08-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish wind energy producer Vestas has held its head above water in the first six months of 2011.
According to its H1 accounts, the company has managed to produce a profit of DKK 59 million in the first six months of 2011, compared to a loss of DKK 1.6 billion in the same period in 2010.
