Title: Hunger_strike_Kurd_to_hospital
Date: 2010-09-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One of 30 Syrian Kurds whose asylum requests have been rejected and who have been on hunger strike outside Parliament since last Tuesday, has been admitted to hospital.
The Asylret (Right to Asylum) organisation says that the man began to vomit blood after which activists convinced him to waive his refusal to accept a drip.
