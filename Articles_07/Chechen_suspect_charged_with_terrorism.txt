Title: Chechen_suspect_charged_with_terrorism
Date: 2010-12-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Charges against a Chechen, who has been on remand since September 10, 2010 following an explosion at a Copenhagen hotel, have been extended to include crimes under Denmark’s terrorism laws.
LD, 24, a Belgium domiciled former boxer, has been on remand since a minor explosion at the hotel on lesser charges of producing an explosive and being in possession of a loaded firearm.
