Title: Danes_see_early_retirement_being_dropped
Date: 2010-10-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A majority of Danes see early retirement being dropped within the next decade, according to a new Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2.
According to the poll, a full 74 percent say the scheme will have been dropped within 10 years, irrespective of which party they belong to.
