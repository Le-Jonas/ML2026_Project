Title: All_guilty_in_mega_theft_case
Date: 2010-09-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The court in Glostrup has returned a verdict of guilty on all 14 people accused of carrying out Denmark’s biggest ever robbery in 2008. Sentence is to be passed at a later date.
The DKK 60 million armed robbery took place on August 10, 2008 when a group of men smashed their way into the Dansk Værdihåndtering cash transit company in Brøndby, using a 22-ton loader tractor as a battering ram and sealing off surrounding roads with stolen garbage trucks which were set on fire.
