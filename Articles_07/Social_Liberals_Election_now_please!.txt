Title: Social_Liberals_Election_now_please!
Date: 2011-01-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Liberal Leader Margrethe Vestager is urging Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen (Lib) to call the 2011 election now, saying that there is no reason to wait following the announcement of the government’s retirement reform. Elections are due in Denmark in November 2011 at the latest.
“Løkke has to call the election now,” Vestager says.
