Title: Central_Bank_Sharpen_the_cut-back_knife
Date: 2010-09-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Central Bank Governor Nils Bernstein has presented his economic analysis, saying that if public consumption is not cut back it will result in an unsustainable public deficit.
Based on budget overruns in recent years, the central bank’s latest economic forecast suggests higher growth in public spending than envisaged in the government’s latest economic report.
