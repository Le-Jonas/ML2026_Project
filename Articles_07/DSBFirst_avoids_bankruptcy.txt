Title: DSBFirst_avoids_bankruptcy
Date: 2011-06-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After several weeks of intense negotiations between the Danish national railways DSB and Swedish transport authorities, parties appear to have found a solution for the troubled rail service provider DSBFirst.
According to reports, the agreement secures continued Øresundstrafikken services in both Denmark and Sweden and avoids what seemed to be an almost unavoidable bankruptcy for DSBFirst.
