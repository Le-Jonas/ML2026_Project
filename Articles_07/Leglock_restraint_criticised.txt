Title: Leglock_restraint_criticised
Date: 2011-08-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There are demands for a review of the use of leglocks on violent prisoners and arrestees following a report on the death in custody of 23-year-old Ekrem Sahim in January.
Sahim died after having been placed in a leglock by seven custody officers at a detention centre in Kolding.
