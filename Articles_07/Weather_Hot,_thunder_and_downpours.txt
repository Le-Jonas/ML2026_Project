Title: Weather_Hot,_thunder_and_downpours
Date: 2011-06-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Temperatures will be pushing 28 degrees centigrade today, but a change in weather is on the way with summer downpours and thunder, according to Denmark’s Meteorological Institute.
Some areas are even likely to get torrential bursts of rain, beginning this afternoon and spreading from the southwest and continuing on Tuesday with temperatures of up to 25 degrees and rain and thunder.
