Title: Illegal_rejections_from_ministry
Date: 2011-01-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

For several years, and up until 2010, the Integration Ministry has ignored a United Nations convention and refused citizenship to at least 22 young stateless Palestinians.
“The ministry’s practice has been illegal as we have acted in contravention of the convention,” says Aarhus University Law Prof. Jens Vedsted-Hansen after having seen Information’s report on the issue.
