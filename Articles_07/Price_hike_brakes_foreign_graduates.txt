Title: Price_hike_brakes_foreign_graduates
Date: 2011-03-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Foreigners who come to Denmark with further education are often faced with the problem that their degrees are not accepted and they therefore have to study the Danish language at a higher level in order to get jobs.
But from January 1, graduates with foreign degrees or further education have had to pay the full price of language training at VUC (Adult and Youth Education Centres) courses, which for most is the only advanced language training available.
