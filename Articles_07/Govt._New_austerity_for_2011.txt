Title: Govt._New_austerity_for_2011
Date: 2010-12-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A new wave of public spending austerity is on its way for next year, with the government suggesting negative growth at -0.3 percent in 2011, according to the government’s Economic Outlook.
In its August report, the government said public consumption would be at 0.6 percent and at 0.5 percent in 2012, according to Ritzau.
