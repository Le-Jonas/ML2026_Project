Title: Nigerian_prostitutes_to_be_deported
Date: 2010-09-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Immigration Service has decided to deport 13 of 18 prostitutes who were arrested earlier this week in Vesterbro in Copenhagen.
Sixteen of those arrested were ethnic Nigerians, one came from another African country and the last from South America. Four of the group have been released, as they carry European passports – three from Italy and one from Belgium.
