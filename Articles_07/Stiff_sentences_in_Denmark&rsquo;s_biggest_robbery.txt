Title: Stiff_sentences_in_Denmark&rsquo;s_biggest_robbery
Date: 2010-09-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The municipal court in Copenhagen’s Glostrup suburb has passed down six seven-year sentences, six eight-year sentences and one 10-year sentence in a case involving the country's biggest ever robbery. The fourteenth robber was committed to psychiatric care for an indeterminate period.
The armed robbery against cash handling company Dansk Værdihåndtering in Glostrup took place in 2008. Only just over DKK 3 million of the DKK 60 million haul has been retrieved.
