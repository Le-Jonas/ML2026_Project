Title: Students_blockade_higher_education
Date: 2010-11-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Hundreds of students have blockaded their Copenhagen schools of higher education this morning in protest against the government’s savings plans.
Denmark’s seven vocational high schools are to save some DKK 144 million, or some DKK 3,000 per student, next year. The schools train among others teachers, day-care teachers, nurses, Social workers, midwives and occupational therapists.
