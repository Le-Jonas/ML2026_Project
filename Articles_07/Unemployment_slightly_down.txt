Title: Unemployment_slightly_down
Date: 2011-01-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest figures from Statistics Denmark show that overall unemployment has stabilised at 6.1 percent based on a slight drop in jobless from October to November 2010.
November showed 900 fewer unemployed, cancelling out the increase of 400 from September to October. There were 167,500 unemployed overall in November.
