Title: Bookmakers_go_for_Thorning
Date: 2011-01-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The bookmakers seem to agree on the odds for the Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning Schmidt becoming the next prime minister of Denmark following an election.
The lowest odds Politiken has found are with Bet365 which offers a 1.40 return on a Thorning-Schmidt bet and 2.75 return on Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen keeping his portfolio. Scandic bookmakers have the two at 1.50 and 2.45 respectively.
