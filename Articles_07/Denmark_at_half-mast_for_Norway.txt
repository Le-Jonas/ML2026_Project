Title: Denmark_at_half-mast_for_Norway
Date: 2011-07-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Justice Ministry has ordered all public buildings to fly the national flag at half-mast from noon Saturday in order to show the country’s official support for Norway following a right-wing terrorist attack in Oslo and a youth camp outside the Norwegian capital in which 91 people were killed.
All public buildings around the country are to fly the Danish flag at half-mast, but no requirement or recommendation has been placed on private organisations or buildings.
