Title: Daylight_armed_robbery_on_Strøget
Date: 2010-10-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There has been an armed robbery at a jewellery shop on Copenhagen’s main pedestrian street Strøget this afternoon.
“They used a gun to threaten staff” the Copenhagen duty officer says of the four men who entered the Ole Mathiesen jewellery shop at Østergade 8.
