Title: Egyptian_Embassy_demo_planned
Date: 2011-01-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Egyptians living in Denmark are to demonstrate in front of their embassy in Copenhagen on Saturday to show solidarity with their countrymen who have taken to the streets to demonstrate against the Egyptian regime.
“We want to show them that the rest of the world is watching,” says Dina Youssef, one of the organizers of Saturday’s demonstration.
