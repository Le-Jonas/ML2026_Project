Title: Porridge_oats_warning
Date: 2011-01-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Food Standards Agency has issued a warning concerning certain porridge oats, produced on Nov. 30th, 2010 and with a best by date of Nov. 29, 2011, as a result of a too high content of a fungal toxin.
