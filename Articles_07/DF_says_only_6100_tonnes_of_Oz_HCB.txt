Title: DF_says_only_6100_tonnes_of_Oz_HCB
Date: 2010-12-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party, which is the government’s safety net in Parliament, says that after Denmark has received 6,100 tonnes of toxic HCB waste from Australia for destruction, no more should be taken.
The issue of whether vessels can begin to freight the highly toxic fungicide from Australia to Kommunekemi in Nyborg has been raised after it became clear that an accident preparedness plan has not yet been developed for the area around the facility.
