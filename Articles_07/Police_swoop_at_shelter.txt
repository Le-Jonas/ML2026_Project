Title: Police_swoop_at_shelter
Date: 2010-12-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police carried out a control operation against foreign homeless people at the En Varm Seng (A warm bed) shelter in the early hours of this morning, arresting 69 of the 100 present.
“Now we have to determine whether they are allowed to be in Denmark or whether they should be deported,” the Copenhagen Police Duty Officer says, adding that apart from checking identities, police also searched the premises for narcotics.
