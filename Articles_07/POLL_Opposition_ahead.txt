Title: POLL_Opposition_ahead
Date: 2011-05-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After two intensive weeks of political agreement-mongering, the latest Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 shows the opposition alliance still ahead.
Were an election to be held now, a clear majority would vote for a change in government, with the Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning-Schmidt as prime minister.
