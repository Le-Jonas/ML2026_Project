Title: Opposition_still_ahead_in_polls
Date: 2011-04-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Despite a cabinet reshuffle, operations in Libya and government proposals for new principles for welfare, the opposition has maintained a solid lead in the latest Megafon opinion poll for Politiken and TV2.
If an election were held today, the poll suggests that 53.8 per cent of the electorate would vote in favour of the Social Democrat-led opposition, while 46.2 per cent would vote for the government alliance.
