Title: Wikileaks_Ex_US_ambassador_on_DK
Date: 2010-12-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The former US Ambassador to Denmark James Cain has been interviewed by a local North Carolina TV station on the type of content of some of the 297 Wikileak documents that involve Denmark.
James Cain, who is now an attorney in Raleigh, North Carolina was ambassador to Denmark from 2005-2009 and says that he wrote some of the documents which, among other things, involved Denmark’s efforts in Afghanistan and his candid view of up-and-coming Danish political leaders.
