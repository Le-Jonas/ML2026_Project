Title: Support_for_DR_Arabic_news
Date: 2010-11-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Liberals are proposing that the Danish broadcasting corporation DR should develop a television channel targeted at immigrant groups who do not speak Danish.
““We are suggesting that DR, as part of its Public Service commitments, broadcasts more news from Denmark, and international news with a Danish angle in some of the major foreign languages such as Arabic, Turkish and English,” says Social Liberal Chairwoman Marianne Vestager.
