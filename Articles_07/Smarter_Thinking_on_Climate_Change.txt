Title: Smarter_Thinking_on_Climate_Change
Date: 2010-10-31
Article Type: World views
Author: Kenneth Lund

Journalist og debatredaktør
Bjørn Lomborg
COPENHAGEN – Politicians and commentators are understandably pessimistic about the chances of an international deal on carbon cuts emerging from the United Nations summit in Mexico this December.
Nothing has been resolved since the Copenhagen climate talks fell apart last year. Fortunately, recent research points to a smarter way to tackle climate change.
