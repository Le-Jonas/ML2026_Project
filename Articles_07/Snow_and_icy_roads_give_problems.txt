Title: Snow_and_icy_roads_give_problems
Date: 2010-11-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Rescue services across the country have been busy overnight and this morning as the year’s first snow, drifting snow and icy conditions have made many roads treacherous.
“The situation is worst in Northern and Eastern Jutland,” says Falck Spokesman Jeppe Ilkjær, adding that drifting snow has been the main problem.
