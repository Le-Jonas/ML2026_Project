Title: Løkke_R._sees_exile_for_Gaddafi
Date: 2011-04-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen says he would consider a solution to the Libyan conflict that included Col. Gaddafi going into exile, even if that exile meant he could avoid prosecution by the International Court of Justice.
“One should never rule out anything in an attempt to find a quick and efficient solution to what could otherwise be a stalemate,” the prime minister says, adding it would be a good thing if Gaddafi left the country.
