Title: Warship_on_way_to_Danish_vessel
Date: 2011-01-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A cargo of weapons may have been ship-jacked by pirates in the Gulf of Aden yesterday when a Danish ship reported it was under fire.
There has been no contact with the 2,000-ton Leopard since that initial report, and a warship is currently under way to the vessel’s position.
