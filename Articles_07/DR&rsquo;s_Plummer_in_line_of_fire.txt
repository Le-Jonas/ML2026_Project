Title: DR&rsquo;s_Plummer_in_line_of_fire
Date: 2010-10-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Director General of Danmarks Radio (DR) Kenneth Plummer has come under fire following revelations that he offered a journalist normally confidential DR material, in return for the journalist refraining from writing about his private life in a book.
The DR Board has ordered Plummer to report on the issue.
