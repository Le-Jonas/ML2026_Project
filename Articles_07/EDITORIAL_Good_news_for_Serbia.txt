Title: EDITORIAL_Good_news_for_Serbia
Date: 2011-05-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Finally. Ratko Mladi? should have been prosecuted long ago.
The massacre of 8,000 Muslims in the Bosnian town of Srebrenica in 1995, which according to the United Nations International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia (ICTY) in the Hague was led by a bloodsoaked, now 68-year-old general, is a stain on the honour of the Serbian nation. It is also a disgrace that until yesterday Serbia had not facilitated his appearance before a judge.
