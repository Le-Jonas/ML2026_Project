Title: Pilots_knowingly_break_safety_laws
Date: 2011-04-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish pilots systematically break the law that is designed to prevent them from arriving too fatigued for work, falling asleep in the cockpit and making mistakes that can lead to accidents.
The law stipulates that pilots must declare themselves unfit for flight if they feel so tired that they can risk making mistakes.
