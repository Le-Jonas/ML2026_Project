Title: 16_bikers_in_court
Date: 2011-03-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Heavily armed police were on guard at the Glostrup City Court Monday, where 16 bikers with affiliations to the Hells Angels go on trial in one of Denmark’s most extensive criminal cases.
All 16 from the Hells Angels group and their AK81 support group all deny charges in connection with six attempted murders, causing grievous bodily harm and being in possession of weapons as well as materiel that can be used for a remote controlled bomb.
