Title: Fehmarn_tunnel_expected
Date: 2011-02-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danes can drive across the Great Belt on a bridge, and from Denmark to Sweden across a bridge, but in a few years they will probably be driving under the Fehmarn Belt from Denmark to Germany.
Denmark’s Parliamentary parties were meeting Tuesday to agree on the type of crossing – a bridge or a tunnel – should link the two countries across the Fehmarn Belt in a project worth some DKK 38 million. The result of their deliberations seems a foregone conclusion.
