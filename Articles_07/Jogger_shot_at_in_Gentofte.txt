Title: Jogger_shot_at_in_Gentofte
Date: 2010-11-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A jogger was hit by several shots during a run in Bernstorffsparken in Gentofte this morning, according to the N. Zealand police.
“He was hit three or four times in the stomach, chest and left arm, but was able to call for help himself,” says Police Spokesman Flemming Poulsen.
