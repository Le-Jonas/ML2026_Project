Title: Soc._Libs._reject_points_system
Date: 2010-11-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Democrats and Socialist People’s Party are at odds with one of their alliance partners over an immigration points system for foreign spouse reunion policy.
The Social Liberal Leader Margrethe Vestager has categorically rejected, and said her party will not vote for, the two parties’ points system for foreign spouse reunions, which has been presented as an alternative to the government’s new system.
