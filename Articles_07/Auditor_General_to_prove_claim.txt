Title: Auditor_General_to_prove_claim
Date: 2010-10-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Auditor General Henrik Otbo is to meet the national auditors next week to furnish proof of allegations that the Health Ministry deliberately withheld information from him in connection with overpayments by the national health service to private hospitals.
“The Auditor General must be given the information asked for. Those are the rules. But I will not draw any conclusions until I have heard his report and found out what was important for his investigation,” says National Auditor Peter Christensen (Lib).
