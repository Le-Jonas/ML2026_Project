Title: Minister_on_school_discipline_drive
Date: 2010-11-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Education Minister Tina Nedergaard (Lib) wants to give schools a better tool to bring more discipline to classrooms by forcing parents to immediately come and pick up their young brats, according to Jyllands-Posten.
“We need more discipline in schools. At the moment we seem to have reverse corporal punishment where the children hit the teachers. It’s not on,” says Nedergaard.
