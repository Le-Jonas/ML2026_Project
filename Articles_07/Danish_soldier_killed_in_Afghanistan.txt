Title: Danish_soldier_killed_in_Afghanistan
Date: 2010-09-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Danish soldier was killed and another seriously wounded this morning when an explosion detonated south of Patrol Base Bridzar northeast of Gereshk in Afghanistan, according to Army Operative Command.
The men were on a Royal Lifeguard Charlie Company foot patrol at 7.40 local time when the explosion took place. Both were evacuated by helicopter to Camp Bastion where one of the two was dead on arrival. The other soldier is currently undergoing treatment.
