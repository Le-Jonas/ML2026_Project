Title: Chaos_on_capital_trains_today
Date: 2010-11-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen S-train services are having major problems today as a result of a dropped cable between Copenhagen’s main railway station and Østerport Station, with train authorities saying that the problems may continue for most of the day.
“Our early forecast was that there will be no trains between Østerport and the main station Hovedbanegaard for the rest of the day – but that can change for the better or worse,” says Information Officer Tina Nørgaard Andersen.
