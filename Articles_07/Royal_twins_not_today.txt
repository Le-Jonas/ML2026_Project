Title: Royal_twins_not_today
Date: 2011-01-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Royal Court has denied media suggestions that Crown Princess Mary is, or plans to be hospitalised today to induce the delivery of the Crown couple's twins.
"It's a hoax. Crown Princess Mary is neither hospitalised nor plans to be hospitalised today," says Royal Court Communications Chief Lene Balleby.
