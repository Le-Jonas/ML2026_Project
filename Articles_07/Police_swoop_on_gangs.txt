Title: Police_swoop_on_gangs
Date: 2011-02-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Several hundred police officers swooped on Copenhagen gang members Wednesday in an early morning sting to try to curb gang violence.
Large numbers of officers are present in Tingbjerg, Høje Gladsaxe and Værebroparken in Bagsværd. As well as at addresses in Skovlunde, Herlev and Ballerup.
