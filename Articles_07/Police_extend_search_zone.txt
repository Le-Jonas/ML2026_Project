Title: Police_extend_search_zone
Date: 2011-03-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police have extended their personal search zone from 2 p.m. Monday to include most of the Nørrebro district of the capital .
The decision to extend the area in which police officers may search without cause, comes following four shooting episodes in the past week and what is said to be the result of an ‘apparent escalation of gang conflict’.
