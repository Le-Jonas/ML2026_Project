Title: Odense_students_evacuated_in_bomb_threat
Date: 2011-05-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two adult education centres in Odense have been evacuated following a bomb threat issued shortly before 9 a.m. this morning, according to Funen police.
According to the report, the threat was issued against the VUC centres on Enggade and Hunderupvej in Odense by an English-speaking man who warned the threat should be taken seriously.
