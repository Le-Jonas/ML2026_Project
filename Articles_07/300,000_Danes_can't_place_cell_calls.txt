Title: 300,000_Danes_can't_place_cell_calls
Date: 2011-03-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

About 300,000 pre-paid cellphone clients across Denmark, whose simcards are with TDC and several smaller companies, are unable to place calls from their mobile telephones at the moment.
“It seems that the system has flagged all pre-paid customers as inactive, but more than that we don’t know right now,” says TDC Media Consultant Rune Fick Hansen.
