Title: SKAT_chasing_1000_billion
Date: 2010-09-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish tax authority SKAT is investigating some DKK 1,000 billion that Danish companies and private individuals have transferred to and from some 55 tax havens, according to a TV-avisen report.
In the light of SKAT’s new figures, Minister for Taxation Troels Lund Poulsen (Lib) has promised the authority extra resources to continue investigations into what is being billed as the biggest Danish tax scandal ever.
