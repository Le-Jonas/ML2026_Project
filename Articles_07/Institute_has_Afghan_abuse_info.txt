Title: Institute_has_Afghan_abuse_info
Date: 2010-09-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish soldiers were involved in torture-like abuse in Afghanistan in 2002, according to allegations in information passed on to the Danish Institute for Human Rights by a person presenting himself as a Danish soldier.
The soldier says he is aware of the existence of pictures of abuse from the time when he was stationed in Afghanistan.
