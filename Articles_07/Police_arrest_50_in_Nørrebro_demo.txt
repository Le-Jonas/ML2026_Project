Title: Police_arrest_50_in_Nørrebro_demo
Date: 2011-05-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

An unannounced demonstration in Copenhagen’s Nørrebro quarter has resulted in 50 arrests near the Dronning Louise bridge.
Over half of the 80-odd demonstrators refused to be searched, to leave the area or say what the illegal demonstration was about.
