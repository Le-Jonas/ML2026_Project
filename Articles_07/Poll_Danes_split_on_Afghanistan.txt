Title: Poll_Danes_split_on_Afghanistan
Date: 2011-01-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Support for Denmark’s participation in the war in Afghanistan has increased somewhat since last summer and seems to have split the Danes into two almost equal camps.
According to a Rambøll Analyse Danmark poll for Jyllands-Posten, 48.9 percent of those asked want Danish combat soldiers to be withdrawn from Afghanistan while 46.1 percent want them to continue their mission for the moment.
