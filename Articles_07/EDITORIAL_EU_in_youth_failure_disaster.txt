Title: EDITORIAL_EU_in_youth_failure_disaster
Date: 2011-05-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The most ardent observers see the North African Twitter revolution as having washed across the Mediterranean.
Tens of thousands of young Spaniards have taken over Puerta del Sol Square in Madrid to protest against mass unemployment that has now reached 21 per cent and the dramatic cutbacks that Prime Minister José Luis Zapatero has been forced to introduce in order to avoid economic collapse.
