Title: Conservatives_eye_SDP_cooperation
Date: 2011-06-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

If the Social Democrats win the next election, the Conservative Party is willing to cooperate with a new government constellation.
The Conservative Leader Lars Barfoed says it is time to end the bloc politics that has dominated Danish politics for the past decade due to the close cooperation between the Liberals, Conservatives and Danish People’s Party.
