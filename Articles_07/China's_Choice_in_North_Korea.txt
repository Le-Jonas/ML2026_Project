Title: China's_Choice_in_North_Korea
Date: 2010-11-29
Article Type: World views
Author: Kenneth Lund

Journalist og debatredaktør
Yuriko Koike
TOKYO – If the most dangerous moment for any dictatorship is when it starts to reform, North Korea looks ready to turn that truism on its head.
Its recent shelling of South Korea suggests that the failing Kim dynasty might set East Asia alight rather than undertake any serious reform. If peace really is the key component of China’s rise, the Chinese must now rein in their mercurial client.
