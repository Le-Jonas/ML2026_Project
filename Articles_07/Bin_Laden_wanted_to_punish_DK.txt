Title: Bin_Laden_wanted_to_punish_DK
Date: 2011-05-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Evidence seized by American forces at Osama bin Laden’s home in Abbottabad in Pakistan shows that the former leader of al-Qaeda urged Islamists to attack targets in Denmark, according to two American officials.
The Associated Press (AP) quotes the two officials as saying that bin Laden was intent on punishing Denmark for publishing caricatures of the Prophet Mohammed.
