Title: Danes_may_lose_early_retirement_contributions
Date: 2011-01-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Hundreds of thousands of Danes under 60 years of age may lose their contributions to the early retirement scheme in the 1980s and 1990s if the government gets its early retirement reform through Parliament, according to Berlingske Tidende.
From 1982 to 1999, the early retirement scheme was financed through the level of contributions to unemployment insurance funds being increased considerably.
