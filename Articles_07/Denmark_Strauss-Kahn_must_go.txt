Title: Denmark_Strauss-Kahn_must_go
Date: 2011-05-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Finance Minister Claus Hjort Frederiksen (Lib) has joined a growing international chorus calling on Dominique Strauss-Kahn to resign his post as head of the International Monetary Fund.
“In a situation in which we are in an economic crisis, and in which the Greek economy is breaking down, with several other countries possibly following, it is extremely difficult that the head of the IMF has such a case. It is intolerable,” Hjort Frederiksen tells TV2News.
