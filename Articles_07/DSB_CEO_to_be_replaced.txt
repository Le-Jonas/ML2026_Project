Title: DSB_CEO_to_be_replaced
Date: 2011-03-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Board of the Danish state railways DSB has removed the corporation’s CEO Søren Eriksen, according to Board Chairman Mogens Granborg.
“Yes, the Board has decided to do that. The reason is the auditor report in the accounts with unsatisfactory wording on cooperation between DSB and DSB First,” Granborg tells Ritzau.
