Title: Teacher&rsquo;s_body_found
Date: 2011-04-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The body of a 44-year-old teacher, who drowned during a serious dragon-boat incident in February, has been found in Præstø Fjord, according to police in southern Zealand.
The teacher and fourteen others were on board a dragon-boat which capsized in bad weather on February 11th. Thirteen young people and a woman teacher were saved, but rescue personnel were not able to find the male teacher.
