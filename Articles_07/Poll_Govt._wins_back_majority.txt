Title: Poll_Govt._wins_back_majority
Date: 2010-09-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

For the first time this year, the centre-right has won back a majority, according to the latest Megafon poll for Politken, which is the first since the conclusion of the Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning-Schmidt’s family tax issue.
According to the poll, if an election were held today, the government and its supporters would garner 50.8 percent of the vote, with the Social Democratic-led opposition winning 49.2 percent.
