Title: TV2_erases_Løkke_footage
Date: 2010-10-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The TV2 Board is expected to become involved in an issue that has got tempers flying at the television station – whether station management let itself be pressured into not showing some less than flattering footage of the prime minister.
The incident took place two weeks ago as Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen was leaving TV2’s headquarters following an interview. Outside, a journalist and cameraman were waiting and asked Løkke Rasmussen to comment on claims of a cover-up in the ongoing controversy about national health payments to private hospitals.
