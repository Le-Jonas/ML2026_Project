Title: Liberal_Alliance_wants_answers
Date: 2011-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Pressure is increasing on Integration Minister Birthe Rønn Hornbech (Lib) in connection with the illegal rejection of nationality applications from stateless people.
The latest development in the case is an indication that Rønn Hornbech already knew in December 2008 that illegal rejections had been handed out by the authorities since 2002 and eventually until 2010.
