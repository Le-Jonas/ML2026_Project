Title: Gallup_shows_Liberals_advancing
Date: 2011-08-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A new Gallup poll for Berlingske shows the Liberal Party moving forward and the government alliance now only 1.4 percentage points from the opposition alliance.
The poll shows the opposition wining 50.6 per cent of the vote if an election were held today, while the government and its support parties would garner 49.2 per cent, according to Berlingske’s online edition.
