Title: Al-Qaeda_urges_4WD_attacks
Date: 2010-10-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Inspire Magazine, which is being dubbed the new propaganda organ for al-Qaeda, is again calling for attacks against among others Danish targets.
The latest edition of the magazine, which is said to be the brainchild of the radical preacher Anwar al-Awlaki, calls for attacks with four-wheel drive vehicles against pedestrians in populous areas in countries which support ‘the Israeli occupation of Palestine and the American invasion of Afghanistan and Iraq’.
