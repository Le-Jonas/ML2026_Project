Title: Search_WikiLeaks_at_politiken.dk
Date: 2010-12-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Politiken is helping to make sure that the US State Department documents that have already been published are available to the public.
To ensure Politiken’s editorial process, and continued availability for Politiken’s journalists despite possible closures of websites, the documents are being imported into Politiken’s own search data base.
