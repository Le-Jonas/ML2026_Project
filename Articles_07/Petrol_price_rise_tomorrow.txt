Title: Petrol_price_rise_tomorrow
Date: 2011-02-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

At 10 a.m. on Wednesday, the price of a litre of 95 octane goes up.
Statoil says that its list price will increase by 15 øre to 11.94 kr. (EUR 1.60, USD 2.16) per litre. Prices were also close to 12 kr. A month ago, but fell to around 11.50 kr.
