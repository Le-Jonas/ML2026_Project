Title: WIKI_Afghan_school_success_a_myth
Date: 2011-04-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish politicians stress that Afghan schools are a major success story in the country, but behind the optimistic statements that a record number of Afghan children now go to school, the reality is that children and teachers are attacked by militias.
US intelligence documents that Information has received through WikiLeaks show the motives, methods and systematic planning of violence against Afghan families who choose to send their children to schools supported by the West.
