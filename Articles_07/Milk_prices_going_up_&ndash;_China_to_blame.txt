Title: Milk_prices_going_up_&ndash;_China_to_blame
Date: 2011-02-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Supermarkets in Denmark have been offering relatively cheap milk for several years now – but consumers can expect price rises soon because of Chinese demand for milk powder, according to epn.dk.
New data from the USDA shows that China’s import of whole milk powder over the past two years has increased sevenfold to 320,000 tonnes, and this year imports will increase to 400,000 tonnes.
