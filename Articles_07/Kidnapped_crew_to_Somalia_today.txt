Title: Kidnapped_crew_to_Somalia_today
Date: 2011-01-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

NATO says that the five crewmembers kidnapped off the Danish vessel Leopard last Wednesday are expected to reach the Somali coast today on board a pirate mother ship the Shiuh Fu No. 1 – itself a shipjacked Taiwanese vessel.
“We expect the ship to reach (the coast) some time today,” says NATO spokesperson in London Lt. Comm. Jacqui Sherriff.
