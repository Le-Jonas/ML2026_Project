Title: Picasso_causes_run_on_museum
Date: 2011-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Louisiana Museum of Modern Art north of Copenhagen is experiencing a run on its most recent exhibition.
In last week’s winter vacation week, some 20,300 visitors flocked to the seafront Louisiana Museum of Modern Art to see more than 70 works by Picasso – 6,000 more visitors than were expected. The museum has introduced a shuttle bus from the local Humlebæk Station to cater for thousands arriving by train, and to prevent parking chaos.
