Title: Holck_not_to_be_extradited_to_India
Date: 2011-07-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Despite Indian government representations to appeal last week’s High Court verdict not to extradite Niels Holck to India on charges of gunrunning, Denmark’s Director of Public Prosecutions has decided not to appeal the ruling to the Supreme Court.
“My decision means that the Eastern High Court ruling not to extradite Niels Holck stands,” Director of Public Prosecutions Jørgen Steen Sørensen said in a written statement.
