Title: Govt._not_essential_for_Conservatives
Date: 2011-07-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After a decade with a Lib-Con government, the Conservative Party may find it opportune to stay out of a new centre-right cabinet, according to party officials.
“You should place yourself where you have most influence – and if that is as a support party for a centre-right government, that is where we should be. As things are at the moment, it might be a good idea to take a period out of cabinet in order to emphasise Conservative views,” says Copenhagen Conservative Chirman Andreas Boisen.
