When Anders Fogh Rasmussen first visited Moscow after being appointed Secretary General of NATO, he was treated to a cold shoulder from his Russian counterparts.
After the meeting, Fogh said that his meetings with Russian leaders had been promising – but a confidential note to the US State Department tells a different story.
