Title: SDP_ready_to_tweak_retirement
Date: 2011-04-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Finance Minister Claus Hjort Frederiksen begins negotiations with parliamentary parties today on the government’s 2020 economic plan, with the opposition Social Democrats invited to initial negotiations first.
On Wednesday, the government announced its 2020 Plan, which included reforms of the early retirement system, a student grant reform, reforms of the incapacity benefit and flex-job systems and stricter public expenditure management.
