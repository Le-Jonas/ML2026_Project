Title: Israel's_Wrong_Friends
Date: 2011-01-12
Article Type: World views
Author: Kenneth Lund

Journalist og debatredaktør
Ian Buruma
JERUSALEM – Israel has been welcoming some rather peculiar visitors of late.
The Dutch populist, Geert Wilders, is a frequent caller, telling sympathetic audiences that Israel is on the front line of the Western war against Islam. And, in December, a delegation of European far-right politicians toured Jewish settlements on the occupied West Bank, pleasing their hosts by reassuring them that this was 'Jewish land'.
