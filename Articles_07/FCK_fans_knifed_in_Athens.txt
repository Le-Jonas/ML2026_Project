Title: FCK_fans_knifed_in_Athens
Date: 2010-09-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish embassy in Athens has confirmed that two Danish fans have been injured following an encounter with Greek fans in the city.
According to reports on MetroXpress.dk, the two are from the Urban Crew Fan Club and were injured during an incident in an Athens bar. Both are in hospital in the Greek capital.
