Title: Opposition_wants_public_hearings
Date: 2011-07-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

With Rupert Murdoch and Prime Minister David Cameron standing to attention before a committee in London, the opposition in Denmark is also looking to allow Parliament to be able to call in witnesses for the same sort of hearings as in the United Kingdom, according to Information.
According to the report, the Social Democrats, Socialist People’s Party, Social Liberals and Red Greens are working on an initiative to introduce hearings into the parliamentary remit.
