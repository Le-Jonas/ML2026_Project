Title: Eritrea_criticises_Danish_fundraiser
Date: 2011-08-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish broadcasting corporation DR and TV2 are to join forces later this month in a fundraiser for famine-hit areas around the Horn of Africa.
“Somalia, Ethiopia, Kenya, Eritrea and Djibouti need emergency aid,” the two channels said in a joint news release Friday – but one of the countries has responded with a more or less – ‘Thanks, but no thanks’.
