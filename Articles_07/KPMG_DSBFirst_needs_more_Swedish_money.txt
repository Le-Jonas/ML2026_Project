Title: KPMG_DSBFirst_needs_more_Swedish_money
Date: 2011-06-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The KPMG auditors report on the financial situation of DSB’s crisis-ridden DSBFirst company is due to be released today, according to DR.
The conclusions in the report suggest tough choices, with the company only able to survive if Swedish rail providers increase their contractual payments by more than DKK 450 million, or some DKK 10 million per month.
