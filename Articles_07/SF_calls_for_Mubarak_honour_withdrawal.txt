Title: SF_calls_for_Mubarak_honour_withdrawal
Date: 2011-01-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party is calling for Denmark’s highest recognition – The Order of the Elephant, which is normally only awarded to royals and heads of state - to be withdrawn from Egypt’s President Hosni Mubarak.
“It is a serious problem that the Danish state honours Mubarak,” says SF Human Rights and Development Spokesman Kamal Qureshi.
