Title: PM_expected_to_launch_early_retirement_reform
Date: 2010-12-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Sources close to the prime minster have told Politiken that it is ‘100 percent certain’ that Løkke Rasmussen will urge the Welfare Agreement parties to introduce a more ambitious reform of the early retirement system than that agreed in 2006.
At the same time, the Liberal Party has begun a major advertising campaign in today’s media in which the prime minister calls for reforms in 2011.
