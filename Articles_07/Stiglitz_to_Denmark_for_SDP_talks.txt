Title: Stiglitz_to_Denmark_for_SDP_talks
Date: 2011-05-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The 2001 Nobel Economics Laureate Joseph Stiglitz has accepted an invitation from the Danish Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning-Schmidt to come to Denmark for discussions on economic issues.
“I am delighted that Professor Stiglitz has has accepted my invitation to come to Denmark. Joseph is a highly interesting economist and I look forward to discussing the challenges of the world economy with him,” Thorning Schmidt tells jp.dk.
