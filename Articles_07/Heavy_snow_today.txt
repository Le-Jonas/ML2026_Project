Title: Heavy_snow_today
Date: 2010-12-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

An area of heavy snow is expected to spread to eastern regions and particularly the capital region this afternoon, with up to 10 cm. of snow, according to a Meteorological Office forecast.
“The Copenhagen area will be covered by about 10 cm. of snow,” says Duty Meteorological Officer Mogens Rønnebæk.
