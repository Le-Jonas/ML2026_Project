Title: PM_regrets_his_SocDem_claim
Date: 2010-12-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen says he regrets his claim in a recent TV debate that Social Democratic ministers jumped hospital queues by seeking treatment at private hospitals.
The prime minister’s statement comes after Social Democratic Deputy Chairman Nick Hækkerup called for proof of the allegations.
