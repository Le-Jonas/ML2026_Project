Title: Bomber_stays_in_isolation
Date: 2010-09-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Copenhagen Municipal Court has extended the remand in isolation of a man charged with an explosion at a Copenhagen hotel 10 days ago.
The man was originally remanded in custody until October 4th, with the first period in isolation. The court has determined that his period in isolation should be extended in order to allow police to continue their investigations.
