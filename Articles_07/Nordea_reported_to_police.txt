Title: Nordea_reported_to_police
Date: 2011-08-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Bankers Association has reported the Nordea bank to the police for not having published the company’s financial interests in the jewelry company Pandora in connection with stock recommendations.
The association is unhappy that Nordea did not publicise its ‘major financial interests’ in the company in an investment analysis.
