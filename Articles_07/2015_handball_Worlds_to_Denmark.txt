Title: 2015_handball_Worlds_to_Denmark
Date: 2011-01-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark has been chosen by the International Handball Federation (IHF) to host the World Handball Championships for women in 2015. Qatar will be holding the 2015 men’s worlds.
The IHF says that Denmark was the only contender for the women’s event after Korea had withdrawn its bid. Apart from Qatar, Norway, France and Poland had also bid for the men’s championships.
