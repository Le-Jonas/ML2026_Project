Title: Kjær_withdraws_from_Cons_positions
Date: 2011-01-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Conservative Party Political Spokesman Henriette Kjær has announced that she is withdrawing from her senior Conservative posts and will not be contesting the next election.
“After careful consideration I have decided to ask Lars Barfoed to be released from my duties as Parliamentary Group Chair and Political Spokeswoman. I will continue in Parliament until the next election and will do the tasks that I am presented with. But I have decided not to contest the next election despite the warm support that I received at my constituency appointment meeting in Aarhus last Monday,” Kjær says in a news release.
