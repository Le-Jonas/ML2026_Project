Title: Frost_and_snow_next_week
Date: 2010-11-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Some parts of Denmark had flurries of snow this morning and temperatures just above freezing – but from the middle of next week, winter is expected to grip Denmark with frost both day and night.
“Winter is knocking on our door now and it looks as if it’s just going to get colder from now on. From around Wednesday we’ll have frost both day and night and as things look at the moment we’ll be getting snow,” says DMI Duty Officer Thyge Raasmussen.
