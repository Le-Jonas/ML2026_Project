Title: Libya_War_worth_every_penny
Date: 2011-05-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The war in Libya has proved to be more expensive than envisaged, but not too much more according to Foreign Minister Lene Espersen.
“Money has not been my focus. We went into this with open eyes and knew that it could cost us. The important thing is that Denmark has been at the forefront, helped to keep civilians safe and ensure that the UN resolution is carried out,” says Lene Espersen following a meeting of the Foreign Policy Committee.
