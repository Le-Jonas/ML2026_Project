Title: Kurds_end_hunger_strike
Date: 2010-10-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A group of 28 Syrian Kurds have ended their hunger strike in a tent outside Parliament.
The group went on hunger strike two weeks ago in protest against Denmark rejecting their asylum requests. Some of those involved have been hospitalised after becoming ill. The rest have now decided to end their protest.
