Title: Mærsk_USD_14.5_bn_in_Q1
Date: 2011-05-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A.P. Møller-Mærsk has released its First Quarter interim management statement showing a 10 per cent increase in revenues to USD 14.5 bn.
The company announced that profit for the period increased by 82 per cent to USD 1.2 billion.
