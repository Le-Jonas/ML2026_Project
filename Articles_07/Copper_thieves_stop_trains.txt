Title: Copper_thieves_stop_trains
Date: 2011-02-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Train services in and around Copenhagen are subject to delays and cancellations this morning after thieves attempted to steal copper cabling from a station in Holte.
“Overnight someone has tried to steal 100 metres of cable. This cable carries signal traffic and so several routes have been affected,” says the Banedanmark Press Officer.
