Title: Ex_Communist_chairman_It_was_wrong
Date: 2010-10-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The former chairman of the Danish Communist Party, now Parliamentary Group Chairman of the Socialist People’s Party Ole Sohn is distancing himself from his former party and regrets sending a flattering letter to East Germany’s erstwhile leader Erich Honecker.
“I believe that the Danish Communist Party, and thus also what I represented, was wrong. That is also why I regret the letters that were sent,” Sohn tells TV2.
