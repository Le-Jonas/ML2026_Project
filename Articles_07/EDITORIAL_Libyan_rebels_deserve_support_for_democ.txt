Title: EDITORIAL_Libyan_rebels_deserve_support_for_democ
Date: 2011-06-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The aim of the military operation in Libya is to protect the civilian population in the country.
But the aim of Europe’s civilian policy in our neighbouring country on the other side of the Mediterranean is to get rid of Colonel Gaddafi, and further the aims of a pluralist Libya that respects human rights and democracy.
