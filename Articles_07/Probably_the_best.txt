Title: Probably_the_best
Date: 2011-02-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Scotland may do its Uisgebaugh crawls, but Carlsberg is fast becoming a must visit for tourists to Denmark, notching up a 25 percent increase in 2010 in the number of tourists visiting the group’s old brewery in Copenhagen.
“With 157,500 guests we have become one of the places that tourists want to visit when they come to Copenhagen,” says Carlsberg Visitors Director Lone Jensen.
