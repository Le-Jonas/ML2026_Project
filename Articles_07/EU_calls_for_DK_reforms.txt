Title: EU_calls_for_DK_reforms
Date: 2011-06-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The European Commission is advising Denmark to address what it sees as problems with Danish private debt, including recommendations for reforms of the property market and changes in property taxes.
According to the recommendations, next year should see Denmark “taking preventive measures to strengthen the stability of the property market and the financial system in the medium term, including reforms of the mortgage and property tax system”.
