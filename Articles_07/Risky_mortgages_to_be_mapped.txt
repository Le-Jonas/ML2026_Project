Title: Risky_mortgages_to_be_mapped
Date: 2011-03-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Economy and Trade Minister Brian Mikkelsen has called for a report on whether banks have given the most risky instalment-free flexible-rate loans to people who cannot afford them if interest rates rise.
According to the statistics, 42 per cent of mortgages are instalment-free flexible-rate loans.
