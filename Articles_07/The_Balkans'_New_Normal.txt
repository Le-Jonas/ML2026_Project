Date: 2010-12-20
Article Type: World views
Author: Kenneth Lund

Journalist og debatredaktør
Ivan Krastev
SOFIA – The Balkans is the European Union’s untold success story.
The EU’s commitment to bringing the region within its borders remains firm. In September, Catherine Ashton, the EU’s High Representative for Foreign Affairs, succeeded in breaking the deadlock in Serbia-Kosovo relations by bringing both sides back to the negotiating table. The EU’s soft power remains as visible as ever.
