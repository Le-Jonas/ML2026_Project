Title: EDITORIAL_Obama_&ndash;_a_hero_in_defeat
Date: 2010-11-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A superpower cannot lose a war, and the President of the United States cannot call a defeat, a defeat.
That was just one of the challenges that faced Barack Obama last year in formulating a new strategy for American participation in the war in Afghanistan. The generals more or less refused to give him other options than choosing a major, or minor, military reinforcement.
