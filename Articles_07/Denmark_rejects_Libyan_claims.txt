Title: Denmark_rejects_Libyan_claims
Date: 2011-03-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Libyan state television claims that Danish F-16s led and took part in an attack against Col. Gaddafi’s residences in Tripoli, but Denmark’s Air Force Tactical Command (AFTC) says that Danish jets have not delivered any of their weapons in the six sorties they have been involved in so far.
Six Danish F-16s and some 120 ground crew are based in Sicily as part of the coalition of the willing, enforcing United Nations Resolution 1973 on Libya.
