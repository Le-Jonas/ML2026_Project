Title: Palestinian_hiphoppers_stuck_in_DK
Date: 2010-12-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Five Palestinians from Gaza’s Darg Team hip hop group have been refused permission to leave Denmark for home due to complicated Middle East visa rules and a lack of domicile on their Palestinian papers.
The group arrived in Denmark legally to take part in the Displaced Expressions Festival in Copenhagen at the beginning of December.
