Title: DPP_Expel_immigrant_gang_members
Date: 2011-05-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party, which is currently negotiating with the government on its 2020 Plan, is calling for 100 of the leading members of immigrant gangs to be immediately deported from Denmark even if they have not committed any crime.
“Given that the police now know the inner circle of these gangs who are responsible for shootings, all the conditions are in place to deport them. A number of them are not Danish citizens and we are sure that it would cause respect if we deport them,” DPP Deputy Chairman Peter Skaarup tells Berlingske.
