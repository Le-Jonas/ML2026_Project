Title: Mass_COP15_arrests_deemed_illegal
Date: 2010-12-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen Municipal Court has ruled that the mass arrest of some 900 people during last year’s COP15 Climate Summit was illegal and has awarded damages of between DKK 5,000 and DKK 9,000 to those who have complained.
The judgment was immediately appealed by the Junior Counsel to the Treasury.
