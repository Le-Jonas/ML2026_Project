Title: Analysis_Budget_with_symbolic_gifts_to_all
Date: 2010-11-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

By Mette Østergaard, Political Editor
The Danish People’s Party got a tight immigration agreement. The Conservatives got minor tax rebates for businesses. The Liberals signalled economic responsibility and gave the West Jutlanders a motorway. Finally, the Christian Democrats demonstrated that they exist and are responsible enough to be voted into Parliament.
