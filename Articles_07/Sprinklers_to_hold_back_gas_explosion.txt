Title: Sprinklers_to_hold_back_gas_explosion
Date: 2011-01-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police have cordoned off a larger area around the Air Liquide gas company on Havnegade 151 in Odense fearing that the building, which contains a large number of gas canisters, may explode.
The company’s sprinkler system has begun dousing the area, and it is hoped an explosion can be avoided.
