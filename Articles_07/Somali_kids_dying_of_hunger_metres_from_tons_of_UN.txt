Title: Somali_kids_dying_of_hunger_metres_from_tons_of_UN
Date: 2011-07-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A few hundred metres from starving people in refugee camps in Mogadishu, World Food Programme storerooms have been packed with undistributed food for several months, according to TV2’s Rasmus Tantholdt who has visited the storerooms.
According to TV2, the food stores, which include many tonnes of grain and oil, have been lying idle in UN storerooms for several months.
