Title: Poll_Opposition_alliance_back_in_lead
Date: 2011-01-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A fresh Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 puts the Social Democratic-led opposition alliance back in the lead if an election were held now.
According to the poll, the Social Democratic, Socialist People’s, Social Liberal and Red-Green parties would poll 50.6 percent of the vote if an election were held now, with the government bloc of Liberals, Conservatives, Danish People’s Party and Liberal Alliance on 49.4 percent.
