Title: Radical_right_eyes_Sorte_Diamant
Date: 2011-01-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In the midst of a controversy surrounding the loan of the Royal Library for a debate to be held by the Hizb ut-Tahrir organisation, Denmark’s right wing Nationale Front has asked the library if it too can use the premises for its debates.
The Hizb ut-Tahrir case has caused widespread controversy as a result of Friday's planned debate on the Afghanistan war, the invitation to which encourages armed resistance and includes a map of Afghanistan with Danish, Swedish and Norwegian coffins superimposed.
