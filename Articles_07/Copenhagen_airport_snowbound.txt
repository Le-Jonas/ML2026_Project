Title: Copenhagen_airport_snowbound
Date: 2010-11-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Departures from Copenhagen’s international airport at Kastrup are being hampered this morning due to heavy crosswinds, drifting snow and slippery runways.
“We expect to be able to get some departures off at around 8 a.m. The problems are with heavy crosswinds and slippery runways,” says Copenhagen Airport Communications Officer Lene Hald.
