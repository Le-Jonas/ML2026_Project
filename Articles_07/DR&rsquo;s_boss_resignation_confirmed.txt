Title: DR&rsquo;s_boss_resignation_confirmed
Date: 2010-10-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish broadcasting corporation DR's news programmes and Ritzau are reporting that the corporation’s Director-General Kenneth Plummer will be resigning today following a scandal involving a breach of confidentiality.
DR Board Chairman Michael Christiansen has confirmed to DR News that Plummer has elected to resign.
