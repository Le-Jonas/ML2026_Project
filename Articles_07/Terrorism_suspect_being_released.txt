Title: Terrorism_suspect_being_released
Date: 2010-12-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One of the four men arrested yesterday on suspicion of plotting a terrorist attack against Jyllands-Posten/Politiken premises in Copenhagen is to be released and will not be presented in court.
According to politiken.dk’s information, the man in question is a 26-year-old Iraqi asylum seeker who lives in Greve south of Copenhagen. Charges against the man are, however, said to have been maintained.
