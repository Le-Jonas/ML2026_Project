Title: Hjort_calls_councils_to_heel
Date: 2010-09-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Finance Minister Claus Hjort Frederiksen has summoned municipal councils for a serious talk about the economy.
“They are going to have to explain what is going on. We are in a situation in which everyone has to be careful in order to get this to work,” Hjort Frederiksen says.
