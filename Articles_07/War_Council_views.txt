Title: War_Council_views
Date: 2011-03-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Some 35 countries of an international coalition meet for a War Council in London today to discuss the coalition’s next steps, how far the coalition is prepared to go in its UN sanctioned operation in Libya and what operations are acceptable.
Politiken.dk asks four centrally placed Danish foreign policy spokesmen for their views, and not least what about other countries where demonstrators are being shot.
