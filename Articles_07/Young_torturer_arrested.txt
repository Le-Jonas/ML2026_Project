Title: Young_torturer_arrested
Date: 2011-06-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 20-year-old Danish man has been detained and is due to appear in remand court today in connection with allegations of the extensive torture of a 61-year-old man in a case said to involve gambling debts.
According to initial reports, the abuse has taken place on several occasions this spring, with the 61-year-old saying that apart from other abuse, the young man had pulled two of his teeth out with pliers.
