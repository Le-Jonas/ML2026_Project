Title: Løkke&rsquo;s_plan_for_election_year
Date: 2010-10-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen (Lib) opened the new session of Parliament today for a parliamentary year that may be interrupted by a general election.
In his address, Løkke Rasmussen laid out the initiatives the government plans to implement during the year, suggesting the governing Liberal and Conservative parties have plans for a number of specific changes.
