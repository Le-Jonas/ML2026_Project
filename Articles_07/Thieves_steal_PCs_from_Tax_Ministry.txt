Title: Thieves_steal_PCs_from_Tax_Ministry
Date: 2010-10-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Tax Ministry has been burgled and at least 15 PCs have disappeared. The theft took place between 10 p.m. Thursday and 7.30 a.m. Friday.
“We have estimated that some 15 PCs and two telephones were stolen,” Press Officer Morten Schoop tells politiken.dk.
