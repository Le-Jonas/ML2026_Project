Title: Prime_Minister_speaks_of_euro_referendum
Date: 2011-02-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark may soon have to reconsider its position on the euro and the Danish opt-out to the common currency, according to Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen.
“We are not a euro-country, despite our currency being tied to the euro,” said Løkke Rasmussen during a debate in Parliament today, referring to a Franco-German proposal to set up a pact within the euro countries.
