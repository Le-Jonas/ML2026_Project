Title: Danish_Crown_wants_to_go_public
Date: 2010-09-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Board of Danish Crown wants to go public and has proposed moving all of the cooperative’s commercial activities into a new Danish Crown A/S (Ltd.), according to Jyllands-Posten.
“It is correct that we have presented such a proposal t the committee of representatives. The idea is to make it easier for Danish Crown to manoeuvre – among other things in relation to cooperation with other companies that are often limited companies. It is easier to cooperate with companies of the same type,” Danish Crown Board Chairman Niels Mikkelsen tells Jyllands-Posten.
