Title: POLL_SDP_storms_ahead
Date: 2011-02-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning Schmidt is making a bee-line for the prime minister’s office, according to the latest poll.
The Greens poll for Børsen puts the Social Democrats at 31.2 percent of the vote, up from 29.5 percent in the previous poll and well up on their 25.5 result in the 2007 general election.
