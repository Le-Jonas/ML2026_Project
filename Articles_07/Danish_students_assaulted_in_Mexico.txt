Title: Danish_students_assaulted_in_Mexico
Date: 2010-10-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Three business school students from Viborg have been hurt after witnessing an armed robbery at a discothèque in Mexico, according to Viborg Stifts Folkeblad.
The attack, in which one student was pistol-whipped, another kicked in the stomach and a third sprained an ankle, took place early on Sunday morning at a disco in Cuernavaca when armed robbers rushed into the room and demanded money, mobile telephones and credit cards.
