Title: DPP_Zero_help_to_immigrants_for_7_years
Date: 2010-09-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party wants an earning principle to be introduced so that immigrants are only eligible to welfare payments after seven years of paying taxes in Denmark.
“We feel this is necessary to maintain sustainability in public finances,” DPP Finance Spokesman Kristian Thulesen Dahl tells TV Avisen prior to budget negotiations.
