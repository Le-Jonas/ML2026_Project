Title: Winter_returning_to_Denmark
Date: 2011-02-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After a period of spring-like weather, with sunshine and temperatures above zero, winter will be returning to Denmark today bringing freezing temperatures and snow.
“The cold wind has already hit North Jutland, where the temperature this morning has fallen below zero. Temperatures will fall elsewhere this afternoon,” says Met Office Duty Officer Thyge Rasmussen adding the wind will be picking up as well.
