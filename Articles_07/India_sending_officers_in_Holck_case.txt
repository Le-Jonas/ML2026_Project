Title: India_sending_officers_in_Holck_case
Date: 2011-05-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Indian authorities are sending an intelligence and a legal officer to Denmark to help Danish prosecutors firm up their case against Niels Holck, whom India wants extradited on charges of dropping weapons to West Bengali insurgents in 1995.
"The team comprising a Central Bureau of Investigation officer and a lawyer will leave for Denmark either on May 14 or May 16. It will go with all available evidence that substantiates Davy's involvement in the crime," The Times of India quotes a senior government official as saying.
