Title: VTEC_beansprouts_not_sold_in_DK
Date: 2011-06-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The bean sprouts suspected of being infected with the VTEC bacteria that has caused sickness in 12 countries across Europe have not been sold in Danish outlets, according to the Coop and Dansk Supermarked chains which cover the majority of food outlets in the country.
Dansk Supermarked says that the beansprouts are in all probability only sold to catering companies, and primarily in North Germany, according to Ritzau.
