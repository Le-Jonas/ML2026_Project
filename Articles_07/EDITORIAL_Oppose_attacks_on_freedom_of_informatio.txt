Title: EDITORIAL_Oppose_attacks_on_freedom_of_informatio
Date: 2010-12-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The United States and anonymous hackers are currently doing everything they can to stifle the Internet medium WikiLeaks, which has been behind the publication of thousands of confidential documents from the US Defense and State departments.
The methods being used are even more far-reaching than those used against terrorist groups and organised crime.
