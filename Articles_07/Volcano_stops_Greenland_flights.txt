Title: Volcano_stops_Greenland_flights
Date: 2011-05-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

<i>Edited by 
<a href="mailto:julian.isherwood@pol.dk">Julian Isherwood</a></i>
Air Greenland has cancelled its flights to Copenhagen Monday as a result of a cloud of ash from Iceland's Grimsvotn volcano which has covered an area from eastern Greenland southwards over Iceland.
As a result, Icelandic air space authorities have closed air space above 6,000 metres in a corridor normally used by flights from Kangerlussuaq (Sdr. Strømfjord).
