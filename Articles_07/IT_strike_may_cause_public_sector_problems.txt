Title: IT_strike_may_cause_public_sector_problems
Date: 2011-06-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A strike which began this morning among 330 IT engineers at the CSC company may cause serious problems for the public sector, which has some 120 contracts with the company.
Some 300,000 civil servants may end up with wrong salary slips and the Tax Authority has put its preliminary income assessments on hold. In the private sector, CSC also has major clients such as TDC, Berlingske Media and Tryg Vesta.
