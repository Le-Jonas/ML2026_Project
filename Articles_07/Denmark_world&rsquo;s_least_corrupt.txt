Title: Denmark_world&rsquo;s_least_corrupt
Date: 2010-10-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark remains at the top of the Transparency International Corruption Perception Index (CPI) 2010 with a score of 9.3 out of 10, sharing first place with New Zealand and Singapore.
But as is the case with other rich OECD countries, the score has not improved compared with previous years, and on the contrary, Denmark’s score has dropped in the past decade.
