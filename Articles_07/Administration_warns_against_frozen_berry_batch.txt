Title: Administration_warns_against_frozen_berry_batch
Date: 2011-08-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Food and Veterinary Administration has issued a warning after having found the norovirus in frozen raspberries that have been sold in Netto outlets across the country.
Norovirus causes what in Denmark is known as Roskilde Sickness, or non-bacterial gastroenteritis, giving nausea, vomiting, stomach pains, diarrhoea and a high temperature.
