Title: Soccer_players_bet_on_own_matches
Date: 2010-10-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish soccer players are alleged to have been betting on their own matches, and in the case of some B1908 players that they would lose, according to reports today.
According to the reports, players placed their bets with Scandic Bookmaker, who claim to have discovered the ruse, annulled the bets and reported the issue to the Danish Football Association (DBU), whose Head of Information Lars Berendt says that other clubs are also being investigated.
