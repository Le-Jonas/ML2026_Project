Title: Problems_for_trains_across_Great_Belt
Date: 2010-12-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There are major train delays across the Great Belt between Funen and Zealand due to a stuck goods train that has stopped services on one of the tracks, and some problems with points on the other track.
“Traffic stopped this morning for about an hour. Normally we can use the other tunnel, but the points froze up,” says DSB Duty Information Officer Lars Flindt Pedersen.
