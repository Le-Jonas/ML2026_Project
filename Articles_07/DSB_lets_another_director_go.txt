Title: DSB_lets_another_director_go
Date: 2011-04-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

DSB, which is embroiled in controversy over its subsidiary DSBFirst, has dismissed another of its directors, this time the Chief Executive Officer of its subsidiary DSB Vedligehold (Maintenance), former DSB Chief Financial Officer Bartal Kass.
Kass is the third senior person at DSB to leave in connection with disclosures in the DSBFirst case, following the removal of DSB CEO Søren Eriksen and the announcement yesterday that Board Chairman Mogens Granborg is leaving his post for health reasons.
