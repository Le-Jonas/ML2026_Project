Title: WikiLeaks'_Flawed_Answer_to_a_Flawed_World
Date: 2010-12-17
Article Type: World views
Author: Esther Dyson

NEW YORK – Long ago, I wrote about the Internet pioneer Julf Helsingius, who ran a precursor to WikiLeaks called anon.penet.fi.
As I said then: »Anonymity in itself should not be illegal. There are enough good reasons for people to be anonymous that it should be [allowed] – at least in some places on the Net (as in real life)«.
