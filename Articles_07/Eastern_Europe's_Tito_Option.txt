Date: 2010-11-19

Andrew Wilson
LONDON – Success stories in what the European Union calls »the neighborhood« have been hard to come by.
First Georgia, then Ukraine, and most recently Moldova have all been big EU hopes. But, in each case, those hopes were dashed. Unfortunately for the EU, this year’s annual summit with Ukraine (on November 22) will likely showcase this failure.
