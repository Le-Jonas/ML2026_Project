Title: Burst_tyre_causes_emergency_landing
Date: 2011-07-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Jet Time Boeing 737-300 with 140 passengers and crew on board undertook an emergency landing in Billund in Jutland Tuesday after one of its tyres burst on take-off. After a change of tyre, the aircraft has resumed its flight.
No-one was hurt in the incident.
