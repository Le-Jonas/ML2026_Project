Title: Bankers_Forked_tongue_Sohn
Date: 2011-01-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Democrats and Socialist People’s Party have repeatedly said that they will not increase property taxes if they take over government at the next election, but Saxo Bank bankers report from a closed meeting with SPP Finance Spokesman Ole Sohn that he suggested just such an increase.
Saxoi Bank CEO Kim Fournais and Saxo-Etrade Bank Director Jens Højer report Sohn’s comments from a closed meeting in Vejle in September, according to Berlingske.
