Title: Councils_want_better_off_patients_to_pay
Date: 2011-01-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Local Government Denmark (KL), which represents Denmark’s 98 local authorities, is proposing a radical change to Denmark’s welfare principles by making advantaged patients supply their own rehabilitation, advice and other forms of help, according to Berlingske Tidende.
Under the current system, all patients are given the same treatment, irrespective of social standing.
