Title: Santa_gets_children's_pacifiers
Date: 2010-12-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

As the Christmas season rapidly approaches, Santa Claus’ post box is rapidly filling with cards and letters with Christmas wishes, and not least pacifiers sent by young children who have decided to quit the habit.
“At Christmastime we get about 100 pacifiers a week, and letters contain drawings, wish lists and small stories,” says Post Danmark Communications Manager Lars Kaspersen. So far in 2010, Post Danmark has handled 3,000 pacifiers addressed to Father Christmas.
