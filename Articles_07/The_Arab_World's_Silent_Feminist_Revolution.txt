Title: The_Arab_World's_Silent_Feminist_Revolution
Date: 2010-12-14
Article Type: World views
Author: Kenneth Lund

Journalist og debatredaktør
Gema Martin Munoz
MADRID – Arab societies often appear rigid and resistant to change to outsiders, because what they see is these countries’ ruling regimes, which mostly do resist development and change.
But this image is nearly the opposite of reality in Arab societies, where enormous dynamism is opening doors to many types of change, albeit at different speeds and in complex, contradictory ways – particularly when change from below is held back from above.
