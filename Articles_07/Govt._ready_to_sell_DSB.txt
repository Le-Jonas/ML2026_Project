Title: Govt._ready_to_sell_DSB
Date: 2010-09-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There are indications that DSB’s days as a state railway are numbered.
The Liberals and Conservatives have plans to privatise DSB in 2022 and use the proceeds of between five and 10 billion kroner to fully electrify the railway system, according to Berlingske Tidende.
