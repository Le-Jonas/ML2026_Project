Title: A4_plays_around_with_portfolios
Date: 2011-04-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Informed guesswork seems to be the order of the day in the editorial offices at A4, which says it has spoken to central actors in Parliament and is thus able to suggest who will fill ministerial portfolios if the current Lib-Con government continues, or if the Social Democratic-led opposition wins the next election.
Interestingly enough, A4 – which is mouthpiece of the Confederation of Danish Trade Unions – says that if the current government continues, Finance Minister Claus Hjort Frederiksen will be back on the backbenches.
