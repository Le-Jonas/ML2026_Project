Title: Chemicals_escape_from_Arla_factory
Date: 2010-12-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A large amount of chemicals have spilled in a cleansing room at the Arla Foods Diary at Taulov south of Fredericia, resulting in a release of poisonous gases that has caused police to close the area's motorway to and from Funen and Jutland.
“These are nitrous gases that should not be inhaled, so people in the area should stay indoors,” Location Emergency Chief Jørn Lindhardt tells TV2 News.
