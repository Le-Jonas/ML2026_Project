Title: Tankers_collide_off_Hirtshals
Date: 2010-12-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two tankers collided this morning some 20 nautical miles west of the North Jutland port of Hirtshals, according to Admiral Danish Fleet (SOK) which has sent rescue vessels, a helicopter and environmental protection vessels to the area.
No-one is reported to have been injured.
