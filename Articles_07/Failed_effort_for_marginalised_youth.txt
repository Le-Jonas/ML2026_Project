Title: Failed_effort_for_marginalised_youth
Date: 2010-09-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Despite extensive political focus on reducing the number of marginalised young people in Denmark, the situation has hardly improved.
Fifteen percent of all those between the ages of three and 19 is so unhealthy that there is a risk of them dropping entirely out of society, according to the new report from the Danish National Centre for Social Research (SFI).
