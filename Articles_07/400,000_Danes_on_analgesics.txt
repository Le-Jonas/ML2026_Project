Title: 400,000_Danes_on_analgesics
Date: 2011-07-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Almost 400,000 Danes received strong analgesics such as morphine or ketobemidone last year, an increase of 100,000 on 2001, the Danish Medicines Agency says according to Information.
The rapid increase in the usage of, for example, opioid analgesics is causing concern among pain therapists, among other reasons because they are highly addictive.
