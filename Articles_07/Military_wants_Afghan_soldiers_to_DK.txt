Title: Military_wants_Afghan_soldiers_to_DK
Date: 2010-10-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish army’s leadership is looking to bring some Afghan soldiers to Denmark to better prepare Danish soldiers for the culture shock they meet when posted to Afghanistan.
The move comes after several returning Danish soldiers have told Jyllands-Posten that, among other things; Afghan soldiers smoke hash, are lazy and openly have sex with teenage boys.
