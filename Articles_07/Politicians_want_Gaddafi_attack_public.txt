Title: Politicians_want_Gaddafi_attack_public
Date: 2011-05-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish politicians are calling on the government to release details of Danish operations in Libya following reports that Danish aircraft were responsible for a recent attack in which Col. Gaddafi’s son and three grandchildren are alleged by Tripoli to have been killed.
According to The Guardian, which cited an unnamed diplomatic source, Danish F-16s were involved in the bombing raid.
