Title: Espersen_calls_extraordinary_cartoon_meeting
Date: 2010-09-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Foreign Minister Lene Espersen has called an extraordinary meeting of the Foreign Relations Committee, which according to Berlingske Tidende has one issue on the agenda – the fifth anniversary of the publication by Jyllands-Posten of 12 caricatures of the Prophet Mohammed.
The report adds, however, that it is unclear why Espersen has found it necessary to call an urgent meeting of the committee.
