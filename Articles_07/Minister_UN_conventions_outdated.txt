Title: Minister_UN_conventions_outdated
Date: 2011-03-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s new Minister for Integration Søren Pind (Lib) says that UN conventions are outdated and that Denmark could withdraw from them.
“As far as I am concerned, the conventions do not mirror modern realities. They bear the mark of a different world,” says Pind apparently referring to the 1961 UN convention designed to curb statelessness.
