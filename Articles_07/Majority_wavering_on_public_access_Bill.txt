Title: Majority_wavering_on_public_access_Bill
Date: 2010-12-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government may be heading for defeat in its plans to revise the rules on access to public sector information, according to Berlingske Tidende.
The media sector and information experts have been highly critical of the Bill, which was presented in Parliament last Wednesday and which curtails access to ministerial information for the press and public.
