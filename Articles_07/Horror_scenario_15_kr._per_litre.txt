Title: Horror_scenario_15_kr._per_litre
Date: 2011-02-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The worst scenario for Danish petrol prices is that the unrest currently sweeping the Middle East reaches Saudi Arabia, which has some 10 percent of world oil production.
“In that situation, oil prices could reach over USD 150 per barrel and we could have petrol prices of 14-15 kr. per litre, (EUR 2; USD 2.7)” says Danske Bank Senior Analyst Christin Tuxen.
