Title: COP15_activists_found_guilty
Date: 2010-11-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two climate activists have been found guilty by the Copenhagen Municipal Court of inciting violence against police officers last December by directing demonstrators wanting to breach fencing around the venue of the COP15 UN Climate Conference.
The two, Tannie Nyboe and Stine Gry Jonassen, were sentenced to four months in prison. Both have repeatedly denied the charges.
