Title: Foreign_poultry_has_more_e-coli
Date: 2010-10-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Figures from the national programme to monitor multi-resistant bacteria show that over 33 percent of tests of foreign poultry have tested positive for multi-resistant coli bacteria, 10 times more that in pork, beef and Danish poultry.
Multi-resistant bacteria make the normal treatment of even commonplace illnesses such as pneumonia difficult or even hopeless .
