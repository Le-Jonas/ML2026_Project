Title: Copenhagen_loses_electricity
Date: 2011-06-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A large part of the centre of Copenhagen was blacked out today due to problems with a DONG Energy transformer, resulting in failed traffic lights, cash registers and bankomats across most of the centre of the capital.
Traffic lights were functioning again across most of the city in the early afternoon, but many shops were still without electricity.
