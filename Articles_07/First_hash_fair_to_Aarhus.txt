Title: First_hash_fair_to_Aarhus
Date: 2011-02-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police officers may be confiscating increasing quantities of strong hash in East Jutland, but organisers are well-advanced with plans to hold Denmark’s first International Hash Fair in the country’s second city of Aarhus this summer, according to Aarhus Stiftstidende.
Guests from home and abroad are being invited from 24th to 26th of June to study and buy legal ingredients for the illegal production of hash, skunk and other cannabis products. Products on display will include fertiliser, heating lamps, planting systems and pipes.
