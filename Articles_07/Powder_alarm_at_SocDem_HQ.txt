Title: Powder_alarm_at_SocDem_HQ
Date: 2010-08-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police have ordered the evacuation of Social Democratic offices on Danasvej in Frederiksberg after a letter was found to contain a powdery substance.
The building will remain evacuated and the road outside cordoned off until experts can establish what the powder is, and whether it is poisonous.
