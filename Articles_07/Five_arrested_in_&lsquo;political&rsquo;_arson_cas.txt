Title: Five_arrested_in_&lsquo;political&rsquo;_arson_cas
Date: 2011-04-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Four young men were detained overnight by the Danish Security and Intelligence Service PET following a lengthy investigation into politically motivated arson attacks, the intelligence service says in a news release.
“The arrests took place after lengthy investigation and those arrested are suspected of being responsible for several politically motived arson attacks. The agency believes the case shows that there are people in extremist environments who are prepared to use very violent methods,” the agency says.
