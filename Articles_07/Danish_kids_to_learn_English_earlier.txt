Title: Danish_kids_to_learn_English_earlier
Date: 2011-05-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish children should begin learning foreign languages earlier than is currently the case, according to recommendations from a working group set up by the Education and Science ministries.
“Our English is not good enough. All Danes can speak some English, but we need more of us to be competent at a higher and more advanced level,” Working Group Chairwoman Bodil Due tells Berlingske.
