Title: DK_newspaper_meets_pirate_hostages
Date: 2011-03-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A representative of the Ekstra Bladet newspaper has been allowed to meet the Danish family that is being held hostage by Somali pirates along with 20 others on board a hijacked Greek vessel.
According to the report, pirates are demanding some DKK 26 million for the family, whose members are said to be demoralised, but otherwise generally well.
