Title: Opposition_voters_back_Nyrup_on_DPP
Date: 2010-11-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

According to a Rambøll opinion poll in Jyllands-Posten, a large majority of opposition voters agree with former Prime Minister Poul Nyrup Rasmussen’s (SocDem) views about the Danish People’s Party.
Last week, Nyrup Rasmussen, who is currently chairman of the European Social Democrats (PES), in connection with controversy surrounding the government’s new immigration pact, repeated a previous statement that the Danish People’s Party is unacceptable in some of its views.
