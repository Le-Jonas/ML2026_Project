Title: Man_out_of_possible_bomb_car
Date: 2010-11-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police have detained a woman in connection with an incident this morning in which a bomb threat was issued against a man in a car. The woman is said by police to be the man's ex-wife.
Earlier Monday the bomb squad removed a man from a vehicle in Vejle which was thought to possibly contain an explosive device. The bomb squad eventually removed the device which did not appear to be a bomb.
