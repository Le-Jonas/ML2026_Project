Title: Politiken&rsquo;s_new_editor-in-chief_appointed
Date: 2011-04-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Bo Lidegaard, 53, a former diplomat and senior civil servant has been appointed editor-in-chief of Politiken following the death in January this year of his predecessor Tøger Seidenfaden.
Described by JP/Politikens Hus’ Managing Director Lars Munch as one of Denmark’s most encyclopaedic intellectuals, Lidegaard will become the newspaper’s 13th editor-in-chief.
