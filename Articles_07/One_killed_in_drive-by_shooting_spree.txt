Title: One_killed_in_drive-by_shooting_spree
Date: 2011-03-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One person was killed and two others wounded overnight in a drive-by shooting in the Copenhagen suburb of Husum.
Copenhagen police say that a dark vehicle drove up to a group of four people, whereupon one of the passengers got out and fired a hail of bullets at the group.
