Title: Red_Greens_Give_the_rebels_missiles
Date: 2011-03-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Defence Spokesman for the Danish Red Green Party Frank Aaen says the situation for Libya’s rebels is so serious that Denmark should airlift Stinger missiles to the Libyan opposition.
“The West has been supplying weapons to Gaddafi for years. We have a hypocritical French president who supports rebels who are being bombarded with weapons delivered by France. So we think it is right to say that we should help the rebels in Benghazi to stop these attacks,” says Aaen.
