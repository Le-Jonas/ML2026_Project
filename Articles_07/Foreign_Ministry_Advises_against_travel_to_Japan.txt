Title: Foreign_Ministry_Advises_against_travel_to_Japan
Date: 2011-03-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Foreign Ministry has amended its travel advisory for Japan, advising against all travel to the country.
At the same time, the ministry advises all those within an 80 km (50 mile) radius of the Fukushima nuclear power station to leave the area, mirroring a similar advisory from the US State Department.
