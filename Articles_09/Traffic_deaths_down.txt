Title: Traffic_deaths_down
Date: 2012-08-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The number of accidents and road deaths continues to fall in 2012, according to the Road Directorate.
“The figures showed a positive trend in 2011, but 2012 looks like being a year in which less than 20 people die in traffic each month. That has never happened before,” says Directorate Project Leader Stig. R. Hemdorff.
