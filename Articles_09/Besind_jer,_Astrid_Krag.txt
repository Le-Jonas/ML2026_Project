Title: Besind_jer,_Astrid_Krag
Date: 2013-05-14
Article Type: Læserbreve
Author: David Field

Dear Astrid Krag
As a research scientist I judge by results. The result of your policy towards the PLO has been to precipitate a major crisis in the Danish health service. Never mind who is to blame; the ‘blame game’’ is not productive. As it is, with you in charge, there is a real danger that one of the jewels in the crown of the Danish State, the socialised primary health care system, will be greatly diminished. As Minister of Health, it is your job to see that this does not happen. At present you appear to be conniving at it, with honeyed simpering words about how much you value the health service etc. etc.. Please show some strength of character and use your position responsibly, to act to see that socialised primary health is not dismantled under your watch. In the words of your prime minister ‘besind jer’.
