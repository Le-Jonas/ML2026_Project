Author: Julian Isherwood

The leadership of the Socialist People’s Party (SF) is expected to take further steps to marginalise one of its troublesome MPs, Özlem Çekiç.
Çekiç has been suspended from her positions as spokeswoman, and is not able to speak on behalf of the Socialist People’s Party, as a result of her opposition to the government’s tax reform.
