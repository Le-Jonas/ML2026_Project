Title: Street_reopened_after_explosives_found
Date: 2012-09-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Borgmester Christiansens Gade in Sydhavn was cordoned off this morning after a passer-by found explosives in the street, but the road has been re-opened after the Bomb Unit has secured the explosives and driven off.
According to police on the scene, there was quite a lot of explosive material, but it was not connected to a detonator or suchlike.
