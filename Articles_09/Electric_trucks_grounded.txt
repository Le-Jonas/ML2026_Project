Title: Electric_trucks_grounded
Date: 2012-08-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen Council’s small electric garbage collection trucks have been grounded following an accident in the pedestrian street Strøget yesterday in which an American tourist died when one of the vehicles set off on its own.
“This was a tragic accident that obviously has affected our staff,” says Copenhagen Collection Centre Chief Claus Robl.
