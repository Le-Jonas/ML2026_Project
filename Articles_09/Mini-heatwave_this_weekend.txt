Title: Mini-heatwave_this_weekend
Date: 2012-08-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

For those pining for a taste of real summer, the Met Office is promising a weekend fit for the beach – and for farmers perhaps harvesting - this Friday, Saturday and Sunday.
Following a rather wet Thursday, DMI says a mini-heat wave will be descending on Denmark, with temperatures up to 30 degrees at the weekend.
