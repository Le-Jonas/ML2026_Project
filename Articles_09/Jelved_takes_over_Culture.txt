Title: Jelved_takes_over_Culture
Date: 2012-12-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Marianne Jelved of the Social Liberals is to take over the culture portfolio following Uffe Elbæks resignation yesterday.
Elbæk decided to resign following several days of intense controversy and criticism surrounding his role in connection with funds to a circus school at which his partner works and where he was previously board chairman.
