Title: The_Thatcherization_of_Denmark
Date: 2013-05-28
Article Type: Læserbreve
Author: David Field

The government treatment of ‘prakiserende læger’ stinks of control and mistrust (’mistillid’). This is not the Denmark that I chose to live in and which I thought of as a ‘civilized form of Britain’, built upon social awareness and mutual trust (‘tillid’).
It is certainly most curious that a ‘left wing’ government be the one to Thatcherize Denmark.
