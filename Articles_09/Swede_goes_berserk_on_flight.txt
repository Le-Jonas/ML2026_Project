Title: Swede_goes_berserk_on_flight
Date: 2013-03-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Swedish man went berserk aboard a charter flight from Goa to Copenhagen and Landvetter, breaking an inner plexiglass window and forcing the aircraft to land in Turkey.
“(The windows) are made of five layers of plexiglass and he kicked the inner layer,” says Communications Executive Rikke Koks Andersen of Apollo Rejser.
