Title: BofP_sets_record
Date: 2012-08-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish balance of payments surplus has set another all-time record according to the latest June figures from Statistics Denmark.
The figures show a surplus of DKK15.3billion, a record for a single month. Compared with May 2012, which was the last record, the surplus has risen by DKK2.6billion, driven particularly by an increased surplus on services of DKK2.3billion.
