Title: Polish_vehicle_gang_smashed
Date: 2012-09-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

South and Southern Jutland police have smashed a Polish gang that has been stealing expensive vehicles in Denmark, according to a police new release.
The gang has particularly targeted Mercedes-Benz commercial vehicles, Toyota Avensis and Skoda Octavia cars and is thought by police to have stolen some 40 vehicles in all.
