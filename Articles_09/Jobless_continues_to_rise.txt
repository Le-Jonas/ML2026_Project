Title: Jobless_continues_to_rise
Date: 2012-08-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Unemployment continues to rise in Denmark, with the latest figures from Statistics Denmark showing an increase of 1,500 in July and bringing the total number of unemployed in Denmark to a seasonally adjusted 165,700 or 6.3 per cent of the labour force.
July was the third month in a row that unemployment has risen, with Nordea expecting the figure to reach some 170,000 by year’s end.
