Title: Afghan_interpreters_accuse_Def._Min
Date: 2013-03-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Afghan interpreters are contesting the reasons given by Defence Minister Nick Hækkerup for not affording them asylum in Denmark, saying some of them at least were employed from the local area in which they functioned, according to TV2 News.
The disclosure refutes statements some time ago by the defence minister that Denmark has not used interpreters from a local region, in that region, for their safety.
