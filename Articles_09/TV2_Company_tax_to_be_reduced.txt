Title: TV2_Company_tax_to_be_reduced
Date: 2013-02-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt is to propose a reduction in Danish company tax from 25 per cent to 22 per cent, according to TV2, although not all companies will be put on the lower rate.
The report adds that both banks and the Dansk Undergrunds Consortium, which extracts North Sea oil, will continue to pay the full rate.
