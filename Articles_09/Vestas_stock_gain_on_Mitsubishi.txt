Title: Vestas_stock_gain_on_Mitsubishi
Date: 2012-08-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Vestas stock took a boost on opening at the Copenhagen Stock Exchange this morning following the company’s confirmation that it is engaged in talks with Mitsubishi.
“Today, there is information in the market that Vestas is in dialogue with Mitsubishi Heavy Industries. Vestas can confirm that there is an on-going dialogue between the two companies regarding a potential strategic cooperation,” the Vestas website says adding that if an agreement is reached it will make a company announcement.
