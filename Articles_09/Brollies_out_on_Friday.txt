Title: Brollies_out_on_Friday
Date: 2012-09-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Those looking for an Indian summer are going to have to wait and hope it seems. The Met Office says that a front will be passing Denmark tomorrow that will dump some 20mm of rain across most regions of the country.
“We thought that the north would remain dry, but it looks as though the whole country will be getting rain,” says DMI Duty Meteorologist Klaus Larsen.
