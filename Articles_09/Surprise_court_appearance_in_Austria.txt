Title: Surprise_court_appearance_in_Austria
Date: 2012-09-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish father of a five-year-old boy, whose Austrian mother contests the father’s parental custody ruling in Denmark, has surprisingly appeared in court in Austria where he is charged with kidnapping his son.
Thomas Sørensen, who has sole custody of his son in Denmark, pleaded not guilty to the charges.
