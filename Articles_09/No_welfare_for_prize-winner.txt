Title: No_welfare_for_prize-winner
Date: 2012-09-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Sorø Council has won a lengthy legal battle in connection with its demand for the repayment of social security from a woman who won prizes in a television show.
Lone Christensen won several larger prizes in a television show, something she is not able to do while receiving social security, according to Dagbladet Ringsted.
