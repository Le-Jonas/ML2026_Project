Title: Bikers_freed_again
Date: 2012-09-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Most of the 114 Hells Angels and Bandidos bikers who were detained by police yesterday in an operation designed to search biker clubhouses, have been released again.
By this morning almost 100 of those detained from 17 clubhouses on Zealand had been released, according to a tally of enquiries to police stations by politiken.dk.
