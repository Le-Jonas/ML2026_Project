Title: Report_Banedanmark_staff_on_strike
Date: 2012-12-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Track maintenance personnel at Banedanmark, which maintains the country’s railway tracks and repairs points and signal faults, are on strike, according to Ekstra Bladet.
“Banedanmark treats its employees badly. We have no idea what is going on and they don’t respect us. I have no idea when I am to get a day off, and that is unacceptable,” Ekstra Bladet quotes Kenneth, who is believed to be the maintenance staff spokesman, as saying .
