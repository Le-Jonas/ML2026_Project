Title: Missing_Dane_turns_up_in_Germany
Date: 2013-05-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police are now able to close the case of a woman who was last seen cycling on May 9 in Åbyhøj where she lives. She has turned up in Germany.
A public appeal for information was issued four days after Majken Jensen’s disappearance, in a case that prompted widespread media coverage.
