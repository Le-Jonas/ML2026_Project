Title: Lufthansa_strike
Date: 2012-08-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Departures and arrivals of Lufthansa aircraft to Copenhagen Airport are expected to be affected today as a result of a strike among cabin personnel between 05.00 and 13.00.
Updated information on flights can be found at
www.lufthansa.com
and
www.frankfurt-airport.com
.
