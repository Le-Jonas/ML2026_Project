Title: Poll_SF_down_again
Date: 2013-03-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 shows support for the Socialist People’s Party continuing to wane, with only 4.2 per cent saying they would vote for the party – the lowest poll ever for SF in a Megafon survey and half a percentage point lower than three weeks ago.
According to the poll, the party has lost over half of its following compared to its election result in November 2011, which was at 9.2 per cent, and comes at a time when several senior SF members have left the party.
