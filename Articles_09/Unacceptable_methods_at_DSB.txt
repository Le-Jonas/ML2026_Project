Title: Unacceptable_methods_at_DSB
Date: 2013-04-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The legal adviser to the government has recommended that termination proceedings should be initiated against a deputy director at DSB following an investigation into the state railway’s cooperation with the Waterfront PR agency.
The investigation, carried out by the Bruun and Hjejle law firm, was commissioned by the Transport Ministry and DSB following suggestions that DSB had paid Waterfront to hamstring a journalist who had specialised in and was highly critical of DSB.
