Title: 14_left_wingers_arrested
Date: 2012-09-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Security and Intelligence Service PET has arrested 14 people affiliated to the left wing extremist environment in Copenhagen on suspicion of planning politically motivated vandalism against police and other public authorities, according to a PET statement.
“PET’s view is that those arrested had plans during the night of carrying out coordinated and politically motivated vandalism targeted at, among others, police and other public authorities,” the PET statement said.
