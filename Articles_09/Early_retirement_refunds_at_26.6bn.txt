Title: Early_retirement_refunds_at_26.6bn
Date: 2012-10-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest figures from the National Labour Market Authority show that a total of DKK26.6billion has been withdrawn by Danes in connection with a reform of the early retirement system.
“That is close to the final figure – we may have minor corrections later but this seems like the final figure,” says NLMA Chief Analyst Frederik Pedersen.
