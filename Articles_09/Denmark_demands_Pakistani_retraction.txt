Title: Denmark_demands_Pakistani_retraction
Date: 2012-10-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A former Pakistani High Court Judge has claimed in the Pakistani newspaper The News, that a Koran was to be burned in Denmark on Saturday, but the Danish embassy in Islamabad has refuted the claim and demanded a retraction.
Justice Fakhr-un-Nisa Khokar, a former MP and Lahore High Court judge speaks in her article in the Lahore newspaper of: “The incident in Denmark where it is announced that the Holy Quran will be burnt on the Next Saturday.”
