Title: Astrid_Krag_announces_candidacy
Date: 2012-09-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After days of rumours and controversy, Health Minister Astrid Krag, 29, is to announce she is a candidate for the leadership of the Socialist People’s Party, following last Friday’s announcement by Villy Søvndal that he is stepping down.
According to Politiken.dk’s information, Krag will make her official announcement this evening at 6.30 p.m.
