Title: Rumours_stop_bank_trading
Date: 2012-09-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Copenhagen Stock Exchange has temporarily suspended trading in the Vestjysk Bank stock, but the OMX Nordic website gave no immediate indications as to why, although a later statement from the bank said it had decided to reorganise.
The changes come in the wake of an article in Børsen this morning saying that several of Denmark’s smaller banks are at risk as a result of more stringent impairment requirements from the Financial Supervisory Authority.
