Title: Firemen_hurt_in_Copenhagen_fire
Date: 2013-01-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two firemen have been hurt while fighting a fire in the roof section of a building on Skt. Jakobs Plads in the Østerbro quarter of Copenhagen.
Both men were reported to be slightly injured when roofing tiles, part of the building’s chimney and embers fell to street level. A fire engine cabin and a hydraulic lift as well as several other vehicles were damaged by cascading debris.
