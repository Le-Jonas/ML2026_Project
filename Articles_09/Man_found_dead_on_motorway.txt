Title: Man_found_dead_on_motorway
Date: 2012-08-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Zealand police are calling for witnesses or information surrounding the discovery early this morning of a partially dressed man who was found on the Vestmotorvejen motorway near Bjæverskov in central Zealand.
The as yet unidentified man, who is believed to be in his 50s, was still alive when police arrived at the scene at around 3 a.m. this morning, but died shortly afterwards.
