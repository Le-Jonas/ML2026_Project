Title: Forgot_handbrake_&ndash;_ended_up_in_canal
Date: 2013-01-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen rescue services are battling to get a truck out of Christianhavns Canal after its driver forgot to pull the handbrake and the vehicle rolled into the waterway.
“The driver said that he had forgotten the handbrake and the truck was probably blown down into the canal by the wind,” Fire Chief Kristian Levy tells DR.
