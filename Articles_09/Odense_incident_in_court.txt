Title: Odense_incident_in_court
Date: 2012-08-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A large police contingent is currently guarding the approaches to the Odense courthouse where a 19-year-old man is being presented in a remand hearing in connection with a shooting incident and subsequent hospital invasion on Monday night.
The 19-year-old is being charged in connection with the forcible entry by some 70 men of the emergency ward at Odense Hospital on Monday night.
