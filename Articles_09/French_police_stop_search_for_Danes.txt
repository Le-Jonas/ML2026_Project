Title: French_police_stop_search_for_Danes
Date: 2012-10-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

French authorities have stopped searching for three Danish yachtsmen who are believed to have been on board a trimaran which was found drifting without its crew in the Bay of Biscay on Saturday.
An air and sea search operation, which has been under way since Saturday, has now been called off, with French authorities asking vessels in the area to keep an eye open for the Danes.
