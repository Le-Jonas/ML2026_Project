Title: Q2_Mærsk_earnings_down
Date: 2012-08-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A.P. Møller-Mærsk has published its Second Quarter interim accounts, showing a pre-tax profit of DK12.53billion, a 28 per cent drop on the same quarter in 2011 and an after tax result down 32 per cent.
The Q2 2012 result shows the group still affected by low freight rates for one of the world’s largest container companies, Mærsk Line.
