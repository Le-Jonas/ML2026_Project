Title: Toyota_recalls_16,000_vehicles_in_DK
Date: 2012-10-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Toyota has recalled some 16,000 vehicles in Denmark due to a potential fault in connection with electrical windows which can result in overheating and may cause the electrical button to melt.
“If a lubricant has been used that is not the original Toyota lubricant, it may cause heating that can melt the button,” says Toyota Press Spokesman Anders Tystrup.
