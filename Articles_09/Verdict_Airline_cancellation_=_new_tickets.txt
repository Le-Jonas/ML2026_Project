Title: Verdict_Airline_cancellation_=_new_tickets
Date: 2012-08-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

If your plane is cancelled, you have the right to passage with another aircraft as soon as possible.
And if the airline you were supposed to fly with is unable to help you with another departure, you are entitled to purchase a new ticket with another airline that your initial airline must pay for, according to a decision by the Copenhagen Municipal Court.
