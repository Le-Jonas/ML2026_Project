Title: SF_wants_to_stop_grant_grabbing
Date: 2012-08-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party (SF) is looking to change the admission criteria for vocational education after disclosures that schools are plagued by students who abuse the grant system.
“Grant grabbing is absolutely unreasonable, we are going to have to look at conditional admission,” says SF Education Committee Member Lisbeth Bech Poulsen.
