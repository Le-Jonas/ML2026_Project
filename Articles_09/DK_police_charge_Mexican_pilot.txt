Title: DK_police_charge_Mexican_pilot
Date: 2012-09-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Mexican pilot of a German-registered Lear Jet, which emergency landed in a cornfield on Bornholm last Saturday, is to be charged with endangering the lives of others.
“We believe that he has endangered the lives of others by flying without the right licence and without the necessary knowledge,” says Insp. Peter Jørgensen.
