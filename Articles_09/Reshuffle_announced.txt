Title: Reshuffle_announced
Date: 2012-10-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt has announced the limited government reshuffle that has resulted from the change in leadership of the Socialist People’s Party (SF).
As expected, Holger Nielsen, who was leader of the Socialist People’s Party from 1991 to 2005, is to be Tax Minister to replace Thor Möger Pedersen, while the party’s new leader Annette Vilhelmsen is appointed as Business and Growth Minister instead of Ole Sohn.
