Title: Cekic_un-gagged_in_party_reshuffle
Date: 2012-10-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Özlem Cekic, who was stripped of all her senior party positions by the previous Socialist People’s Party leadership has been reinstated by the new leadership and has been appointed party health spokeswoman.
Cekic, who is a nurse of profession, was dethroned in August after refusing a party whip and voting against the government’s Tax Agreement.
