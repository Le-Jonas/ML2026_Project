Title: Greenland&rsquo;s_Santa_went_bust_last_year
Date: 2012-10-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Last year, children across the world waited for an answer from Santa Claus in Greenland that never came. Reason: his executive secretary went bankrupt!
Thousands of children send letters to Santa Claus and his trusty Rudolf each year and normally, all thousands get a letter back. Due to his busy schedule, however, the job of answering the many letters has been outsourced to a local tourist office.
