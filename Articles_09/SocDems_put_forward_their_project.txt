Title: SocDems_put_forward_their_project
Date: 2012-08-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After a year’s crisis in the polls, the Social Democratic leadership has today outlined its political project.
Finance Minister Bjarne Corydon, Employment Minister Mette Frederiksen, Housing Minister Carsten Hansen and Group Chairman Henrik Sass Larsen have written an OPED article in today’s Politiken, in which they herald a showdown with the Liberalism of the past decade.
