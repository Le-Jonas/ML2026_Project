Title: Major_police_biker_and_gang_operation
Date: 2013-03-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police on the capital island have carried out a major operation targeting the biker and gang environment on Zealand and have carried out searches at some 50 addresses with 26 arrests having been made, according to a police news release.
“We have detained people who are suspected of several attempted killings, assaults and other types of serious crime, and we have been looking for illegal items. We are monitoring these groups very closely and will come down hard on all types of crime,” says Ch. Insp. Jørgen Skov of the Copenhagen Police Investigation Unit.
