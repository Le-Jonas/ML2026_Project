Title: Commission_begins_proceedings
Date: 2012-08-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The commission looking into what went on behind the scenes in connection with a Tax Authority investigation into the personal tax affairs of the prime minister’s husband, begins hearing witness testimony today.
The three commission judges are to examine how the authorities acted, or reacted, in connection with media suggestions that Stephen Kinnock had avoided paying tax in Denmark at a time when he was working in Switzerland, and where he did pay tax.
