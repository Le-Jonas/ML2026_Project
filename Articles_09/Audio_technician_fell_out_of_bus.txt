Title: Audio_technician_fell_out_of_bus
Date: 2012-08-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Swedish man who was found partially clothed and seriously injured on a Danish motorway has now been positively identified by members of his family.
Police say 58-year-old Tom Hofwander, who died shortly after being found, was an audio technician for the Swedish pop singer Veronica Maggio. Maggio and her entourage had been performing at the Skanderborg Festival.
