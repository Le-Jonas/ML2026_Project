Title: Holger_K_to_discuss_SF_in_coalition
Date: 2013-02-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party’s former leader and current tax minister, Holger Nielsen, is prepared to discuss whether the party should withdraw from the government, although he feels it would be ‘very foolish’ to do so.
Speaking to the media on his way in to an SF parliamentary group meeting, he says he is prepared to take the discussion with the local SF politicians who have severely criticised the party for being part of the government’s Growth Package that was announced yesterday.
