Title: Denmark_pleads_for_Khawaja_pardon
Date: 2012-09-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

All eight Danish parliamentary parties have signed an appeal to the King Hamad bin Isa al-Khalifa of Bahrain to release the Danish-Bahraini dissident Abdulhadi al-Khawaja, following a civilian appeals court decision earlier today to confirm the life sentence handed down by a military court last year.
The letter also calls for the release of al-Khawaja’s daughter Zainab.
