Title: Youths_give_themselves_up
Date: 2012-09-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Three young men of 15,16 and 18 who are suspected of killing one man and wounding another in Vanløse, gave themselves up overnight after police had published their names.
“It is good that the three came to their senses and reported in to the Copenhagen police. We have been working with them all night and they will be jointly charged with murder,” says Insp. Hans Erik Raben of the Copenhagen Police.
