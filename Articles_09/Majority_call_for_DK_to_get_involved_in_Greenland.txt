Title: Majority_call_for_DK_to_get_involved_in_Greenland
Date: 2013-01-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party is suggesting that Denmark should set up a new state-owned corporation, which along with pension funds and major companies could secure financing to invest in raw material prospecting and extraction in Greenland.
The idea has met with the approval of a non-governmental parliamentary majority.
