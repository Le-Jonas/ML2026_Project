Title: Danish_mag_to_show_topless_Duchess
Date: 2012-09-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Danish magazine has decided to print a 16-page special edition on Britain’s Duke and Duchess of Cambridge, including controversial pictures taken of the Duchess during a recent holiday in France.
The editor-in-chief of the magazine says it is in his magazine’s ‘DNA’ to satisfy reader curiosity.
