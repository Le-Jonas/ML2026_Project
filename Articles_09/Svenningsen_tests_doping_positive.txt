Title: Svenningsen_tests_doping_positive
Date: 2012-08-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Søren Svenningsen, a member of the Board of the Danish Cycling Union, chairman of the Holte Cycling Club and the former owner of Team Capinordic has immediately resigned from his posts after having been tested positive for performance enhancers.
Svenningsen was tested in connection with an H40 contest in Denmark. The tests are said to have contained traces of two glucocorticoids as well as testosterone.
