Title: Concern_at_Afghan_attacks
Date: 2012-09-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

NATO Secretary-General Anders Fogh Rasmussen says that the number of attacks against NATO soldiers by Afghans is ‘worrying’ but that the alliance is doing what it can to stop them.
“We are doing everything we can with our Afghan partners to reduce the risks as much as we can,” Fogh Rasmussen says during his monthly news conference, adding that NATO carries out extensive investigations into each killing.
