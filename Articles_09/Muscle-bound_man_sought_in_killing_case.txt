Title: Muscle-bound_man_sought_in_killing_case
Date: 2012-09-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police are searching for a 25-year-old, 185cm, muscular man in connection with the point blank street murder on Matthaeusgade in Copenhagen last night of a 48-year-old kiosk owner of Pakistani origin.
The killing, with a single shot to the head, may have been part of internal strife within the Copenhagen Pakistani kiosk environment.
