Title: 2,598_public_library_E-books_gone
Date: 2012-11-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Negotiations between libraries and the Danish Publishers Association (DPA) have fallen through, resulting in the disappearance from public library e-shelves of some 2,598 e-volumes.
After an outage of the digital lending system yesterday afternoon, only 1,572 e-books were left, mostly from publishers who are not members of the DPA.
