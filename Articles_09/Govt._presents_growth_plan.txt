Title: Govt._presents_growth_plan
Date: 2013-02-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government has announced its growth plan for Denmark, reducing company taxes by DK 10 billion and increasing public investment by DKK 6 billion.
Overall the centre-left government expects to create 150,000 jobs in the private sector up to 2020 and increase growth by some DKK 40 billion over the next seven years, according to the “Growth Plan DK – Strong Companies, more jobs” incentive.
