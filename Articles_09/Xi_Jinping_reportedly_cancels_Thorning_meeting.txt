Title: Xi_Jinping_reportedly_cancels_Thorning_meeting
Date: 2012-09-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

China’s Vice-President Xi Jinping has reportedly cancelled a meeting with Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt, fuelling suggestions that he may be ill.
Xi Jinping has been strongly mooted as a successor to President Hu Jintao at the next party congress.
