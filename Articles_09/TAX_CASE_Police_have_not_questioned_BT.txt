Title: TAX_CASE_Police_have_not_questioned_BT
Date: 2012-09-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

It may have been the tabloid BT that printed leaked confidential personal tax information in the prime minister’s husband Stephen Kinnock’s tax case, but the tabloid’s personnel have not been questioned by the police.
“I can confirm that we have not been questioned by Copenhagen Police in the case,” Editor-in-Chief Olav Skaaning Andersen tells Berlingske.
