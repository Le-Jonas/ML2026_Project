Title: Controversial_Muslim_in_Copenhagen
Date: 2013-03-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Khalid Yasin, the controversial American Muslim lecturer who supports the death penalty for homosexuals and has said that HIV is a Western conspiracy, is to visit Denmark Friday, shortly after a similar speaking appearance in Britain was cancelled.
Yasin has been invited to lecture by the Viden om Islam ([VIOMIS] Ed: Knowledge about Islam) organisation at the Metropol in Nørrebro. He is to speak on the subject of “We Are 1”, on solidarity and community in Islam, as part of a charity event in favour of Syria.
