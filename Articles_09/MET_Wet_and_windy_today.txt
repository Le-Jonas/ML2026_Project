Title: MET_Wet_and_windy_today
Date: 2012-09-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The week ahead promises relatively un-dramatic and autumnal weather, once Monday’s weather drama has ended during the night.
“Monday will start off quite peacefully – but will develop into the week’s most dramatic day,” says Duty Met Officer Jørn Thomsen of the Danish Meteorological Institute, adding relatively heavy rain and high winds are expected to develop as the day wears on.
