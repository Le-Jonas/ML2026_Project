Title: Police_call_for_help_in_mysterious_death
Date: 2012-10-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Central and West Jutland Police have appealed to the public for help in determining what happened to a 36-year-old woman who was found dead in the water off the emergency road near Hanstholm.
Police are particularly seeking the help of the driver of a large, black vehicle which was parked at the beach at around 1.30 p.m. on Sunday, and a group of five to seven people with an Alsatian dog who were walking along the beach on Sunday afternoon.
