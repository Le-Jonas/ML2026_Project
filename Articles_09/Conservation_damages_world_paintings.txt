Title: Conservation_damages_world_paintings
Date: 2013-05-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The conservation method used to preserve historical national artworks in the 1950s and 1960s is in fact damaging them instead, according to new Danish research.
Instead of protecting paintings from damp, the wax and resin used has made the works more susceptible and can make them shrink and lose their colour.
