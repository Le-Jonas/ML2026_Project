Title: Vilhelmsen_wins_overwhelming_victory
Date: 2012-10-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party’s 13,000 members have chosen their new party leader.
Annette Vilhelmsen, 52, won an overwhelming victory, garnering 4,793 votes or 66 per cent of the vote compared to Health Minister Astrid Krag’s 2,472 votes or 34 per cent of the ballot.
