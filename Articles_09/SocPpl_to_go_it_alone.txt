Title: SocPpl_to_go_it_alone
Date: 2012-09-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Democratic-Socialist People’s Party policy tandem that was forged up to the last election and gave rise to a common platform for the two parties, will not continue up to the next election.
Both candidates for the leadership of the Socialist People’s Party have made it clear that they plan to profile their own party and its policies up to the next election in an attempt to win back favour in the electorate.
