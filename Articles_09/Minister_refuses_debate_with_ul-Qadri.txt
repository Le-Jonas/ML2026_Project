Title: Minister_refuses_debate_with_ul-Qadri
Date: 2012-09-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Integration Minister Karen Hækkerup is pulling out of a conference at Copenhagen Business School next week as she was not aware that she would be sharing the podium with the Pakistani founder of the Sufi-based Minhaj ul-Quran, Muhammad Tahir ul-Qadri.
“I agreed that I would speak at an event that would address radicalisation and political fanaticism,” says Hækkerup of the New-Danish Youth Council’s event on preventing the radicalisation of young people.
