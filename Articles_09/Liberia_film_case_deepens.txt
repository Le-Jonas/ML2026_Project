Title: Liberia_film_case_deepens
Date: 2012-08-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Danish film about alleged corruption in Liberia allowing businessmen to buy diplomatic titles has developed into a war of words between the film’s producer and authorities in Liberia and a lawyer who allegedly received payment for introductions.
Varney Sherman, one of Liberia’s top lawyers, who holds a senior honorary position in President Ellen Johnson Sirleaf’s party, has firmly denied having taken a USD35,000 payment in order to help secure a diplomatic title for the Danish film-maker Mads Brügger.
