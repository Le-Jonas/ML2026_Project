Title: Bagger_in_business_again
Date: 2012-09-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Stein Bagger, who is three and a half years into a seven-year sentence for the IT-Factory scam is taking a business internship in a company controlled by one of his former business partners, according to Børsen.
The report says Bagger has been attached to a moving and storage company controlled by Royne Carlsson and managed by another friend Rene Andersen. Apparently Bagger has been working at the company under the name of Steen Bech.
