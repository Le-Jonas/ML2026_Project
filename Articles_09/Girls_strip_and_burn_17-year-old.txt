Title: Girls_strip_and_burn_17-year-old
Date: 2012-09-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 17-year-old girl was subjected to a violent attack, threats and being stripped by three other girls near Karlslunde Lake shortly before midnight on Monday night, the Central and West Zealand Police have disclosed today.
The 17-year-old had agreed to meet the other three and all four walked down to Karlslunde Lake where a quarrel broke out, ending in the 17-year-old being kicked in the head and body, burned with a cigarette and a lighter, threatened and stripped.
