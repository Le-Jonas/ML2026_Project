Title: Police_warn_of_gangland_war
Date: 2012-12-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police are warning that a new spate of gang warfare may be on the way and have been searching gang member houses in an attempt to prevent an outbreak of violence.
“Zealand police have been searching several addresses in Roskilde, Hedehusene and Copenhagen,” Copenhagen West Police say in a news release, adding the operation has been carried out in cooperation with the National Investigation Centre, SKAT and Gang task Force East.
