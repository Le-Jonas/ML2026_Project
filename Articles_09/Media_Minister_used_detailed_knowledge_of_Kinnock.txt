Title: Media_Minister_used_detailed_knowledge_of_Kinnock
Date: 2012-09-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Former Tax Minister Troels Lund Poulsen (Lib) had detailed knowledge of the Stephen Kinnock-Helle Thorning-Schmidt tax case and actively tried to influence the Tax Authority decision, according to Berlingske.
Documents and witnesses that the newspaper has interviewed show that Lund Poulsen felt he was so well versed in the case that he was able to challenge senior Tax Ministry civil servants immediately after receiving the Tax Authority decision.
