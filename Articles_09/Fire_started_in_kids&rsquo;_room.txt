Title: Fire_started_in_kids&rsquo;_room
Date: 2012-09-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Some 10 flats in a building in Kolding have been destroyed following a fire on Sunday evening which appears to have started in a children’s room.
Some 120 people were evacuated from the building and those whose flats were not affected will be unable to return to their flats today.
