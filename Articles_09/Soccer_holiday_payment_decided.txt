Title: Soccer_holiday_payment_decided
Date: 2012-09-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Football Players’ Association has won its case at the Court of Arbitration in which three test players have been demanding vacation money from an already cash-strapped Brøndby IF.
The court has decided that Brøndby IF must back pay Peter Madsen, Thomas Rasmussen and Martin Ericsson vacation payments - some DKK4million.
