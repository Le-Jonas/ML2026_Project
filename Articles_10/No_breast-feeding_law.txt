Title: No_breast-feeding_law
Date: 2013-06-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Equality Minister Manu Sareen is not prepared to legislate to force cafés, restaurants and other public places to allow women to breast-feed their babies in establishments, as proposed by the Red Green Party.
The Red Greens put forward their proposal following a decision by the Equal Treatment Board, which ruled last week that a café owner had the right to refuse a woman who was breast-feeding her baby.
