Title: Greenland&rsquo;s_new_finance_officer_resigns_after_2_days
Date: 2013-09-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Greenland’s new Permanent Under-Secretary for Finances Carsten Frank Hansen has resigned his post after two days, following disclosures that he has been charged, though not yet indicted, with a breach of trust, according to Sermitsiaq.
“I am sorry that I didn’t make the information public. It was not on purpose as I have no wish to lie,” Hansen says.
