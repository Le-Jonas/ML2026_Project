Title: Diabetes_to_double_by_2025
Date: 2013-07-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Diabetes Association says that over half a million Danes will be suffering from diabetes in coming years, causing major problems for the general health budget.
There are currently some 300,000 people with diabetes in Denmark – double the number a decade ago, according to Berlingske.
