Title: Chapter_1_By_the_way_I_think_I_am_coming_back_to_Syria
Date: 2013-10-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Ram Hiramic

Damascus
I just can't take my eyes off my brother's bed and the pajamas that he wore on his last night here. He messaged me the other night saying that they were finally on their way, after all the suffering they went through in Egypt, and were now on board the ship.
On average the journey will take 4 to 5 days, but there is no way to contact them until they arrive in Italy. But what I just read on the Internet has freaked me out. A boat sank close to the Italian shore. All sorts of horrible ideas come into my head. What if my two brothers were on that boat? Dear God, please protect them … take my life and let them be safe. I can't tell my mom, she will faint if I do. I will have to hide the news until I make sure that they are safe.
