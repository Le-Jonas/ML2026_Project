Title: Regional_salmonella_campaign
Date: 2013-06-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

An action plan is to be put into effect to bring down the number of cows infected with salmonella, according to Food Minister Mette Gjerskov.
“It is obvious that the 2008 action plan is not working. We need to start a new one, with particular emphasis on cattle,” Gjerskov says.
