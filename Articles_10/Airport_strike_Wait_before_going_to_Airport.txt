Title: Airport_strike_Wait_before_going_to_Airport
Date: 2013-09-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A wildcat strike among security personnel is causing major delays at Copenhagen’s Kastrup Airport and has prompted airport authorities to urge travellers due to fly out to delay their arrival at the airport and keep themselves informed of the situation on the airport website.
