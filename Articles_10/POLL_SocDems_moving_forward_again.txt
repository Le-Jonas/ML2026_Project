Title: POLL_SocDems_moving_forward_again
Date: 2013-06-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 shows the centre-left having whittled away 2.5 percentage points of the centre-right lead, with the senior coalition Social Democratic party moving 2.2 percentage points up from its historically low 15.9 per cent in the last Megafon poll, to 18.1 per cent now.
“It’s good news and we’re happy with any progress,” says Social Democratic Political Spokesman Magnus Heunicke.
