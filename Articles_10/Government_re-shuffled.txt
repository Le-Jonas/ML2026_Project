Title: Government_re-shuffled
Date: 2013-08-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The prime minister has announcd she has reshuffled her cabinet, with the Socialist People’s Party Leader Annette Vilhelmsen destined for the Social Affairs Ministry while the Social Democratic Chief Whip Henrik Sass Larsen is to take over her Business and Investment Ministry.
Ingoing and outgoing ministers are due at Amalienborg Palace at 11 a.m. to be presented to Crown Prince Frederik, who is regent during Queen Margrethe’s absence on holiday.
