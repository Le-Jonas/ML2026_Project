Title: Ferrari_ploughs_into_bus_stop
Date: 2013-08-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The driver of a yellow Ferrari lost control of his vehicle this afternoon and plummeted into a bus stop near Nørrebro Station in Copenhagen.
Eyewitnesses reported that the accident happened when the front of the vehicle, which was travelling at speed, lifted and ploughed into the bus stop, according to Ritzau.
