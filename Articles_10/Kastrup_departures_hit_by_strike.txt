Title: Kastrup_departures_hit_by_strike
Date: 2013-09-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Security personnel at Kastrup International Airport have gone on strike, preventing passengers from going through security checks for international flights.
“This is a wildcat strike and we don’t have that much information at the present. Security check booths for international flights are closed,” says Copenhagen Airport Media Spokeswoman Anne Munck Mikkelsen.
