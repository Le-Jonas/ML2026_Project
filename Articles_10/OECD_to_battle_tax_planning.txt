Title: OECD_to_battle_tax_planning
Date: 2013-06-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

EU Correspondent, Brussels.
The Danish Tax Authority’s case against Novo Nordisk is yet another example of the world’s tax regulations being antiquated and in need of change, according to one of the world’s most influential tax experts, the OECD’s Director of the Centre for Tax Policy and Administration Pascal Saint-Amans.
