Date: 2014-03-16
Article Type: Anmeldelser, cd-beat
Author: Sun Kil Moons ’Benji’ er et uhørt intimt album, der vil holde mange år frem.

Simon Lund
Musikredaktør
Det begynder med et dødsfald. Et af de mere absurde og tragiske af slagsen.
»Carissa burned to death last night in a freak accident fire/ in her yard in Brewster her daughter came home from a party and found her/ same way as my uncle, who was her grandfather/ an aerosol can blew up in the trash, goddamn, what were the odds?«.
