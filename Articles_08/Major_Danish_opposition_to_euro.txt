Title: Major_Danish_opposition_to_euro
Date: 2011-12-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Statistics Denmark euro survey for Danske Bank shows that a large majority of Danes would reject membership of the euro if a referendum were held now.
The survey, which was carried out in the first two weeks of December, shows a 59.2 per cent majority against joining the euro and a further 11.6 per cent who would possibly say no, with only 26.7 per cent in favour or perhaps in favour.
