Title: Red_Green_split_over_military
Date: 2012-07-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

As the Red Green Party moves towards updating its platform, a rift has developed within the party regarding the existence of the Danish military.
In recent weeks, several members of the party’s powerful Board have been calling for the revolution that is described in the party’s manifesto and which includes the disbanding of the police and military.
