Title: Vestager_All_European_countries_must_act
Date: 2011-11-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The countries of Europe must show that they are able to carry out responsible economic policies if Europe is to get out of the crisis that has prompted Moody’s to warn that all European countries are in danger of economic collapse, according to Economy Minister Margrethe Vestager (SocLib).
“There is a feeling of deep concern throughout the EU. No-one is playing around. It’s a question of staying on course and showing Moody’s and the other market players that Europe can take the decisions and see them implemented through rigorous economic management,” Vestager tells TV2News.
