Title: Fears_of_a_new_Danish_recession
Date: 2011-11-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Economists are warning that Denmark may be on the brink of a new recession after export figures for September show a drop of 1.8 per cent.
“Danish export companies are now feeling the effect of the European debt crisis in falling sales,” says Nykredit Markets Senior Economist Tore Stramer.
