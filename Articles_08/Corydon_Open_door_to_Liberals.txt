Title: Corydon_Open_door_to_Liberals
Date: 2012-06-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Liberal Party is welcome back into negotiations with Finance Minister Bjarne Corydon regarding a tax reform.
“The Liberals are welcome. We haven’t thrown them out. They have been busy trying to find explanations as to why they don’t want to continue discussions with us. My door is very open,” Corydon says.
