Title: Executions_increased_in_2011
Date: 2012-03-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The year 2011 saw more executions than in 2010, according to Amnesty International’s annual report on the death penalty.
At least 676 people were executed in 20 countries last year, an increase of 149 on 2010, with the increase predominantly in the Middle East where executions increased by some 50 per cent.
