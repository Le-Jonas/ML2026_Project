Title: DK_ambassador_Greek_plebiscite_is_risky
Date: 2011-10-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s ambassador to Greece, Tom Nørring, says that a plan to hold a referendum in Greece on the EU’s bailout package is a risky affair.
“It is very risky and some commentators speculate that this is Papandreou’s alternative to calling a general election. But these are speculations. It’s all very new,” Nørring says.
