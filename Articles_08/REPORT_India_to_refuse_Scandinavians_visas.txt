Title: REPORT_India_to_refuse_Scandinavians_visas
Date: 2012-07-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

India has become so incensed with being treated differently from Western nations that it has ‘clamped visa curbs’ on visitors from Denmark, Sweden and Norway, according to a report in India’s The Telegraph.
The report, citing highly placed sources says that the move “will affect people-to-people, business and official-level contacts with these three Scandinavian countries” and has been sent in a message to all European Union countries.
