Title: Techno_drug_party_&lsquo;frightening&rsquo;
Date: 2012-02-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police say they are stunned by the amount of drugs found on young people attending a techno party in Copenhagen on Saturday, with some of those found in danger of their lives.
“We have noted 106 cases of drugs and other offences,” says Insp. Bjarne Christensen of Copenhagen Police Special Patrols.
