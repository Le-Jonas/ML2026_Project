Title: Løkke_urges_Gaddafi_to_surrender
Date: 2011-08-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

As Libyan rebels continue their takeover of the Libyan capital Tripoli, Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen says that Col. Gaddafi should now give himself up.
“What we are witnessing now is hopefully the end game. The rebels are in Tripoli and Gaddafi should now, if he has even the slightest modicum of respect for his people, surrender so the transition to a more democratic Libya can take place without any more bloodshed,” Løkke Rasmussen tells TV2News.
