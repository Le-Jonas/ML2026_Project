Title: Biggest_ever_cartel_case_under_way
Date: 2012-03-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In what promises to be the biggest ever cartel case in Denmark, 18 companies and 23 senior employees in the construction industry have been charged with coordinating quotes for private and public construction projects in recent years.
The prosecutor for economic crime (SØK) says the total amount of charges stands at 41, with 60-70 people being questioned in the case.
