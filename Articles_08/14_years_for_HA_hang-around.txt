Title: 14_years_for_HA_hang-around
Date: 2011-11-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 25-year-old Hells Angels hang-around has today been sentenced to 13 years and seven months in prison after being found guilty of the murder of a young immigrant in Tingbjerg in Copenhagen in August 2008.
The sentence came after three magistrates and five of six jurors found Fabricius guilty of killing 19-year-old Osman Nuri Dogan in a rain of bullets on the Tingbjerg Estate in Copenhagen in August 2008.
