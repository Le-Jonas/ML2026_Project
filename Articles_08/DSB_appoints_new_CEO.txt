Title: DSB_appoints_new_CEO
Date: 2011-12-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s national railway group DSB has announced that Jesper T. Lok has been appointed as the corporation’s new CEO, taking over from Christian Roslev who resigned because of stress after only three months in the job.
Lok comes from a position as CEO of the towage, salvage and rescue company Svitzer, which is part of the A.P. Møller-Mærsk group.
