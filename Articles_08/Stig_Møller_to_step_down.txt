Title: Stig_Møller_to_step_down
Date: 2011-12-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One of the most popular Conservative Party politicians – former Foreign Minister Per Stig Møller has announced he will not be standing at the next election, and raises doubts as to whether he will serve a full electoral period.
“I would rather not have contested the election we have just had, but I was persuaded to continue. And I have said that I will under no circumstances contest the next election,” Stig Møller tells Berlingske.
