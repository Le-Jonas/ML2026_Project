Title: Bankruptcies_up,_foreclosures_down
Date: 2012-02-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Statistics Denmark has just released its January figures for bankruptcies and foreclosures, showing a sharp increase in liquidations, but a drop in foreclosures.
January saw a seasonally adjusted 511 bankruptcies compared with 449 in December 2011 – the highest number of monthly bankruptcies in any month in the past 12. Some 851 people were employed in the companies concerned.
