Title: Toll_ring_dropped
Date: 2012-02-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government has finally dropped its controversial congestion charge toll ring around Copenhagen and according to reports is to spend DKK1 billion on cheaper fares and investment in public transport across the country.
Financing is to be found by tightening tax rules on leasing and demonstration vehicles. At the same time, a commission is to be set up to study ways to solve Copenhagen’s congestion problems, with a report to be ready by January 1, 2013.
