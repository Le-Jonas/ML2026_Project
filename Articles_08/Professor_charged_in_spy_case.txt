Title: Professor_charged_in_spy_case
Date: 2012-04-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 49-year-old professor at Copenhagen University is under suspicion for having helped Russian agents in Denmark and has been arrested and charged under Denmark’s mild espionage paragraph 108, according to Weekendavisen Friday.
The paragraph deals with those who without having divulged state secrets have helped foreign intelligence agencies to operate in Denmark.
