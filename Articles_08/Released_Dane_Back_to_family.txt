Title: Released_Dane_Back_to_family
Date: 2012-01-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Poul Hagen Thisted, who was rescued from Somali pirates by U.S. Seals last week is on his way home to Denmark, the Danish Refugee Council has announced.
“My thanks go out for the prayers and support I have received, but right now my top priority is to be with my family,” Thisted says in declining to be interviewed.
