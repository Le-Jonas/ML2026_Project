Title: Bandidos_and_Hells_Angels_scrap_peace_deal
Date: 2011-09-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After almost a decade and a half under a peace agreement forged following the biker wars of the 1990s, the Bandidos and Hells Angels have confirmed that their peace deal has been scrapped.
“It’s very simple. It no longer exists,” Bandidos Deputy President Michael Rosenvold tells Jydske Vestkysten.
