Title: Crown_Prince_as_IOC_monitor
Date: 2011-09-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Crown Prince Frederik, who has been a member of the International Olympic Committee for two years, has been elected to the IOC Coordination Commission that is to monitor preparations in Pyeongchang for the 2018 Winter Olympics in the Taebaek mountains in South Korea.
“I am looking forward to the task. Members travel to the host city as advisers on how to optimise the preparations and carry out the Olympic Games,” Crown Prince Frederik says.
