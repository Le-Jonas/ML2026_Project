Title: Danish_pensioner_killed_in_Philippines
Date: 2012-06-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 65-year-old Danish pensioner has been found dead in the Philippines in what appears to have been a murder.
According to Svend-Erik Hansen’s daughter, who has sent an e-mail to bt.dk, her father was killed in his holiday house which had been cleared of valuables.
