Title: Boil_water_in_Nørrebro
Date: 2011-08-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen Energy says that people in some parts of Copenhagen – Nørrebro and some parts of the Inner City – should continue until at least Tuesday evening to boil their tap water as a result of bacterial pollution.
“The amount of bacteria in drinking water has dropped, but there is still so much that we are telling people to continue to boil their water,” says Copenhagen Energy Press Spokeswoman Astrid Skotte.
