Title: Vestas_stock_down
Date: 2012-07-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After a turbulent day with rumours of a Vestas takeover, the company’s stock ended 2.42 per cent down on its opening price at DKK27.85.
Earlier Monday, Vestas stock dropped some 10 per cent following a dramatic recommendation downgrade by the Norwegian ABG Sundal Collier investment bank and widespread market sentiments of a downturn in the European wind turbine market.
