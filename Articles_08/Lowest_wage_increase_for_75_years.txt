Title: Lowest_wage_increase_for_75_years
Date: 2011-10-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Wage increases for the Danish woman or man on the street are as low now as they were in the 1930s, according to calculations by the Danish Employers Association (DA) and reported in Berlingske.
DA Deputy Director Fini Beilin says that Denmark is also in an economic situation close to that of the 1930s.
