Title: Mentally_unstable_man_stabs_three
Date: 2012-03-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Three people have been injured, one seriously, following a stabbing attack against personnel at the Mental Health Unit of Odense University Hospital, Ritzau reports.
“A male doctor has been seriously injured and two female employees have also been hurt,” says Duty Officer Lars Fredensborg of the Funen Police.
