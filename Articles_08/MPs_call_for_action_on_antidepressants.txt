Title: MPs_call_for_action_on_antidepressants
Date: 2011-10-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Both sides of the parliamentary divide are calling for an investigation into why the Danish Medicines Agency failed to inform general practitioners of the possible serious side-effects of taking anti-depressants during pregnancy.
According to Ekstra Bladet the Agency was already aware of reports of serious side-effects and deaths of Danish babies in 2007, but the information was not made public until 2011.
