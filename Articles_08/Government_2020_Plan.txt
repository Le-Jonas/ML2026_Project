Title: Government_2020_Plan
Date: 2012-05-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Party leaders and senior ministers of the tripartite Danish coalition present their long-awaited 2020 Plan today, outlining the economic plan that the centre-left government would like Denmark to follow for the next eight years.
Presented by Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt (SocDem), Economy Minister Margrethe Vestager (SocLib) and Foreign Minister Villy Søvndal (SocPpl) the three are outlining the programme which they see as balancing Denmark’s budgets up to 2020.
