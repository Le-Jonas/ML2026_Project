Title: OBIT_Ib_Spang_Olsen
Date: 2012-01-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One of Denmark’s legendary artists and authors, Ib Spang Olsen, has died at the age of 90 after a short illness, according to his family.
Spang Olsen was best known for his illustrations and not least for his work with Halfdan Rasmussen’s books of children’s rhymes. He was also an author of children’s books in his own right.
