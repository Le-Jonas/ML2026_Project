Title: Hedegaard_derides_US_on_climate
Date: 2011-09-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s EU Climate Commissioner Connie Hedegaard has delivered an unusually harsh criticism, of the United States, claiming the US is ignoring scientific facts in the climate debate.
“I am shocked that the United States has a debate that is so far from scientific fact,” Hedegaard says adding that it is difficult to see “bright spots” in the American climate debate.
