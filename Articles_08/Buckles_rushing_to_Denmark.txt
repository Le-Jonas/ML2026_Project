Title: Buckles_rushing_to_Denmark
Date: 2011-10-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

With increasing signs that investors are unhappy with G4S plans to take over ISS, the G4S Chief Executive Nick Buckles is reported to be preparing for urgent meetings in Copenhagen on Monday to drum up support for the acquisition.
The move comes after several major investors have openly joined a chorus of critics of the proposed deal, according to Børsen.
