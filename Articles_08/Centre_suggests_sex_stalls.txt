Title: Centre_suggests_sex_stalls
Date: 2011-11-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Working groups in Odense on Denmark’s middle island of Funen have been discussing how to refurbish the Østerbro Quarter of the city which has become increasingly run down.
One suggestion on how to improve conditions for the quarter’s street prostitutes has been to develop zones to make the quarter safer, according to Fyens Stiftstidende.
