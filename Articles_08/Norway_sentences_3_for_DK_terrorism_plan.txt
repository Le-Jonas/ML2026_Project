Title: Norway_sentences_3_for_DK_terrorism_plan
Date: 2012-01-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Oslo Municipal Court has found two men guilty of preparing a terrorist attack in Denmark, while a third man was found guilty of aiding and abetting the two others in the first Norwegian judgment involving terrorism plans.
Mikael Davud, 40, a Norwegian of Uighur extraction was found guilty and sentenced to seven years while his accomplice SSSB, 38, an Iraqi Kurd was sentenced to 3 years and six months for procuring explosive material.
