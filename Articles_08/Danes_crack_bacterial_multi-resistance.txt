Title: Danes_crack_bacterial_multi-resistance
Date: 2011-12-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish researchers have patented a new medicine that can cure patients suffering from infections with bacteria that are multi-resistant to antibiotics, according to Videnskab.dk.
According to the report, the medicine, which is something of a revolution in the battle against multi-resistant bacteria, may be available within the next two years.
