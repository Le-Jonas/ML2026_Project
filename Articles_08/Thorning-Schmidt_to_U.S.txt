Title: Thorning-Schmidt_to_U.S
Date: 2012-02-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt travels to Washington tomorrow on an official visit for bilateral and EU talks with President Obama.
“First and foremost the visit will confirm the good relations between Denmark and the United States – between the Obama administration and the new Social Democratic-led government in Denmark,” says Social Democratic Foreign Policy Spokesman Jeppe Kofoed.
