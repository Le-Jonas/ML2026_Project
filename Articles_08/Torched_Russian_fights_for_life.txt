Title: Torched_Russian_fights_for_life
Date: 2012-01-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 50-year-old Russian man, who was doused with petrol and set alightt in Aarhus over the weekend, is fighting for his life at Rigshospitalet after suffering extensive burns.
Another Russian man is to appear in remand court this afternoon.
