Title: Five_men_charged_with_terrorist_arson
Date: 2012-04-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Five Danish men have been charged under Denmark’s anti-terrorism laws with arson or conspiracy to commit arson in the Greater Copenhagen area, according to the director of public prosecutions in a news release.
According to the indictment, charges involve several cases of arson or attempted arson during 2010 and 2011. The charges also involve conspiracy to commit arson at several buildings, including the Police Academy, the Danish National Police, the Security and Intelligence Service and the Greek Embassy.
