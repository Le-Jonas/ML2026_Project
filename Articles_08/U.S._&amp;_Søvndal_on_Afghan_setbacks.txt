Title: U.S._&amp;_Søvndal_on_Afghan_setbacks
Date: 2012-03-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Security and peace in Afghanistan may suffer when NATO’s troops leave Afghanistan at the end of 2014, according to the U.S. Special Envoy for Afghanistan and Pakistan Marc Grossman during a meeting with the Danish Foreign Minister Villy Søvndal in Copenhagen.
Grossman’s statement comes a week after Søvndal told Politiken that there could be a setback in Afghanistan when foreign soldiers leave the country and Afghanistan itself takes over responsibility for security in the country.
