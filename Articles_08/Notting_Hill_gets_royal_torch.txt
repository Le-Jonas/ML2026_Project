Title: Notting_Hill_gets_royal_torch
Date: 2012-07-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

London’s Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea will have a royal visit in 20 minutes when Denmark’s Crown Prince Frederik graces Notting Hill as he relays the Olympic flame through the West London area.
At about 16.20 London time, the Danish Crown Prince will be running the flame some 360 metres from Ladbroke Grove through Notting Hill Gate to pass the flame on to the next runner at Hillgate Street.
