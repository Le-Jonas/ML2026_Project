Title: Seeberg_Fogh_threatened_Stig_Møller_on_Iraq
Date: 2012-04-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Gitte Seeberg, who is a former senior Conservative, suggests in a new book that former Foreign Minister Per Stig Møller was ordered to change his mind in 2003 and support Denmark’s participation in the Iraq War, according to Jyllands-Posten.
At the time, Seeberg was foreign policy spokeswoman for the party.
