Title: Theft_of_1,000_Chrysanthemum_bombs
Date: 2011-12-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police are highly concerned following the theft of about 1,000 Chrysanthemum fireworks from a secure store near Viborg, according to Viborg Stifts Folkeblad.
The round shell fireworks were stolen between December 12 and 14, with thieves gaining access to the store using a blowtorch to cut into metal doors.
