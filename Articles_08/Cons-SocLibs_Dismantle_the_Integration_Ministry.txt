Title: Cons-SocLibs_Dismantle_the_Integration_Ministry
Date: 2011-08-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The first concrete result of the pact between the Conservative and Social Liberal parties may be the dismantling of the Integration Ministry in order to make it simpler for Denmark to enlist foreign labour.
“It is extremely important to see immigration as a necessary resource. Some choose Denmark, but others avoid it, so it is important that we make it as easy as possible for people to choose Denmark,” Conservative Integration Spokesman Naser Khader tells Berlingske.
