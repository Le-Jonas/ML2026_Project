Title: 50_doctors_and_nurses_monitored
Date: 2011-11-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Zealand Health Inspector has an unusually large number of 50 doctors and nurses being monitored for medicine or alcohol abuse, according to Sjællandske.
“We see more supervision cases than previously. The general public’s acceptance threshold is lower, and they are more willing to report issues. Everyone agrees that it is not acceptable,” Health Inspector Claus Iversen of Region Zealand tells Sjællandske.
