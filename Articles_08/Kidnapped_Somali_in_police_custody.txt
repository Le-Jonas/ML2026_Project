Title: Kidnapped_Somali_in_police_custody
Date: 2011-10-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Somali man who was kidnapped along with a 60-year-old Danish man and a 32-year-old American woman in Somalia yesterday, is in custody as police investigate his role in the kidnapping.
The three, who all work for the Danish Demining Group under the Danish Refugee Council were kidnapped by a group of 10 heavily armed men on Tuesday in the town of Galkayo in Northern Somalia.
