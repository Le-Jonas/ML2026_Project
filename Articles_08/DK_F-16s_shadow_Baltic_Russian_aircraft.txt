Title: DK_F-16s_shadow_Baltic_Russian_aircraft
Date: 2011-11-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Lithuania-based Danish F-16s of NATO’s Baltic Air Policing operation were scrambled yesterday to shadow four Russian Air Force aircraft in the Eastern Baltic, the Danish Air force confirmed Tuesday.
The Danish aircraft were scrambled twice to escort an unusual number of Russian military aircraft which had taken off from the Russian Baltic exclave of Kaliningrad.
