Date: 2011-12-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The final withdrawal of American troops from Iraq marks the end of one of the most tragic wars of recent years.
The war is an icon of the ideologically-driven foreign policy of the Bush era. An era in which manipulated intelligence and radical stereotypes were allowed to out-trump international law and considered reflection. All of those who allowed themselves be drawn in share responsibility. Including Denmark.
