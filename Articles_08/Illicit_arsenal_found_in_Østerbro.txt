Title: Illicit_arsenal_found_in_Østerbro
Date: 2012-03-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Task Force East, which is the Police Gang Unit, seized a major arsenal of weapons and explosives Thursday in a cellar at Forbindelsesvej in Østerbro and have yet to document the extent of the arsenal, according to Copenhagen Police in a news release Friday.
“We found so much that we don’t yet have a final tally of what was discovered,” says Police Inspector Magnus Andresen of Task Force East.
