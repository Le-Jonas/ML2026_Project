Title: Supreme_Court_Attack_was_terrorism
Date: 2012-05-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Supreme Court has returned its verdict on Muhudiin Muhammed Geele, 30, who attacked the Danish cartoonist Kurt Westergaard with an axe on New Year’s Day in 2010, determining that the incident was a terrorist act.
Geele has been sentenced to 10 years in prison after which he has been permanently banished from Denmark.
