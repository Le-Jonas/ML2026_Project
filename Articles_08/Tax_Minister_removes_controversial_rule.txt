Title: Tax_Minister_removes_controversial_rule
Date: 2012-06-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Tax Minister Thor Möger Pedersen has accepted that it is unreasonable that older employees should pay more tax than younger ones.
“I agree that it is unreasonable that those who could have taken out their pensions but have cosen to continue working do not benefit from increasing the level at which top-end tax is paid,” Möger Pedersen says.
