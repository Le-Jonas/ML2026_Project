Title: Al-Khawaja_appeal_put_off_for_a_week
Date: 2012-04-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: The appeal case for Abdulhadi al-Khawaja, who is over two months into a hunger strike in Bahrain, has been postponed for a week until April 30, according to the Bahrain Human Rights organisation in a Twitter release.

Al-Khawaja’s appeal case was to have been heard along with those of 13 others today.
