Title: Roof_fire_breaks_out_in_Valby
Date: 2012-01-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Some 200 people were evacuated Tuesday afternoon when fire broke out in the roof area of a three storey apartment building on Valbygårdsvej in Copenhagen.
“Our first task was to stop the fire from spreading sideways so it didn’t take all of the building. We have managed to do that now we are trying to stop the fire from spreading downwards,” Fire Chief Mads Graversen tells TV2.
