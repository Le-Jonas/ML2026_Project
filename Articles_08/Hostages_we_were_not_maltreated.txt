Title: Hostages_we_were_not_maltreated
Date: 2011-09-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish family that was held by Somali pirates for over six months was not maltreated according to the mother of the family.
“None of us were beaten and there was no maltreatment,” says Birgit Quist Johansen on her Facebook profile according to Sjællandske.
