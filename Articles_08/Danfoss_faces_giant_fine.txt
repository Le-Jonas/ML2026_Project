Title: Danfoss_faces_giant_fine
Date: 2011-12-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The European Commission is expected to hand a giant fine to the Danfoss company today, according to widespread media reports.
According to TV2 News, a fine of between DKK 600 million and DKK 700 million is to be levied on Danfoss for having taken part in a global cartel on the compressor market.
