Title: Mærsk_rejects_new_oil_discussions
Date: 2012-02-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The centre-left government has recently put forward proposals for a service check on the North Sea oil agreement with suggestions that it could be re-negotiated, but the A.P. Møller-Mærsk group has made it clear that it is not inclined to re-negotiate.
“We see no basis for a re-negotiation of the North Sea agreement,” A.P. Møller-Mærsk Public Affairs Executive Anders Würtzen tells Jyllands-Posten.
