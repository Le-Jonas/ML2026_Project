Title: DK_bishops_aghast
Date: 2012-05-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

“Perverse”, “Fundamentalist” are just some of the words that Danish bishops have uttered after theologists have suggested that Anders Behring Breivik’s massacre of 77 people in Utøya was an act of God.
According to several theologists interviewed by Kristeligt Dagblad, God had a meaning with the Norwegian tragedy.
