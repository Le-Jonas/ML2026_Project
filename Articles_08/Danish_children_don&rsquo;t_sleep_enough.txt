Title: Danish_children_don&rsquo;t_sleep_enough
Date: 2012-03-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danish teenagers are not the only ones who get too little sleep and are tired when they arrive at school – pre-school teachers report that small children have the same problem, According to Jyllands-Posten.
A YouGov questionnaire for Momentum among pre-school teachers shows that as few as half of the children who arrive in the mornings have had enough sleep. Even 40 per cent of parents say their children have not had enough rest every day.
