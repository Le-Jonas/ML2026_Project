Title: DK_out_of_recession
Date: 2012-05-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark dragged itself out of recession in the First Quarter, although the Danish economy remains somewhat sluggish, according to Statistics Denmark which has calculated GDP growth in the quarter at 0.3 per cent.
The increase compares with a drop of 0.1 per cent and 0.2 per cent in the final quarters of 2011.
