Title: School_assembly_surprised_by_sex_video
Date: 2012-05-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Teachers and pupils at a high school near Copenhagen were taken by surprise during morning assembly a week ago when a video show suddenly turned into a sex show between a young male student and a young woman.
Some 400-500 teachers and students were gathered at an outgoing ceremony for 3rd year students at Gammel Hellerup Gymnasium to see some light-hearted video footage of school-leavers.
