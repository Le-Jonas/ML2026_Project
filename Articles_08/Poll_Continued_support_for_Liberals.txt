Title: Poll_Continued_support_for_Liberals
Date: 2011-12-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 gives the Liberal Party 32.9 per cent of the vote, affording the former senior government coalition party 18 seats more than the Social Democrats, and the centre-right parties a majority.
Were an election held today, the poll suggests that the centre-right opposition would garner 91 seats in the 179-seat Folketing, with the governing centre-left and its Red Green support party taking 84 seats.
