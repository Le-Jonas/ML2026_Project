Title: Wise_Man_Crisis_until_at_least_2014
Date: 2011-12-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prospects for the Danish economy have got worse since the government’s Economic Council presented its pessimistic report on the economic state of the nation two months ago, according to the Economic Council Chairman Jørgen Whitta-Jacobsen.
The report forecast that growth will remain at around one per cent of GDP until 2013.
