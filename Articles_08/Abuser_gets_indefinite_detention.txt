Title: Abuser_gets_indefinite_detention
Date: 2012-03-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Aalborg Municipal Court has sentenced a 56-year-old man to indefinite detention for violence, rape and other sexual offences against nine children, seven of whom were his own children.
At the same time the man was sentenced to pay a total of DKK915,000 in damages to those concerned and is barred from ever having children under 18 in his house.
