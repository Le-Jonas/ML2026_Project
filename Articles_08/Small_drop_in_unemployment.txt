Title: Small_drop_in_unemployment
Date: 2012-06-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s unemployment figures for April show 100 fewer unemployed and a relatively stable seasonally adjusted unemployment figure of 161,700 or 6.2 per cent of the labour force, according to the latest monthly figures from Statistics Denmark.
Unemployment in Denmark has been between 6.1 per cent and 6.2 per cent since February 2011.
