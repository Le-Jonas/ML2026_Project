Title: Thorning_calls_for_broad_cooperation
Date: 2011-10-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt, who held her and Denmark’s first woman prime minister’s maiden speech as premier today, said her administration heralded new times for government in Parliament and across the country.
“I will reintroduce broad cooperation. We are all of Denmark’s government,” Thorning-Schmidt told a packed chamber.
