Title: Opposition_calls_for_Sohn-USSR_probe
Date: 2011-11-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party and the Liberals are calling for an investigation into allegations that the Danish Communist Party (DKP) received illicit funding from the Soviet Union during the period that Trade and Growth Minister Ole Sohn (SocPpl) was chairman of the party.
The demands come following allegations in Jyllands-Posten by the KGB’s last station chief in Copenhagen at the weekend that the Soviet secret service provided surreptitious cash funding for the DKP. Nikolai Shatskikh alleged that the KGB continued to provide cash to the DKP up until the demise of the Soviet Union in 1991.
