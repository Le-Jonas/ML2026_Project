Title: Søvndal_sees_Afghan_setback
Date: 2012-03-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

As one of the first leaders of the Afghan alliance, Foreign Minister Villy Søvndal says that some of the results that have been achieved in Afghanistan may be lost.
When Danish soldiers leave the country after 13 years of war in 2014, the Afghans will not be able to manage many of the tasks at hand.
