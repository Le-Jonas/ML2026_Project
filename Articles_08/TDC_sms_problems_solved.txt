Title: TDC_sms_problems_solved
Date: 2012-02-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Many of TDC’s two million mobile subscribers have been having problems transmitting sms and mms messages since yesterday evening, but the problem has now been solved.
“Everything is working properly again and we apologise for the inconvenience,” says TDC Head of Press Lasse Bastkjær Jensen.
