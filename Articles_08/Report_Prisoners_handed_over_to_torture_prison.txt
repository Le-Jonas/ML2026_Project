Title: Report_Prisoners_handed_over_to_torture_prison
Date: 2012-03-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Afghanistan Independent Human Rights Commission (AIHRC) says that it has found credible evidence that the prison to which Denmark hands over Afghan prisoners practices torture.
“Researchers found credible evidence of torture at nine separate NDS facilities,” the AIHRC says in its report, listing NDS (National Directorate of Security) LashkarGah as one of the nine.
