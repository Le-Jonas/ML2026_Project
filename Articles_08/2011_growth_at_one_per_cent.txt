Title: 2011_growth_at_one_per_cent
Date: 2012-02-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest GDP figures from Statistics Denmark show fourth quarter growth at 0.2 per cent, resulting in a seasonally adjusted one per cent growth for 2011 as a whole.
Private consumption increased while public consumption and investment dropped, Statistics Denmark says adding that while domestic demand dropped by 0.2 per cent overall, net exports increased.
