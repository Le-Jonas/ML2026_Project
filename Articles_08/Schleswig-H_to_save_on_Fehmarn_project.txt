Title: Schleswig-H_to_save_on_Fehmarn_project
Date: 2012-06-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The new state government coalition in Schleswig-Holstein, which includes the Danish minority SSW party, is to axe some DKK440million in investments on the German side of what is to be a link across the Fehmarn belt between Denmark and Germany.
At the same time, the SDP, Green, SSW coalition says it plans to keep to the Danish-German agreement on a Fehmarn link, the upcoming SSW Culture, Europe and Justice Minister Anke Spoorendonk tells nordschleswiger.dk.
