Title: Murder_suspects_apprehended
Date: 2012-01-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police are to present two Bulgarian men in their early 20s in a remand hearing today charged with robbery and the murder of an 81-year-old Danish woman on New Year’s Eve. Both men deny the charges.
The two men were detained Tuesday along with their 14-year-old and 19-year-old Bulgarian girlfriends, both of whom were released. All four are in Denmark legally, with the men having been in Denmark for some 18 months as cleaners.
