Title: Denmark_to_monitor_pirates_by_plane
Date: 2011-10-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark is upgrading its anti-piracy capability off Somalia from January 2012 by sending a surveillance aircraft to the area, according to Ritzau.
According to the report, the Parliamentary Foreign Policy Committee Friday decided to deploy the aircraft, initially for a period of two months, as part of the international coalition currently patrolling the area.
