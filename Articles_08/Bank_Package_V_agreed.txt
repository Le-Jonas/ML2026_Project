Title: Bank_Package_V_agreed
Date: 2012-03-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Trade and Growth Minister Ole Sohn has successfully negotiated a Development Package with all Parliamentary parties apart from the Red Greens.
The agreement, which is popularly being labelled Bank Package V, is designed to help farmers while at the same time restructuring the FIH Erhvervsbank in order that the bank can continue to service small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
