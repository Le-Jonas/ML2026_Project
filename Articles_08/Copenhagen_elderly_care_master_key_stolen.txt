Title: Copenhagen_elderly_care_master_key_stolen
Date: 2012-03-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Elderly Care Service master key to secure emergency help boxes, which contain front-door keys for elderly who receive council help in Copenhagen, has been stolen.
The thief has potential access to homes in the capital, and according to TV2 several front-door keys have already been taken from six emergency help boxes overnight.
