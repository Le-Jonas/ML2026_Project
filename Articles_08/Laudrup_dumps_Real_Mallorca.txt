Title: Laudrup_dumps_Real_Mallorca
Date: 2011-09-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s soccer icon Michael Laudrup has reached agreement with management to end his Head Coach contract with the Balearic team RCD Mallorca, following deep divisions with the club’s Director of Football Lorenzo Serra Ferrer.
“It was what had to be done. And the timing was right. Now Mallorca is in the middle of Primera División and that means that people can focus on the team and not the disagreements,” Laudrup says.
