Title: MPs_Tighten_boss_bonus_rules!
Date: 2012-03-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Political parties have expressed surprise at an Aarhus University survey showing that senior executive public servants are receiving heavy bonuses without actually having achieved the goals set for their bonuses.
According to the survey of senior executives on state boards, bonuses are at almost DKK100.000 on average.
