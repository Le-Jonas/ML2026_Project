Title: Refugee_centre_arrest
Date: 2012-02-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A young Syrian man has been arrested at a refugee centre in Langeland after he went on a window-smashing spree and set fire to fittings in his building, according to Funen police.
Police were called to the centre this morning after a caller reported a man had ‘run amok’. Nine people were evacuated as a result of the incident, in which no-one was hurt.
