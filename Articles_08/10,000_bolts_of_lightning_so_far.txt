Title: 10,000_bolts_of_lightning_so_far
Date: 2012-06-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Southern and western regions of Denmark awoke this morning to thunder, lightning and tropical downpours.
From midnight and up to 8 a.m. this morning, 10,125 bolts of lightning were registered, predominantly in Jutland and on Funen. Some of the bolts caused fires.
