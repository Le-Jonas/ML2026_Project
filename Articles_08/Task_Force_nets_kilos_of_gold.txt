Title: Task_Force_nets_kilos_of_gold
Date: 2012-05-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish police Task Force Break-in has carried out lucrative searches of some 15 addresses on the capital island Zealand, with German police also searching three addresses in Berlin, and reports finding ‘Several kilos of gold and silver’.
Fourteen people have been arrested in connection with the operation, most of whom are also expected to be charged with a long list of break-ins in Zealand.
