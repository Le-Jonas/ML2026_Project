Title: Child_arrest_furore
Date: 2011-11-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The children’s rights organisation Børns Vilkår has reacted strongly to disclosures that a 7-year-old boy was arrested at 10.30 p.m. at home and taken for two hours of questioning at a police station without his parents being allowed to be present.
The young boy is one of 10 youths between the ages of 7 and 17 suspected of being responsible for a series of fires in the Esbjerg region.
