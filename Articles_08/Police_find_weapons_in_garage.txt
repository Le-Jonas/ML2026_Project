Title: Police_find_weapons_in_garage
Date: 2012-07-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Following a tip-off, Police Wednesday found a small arsenal of illegal weapons in a garage in Silkeborg.
The 48-year-old tenant of the garage, in which pistols, rifles, automatic weapons, ammunition and explosives were found, has been arrested and charged with the illegal possession of firearms.
