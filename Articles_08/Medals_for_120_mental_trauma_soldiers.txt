Title: Medals_for_120_mental_trauma_soldiers
Date: 2011-12-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Veterans of Danish operations who have been diagnosed with mental traumas as a result of their service are now to receive medals in the same way as comrades who have been physically impaired.
The defence forces have decided to decorate 120 mental trauma veterans who have been wounded as a result of their service, with a silver medal.
