Title: Man_attacks_two_at_job_centre
Date: 2012-03-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 42-year-old man has been arrested in Elsinore after attacking two public servants with an axe, according to North Zealand Police Media Coordinator Henrik Suhr.
The two men, who worked at a local council-run job centre, were taken to hospital and are said to be in a stable condition.
