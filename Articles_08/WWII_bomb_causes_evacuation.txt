Title: WWII_bomb_causes_evacuation
Date: 2012-05-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

South and Southern Jutland police are evacuating an area of summerhouses near Lakolk on the island of Romø after what seems to be a World War II bomb has come to light in connection with excavation works.
Everyone at Lakolk between Vesterhavsvej and Gl. Skinnevej, and the beach between the two roads is being told to leave the area.
