Title: Soldier_charged_in_Afghan_killing_episode
Date: 2011-11-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Judge Advocate has charged a Danish soldier for contravening rules on the use of force in connection with the death of a civilian Afghan, according to the Army Operational Command (HOK).
The episode in question took place on October 23 as a group of Danish soldiers from Patrol Base Bridzar in Helmand saw four people who they believed were in the process of placing a roadside bomb.
