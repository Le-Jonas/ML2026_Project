Title: 16_Copenhagen_churches_to_close
Date: 2012-04-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Copenhagen Diocesan Council has decided to close 16 churches in the capital region with a 17th church hanging in the balance, according to Council Chairwoman Inge Lise Pedersen.
“The 16 churches are certain (Ed: closures) but we still have some questions regarding the 17th,” Pedersen tells politiken.dk.
