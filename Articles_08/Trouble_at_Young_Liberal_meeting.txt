Title: Trouble_at_Young_Liberal_meeting
Date: 2012-03-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One person was arrested, a young girl got a head injury from a police baton and a police officer had to be taken to hospital following unrest yesterday evening outside the offices of the Young Liberals in Copenhagen where a debate with a neo-Nazi had been underway.
Some 100 demonstrators arrived outside the Young Liberal centre early yesterday evening to demonstrate against a debate due to take place between the Liberal Youth Organisation and a leading Nazi sympathiser, Daniel Carlsen.
