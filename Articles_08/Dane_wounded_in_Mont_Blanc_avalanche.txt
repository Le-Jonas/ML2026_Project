Title: Dane_wounded_in_Mont_Blanc_avalanche
Date: 2012-07-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

At least nine climbers have been killed and several people, including a Dane, have been injured in an avalanche near Mont Blanc in the French Alps.
Two Danes were in a larger group of climbers on the 4,465m Mont Maudit near Chamonix. Mont Maudit, which is a well-travelled route onto Mont Blanc, means ‘the cursed mountain’ in French.
