Title: WEF_Crown_Princess_and_Goulani_chosen
Date: 2012-03-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The World Economic Forum has announced two Danes among its choices for the Young Global Leaders Class of 2012 to join a forum of under-40’s who provide youthful input to discussions on the future of the globe.
Crown Princess Mary has been chosen for her work with the Mary Foundation. The Mary Foundation seeks to improve the lives of children, adults and families who, as a result of their environment, heredity, illness or other circumstances, find themselves socially isolated or excluded from society.
