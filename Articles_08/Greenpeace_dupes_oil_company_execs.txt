Title: Greenpeace_dupes_oil_company_execs
Date: 2011-12-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Oil company executives from among others Shell, BP and Statoil thought they were going to an exploration seminar held by Greenland’s Raw Materials Directorate today – instead they were duped into attending a Greenpeace lecture against exploration.
Some 18 executives, who were hoping to receive information on oil exploration in Greenland were tricked into another location to hear a lecture on why they should not bid for exploration licences in and around Greenland.
