Title: Nationalbanken_moves_to_help_banks
Date: 2011-09-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish central bank Nationalbanken is increasing Danish private banks’ access to liquidity by expanding the current guarantee system by up to DKK 400 billion, the central bank says in a news release.
“Danmarks Nationalbank widens the banks' access to collateralised credit facilities at Danmarks Nationalbank by expanding the existing collateral basis – primarily government and mortgage bonds – to include the banks' credit claims of good quality,” the bank says.
