Title: Disturbed_man_arrested_for_Prime_Min._threat
Date: 2012-05-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen Police have today arrested a 39-year-old man described as ‘mentally disturbed’ for issuing threats against Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt.
The man, who Copenhagen Police say has previously threatened to cause bomb explosions around Copenhagen, is to be presented in remand court today.
