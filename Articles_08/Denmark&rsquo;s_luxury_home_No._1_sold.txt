Title: Denmark&rsquo;s_luxury_home_No._1_sold
Date: 2011-11-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The house that is said to be one of Denmark’s most expensive luxury homes is now said to have been sold, according to the Home estate agents in a statement..
The villa, at Hambros Allé 32 in Hellerup, has previously been owned by Erik Damsgard and more recently by the former boxer Hans Henrik Palm, and was offered for sale at DKK 60 million last September.
