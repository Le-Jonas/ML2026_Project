Title: EDITORIAL_Now_peace_must_be_won_in_Libya
Date: 2011-08-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

It is almost too good to be true: an oppressed nation in one of Europe’s neighbouring countries rises in protest against decades of misgovernment.
After a bloody battle, in which NATO with its United Nations mandate has supported the rebellion from the air, the rebels have broken through and seized the capital, without the massacre that was feared.
