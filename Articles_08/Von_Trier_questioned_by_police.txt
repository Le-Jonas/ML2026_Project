Title: Von_Trier_questioned_by_police
Date: 2011-10-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Lars von Trier’s controversial Nazi statements at the Cannes Film Festival earlier this year continue to dog the Danish film director.
Von Trier released a press release today in which he said that he has been questioned by the North Zealand Police in connection with preliminary charges raised against him by the Grasse prosecutor.
