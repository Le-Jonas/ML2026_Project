Title: Court_releases_terrorist_suspects
Date: 2012-05-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Eastern High Court decided today that two men from the extreme left who have been charged under anti-terrorism laws should be released, the prosecution has confirmed.
The two are from a group of five who have been charged under Danish anti-terrorism laws with arson and conspiracy to commit arson in the Copenhagen area and have been in prison since they were arrested a year ago.
