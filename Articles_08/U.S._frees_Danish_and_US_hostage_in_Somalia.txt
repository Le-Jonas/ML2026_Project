Title: U.S._frees_Danish_and_US_hostage_in_Somalia
Date: 2012-01-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A small American force in two helicopters has freed Poul Hagen Thisted and Jessica Buchanan of the Danish Demining Group who have been held by Somali kidnappers for several months.
According to Somaliareport.com, unconfirmed reports say that eight pirates were killed in the action, which was launched from Galkayo airport.
