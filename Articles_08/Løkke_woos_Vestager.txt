Title: Løkke_woos_Vestager
Date: 2011-09-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

As election hour rapidly approaches, Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen has put out heavy feelers to the Social Liberal Leade Margrethe Vestager to form an alliance with a Liberal-led government if it wins tomorrow’s election.
“It is important that the reform alliance with the Social Liberals, that came out of the crisis, stays in place after the election. That is something Denmark needs,” Løkke Rasmussen says.
