Title: New_DSB_boss_quits
Date: 2011-10-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Director of DSB Christian Roslev, who was appointed on July 1 to steer the corporation through its crisis, is leaving the ailing organisation with immediate effect ‘for health reasons’.
“This has not been an easy decision for me as I have appreciated cooperation with DSB’s Board, management and employees,” Roslev says in a written release, adding he would not be making any further comment.
