Title: Tycho_Brahe_result_postponed
Date: 2011-11-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The results of a Danish-Czech-Swedish examination of fragments of the earthly remains of the 16th century Danish astronomer Tycho Brahe has had to be postponed until 2012 due to a lack of funding, according to Prof. Jens Vellev who is leading the project.
Tycho Brahe’s remains were exhumed from the Church of Our Lady in front of Týn in Prague last November, allowing specimens of bone, teeth and hair to be removed before the coffin was reinterred in the church.
