Title: Multiple_shooting_in_Greenland
Date: 2012-02-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police in Greenland are making their way by helicopter to the small hamlet of Nutaarmiut in west central Greenland after reports that three people have been killed and a further two are seriously injured following a shooting incident.
“We have been informed by a citizen that three people have been shot and killed in Nutaarmiut and two people are said to be injured,” says Claus Risbjerg of the Greenland Central Police Authority.
