Title: Amager_man_tried_to_smuggle_dna
Date: 2011-12-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Man from Amager, who has been found guilty on six counts of murder and rape, is being charged with a further crime in having tried to smuggle dna out of prison and have it planted in order to prove his innocence.
MLH, or The Man from Amager as he is also known, was found guilty on Monday of two murders and six rapes and attempted rapes over the past 23 years, with the final rape in 2010 providing dna evidence leading to his arrest.
