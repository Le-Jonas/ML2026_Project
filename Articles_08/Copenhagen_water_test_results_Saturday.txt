Title: Copenhagen_water_test_results_Saturday
Date: 2011-08-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Technicians expect to have results of tests of drinking water from large parts of Copenhagen later Saturday morning after a general warning has been issued to large parts of the capital to boil tap water before drinking it.
“We expect to have the results of the tests around noon,” Copenhagen Energy Press Spokesman Thorkild Thomsen tells Politiken.dk.
