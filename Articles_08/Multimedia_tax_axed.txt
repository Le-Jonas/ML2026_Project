Title: Multimedia_tax_axed
Date: 2011-11-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government and Red Greens have finally reached agreement on how to axe a controversial multimedia tax, which is levied on employees who have mobile telephones or broadband placed at their disposal by their employers.
The overall tax and duty elements of the 2012 Budget, including the issue of the multimedia tax, are expected to be made public this afternoon.
