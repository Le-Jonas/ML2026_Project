Title: Kenya_to_take_4_Absalon_pirates
Date: 2012-02-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Kenyan authorities have agreed to prosecute four of the remaining Somali pirates on board the Danish warship Absalon, which rescued 14 Pakistanis and Iranians who had been held hostage by pirates since November.
Seventeen other pirates who were being held on the Absalon have been released.
