Title: Police_nab_1,000_motorists
Date: 2011-10-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In the space of just four hours on Monday, police stopped over 1,000 motorists on the Køge motorway for ignoring speed reduction signs at roadworks.
“It is alarming that so many ignore speed restrictions where people are working,” says Traffic Police Chief Thomas Jørgensen.
