Title: EDITORIAL_Recognition
Date: 2011-09-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In the midst of Denmark’s domestic political drama surrounding government negotiations, a major international drama will reach its preliminary climax this week in connection with the Palestinian desire for recognition and United Nations membership.
The Palestinian initiative has been provoked by a situation in which Israel has supported negotiations towards a two-state solution for many years now, but in reality has not wanted progress towards that goal.
