Title: Man_admits_Greenland_massacre
Date: 2012-02-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 22-year-old man has admitted killing three and attempting to kill another four people in a small Greenland settlement, in a killing spree that has shocked the Arctic nation.
The man, who was arrested by police flown in to the west central Greenland hamlet of Nutaarmiut has been remanded for four weeks in a closed-door hearing in nearby Upernavik.
