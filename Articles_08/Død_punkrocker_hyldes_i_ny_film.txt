Title: Død_punkrocker_hyldes_i_ny_film

»What I do is not about being comfortable with the world. I was tossed into this place, and I will be tossed out again. All this stuff in between is a big fucking fight«, har Jay Reatard engang udtalt.
For cirka to et halvt år siden døde den kun 29-årige Jay Reatard, med det borgerlige navn Jimmy Lee Lindsey, af en overdosis.
