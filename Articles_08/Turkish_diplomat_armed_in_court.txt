Title: Turkish_diplomat_armed_in_court
Date: 2011-09-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Turkish Embassy in Copenhagen has been reprimanded after it has been disclosed that one its diplomats was found to be armed as he followed court proceedings against the Copenhagen-based Kurdish broadcaster ROJ TV.
ROJ-TV is currently on trial in Denmark on charges of supporting the Kurdish separatist organisation PKK, which is listed as a terrorist organisation by the European Union and the United States.
