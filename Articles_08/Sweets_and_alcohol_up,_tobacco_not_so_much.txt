Title: Sweets_and_alcohol_up,_tobacco_not_so_much
Date: 2011-11-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The new centre-left government’s budget is due out today and will include considerable price hikes on wine, beer and sweets, but a lower than expected increase in the price of tobacco products.
“Healthy choices should be made easy and unhealthy choices more expensive. This is what is needed if Danes are to live longer,” says Health Minister Astrid Krag (SocPpl).
