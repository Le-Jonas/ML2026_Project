Title: Evacuees_return_home
Date: 2011-10-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Evacuees were allowed to return to their homes overnight in Brørup in Jutland after having been evacuated while sappers emptied a house of some 150 boxes of fireworks apparently connected with a major explosion in Store Andst on Saturday.
Saturday’s explosion in a lock-up in Store Andst cost the lives of two people, one of whom owned the house in Brørup. The identity of the second person remains uncertain.
