Title: Online_company_tax_figures
Date: 2012-07-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Tax Minister Thor Möger Pedersen (SocPpl) has introduced a new set of rules which will enable the public to see how much tax companies pay – or don’t pay.
The new set of rules will enable the Tax Authority (SKAT) to publish hitherto confidential details about company and multinationals’ tax affairs on a special website in what the minister says will shave a ‘positive effect on tax payments,” according to Jyllands-Posten.
