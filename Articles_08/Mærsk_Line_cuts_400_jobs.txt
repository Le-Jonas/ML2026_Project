Title: Mærsk_Line_cuts_400_jobs
Date: 2012-06-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The A.P. Møller-Mærsk-owned Mærsk Line container shipping company is to axe 400 jobs, 250 of which are to be found in Copenhagen in a restructure of the company’s headquarter operations, the company says in an announcement.
“It is expected that a consequence of the new organisation is a reduction of the number of positions in Centre by approximately 400, of which 250 in Copenhagen,” the company says on its website.
