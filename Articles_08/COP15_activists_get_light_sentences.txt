Title: COP15_activists_get_light_sentences
Date: 2011-08-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen Municipal Court handed down 14-day suspended sentences to 11 Greenpeace activists for planning and carrying out a happening at the royal gala dinner during the United Nations COP15 Climate Summit in 2009.
The court did not follow the prosecution demand for up to a 70-day suspended sentence and banishment from Denmark for 6 years for foreign members of the group.
