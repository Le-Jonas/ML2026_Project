Title: Jelved_asked_to_zip_it
Date: 2011-09-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Tripartite negotiations on a new government are complicated and the Social Liberals and Socialist People’s Party have sent out a whip telling party faithful to stop making public statements about policy.
For the Social Liberals, former leader Marianne Jelved in particular has continued to answer media approaches and provide information on the party’s position on central aspects of party policy, but has now been asked to stop the flow.
