Title: Mosque_arson_investigation
Date: 2011-11-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen police are investigating the possible motives behind a case of arson at the Islamic Culture Centre in Brønshøj early this morning.
According to Copenhagen Police Fire Squad Spokesman Per Hermansen a window was smashed at about 4 a.m. this morning at the Culture Centre on Horsebakken, which houses one of Denmark’s oldest mosques.
