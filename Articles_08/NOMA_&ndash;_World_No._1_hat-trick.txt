Title: NOMA_&ndash;_World_No._1_hat-trick
Date: 2012-05-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

As only the second restaurant ever, the Copenhagen restaurant Noma has topped the list of Restaurant Magazine’s prestigious ‘The World’s 50 best restaurants’ list for the third year in a row at a ceremony at Guildhall in London.
The only other restaurant to have matched René Redzepi’s hat-trick for Noma is the legendary El Bulli restaurant in Spain of Ferran Adrià, which closed in July 2011.
