Title: Unusual_guest_to_govt._negotiations
Date: 2011-09-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Negotiations on forming the next government have been held secretly and with very little coming into the public domain since Social Democratic Leader Helle Thorning Schmidt embarked on efforts to form a tripartite government following last week’s general elections.
But negotiations appear to be moving forward after TV2 News could report that Thorning-Schmidt arrived at the talks today flanked by the Permanent Secretary at the Prime Minister’s Office Christian Kettel.
