Title: SydBank_ATMs_go_euro
Date: 2012-07-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

SydBank is to be the first major Danish bank to offer a choice of kroner or euro in its ATMs, making it possible to draw euro outside normal banking hours, according to Frederiksborg Amts Avis.
“There is a single regional bank that has done this, but we are the first major bank to do so,” says SydBank Hillerød Branch Manager Henrik Følbæk Nielsen.
