Title: EU_and_Japan_to_develop_FTA
Date: 2012-02-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Trade and Investment Minister Pia Olsen Dyhr has reached agreement with her Japanes counterpart to develop a free trade agreement between the European Union and Japan.
“We have agreed that the foundations should be concluded during the Danish EU Presidency and that we will both turn on the turbo-engine to make sure we can reach that goal,” Olsen Dyhr tells Ritzau.
