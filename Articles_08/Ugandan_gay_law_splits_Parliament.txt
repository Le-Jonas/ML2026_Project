Title: Ugandan_gay_law_splits_Parliament
Date: 2012-03-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

There is increasing criticism of the Development Aid Minister Christian Friis Bach (SocLib) who is not inclined to follow the policy of his predecessors and threaten to cut aid to Uganda if it passes a law an anti-homosexuality law which may include the death penalty.
Unlike his predecessor and the British Prime Minister David Cameron, Friis Bach has declined to warn Uganda that Denmark’s DKK300 million development aid to the country is in danger if Uganda passes the law.
