Title: Thorning_Major_step_forward
Date: 2011-10-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Final decisions on the Eurozone’s way out of the current crisis have been postponed until Wednesday when European heads of state and government will be meeting for another set of summits to decide on the European Financial Stability Facility (EFSF) and bank recapitalisation.
“We have taken a major step forward, but a solution will not come before Wednesday,” said Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt following Sunday’s summits.
