Title: Aarhus_youths_feared_drowned_in_Uganda
Date: 2011-12-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two young men from Åbyhøj near Århus are missing and feared to have drowned while swimming in Lake Bunyonyi in south west Uganda.
“We were approached by the Foreign Ministry telling us that two young men were missing in Uganda and were asked to inform their parents,” says Insp. Mogens Brøndum of the East Jutland Police.
