Title: OBIT_Mærsk_McKinney-Møller,_98
Date: 2012-04-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller, Denmark’s shipping magnate and one of the country’s most influential captains of industry has died at the age of 98, the A.P. Møller-Mærsk Group has announced.
“We are sorry to have to inform you that partner and shipowner Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller has passed away on 16 April 2012,” the company says on its website.
