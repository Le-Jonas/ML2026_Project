Title: Jaw-like_fragments_in_peanuts
Date: 2012-03-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Veterinary and Food Administration has advised that the SuperGros A/S wholesaler has protectively withdrawn a large consignment of peanuts after a jaw-like object from what is believed to be a small animal has been found in a package.
