Title: WARNING_Counterfeit_notes_circulating
Date: 2012-02-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

South Zealand and Lolland-Falster police have issued a warning that counterfeit DKK1,000 notes are in circulation following 11 reports from outlets this week.
So far reports of false notes have come from Slagelse, Næstved and Kalundborg. According to the police several of the notes appear to have the serial number 240248 F.
