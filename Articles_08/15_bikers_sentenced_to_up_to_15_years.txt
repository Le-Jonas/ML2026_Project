Title: 15_bikers_sentenced_to_up_to_15_years
Date: 2011-09-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A prominent member of the Hells Angels and 14 of his companions were sentenced today in what is being called Denmark’s biggest ever case against bikers.
Brian Sandberg was sentenced to 11 years and 10 months for attempted murder; five others were sentenced to over 10 years and the others in the group less than 10 years. The longest sentences in the group were two sentences of 15 years.
