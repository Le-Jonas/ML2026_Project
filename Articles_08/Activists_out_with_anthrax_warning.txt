Title: Activists_out_with_anthrax_warning
Date: 2012-07-09
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A drug addict died at the national hospital Rigshospitalet on Sunday from an anthrax infection, prompting a voluntary organisation to take to the streets to warn intravenous drug addicts that tainted heroine may be on the streets.
Nanna W. Gotfredson, a lawyer and one of the founders of the Street Lawyer (Gadejuristen.dk) organisation said the organisation would be setting up posters to warn addicts.
