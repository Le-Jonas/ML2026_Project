Title: NatBank_governor_warns_against_growth_packages
Date: 2011-09-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Governor of the Danish central bank Nationalbanken Nils Bernstein has warned against using fiscal policy to adjust the economy in the current economic situation in Denmark.
“The economy is slowly moving back onto a normal track, so there is no reason to increase economic activity through fiscal policy,” Bernstein says in a comment on the Nationalbanken’s Third Quarter Report.
