Title: Weapons_found_outside_court
Date: 2012-02-24
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Eastern High Court in Copenhagen is under extra security in connection with a gang-related hearing, with police having found three knives and an axe under cars nearby.
“We have found three knives and an axe under cars in the area,” says Copenhagen Police Duty Officer Henrik Olesen. No-one has been arrested, but several in a group who had arrived to enter the court were wearing bullet-proof vests.
