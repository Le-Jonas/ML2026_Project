Title: Fears_of_oil_pollution_near_Holbæk
Date: 2012-07-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The authorities are trying to determine the extent of an apparent oil leak at Knabstrup near Holbæk.
Preliminary surveys suggest a leak in an oil pipeline from a refinery in Kalundborg which has polluted surrounding earth, a stream and marshland off Knabstrup Møllebakke.
