Title: EDITORIAL_France_&ndash;_hope_for_Europe
Date: 2012-05-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In the midst of an economic crisis that affects us all, France has chosen a new president.
François Hollande is to be part of finding the possible solution to a crisis that extends far beyond French borders. As a result there is every reason for a double set of congratulations, given the result of yesterday’s presidential election.
