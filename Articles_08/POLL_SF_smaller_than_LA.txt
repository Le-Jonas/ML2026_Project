Title: POLL_SF_smaller_than_LA
Date: 2012-04-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Socialist People’s Party (SF) national congress opens on Friday with turmoil in the ranks and poor opinion polls.
The latest poll by YouGov for metroXpress gives the party only 5.5 per cent of the electorate if an election were held now, compared to 5.7 per cent for the Liberal Alliance.
