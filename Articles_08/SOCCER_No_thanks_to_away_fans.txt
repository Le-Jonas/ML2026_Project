Title: SOCCER_No_thanks_to_away_fans
Date: 2012-03-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

FC Copenhagen no longer wants its fans to come to away matches as trouble in the stands, of the type experienced last weekend, can result in lost points.
“We would prefer no-one in the stands when we play away matches. That gives us the best chance to focus on the results we have influence over - on the pitch,” FCK Director Anders Hørsholt tells Ekstra Bladet.
