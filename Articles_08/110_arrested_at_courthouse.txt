Title: 110_arrested_at_courthouse
Date: 2012-03-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police arrested some 110 people at the Glostrup courthouse on Tuesday afternoon who were apparently attempting to enter the courthouse to attend a case.
“They arrived together,” said Insp. Michael Kristiansen. Some of those arrested had been wearing masks and were asked to remove them. “Some began throwing things at officers,” Kristiansen says.
