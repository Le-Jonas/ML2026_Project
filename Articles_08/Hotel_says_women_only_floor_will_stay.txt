Title: Hotel_says_women_only_floor_will_stay
Date: 2011-11-22
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Equal Treatment Board may have said it’s against the law, but the Bella Sky Hotel in Copenhagen has no plans to close its women only Bella Donna 17th floor, which is strictly reserved, furnished and designed for women.
“I thought it was funny when the case was sent to the Council six months ago and it gave us fantastic marketing around the world,” Hotel Director Arne Bang Mikkelsen tells DR News.
