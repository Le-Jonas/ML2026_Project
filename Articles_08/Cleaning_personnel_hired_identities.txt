Title: Cleaning_personnel_hired_identities
Date: 2012-03-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A further three people have been remanded in custody by the Copenhagen Remand Court today on charges of using false identities, following controls of cleaning personnel at Copenhagen University.
Nine of 33 employees of the Forenede Service A/S cleaning service company were taken for questioning after a raid by the Copenhagen Police Foreigners Control Unit at Copenhagen University on Monday.
