Title: Tax_Ministry_reports_spin_doctor
Date: 2011-12-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Tax Ministry has asked Copenhagen police to initiate an investigation into former Tax Minister Troels Lund Poulsen’s (Lib) special adviser Peter Arnfeldt on allegations of having attempted to disseminate confidential private papers regarding Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt’s family tax affairs, the ministry tells Politiken.dk.
According to a letter sent to Copenhagen Police yesterday, Peter Arnfeldt “appears to have attempted to disseminate confidential information” and asks the police to “take the necessary steps,” says Tax Ministry Press Spokesman Finn Serup.
