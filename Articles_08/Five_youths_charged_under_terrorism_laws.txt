Title: Five_youths_charged_under_terrorism_laws
Date: 2011-08-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Copenhagen Police have extended the indictment against five young men already on remand on arson charges to include terrorism.
Four of the five, who are between 20 and 23 years of age, have been on remand since April when they were arrested in connection with arson at the Copenhagen Police Academy in the suburb of Brøndbyøster. The fifth man was arrested at a later stage.
