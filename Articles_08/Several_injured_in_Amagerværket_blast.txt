Title: Several_injured_in_Amagerværket_blast
Date: 2012-05-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

An attempt to blast a silo that was being removed from the Amagerværket power station has gone wrong and at least three people who were watching have been injured, according to Copenhagen Police.
At least one of those injured has suffered serious burns and has been taken to the Rigshospitalet Trauma Centre.
