Title: German_Fehmarn_uncertainty
Date: 2011-12-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The German government has announced that it is postponing the development of a railway link in connection with the new Fehmarn Tunnel, according to Der Nordschlewiger.
According to the report, Traffic Minister Peter Ramsauer has decided to reduce planned government investment in new infrastructure in Germany by 25 per cent due to the economic crisis.
