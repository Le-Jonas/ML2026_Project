Title: EDITORIAL_Prisoner_exchange
Date: 2011-10-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

For years, Israel has relentlessly maintained two principles: It will not negotiate with terrorists, and it will do whatever necessary to get captured soldiers and citizens back home.
This latter principle is one that Israel has kept to – but as a result it has been unable to sustain the former: It can be necessary to bargain with terrorists.
