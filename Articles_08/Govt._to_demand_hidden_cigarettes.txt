Title: Govt._to_demand_hidden_cigarettes
Date: 2011-12-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A majority in Parliament is looking to force outlets to hide tobacco products from public view as a way of getting people to stop smoking.
“If cigarettes are in full view they appear as an advertisement. They should be hidden away, for example behind a curtain, so consumers are not tempted,” Social Liberal Health Spokeswoman Camilla Hersom tells Berlingske.
