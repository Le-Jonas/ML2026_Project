Title: Danske_Bank_chooses_new_CEO
Date: 2011-12-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Danske Bank’s Board Chairman Eivind Kolding, 52, has been chosen as the bank’s new CEO to take over from Peter Straarup.
Kolding, who takes over his new job on February 15, comes from a lengthy career with A.P. Møller-Mærsk. He is currently CEO of Maersk Line, which is the world’s largest container line.
