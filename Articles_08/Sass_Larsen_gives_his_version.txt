Title: Sass_Larsen_gives_his_version
Date: 2011-10-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Henrik Sass Larsen, who withdrew from cabinet-making after the recent general election, has given his version of the events that led to the Security and Intelligence Agency PET’s decision to deny him security clearance.
In an interview with TV2News, he says that some sms messages suggested that he had in some way been dependent on a senior Bandidos biker and was under his protection.
