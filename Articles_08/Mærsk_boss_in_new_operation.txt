Title: Mærsk_boss_in_new_operation
Date: 2012-03-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Chief Executive Officer of the A.P. Møller-Mærsk group Nils Smedegaard Andersen is extending his leave of absence for a further six to eight weeks following his heart surgery in December.
Smedegaard Andersen, who was to have returned to work today, is to undergo corrective heart surgery on Wednesday at Rigshospitalet, the A.P.Møller-Mærsk group has announced.
