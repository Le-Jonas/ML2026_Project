Title: COMMISSION_Vote_for_16-year-olds
Date: 2011-12-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A majority in the Suffrage Commission, which publishes its report on the voting age today, is recommending that 16-year-olds should be given the right to vote.
“Society can only be interested in involving young people in the democratic processes and the development of society. The earlier the better,” says Social Democratic Group Chairman Mogens Jensen.
