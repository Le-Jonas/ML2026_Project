Title: Vicars&rsquo;_revolt_against_closures
Date: 2012-04-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Seven out of 17 churches in the Copenhagen area that have been told they are to be closed are refusing to accept the recommendations, according to a Kristeligt Dagblad survey.
The churches involved are Enghave Kirke, Gethsemane Kirke, Sankt Lukas Kirke, Absalon Kirke, Davids Kirke, Sankt Pauls Kirke and Aalholm Kirke.
