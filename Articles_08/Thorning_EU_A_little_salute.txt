Title: Thorning_EU_A_little_salute
Date: 2012-06-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

In the wake of agreements at the EU summit in Brussels overnight, Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt says that Denmark and the rest of Europe will benefit from the decisions.
“We have taken a step in the direction of managing the euro. That is very good for Europe and Denmark,” the prime minister says in Brussels.
