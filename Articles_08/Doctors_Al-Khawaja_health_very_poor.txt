Title: Doctors_Al-Khawaja_health_very_poor
Date: 2012-04-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The International Rehabilitation Council for Torture Victims (IRCT) says that after two months on hunger strike in Bahrain, its doctor found the condition of the Danish-Bahraini activist Abdulhadi al-Khawaja to be very poor.
“As a result of the hunger strike, al-Khawaja is in extremely poor health,” the IRCT says in a news release.
