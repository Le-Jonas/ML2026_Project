Title: Major_fire_in_Køge
Date: 2011-11-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A large storage facility near Køge is on fire and people in the area have been advised to stay indoors and close windows and doors due to heavy smoke.
“The fire is so extensive that we have chosen to use sirens to warn people and ask them to go inside,” says Carsten Andersen of the Central and West Zealand Police.
