Title: Burglar_king_goes_to_jail
Date: 2012-03-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 30-year-old man has been sent to jail for three years for his part in the theft and fencing of some 14 million kroners worth of designer goods.
The man has confessed his role in what has been a particularly energetic group of thieves, with Copenhagen Police saying that break-in statistics in the capital region are already looking a lot better.
