Title: Young_man_in_slave_case_detained
Date: 2011-09-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 19-year-old man from the Irish family accused of holding more than 100 slave workers in Britain, has been detained at Kastrup Airport and is to be returned to Britain.
A European warrant had been issued for the youth, who belongs to the Connors family, members of which were detained north of London last Sunday,
