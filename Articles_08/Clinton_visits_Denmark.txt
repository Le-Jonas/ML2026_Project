Title: Clinton_visits_Denmark
Date: 2012-05-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

U.S. Secretary of State Hilary Clinton is in Denmark today on an official visit, the first official visit by a secretary of state since Barack Obama became president.
Clinton is to meet Queen Margrethe at 10.30, after which she is to meet some 400 Danish students at Den Sorte Diamant and answer questions put to her on Twitter and Facebook. TV2 News will be broadcasting live from the debate.
