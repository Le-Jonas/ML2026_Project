Title: Noma_goes_to_Olympic_London
Date: 2012-04-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One of the world’s best chefs is to move his restaurant to Claridge’s in London during this year’s Olympic Games, with the world’s best restaurant using seasonal British instead of local Nordic ingredients.
“It’s all very exciting for us to be able to challenge ourselves in another country and see what we can create,” Redzepi tells Bloomberg.
