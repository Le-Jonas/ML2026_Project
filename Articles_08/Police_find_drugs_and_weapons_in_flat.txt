Title: Police_find_drugs_and_weapons_in_flat
Date: 2012-02-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Five men and a woman are to appear in a remand hearing today after police found 28.5 kgs amphetamines, 3 kgs cocaine, 3 pistols, two shotguns and a hand grenade in a flat in Lyngby yesterday.
“We had a good idea that the flat could contain a stock of amphetamines so we chose to act immediately,” says Insp. Steffen Thaaning Steffensen of the Copenhagen Police Narcotics section.
