Title: Mollydooker_beats_the_system
Date: 2012-07-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Danish wine importer has challenged the Swedish alcohol monopoly Systembolaget and won, making Australia’s Mollydooker wine available to a thirsty Swedish public.
Lars Møller of The Wine Company brought his case before the Swedish Alcohol Assortment Complaints Commission after Systembolaget initially accepted, but then banned Mollydooker The Boxer Shiraz from its shelves, according to Vinavisen.dk
