Title: DKK_10_bn_government_growth_plan
Date: 2011-08-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

<i>Edited by 
<a href="mailto:julian.isherwood@pol.dk">Julian Isherwood</a></i>
The current Danish government is prepared to spend over DKK 10 billion on creating growth in the Danish economy according to the “Sustainable Growth” package presented Tuesday by Prime Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen and Conservative Leader Lars Barfoed.
According to the package, the government plans to bring forward public investment in infrastructure projects in particular.
