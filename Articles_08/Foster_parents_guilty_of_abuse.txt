Title: Foster_parents_guilty_of_abuse
Date: 2012-04-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The foster parents in the Mern case in Denmark have been found guilty of abusing nine children in their care and have been sentenced to two years and two years and six months in prison.
The case, which has been running in the lower court for some four months, involves the abuse and maltreatment of nine children in the couple’s care between 1990 and 2010.
