Title: Danes_land_massive_dairy_order
Date: 2012-04-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

GEA Process Engineering of Søborg near Copenhagen has landed a major order from the Fonterra Cooperative Group in New Zealand for the world’s largest dairy spray drying plant worth EUR70 million.
“GEA Process Engineering has been in the business of developing and supplying dairy spray dryers for nearly 80 years now and we see dairy as one of our most important markets. Therefore, we have a continued focus on innovation and see this milestone order as proof of our leading technological position,” says GEA Process Engineering Executive Vice President Niels Erik Olsen.
