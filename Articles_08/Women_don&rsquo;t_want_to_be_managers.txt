Title: Women_don&rsquo;t_want_to_be_managers
Date: 2012-03-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Danish Chamber of Commerce survey about management ambitions shows that 52 per cent of Danes believe that “interested in management” is a statement best suited to men, with only one per cent believing it is best suited to women.
The survey was carried out among 1542 Danes in connection with European Commission discussions about introducing quotas to get more women onto company boards.
