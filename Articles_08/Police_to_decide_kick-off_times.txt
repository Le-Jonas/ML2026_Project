Title: Police_to_decide_kick-off_times
Date: 2012-03-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Crowd unrest during last weekend’s soccer match between AaB and FCK has caused Justice Minister Morten Bødskov to act with a suggestion that police authorities should be the ones to decide kick-off times for matches.
The referee stopped the AaB-FCK match briefly due to crowd unrest and fireworks being released onto the pitch.
