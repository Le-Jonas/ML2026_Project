Title: IC4_trains_can_brake
Date: 2012-06-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Despite a couple of braking issues with IC4 trains in the autumn of 2011, the model’s braking system does not seem to have inherent problems according to a report to be published today.
IC4 sets have been grounded since Nov. 15 as a result of the two brake mishaps, which did not cause any accidents.
