Title: Nordea_stock_trading_again
Date: 2012-07-17
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Trade in Nordea Bank AB stock was temporarily suspended today after a human error resulted in a stock exchange announcement by the bank being sent out a day early.
Nordea had planned to announce its overall group accounts tomorrow at 7 a.m. The stock exchange announcement, which was sent out early at 10.45 a.m. today, covered the bank’s Norwegian activities.
