Title: Unemployment_up_by_900
Date: 2012-06-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest unemployment figures from Statistics Denmark shows an increase in jobless from April to May of 900, with a total of 162,500 or 6.2 per cent of the labour force out of work.
Unemployment has remained between 6.1 and 6.2 per cent since February 2011.
