Title: Denmark_condemns_Iran
Date: 2011-11-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Foreign Minister Villy Søvndal has issued a condemnation of Iran in connection with the storming of the British Embassy in Teheran.
“On behalf of the government, I would like to condemn in the sharpest possible way the fact that demonstrators have stormed the British Embassy in Teheran. These attacks are outrageous and against international conventions,” Søvndal says on the foreign ministry website.
