Title: Vestager_Status_shows_worse_deficit
Date: 2011-10-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A detailed study of the former government’s budget proposal for next year shows that the deficit will be greater than the DKK 85 billion envisaged, Economy Minister Margrethe Vestager tells Berlingske.
“This means of course that there will be fewer funds. It also means that priorities will be even stricter. There is not funding for much of what we want to do,” Vestager tells Berlingske.
