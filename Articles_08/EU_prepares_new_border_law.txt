Title: EU_prepares_new_border_law
Date: 2011-09-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The European Commission is preparing a change in EU law which will give Brussels the decision-making rights to internal border controls, according to a report in Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung (FAZ).
According to the
FAZ report,
the new regime will remove the final decision-making authority on internal borders from national governments, allowing only limited national border regimes in connection with for example soccer games, larger political events, attacks, epidemics or natural disasters.
