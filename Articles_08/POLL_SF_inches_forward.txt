Title: POLL_SF_inches_forward
Date: 2012-04-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2 shows the Socialist People’s Party (SF) regaining some of its losses following the party’s congress two weeks ago.
According to the poll, the party would glean 7.7 per cent of the vote if an election were held now, a 2.1 percentage point increase on the last Megafon poll, which at 5.6 returned the lowest following ever in a Megafon opinion poll.
