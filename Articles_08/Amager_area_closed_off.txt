Title: Amager_area_closed_off
Date: 2012-06-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A relatively large area of Kløvermarken in Amager has been cordoned off due to a chemical spill in Vermlandsgade, according to DR.
“There has been a chemical spill at a company in Vermlandsgade, and there’s a yellow cloud down there,” says Copenhagen Police Duty Officer Mads Firlings.
