Title: Liberals_refuse_to_attend_negotiations
Date: 2012-06-18
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Negotiations between the government and Liberals on a tax reform have collapsed.
In today’s edition of Politiken, Economy and Domestic Affairs Minister Margrethe Vestager says she would like to see the Liberals today for negotiations on the tax reform – but the Liberals have declined.
