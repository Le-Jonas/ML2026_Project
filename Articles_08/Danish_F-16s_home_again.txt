Title: Danish_F-16s_home_again
Date: 2011-11-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish Air Force F-16 squadron that has been part of NATO operations over Libya, returned home to Denmark Wednesday after having flown 600 sorties and patrols from the Sigonella base in Sicily.
Originally destined to land at the Skrydstrup air force base, the six F-16s had to be re-directed to Karup due to bad weather and low fuel.
