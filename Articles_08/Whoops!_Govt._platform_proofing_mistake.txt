Title: Whoops!_Govt._platform_proofing_mistake
Date: 2011-12-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The SocDem-SocLib-SocPpl government platform says that the elderly can buy extra services from local councils – but the inclusion of the new experiment was a proofing mistake according to Economy Minister Margrethe Vestager (SocLib).
“”This was a proofing mistake,” Vestager tells Kommunen.
