Title: Voters_thumbs_down_Thorning
Date: 2011-11-07
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Democrats and Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt continue to battle with bad opinion polls – both voter sympathies for the party and the prime minister’s popularity.
In the latest opinion poll by Greens for Børsen, 46 per cent of those asked said they were unhappy with Helle Thorning-Schmidt’s handling of the premiership, with only 34 per cent saying they are satisfied.
