Title: Police_find_40_stolen_military_weapons
Date: 2011-11-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s anti-gang police unit has found an arsenal of 40 weapons which were part of a consignment stolen from an armoury at the Antvorskov Barracks on Jan. 4, 2009.
The weapons found at a cache in the Copenhagen suburb of Brøndby, include M95 and M96 rifles, seven Neuhausen pistols and two grenade launchers.
