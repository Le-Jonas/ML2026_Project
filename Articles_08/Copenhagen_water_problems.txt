Title: Copenhagen_water_problems
Date: 2011-11-04
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Many Copenhageners are without tap water this morning following several breaks in the water mains in the capital.
“The pressure in the network has been too high and caused the piping to break,” says Copenhagen Energy Spokesman Torben Haman Hansen, who says that the mains have been damaged in areas of Amager, Nørrebro, Østerbro and Valby.
