Title: Møller-Mærsk_returns_18bn_profit
Date: 2012-02-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A.P. Møller-Mærsk came out of 2011 with a profit after tax of DKK18.1bn, a 36 per cent drop on 2010, according to the company’s annual accounts.
Pre-tax earnings were at DKK50bn or a drop of seven per cent on 2010.
