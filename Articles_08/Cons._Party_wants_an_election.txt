Title: Cons._Party_wants_an_election
Date: 2011-08-25
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Conservative Party Political Spokeswoman Carina Christensen is urging the prime minister to call elections.
“One should be careful about giving the prime minister good avice, but Denmark needs an election. Many things just stop because people are waiting for this election. It would be a good thing to get it over and done with,” Christensen tells Politiken.dk.
