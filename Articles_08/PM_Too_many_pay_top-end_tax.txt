Title: PM_Too_many_pay_top-end_tax
Date: 2012-05-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The prime minister has praised the Socialist People’s Party (SF) initiative to reduce the number of people paying top-end tax.
“A fine proposal from SF..… But tax cuts must be fully financed, otherwise we erode our options for maintaining our welfare society,” Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt says.
