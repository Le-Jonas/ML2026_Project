Title: Govt._and_opposition_agree_on_budget_law
Date: 2012-03-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The government and opposition have reached agreement on a budget law that introduces a ceiling on state, region and council budgets, politiken.dk has learned.
The agreement means that every four years Parliament will determine a ceiling on budget deficits, with the first law to be effective in 2014.
