Title: Liberals_to_pay_for_smoking_cabin
Date: 2012-03-08
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Liberal Party has decided to pay the bill for the smoking cabin that was rented and installed in the prime minister’s office during Lars Løkke Rasmussen’s tenure as prime minister.
“The issue risks moving the focus away from the important political work that the Liberal Party has to carry out. So the party has decided to pay the bill,” Party Secretary Claus Søgaard-Richter says.
