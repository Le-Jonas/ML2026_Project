Title: Thunderclouds_on_the_way
Date: 2012-07-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The glorious summer weather will continue for the next couple of days, but on Friday evening a cold front will be crossing Denmark from the southwest bringing rain and thunderclouds with it.
Behind the front, cooler weather will be passing across Denmark from the southwest with temperatures dropping to between 17 and 22 degrees.
