Title: Cold_snap_to_continue
Date: 2012-02-06
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark has been in the deep freezer since the beginning of February, and although the next few days will see slightly milder temperatures, the mercury will remain below zero during the day, and well below zero at night. On Thursday, the Met Office is also forecasting snow and drifting snow.
“We are going to have to get used to the rather extreme temperatures. As far as we can see in the prognoses, there is no major change in the weather in sight,” says Met Office Senior Climatologist John Cappelen, who does, however, say it is possible that daytime temperatures could crawl just above zero in some areas next week.
