Title: POLL_Red_Greens_sponge_up_centre-left_votes
Date: 2012-03-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Red Green Party appears to be hovering up votes from the government parties, according to the latest Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2.
Today’s poll gives the party its highest ever following in a Megafon poll – at 9.2 per cent compared with 6.7 per cent at the last election in September 2011.
