Title: Unemployment_slightly_down
Date: 2012-04-26
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The latest figures from Statistics Denmark show a marginal drop in unemployment in March of 600 people, with 161,800 now unemployed at an unchanged 6.2 per cent of the labour force.
At the same time the labour force has shrunk by 30,000, according to the latest calculations.
