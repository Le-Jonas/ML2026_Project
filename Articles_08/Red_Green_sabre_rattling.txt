Title: Red_Green_sabre_rattling
Date: 2012-04-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Red Green Party is adamant – it must be given influence on the government’s welfare reform packages, or next year’s budget – and thus the government - is in danger.
“The more the government erodes away with the centre-right, the more the Red Greens will demand improvements in the budget,” Red Green Party Leader Johanne Schmidt-Nielsen says in Jyllands-Posten of transfer payment reforms that the government is planning with the centre-right.
