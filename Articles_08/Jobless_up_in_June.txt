Title: Jobless_up_in_June
Date: 2012-07-31
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Overall unemployment in Denmark rose by a seasonally adjusted 1,500 in June according to the latest figures from Statistics Denmark.
The new figures bring the number of people out of work to 164,200 or 6.3 per cent of the labour force compared to 6.2 per cent over the previous four months.
