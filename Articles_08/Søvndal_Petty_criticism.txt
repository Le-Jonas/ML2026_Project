Title: Søvndal_Petty_criticism
Date: 2012-03-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Foreign Minister Villy Søvndal is tired of the criticism that has been levied at him since last year’s election campaign over his dress and not least his linguistic abilities.
“The criticism of my person has been petty and strange. I am tired of critics being more interested in how I speak English, or whether I can tie a tie, than what I stand for. Good heavens, how petty can you get,” Søvndal tells Århus Stifstidende.
