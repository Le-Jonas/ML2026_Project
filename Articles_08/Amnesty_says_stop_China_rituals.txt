Title: Amnesty_says_stop_China_rituals
Date: 2011-10-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Amnesty International has called on the Danish government to use the occasion of the first Chinese foreign ministerial official visit in 11 years to openly address issues of human rights in China.
“The old rituals must stop now,” says Amnesty International Denmark Secretary-General Lars Normann Jørgensen.
