Title: Minister_finds_Braine_money
Date: 2012-05-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish World War I cemetery in Braine in Picardy in northern France will after all receive funding for its upkeep from Denmark.
Plans by Culture Minister Uffe Elbæk to axe funding for the cemetery as part of a government savings drive caused controversy, particularly from Mayor Jens Christian Gjesing of Haderslev in Southern Jutland. Haderslev is twinned with Braine where the cemetery contains the graves of southern Jutlanders who died in German uniform during World War I.
