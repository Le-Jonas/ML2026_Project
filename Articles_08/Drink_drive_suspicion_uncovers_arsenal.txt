Title: Drink_drive_suspicion_uncovers_arsenal
Date: 2012-07-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A young man who was stopped by police near Ikast on suspicion of driving under the influence of alcohol was instead found to have a cache of illegal weapons in his vehicle, according to reports Monday.
“He was stopped because we suspected he was driving under the influence,” says Supt. Ole Henriksen of the Central and West Jutland Police Force.
