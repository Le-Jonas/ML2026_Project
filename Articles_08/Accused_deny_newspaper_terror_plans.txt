Title: Accused_deny_newspaper_terror_plans
Date: 2012-04-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Four men charged with planning a terrorist attack at the JP/Politikens Hus newspaper building in central Copenhagen have pleaded not guilty to the charges.
The Glostrup courtroom was packed with the public and Danish , Swedish and international media as the four handcuffed men – three 31-year-olds and a 46-year-old - were led in under tight security.
