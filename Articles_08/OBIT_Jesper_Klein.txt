Title: OBIT_Jesper_Klein
Date: 2011-08-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

One of Denmark’s most popular actors and entertainers Jesper Klein has died after a short illness at the age of 66.
In a lengthy career which spanned radio, television, the stage and film, Klein’s unique vocal ability enabled him to play almost any part, and as a result he was also a much-used narrator.
