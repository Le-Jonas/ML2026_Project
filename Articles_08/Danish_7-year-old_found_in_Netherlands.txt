Title: Danish_7-year-old_found_in_Netherlands
Date: 2011-11-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A 7-year-old whose mother removed him from his father in Denmark, has been found in a refugee camp in Holland, according to the Jutland police.
Danish and Dutch authorities are now preparing to return the Danish boy to Denmark where his father has custody. A brother, over whom the mother has custody, is also to be returned to Denmark.
