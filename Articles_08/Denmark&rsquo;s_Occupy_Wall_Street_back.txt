Title: Denmark&rsquo;s_Occupy_Wall_Street_back
Date: 2011-10-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Police arrested 11 people last night for continuing their demonstration past the permitted deadline, but the group, which is Denmark’s counterpart to the US Occupy Wall Street demonstrations, is back at Town Hall Square in Copenhagen.
“They took us along for about an hour and we were charged with ‘crowding’. Shortly after our release there were some 30-35 people here again,” says one of the demonstrators, Casper Møller.
