Title: NEW_GOVT._The_ministerial_list
Date: 2011-10-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s new Social Democratic Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt presents her
Social Democratic-Social Liberal-Socialist People’s Party
cabinet today, with 23 ministers.
Several of the ministers – such as Bjarne Corydon (SocDem), Nicolai Wammen (SocDem), Uffe Elbæk (SocLib) and Christian Friis Bach (SocLib) are newcomers to Parliament. Two others are not MPs – Martin Lidegaard (SocLib) and Thor Möger Pedersen (SocPpl).
