Title: POLL_Social_Democrats_plunge
Date: 2012-06-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

After less than nine months in the driving seat, the Social Democratic Party of Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt has returned its worst ever poll result from Megafon.
If an election were held today, the Social Democrats would poll 16.9 per cent of the vote according to the Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2.
