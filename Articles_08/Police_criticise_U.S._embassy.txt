Title: Police_criticise_U.S._embassy
Date: 2012-03-30
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The U.S. Embassy in Copenhagen has issued a warning to U.S. citizens in connection with two demonstrations planned for tomorrow in Aarhus.
“The U.S. Embassy advises all U.S. citizens to avoid the vicinity of these events. If you find yourself near one of these demonstrations, please exercise caution and leave the area as soon as possible,” the embassy says on its website.
