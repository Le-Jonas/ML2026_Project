Title: MÆRSK_N._Sea_profit_of_DKK67bn
Date: 2012-03-01
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Since 1994, A.P. Møller-Mærsk has posted a profit of over DKK67bn after tax on its activities in the North Sea, according to calculations in Jyllands-Posten based on the company’s internal accounts.
Previously only total earnings for the 2004-2010 period have been made public, showing a profit of DKK33bn after tax.
