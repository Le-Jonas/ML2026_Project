Title: Postal_votes_burn_in_fire
Date: 2011-09-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A fire at the Citizen Service Centre (CSC) at Søndersø Town Hall on the island of Funen has wrecked postal votes up to next week’s general elections in Denmark.
Mayor Morten Andersen tells TV2Fyn that the North Funen Council will now painstakingly try to reconstruct the burnt votes.
