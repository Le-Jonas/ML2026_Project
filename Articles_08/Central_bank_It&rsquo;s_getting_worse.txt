Title: Central_bank_It&rsquo;s_getting_worse
Date: 2011-10-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The worsening European debt crisis is hitting Danish consumers and companies harder than the central bank Nationalbank forecast just a month ago.
But despite the inherent message of lower growth now and in the coming period, Nationalbank’s Governor Nils Bernstein has warned the government against introducing an economic kick-start.
