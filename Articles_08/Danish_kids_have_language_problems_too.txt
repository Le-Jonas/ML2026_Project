Title: Danish_kids_have_language_problems_too
Date: 2012-03-28
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Immigrant bi-lingual children are not the only ones to have problems with the Danish language according to a new Copenhagen Council survey.
The language capabilities of over a thousand Copenhagen five-year-olds were evaluated by pre-school teachers, with 655 seen as having a need for help. Ten per cent of these were ethnic Danish children.
