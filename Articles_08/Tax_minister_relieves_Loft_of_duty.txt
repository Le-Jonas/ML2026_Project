Title: Tax_minister_relieves_Loft_of_duty
Date: 2012-03-21
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Tax Minister Thor Möger Pedersen has relieved his permanent secretary, Peter Loft, of his duties at the ministry.
“I have today relieved Peter Loft from his duties as permanent secretary at the Tax Ministry until further notice,” the minister says in a press release.
