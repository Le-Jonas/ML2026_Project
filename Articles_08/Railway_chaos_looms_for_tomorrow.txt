Title: Railway_chaos_looms_for_tomorrow
Date: 2011-12-14
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

From Thursday morning the national railways service DSB is cancelling about half ot its departures across the country due to a strike among maintenance personnel, according to a corporation announcement.
Cancellations will affect S-trains, regional services and long-distance trains in an effort to secure services in connection with the Christmas vacations. Reservation services have been suspended.
