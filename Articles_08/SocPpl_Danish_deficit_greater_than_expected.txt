Title: SocPpl_Danish_deficit_greater_than_expected
Date: 2011-10-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A Socialist people’s Party MP has disclosed that the new government’s audit of ministerial spending shows a deficit of DKK 100 billion in 2012, rather than the DKK 85 billion forecast by the previous government.
“The fact of the matter is that the deficit is much greater than we had expected when the election was called,” MP Anne Baastrup tells DR P1.
