Title: Social_Liberals_on_track
Date: 2011-09-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Negotiations on forming the next government continue today, but it seems that most of the highly contentious issues in the tripartite cabinet negotiations between the Social Democrats, Socialist People’s Party and the Social Liberals have been solved.
Social Liberal Leader Margrethe Vestager is later today expected to meet her party’s Executive Board to put the various agreements before them.
