Title: Pressure_on_former_tax_minister_grows
Date: 2011-11-15
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Former Tax Minister Troels Lund Poulsen (Lib) is under increasing pressure to comment on allegations that his permanent under-secretary meddled in last year’s Copenhagen Tax Authority investigation of Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt’s (SocDem) private tax affairs.
Lund Poulsen has hitherto refused to comment on the issue, claiming he has a duty of confidentiality.
