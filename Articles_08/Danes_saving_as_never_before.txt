Title: Danes_saving_as_never_before
Date: 2012-03-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Financial insecurity in the form of increasing unemployment and falling property prices is making the average Dane save more and more.
According to figures from the Arbejdernes Landsbank, a typical Danish family saves up an average of DKK33,000 each year. The average over the past decade is at DKK23,000 according to Berlingske.
