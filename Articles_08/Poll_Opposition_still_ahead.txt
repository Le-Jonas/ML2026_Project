Title: Poll_Opposition_still_ahead
Date: 2011-09-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

With three days left before general elections in Denmark, the opposition is ahead and the country apparently heading for its first woman prime minister.
According to a new Megafon poll for Politiken and TV2News, the opposition alliance is heading for 92 mandates compared to the government’s 83 seats in the 179-seat Folketing or Parliament.
