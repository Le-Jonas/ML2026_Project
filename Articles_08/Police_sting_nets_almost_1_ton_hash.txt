Title: Police_sting_nets_almost_1_ton_hash
Date: 2011-10-05
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Five months of investigation have now culminated in 14 arrests and the confiscation of almost a ton of hash, according to police who have unearthed a Danish hash smuggling organisation.
The case started when police received information from Morocco that a ship carrying hash was on its way to Denmark.
