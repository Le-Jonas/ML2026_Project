Title: Moody&rsquo;s_threatens_to_downgrade_114_banks
Date: 2012-02-16
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Several leading Danish banks are under review by the Moody’s credit rating agency, and may have their ratings downgraded.
“Moody's Investors service has today announced rating actions affecting 114 financial institutions in 16 European countries,” Moody’s says in a release.
