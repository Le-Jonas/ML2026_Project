Title: DanID_warns_of_new_identity_theft_attempts
Date: 2012-05-03
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

DanID, which is the company behind the login systems for banks, among others, is warning of a new wave of attempts at identity theft from its online banking customers.
The difference between the current threat and similar previous threats is that all netbanks are involved rather than a single one.
