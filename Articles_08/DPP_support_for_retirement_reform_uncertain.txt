Title: DPP_support_for_retirement_reform_uncertain
Date: 2011-09-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Danish People’s Party (DPP) will not guarantee its continued support for the tit-for-tat agreement with the current government on early retirement reform in exchange for permanent customs controls at Danish borders.
The DPP Deputy Chairman Peter Skaarup says that the party’s support for the controversial reform of the early retirement system will not be finally decided upon until after the election on September 15th.
