Title: British_de-miners_hurt_in_Libya
Date: 2011-11-02
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Two British de-miners who work for the Danish Danchurchaid organisation have been injured while removing unexploded munitions from an area close to the Libyan city of Misrata, according to Danchurchaid.
“Two of our people were injured while they were working to remove unexploded munitions,” says Richard MacCormac, head of Danchurchaid’s de-mining activities..
