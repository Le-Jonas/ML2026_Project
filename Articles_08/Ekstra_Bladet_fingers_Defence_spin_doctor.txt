Title: Ekstra_Bladet_fingers_Defence_spin_doctor
Date: 2011-12-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The tabloid Ekstra Bladet publishes today excerpts of a taped conversation purporting to describe who leaked sensitive information about a 2007 deployment of elite Danish forces to Iraq.
The newspaper has announced it is to make the sound file, which was handed in to the newspaper on a USB stick on Friday, publicly available at noon today.
