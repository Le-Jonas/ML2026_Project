Title: Bornholm_Express_helps_9_sailors
Date: 2012-07-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

A German yacht with a broken rudder, and which had begun to take in water, broadcast a mayday message this morning before all nine aboard abandoned ship and took to the vessel’s liferaft.
The Bornholm Express ferry was first to reach the scene and remained with the group until a rescue launch from Denmark’s Baltic island of Bornholm arrived on the scene.
