Title: Billions_for_banks_again
Date: 2011-08-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Healthy Danish banks can look forward to a new round of support from the State, provided they are prepared to take over unhealthy banks.
A majority in Parliament is expected to begin debating the new Bank Package IV, which has emerged after negotiations over the past few days, either today or tomorrow, according to Børsen, which says that politicians have been worried that election campaigning could endanger its passage.
