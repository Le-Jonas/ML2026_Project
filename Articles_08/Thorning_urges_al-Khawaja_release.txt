Title: Thorning_urges_al-Khawaja_release
Date: 2012-04-10
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Prime Minister Helle Thorning-Schmidt has called for the release from prison in Bahrain of Abdulhadi al-Khawaja, a Danish-Bahraini activist who is two months into a hunger strike and reported to be in a serious condition.
“Denmark urges Bahrain to release the Danish-Bahraini citizen and human rights activist al-Khawaja,” Thorning-Schmidt said Tuesday during her weekly news conference, adding that she had written to King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa of Bahrain on the subject.
