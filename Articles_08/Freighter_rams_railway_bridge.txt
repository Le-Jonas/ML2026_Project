Title: Freighter_rams_railway_bridge
Date: 2012-03-29
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Train passengers planning to travel north of the Limfjord in northern Jutland can expect longer travel times for the next six months after a Finnish freighter rammed the only railway bridge over the fjord overnight.
The freighter seriously damaged the bridge which carries railway traffic between Aalborg and Nørresundby.
