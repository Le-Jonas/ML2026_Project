Title: Denmark_denies_India_visa_reports
Date: 2012-07-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s Foreign Ministry says it has no indication that Indian authorities have changed their visa practices in relation to Denmark, Sweden and Norway as claimed by the Indian newspaper The Telegraph.
“The Foreign Ministry has neither received formal information about such a step from the Indian authorities nor recently experienced the introduction of such measures,” the Foreign Ministry says in a news release.
