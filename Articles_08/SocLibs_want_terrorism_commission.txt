Title: SocLibs_want_terrorism_commission
Date: 2011-09-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Social Liberals, who are doing well in the polls up to Thursday’s election, are looking for a review of the Danish terrorism strategy.
“In the special situation in which we all feel like Americans somehow, because (Ed: 9-11) was an attack against the free society, legislation was perhaps somewhat more drastic than we would have introduced today. We feel that we may have gone too far back then,” Social Liberal Leader Margrethe Vestager tells Politiken.dk.
