Title: EDITORIAL_Formula_1_scandal
Date: 2012-04-13
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

With the life of the Bahraini-Danish human rights activist Abdulhadi Khawaja hanging in the balance, Bahrain is preparing for its biggest annual media stunt – the Formula 1 Grand Prix at the Sakhir circuit.
We find it inconceivable that the Formula 1 will race in Bahrain while Khawaja remains in prison.
