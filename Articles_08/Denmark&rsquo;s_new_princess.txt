Title: Denmark&rsquo;s_new_princess
Date: 2012-01-27
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Denmark’s newest member of the royal family has been shown to the public for the first time as Princess Marie and Prince Joachim left the Rigshospitalet hospital to go home three days after delivery.
The baby princess was born on Tuesday morning after a four-and-a-half hour delivery and measured 49 cms and 2,930 grammes at birth.
