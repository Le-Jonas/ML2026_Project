Title: Ombudsman_enters_Aarhus_Uni_fray
Date: 2012-02-23
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The Ombudsman has decided to investigate the controversy surrounding Aarhus University’s German Music Professor Linda Maria Koldau, who has received a caution from the university, seemingly in connection with her criticism of the institution.
Koldau has publicly criticised the Faculty of Humanities at the university, among other things as having a ‘top-down’ educational strategy which she says has deprived several generations of students the education they have a right to.
