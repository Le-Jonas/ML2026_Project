Title: Bank_robberies_down
Date: 2012-06-19
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The number of bank robberies in Denmark appears to be dropping considerably, according to Financial Services Union Denmark (FSUD).
In the First Quarter of this year, 21 bank robberies were registered – a reduction of 36 and 61 per cent compared with 2011 and 2010.
