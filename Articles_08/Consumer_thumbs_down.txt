Title: Consumer_thumbs_down
Date: 2011-10-20
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

Consumer confidence in Denmark has dropped for the fourth month in a row and has landed on an indicator of minus 6.6, according to the latest figures from Statistics Denmark.
The statistics reflect the population’s view of the current and future economic situation, with Statistics Denmark asking families how they see their and Denmark’s economic situation now compared to a year ago, and how they see developments in a year’s time.
