Date: 2011-10-12
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Julian Isherwood

The violent clashes in Egypt in recent days between the military and peaceful demonstrators augur ill for the continued reform process in the populous Arab country.
Since February, the Egyptian military has played a moderating role. It was a historic moment when the military establishment announced in the first days of the rebellion that troops would not be used against unarmed civilians.
