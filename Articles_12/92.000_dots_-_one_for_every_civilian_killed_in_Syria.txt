Title: 92.000_dots_-_one_for_every_civilian_killed_in_Syria
Date: 2016-02-11
Article Type: Danmark
Author: Kristian Jensen

Journalist
Nobody really knows the exact number of how many civilians have lost their lives during the Syrian civil war. Some
NGOs
suggest more than 300.000 have been killed while the
UN
simply stopped publishing a death toll due to lack of certainty.
The 92.000 used in this visualisation are based on data from Violations Documentation Center (
VDC
), which register the
name
,
age
,
gender
and
location
of the death. Though it isn't the perfect number, we know that every single dot represents the death of a known civilian.
