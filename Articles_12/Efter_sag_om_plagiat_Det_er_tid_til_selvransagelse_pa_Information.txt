Title: Efter_sag_om_plagiat_Det_er_tid_til_selvransagelse_pa_Information
Date: 2015-12-18
Article Type: Medier
Author: Anders Legarth Schmidt

Journalist
For mere end fire år siden bragte den anerkendte britiske avis The Guardian en artikel om den fyrede chefredaktør fra ugeavisen News of the World, der var ramt af en stor aflytningsskandale. Indledningen lyder:
»In 1997, when she was a 29-year-old feature writer on the News of the World, Rebekah Wade made a trip to Westminster to intercept the Conservative MP Jerry Hayes. The paper was planning to run an exposé of the married MP's affair with his 18-year-old lover, she told him«.
