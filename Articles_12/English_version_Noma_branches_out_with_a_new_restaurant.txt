Title: English_version_Noma_branches_out_with_a_new_restaurant
Date: 2015-11-07
Article Type: Mad
Author: The Noma family is about to expand with a sister restaurant. Around May 1, 2016, and just a stone’s throw away, the renowned restaurant on Strandgade is to open a less formal version of one of the world’s most celebrated gastronomic icons.

»We have found the team. From the maître, chef de cuisine, sous chef, sommelier and restaurant manager. We are ready«, says René Redzepi, Noma’s owner and co-founder.
