»Don't go chasing waterfalls«, »So I creep yeah...« eller hvad med »I don't want no scrub. A scrub is a guy that can't get no love from me. Hanging out the passenger side. Of his best friend's ride - trying to holler at me«?
Popgruppen TLC skabte i sin storhedstid i 1990'erne en stribe hits, der eroberede radioæteren, MTV og hitlisterne verden over og resulterede i grammyer og mere end 65 millioner solgte albums.
