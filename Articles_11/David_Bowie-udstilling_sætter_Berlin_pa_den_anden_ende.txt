Date: 2014-05-30
Article Type: Musik
Author: Erik Jensen

I can remember/ Standing by the wall/ And the guns shot above our heads/ And we kissed as though nothing could fall/ And the shame was on the other side.
David Bowie: ’Heroes’
Derude i det tomme ingenmandsland, hvor himlen over Berlin ramlede ind i Murens beton i samme grå nuancer, mødtes to elskende i et langt kys. Der, hvor alt håb ellers var ude, lyste lidenskaben med et op i tomheden.
