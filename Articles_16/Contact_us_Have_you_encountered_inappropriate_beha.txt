The debate about an inappropriate, discriminating, and sexist work culture in the restaurant industry is continuing.
Ibyen wants to investigate the work culture for employees in restaurants.
Have you encountered problematic behavior while working as a trainee, waiter, chef, or other job in a restaurant, and would you share your experience with us (anonymously if preferred)?
